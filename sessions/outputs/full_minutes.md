## 1 - Road to Greenest
_~18:00, 22 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. Eguzkilore is camped at a roadside fire, cooking a roe deer he hunted on a spit. The smell carries.
2. Drawn by the cooking smell, the rest of the party arrives in turn:
   - Nuberroca approaches first, hungry; Eguzkilore shares the food.
   - Sinsong arrives next. At some point during the gathering he casts a spell — his bald head glows brightly with the casting. Nuberroca, struck by the light, nicknames him *"Cabezabombilla"* (lightbulb-head) on the spot — the name sticks.
   - Nyx and Amidamaru arrive together — they had crossed paths earlier on the road and were already traveling as a pair.
   - Mialee arrives last, alone.
3. Over the meal, Nuberroca shares his mission: his alpine tribe's elders sent him out to investigate the rise of the undead in the pass. In response, Sinsong gives Nuberroca a vial of holy water, telling him it may help against the undead. Nuberroca pockets it to show the elders later — he doesn't know what it is, but takes Sinsong's word for its use.
4. The group decides to head to Greenest together. Approaching at dusk, they see the town under attack — fires across the rooftops, smoke rising, raiders in the streets. A **blue dragon** is visible in the sky, throwing lightning.
5. The whole party is *awestruck* by the sight of the dragon — its size and majesty, even as it kills.

## 2 - The looted house

1. The party moves into the town streets, looking to help where they can, and finds a townhouse being looted by a mixed group of kobolds and cultists, some of the cultists wearing Greenest guard livery.
2. Counting roughly half a dozen looters, the party sets up a stealth approach — the plan is to take them before any can call out and bring more raiders down on the street.
3. Sinsong casts a spell to prepare for the rush; his bald head flares with the casting and the light spills out into the street, giving away the party's position before they can move. **Stealth blown.**
4. Nuberroca charges first, sprinting through the doorway before the raiders can rally. Despite the lost stealth, the rest of the party still catches the looters by surprise — the goliath crashing in pins their attention long enough for the others to close.
5. The fight is short. The kobolds break and scatter, picked off by precise arrows as they run. The liveried cultists are cut down at the doorway. The house is cleared in under a minute, the looters dead.

## 3 - A desperate family
_~19:00, 22 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. In the town streets, while trying to help where they can, the party comes upon a family pinned by a kobold pack: a wounded mother holding the kobolds off to buy time, while her badly wounded husband herds their three children toward safety.
2. The mother is overwhelmed and killed before the party can close the distance — the kobolds drag her down while the heroes are still moving in.
3. The party engages, with the priority of keeping the father and the children alive.
4. Eguzkilore and Mialee loose arrows from cover, dropping kobolds before they can reach the children.
5. Nuberroca crashes into the front of the pack, axe swinging. Amidamaru pushes in behind him with his sword, finishing off the kobolds the goliath's charge knocks aside.
6. A kobold breaks through to the father; the badly wounded husband manages to fend it off himself.
7. Nyx rushes to help him and puts herself between the man and the kobold.
8. Sinsong swings his mace to help — and fumbles, the strike landing on Nyx instead of the kobold.
9. The kobolds are swiftly defeated, but the party comes out of it bloodied. The children and the husband huddle around the dead woman, weeping. The party gathers around them, offering what comfort they can while quietly acknowledging that they cannot linger here — the streets are still hostile, and the keep is the only safe place left.
10. The husband composes himself enough to introduce himself as **Cuth**, then echoes the urgency: they must make for the keep without delay. The party falls in with Cuth and the three children and presses on.

## 4 - River-bank skirmish

1. The most direct route to the keep is clogged with raiders — too many to push through with **Cuth** and his three children in tow. The party detours toward the river, hoping to skirt the worst of the fighting along the bank.
2. The detour brings them in sight of a patrol crossing the river to flank the keep approach: a band of kobolds with two large drakes wading the shallows alongside them.
3. The party tucks Cuth and the three children behind cover and moves to ambush the patrol before it can spot them.
4. Mialee takes an elevated vantage on the river bank and opens with her bow from cover.
5. Eguzkilore looses arrows alongside her, dropping the leading kobolds before they understand they're being shot at.
6. Nuberroca and Amidamaru break for the drakes — the most dangerous threats in the patrol — and meet them in the river itself. Nuberroca splashes into the shallows, water sloshing around his thighs, and brings one drake down in two heavy axe strokes. Amidamaru, more at home in water than on land, slips alongside the second with his sword, working the joints below the waterline, and finishes it as it tries to turn on him.
7. Nyx slips through the bushes along the shoreline, working around the patrol's flank, and drops a kobold from behind as it tries to bring its sling up.
8. With the drakes down and the flank broken, the patrol shatters: most kobolds turn to flee across the river, while two scrabble up the near bank to get at Mialee.
9. Mialee drops one of the climbers before the other reaches her vantage and wounds her at close range.
10. Sinsong moves up the bank and, with a heavy mace strike to the back, drops the kobold pressing Mialee.
11. The remaining kobolds break and run for the far bank, vanishing into the smoke.
12. The party retrieves Cuth and the children from cover and presses on toward the keep, the family in tow.

## 5 - Inside the keep
_~20:00, 22 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. The party reaches the keep gates with Cuth and his three children. The guards admit them, take charge of the family, and direct the party on to the governor.
2. Before heading inside, one of the heroes looks back from the wall: the raiders have closed a ring around the keep. The way they came in has shut behind them. The party is *locked in*.
3. **Tarbaw Nighthill**, governor of Greenest, receives them in worse shape than the title suggests — a man in his sixties with half his face wrapped in bandages, his right arm in a sling, and his pale blue tunic stained dark down one side. He has clearly had no time for more than the roughest first aid. He thanks the party for the rescue and gets straight to the situation.
4. Tarbaw lays out the situation plainly: the keep is ringed, and they need a way out — for messengers, for any defensive sortie, and for civilians still trying to reach the keep from outside. He mentions one option: an old tunnel under the keep that leads beyond the raider line. It has not been used in years and is reportedly infested with vermin; it has not been cleared.
5. At Tarbaw's call, his castellan steps forward: **Escobert the Red**, a shield dwarf whose tangled mop of bright red hair has earned him his name, an enormous ring of iron and brass keys jangling at his belt — he holds every lock in the keep. Escobert leads the party down to the tunnel entrance and hands them the keys.

## 6 - The Old Tunnel

1. The tunnel was built generations ago as a covert way to fetch water from the river during sieges — a covered passage running from the keep cellars down to the riverbank. Along its length, three small storage rooms are carved into the wall; at the far end, a collapsed section of stone opens into a small cave the original masons never sealed off.
2. The party descends with **Escobert**'s keys. The air is *dark, thick, and stale*, and the tunnel is alive with vermin: giant rats scurrying underfoot, and spider swarms boiling out of every corner as the lanterns reach them.
3. The party works through the tunnel. Eguzkilore and Mialee pick off the giant rats with arrows from a few paces back; Nuberroca crushes the rest at close range. When the spider swarms boil too close, Amidamaru drives them back with fire from his hand — bolts hissing into the dark, the swarms recoiling rather than pressing. The party clears most of the tunnel this way.
4. The fight is ugly even with the strategy — close work in a confined passage, weapons fouling on each other. Sinsong swings at a rat in the press and clips Mialee in the head with his mace instead.
5. They push into the first of the storage rooms. As the door opens, giant spiders drop from the ceiling — the room is a *long-undisturbed nest*, and the giants pour out in numbers the party did not expect.
6. Sinsong casts Sleep on the lead giant spider. The spell lands; the giant folds where it stands. The rest of the party closes and kills it in melee while it is helpless — a clean victory on the first of them.
7. The fight in the room turns into a brawl. Eguzkilore peppers one of the giants with arrows from the doorway until it is bristling with shafts, slowed and bleeding; Nuberroca cleaves the wounded creature down with his axe. Amidamaru's fire keeps the smaller spiders at bay so the others can work.
8. Another giant spider lands a bite on Nyx, and she goes down under the venom — when the others can next glance her way, she is breathing shallowly but holding, no longer bleeding out.
9. Mialee, still reeling from the earlier mace blow, takes a bite of her own and goes down beside Nyx, bleeding hard, the press of spiders giving no one room to reach her.
10. ***[EPIC]*** The fight bleeds back out into the tunnel. As Sinsong shifts to support the line, a spider swarm boils over him in a rush — fangs and venom finding every gap — and he is *dead before anyone can reach him*.
11. The party breaks for the keep, leaving the storage room behind. Four still on their feet, every one of them heavily wounded — Nuberroca and Amidamaru both a single bad hit from going down themselves:
    - Nuberroca scoops up Sinsong's body and Mialee, one slung over each shoulder — Mialee's bleeding has stopped at some point in the chaos, no one quite sure when.
    - Amidamaru picks up Nyx and falls in behind him.
    - Eguzkilore retreats last, alone, arrows nocked, covering the others as they go.
12. As the party clears the tunnel mouth, the last of the giant spiders withdraw deeper into the dark — both sides disengage. The lanterns' light pulls back; the tunnel goes quiet behind them.

## 7 - Aftermath
_~21:00, 22 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. The party stumbles back into the keep with their dead and wounded. Sinsong's body is laid out in the hall; the others triage the living — Mialee and Nyx still unconscious, Nuberroca and Amidamaru on their feet but bleeding from a dozen lesser wounds.
2. Bringing Sinsong back becomes the only thing that matters. The party fans out through the keep, asking every guard, healer, and refugee they pass — is there a cleric here, someone with the power to bring a dead man back?
3. They find one. A cleric stationed at the keep hears them out and agrees to perform the rite — for **600 gp**.
4. The party does not have the gold. They argue, plead, empty their pouches in front of him — it isn't even close. After a long look at Sinsong's body and at the exhausted handful still standing over it, the cleric agrees to perform the rite *on credit*.
5. The rite is performed. Sinsong jolts upright with a strangled scream, clawing at his face and arms as if dragging spiders off them, shouting about the dark and the legs and the bites. It takes the party a long minute to bring him back to his senses.
6. With Sinsong breathing again and the wounded patched up, the party finds a quiet corner of the keep and rests for a while. They eat what little they can, see to their wounds, and talk over what just happened — how badly the tunnel went, what they could have done differently, what they need to be sharper at next time. By the end of the hour the change is unmistakable — their hands are steadier than the rest alone could explain, their wounds are closing visibly, and the lessons of the disaster are pressed into them.

## 1 - Finishing the tunnel
_~22:00, 22 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. The heroes are at the keep, picking up after the tunnel disaster and Sinsong's resurrection. The night has not let up; the assault is still pressing on Greenest from every side.
2. **Tarbaw** briefs them: civilians are sheltering in the temple of Chauntea; the church is under attack and needs relief. Watching the party still bandaged from the tunnel disaster, he presses three of the keep's last healing potions on them before sending them out.
3. The party returns to the lower tunnel and finishes clearing it of the residual giant rats and stray spiders left from the disastrous spider-nest fight.
4. The tunnel is now fully cleared and rehabilitated as a covert way in and out, beyond the raider line.
5. Sinsong, still recovering, remains at the keep helping the wounded. Eguzkilore takes the battlements, pinning raiders to cover his friends' sortie.
6. The party emerges from the cleared tunnel and runs straight into a small kobold patrol.
7. The fight is short. Nuberroca subdues one kobold *alive* — pinning rather than killing — and questions it on the spot.
8. ***[EPIC]*** The kobold is collaborative but renders barely any useful information. Rather than dispatch it, Nuberroca restrains the kobold and keeps it as a *"pet"*, strapped to his chest like a baby.
9. Approaching the temple, Nyx and Mialee scout ahead in good stealth; Nuberroca trails slightly behind with Amidamaru and the restrained kobold. The goliath's bulk forces him to hang back rather than lead, and even there his heavy footfalls keep threatening to give them away.
10. The two scouts carry the party past further patrols unseen.

## 2 - Sanctuary rescue
_~23:00, 22 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. The temple of Chauntea is surrounded by an external wall, already breached at the front by the time the party arrives. From the outside, they can see a loose patrol circling the perimeter — three cultists, ten kobolds, and two ambush drakes, dispersed.
2. The party closes on the wall as soon as the patrol leaves the area. Mialee climbs onto Nuberroca to peer into the temple compound, and from the elevated angle she spots two more enemy groups:
   - At the back door, four cultists and four kobolds — clustered tightly — are trying to burn it down, failing miserably and producing a prodigious smoke cloud that obscures the area.
   - At the front door across the compound, a menacing-looking cultist leader directs two cultists working a battering ram against the temple door, with six kobolds standing guard around them.
3. The rhythmic thuds of the ram already echo through the temple — the party can hear them clearly even before Mialee gets eyes on the front-door group.
4. The party scales the perimeter wall at the rear, falls on the arson detail, and kills them swiftly under cover of the cultists' own smoke. The ram keeps battering the main door.
5. The cuts the cultists landed on Nuberroca start closing with a *blue glow* while Mialee strips the robes off two of the dead cultists — one for herself and a spare for a partner — before pushing in.
6. Inside the temple, the civilians are panicked; calming them takes several minutes, and the heroes succeed only with the help of **Eadyan Falconmoon**, the half-elf priest in charge of the temple. Through the whole dialogue, the front-door ram keeps beating, each strike echoing through the sanctuary — a constant reminder that they are still in danger.
7. As the group is finishing the extraction through the back door, the main door finally gives way in a shower of splinters — the cultist leader's ram team is breaking through. At almost the same moment, outside, the loose patrol has drifted dangerously close to the back door.
8. Mialee creates a magical illusion at the main entry, pulling the attention of both cultist groups back toward the temple's entrance. The party uses the gap to slip out the back of the temple.
9. At the perimeter wall, Amidamaru and Nuberroca lift and steady the civilians across between them, one by one — slow but unobserved. Up close, the kobold strapped to Nuberroca's chest sets off fresh panic in some of the civilians as the goliath hoists them up.
10. Reaching the river with all civilians unharmed, the group heads back along the stream toward the keep.

## 3 - Ambush in the river
_~00:00, 23 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. The way back follows the stream in the dead of night. Frogs croak in the shallows. The only visible lights are the distant fires and a luminous stone Amidamaru gave Nuberroca to keep them from stumbling in the dark.
2. Almost back at the tunnel mouth, the stone gives them away: a cultist patrol — five kobolds and three cultists directed by an acolyte — spots the light and ambushes the party.
3. The opening is brutal: kobolds fire their slings from cover along the bank while cultists rush in from concealed positions among the trees. The party is caught flat-footed.
4. Panic takes the civilians — some plunge into the shallows, others crash into the underbrush along the bank. **Eadyan** tries to hold them together, but the chaos outruns him; within breaths, **half the group is lost in the dark**.
5. Nuberroca answers with a counter-charge, axe up, the cloth-strapped kobold on his chest squealing as the goliath crashes into the lead cultists. Nyx moves closer to the goliath, shooting arrows as she goes.
6. The patrol's first blows land hard on the charging goliath, but a *blue glow* runs across each fresh cut and closes it partway.
7. The barbarian's charge halts when the acolyte commands him to retreat. With his *body betraying him*, the goliath falls back, leaving Nyx exposed.
8. In Nuberroca's absence the patrol surges around Nyx — kobolds and cultists pile on the small ranger, landing hits before she can give ground.
9. Nyx drops the lead kobold with a precise thrust of her shortsword, but the rest keep coming; she holds, bloodied and pressed.
10. Amidamaru intervenes to save Nyx, wading in beside her. He cuts down two kobolds in the surge before a cultist drives a spear into him and drops him.
11. Nuberroca recovers his senses, downing the acolyte with a precise axe throw as an act of revenge.
12. Hidden along the bank, Mialee picks her moment and subdues one of the remaining cultists with a clean knockout. With the acolyte dead and one of theirs taken, the rest of the patrol breaks for the trees.
13. Nuberroca hurls a hand-axe after one of the fleeing cultists; the throw buries itself between the shoulder blades and drops him short of the treeline.
14. With the fight done, the goliath turns to Amidamaru — the artificer's bleeding has stopped at some point in the chaos, breath shallow but holding.

15. Sizing up Amidamaru's deadweight, the goliath decides he can't carry both the artificer and the kobold strapped to his chest. He rips the bundled kobold off and hurls it up into the high branches of a nearby tree — out of reach, out of earshot, unable to alert anyone.
16. Only as he stoops to lift Amidamaru does the goliath see the obvious: the stream will carry the weight for him. He ties a rope around the artificer and drags him through the shallows, the current bearing what his shoulders no longer have to — the kobold-throw, in hindsight, unnecessary.
17. The party rounds up the scattered civilians, returning to the keep with the rescued people, the captured cultist, and the dragged Amidamaru.

## 4 - Recovery at the keep

1. Inside the keep the air is loud with the night's wounded — soldiers and townsfolk alike. **Eadyan** and the rescued civilians are taken in; healers move to those who need them most.
2. Sinsong, helping at the keep tonight rather than in the field, meets the party as they bring the cultist in and takes the prisoner's robe — disguise material for whatever the night still has.
3. The cultist is then handed to **Escobert the Red**, who sets to interrogation in one of the cellars below the great hall.
4. The party finds a quiet corner and takes a short rest. This one is *unusually restorative*: aches fade faster than they should, lesser cuts close on their own, and old wounds feel lighter by the end of the hour. The party comes out of it cleaner than anticipated.
5. Once rested, they take the time to restock and reset. Keep quartermasters refill ammunition; Nuberroca gets a fresh pair of hand-axes; the smiths patch dented armour and re-edge chipped weapons; the party tends to the wounds the night left them.
6. Refreshed and rearmed, the party climbs to the keep's battlements to find Tarbaw and learn what the night still wants of them.

## 5 - Dragon attack
_~01:30, 23 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. The party reaches **Tarbaw** at the parapet, mid-conversation about the night's run of missions, when one of the watch guards shouts the warning — *the dragon is coming* — his voice fraying with terror as he points to the sky.
2. The blue dragon arrives fast, opening from above with a *lightning breath* that sweeps the rooftop in a single arc, leaving a scorched line in the stone and the remains of a guard smoldering.
3. The dragon dives in for its first close pass, sweeping through the battlements: it lashes with its claws, snatches one guard from the parapet and devours him without slowing, raking Tarbaw in passing — face cuts re-opened, blood pouring.
4. By the end of that first pass two of the keep's guards are dead and several more wounded; the parapet is slick with blood.
5. Mialee, frightened beyond reason, presses herself behind cover and runs down the stairs, choosing her safety over the fight.
6. Nuberroca answers with axe and rage, charging into the open to challenge the dragon directly. Nyx and Amidamaru engage from the parapet with everything they have, slowly overcoming their fears.
7. Challenged, the dragon pulls back to range. Most of its attacks come at distance now — the lightning breath sweeping the rooftop again and again.
8. Between passes the party keeps firing on it: Amidamaru looses arrow after arrow, Nuberroca hurls hand-axes when its arcs bring it within reach, and the keep guards work their crossbows from the wall. Most of the volley shatters against the dragon's powerful scales — arrows snapping, bolts skittering off into the dark, hand-axes chiming like struck anvils.
9. ***[EPIC]*** Nyx, sighting between the dragon's passes, threads a longbow shot into one of its eyes — a *precise arrow* that scores where the scales don't shield. The dragon pulls higher with a roar of rage, blood streaking the side of its head.
10. On the next close pass, the dragon dives in to rake Nuberroca's flank with its claws before pulling up; the goliath's retaliating axe-stroke opens a cut along its foreleg as it banks away.
11. After a lightning discharge that only wounds one guard, the dragon dives in again, snatching another guard from the parapet — a snapping bite, and the man is gone.
12. On its next pass, the dragon slashes Nuberroca again and lifts a guard off the wall, hurling him over the parapet — the man falls to his death on the ground far below.
13. With the parapet near-empty around them and another claw-rake bearing in, the surviving party retreats off the rooftop into the stairwell down to the keep — the doorway narrow enough to deny the dragon's bulk.
14. The dragon presses against the stair opening from above, forcing a foreleg through, claws raking blindly for the heroes as they crouch below the threshold.
15. Nuberroca, axe braced under the lintel, lands a clean stroke on the reaching claw — not deep, but enough to draw blood and sting.
16. ***[EPIC]*** Bleeding from the eye-shot, the foreleg cut, and now the wound on its claw, the dragon pulls its leg back out of the stairwell, looks down at the heroes saying *"esperaba a un grupo de héroes, no me han pagado para esto"*, and turns away from the village.
17. The party watches it vanish into the night, the keep still standing.
18. They climb back up onto the rooftop and turn to the wreckage — wounded guards strewn across the parapet, the dead among them, and Tarbaw unconscious in his own blood — and set to tending whoever can still be saved.
19. As the party works through the parapet's wounded, Tarbaw recovers quickly — by the time they lift him clear, his bleeding has stopped and his eyes are flickering open.

## 1 - Rooftop briefing
_~02:00, 23 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. The party is on the rooftop of the Greenest keep after the dragon's retreat. **Tarbaw Nighthill** is in poor shape from the dragon attack: the half-tended cuts on his face have torn open and are weeping blood through hastily wrapped bandages, his arm hangs in a hastily re-tied sling, and he sways on his feet, visibly fighting to stay upright as he speaks. Every sentence comes shorter than the last.
2. A keep guard arrives with a low-voiced report: cultists are burning the mill, Greenest's main grain stockpile and bakery. Guards will need 15–20 minutes to muster a force. Tarbaw asks the party if they can move faster.
3. Mialee voices the strategic case: *"si esta gente pierde el molino, se va a morir la mitad del pueblo de hambre."* Nuberroca asks for healing supplies before they go; Tarbaw replies that he already gave them three healing potions and that the rest are needed for the wounded from the dragon attack.
4. The party decides to leave by the rehabilitated lower tunnel rather than the front gate, which is less secure. Sinsong and Eguzkilore each put on a cultist robe as disguise.

## 2 - Approach to the mill

1. Party leaves through the tunnel and works southwest along the river toward the mill. It is the dead of night; some fires elsewhere in town are dying.
2. From cover, the party spots another armed group moving among the houses to the west; they choose to skirt them rather than engage and press on toward the mill.
3. Mialee notices the smoke from the mill is *white*, not the dark colour expected from burning grain or wood. Nyx seconds the observation a moment later. The party files it as suspicious and presses on.
4. Sinsong casts Guidance on Mialee so she can sneak ahead and scout. Mialee creeps in close enough to identify the patrol's composition without being seen: a guard drake, several kobolds, and one leading cultist.
5. As the rest of the party closes in, the drake catches their scent and moves toward them.
6. ***[EPIC]*** Nyx opens with a longbow shot, a critical hit that drops the guard drake in a single arrow. The patrol's dangerous element is gone before the rest can react.
7. Mialee stealthily attacks from cover, killing a kobold in a single hit.
8. Eguzkilore looses an arrow that ends another kobold.
9. Sinsong casts Fire Bolt, ending a distracted kobold before he can react.
10. Amidamaru casts Fire Bolt at the leading cultist, a direct hit that kills him outright.
11. The surviving kobolds break and flee.

## 3 - The mill ambush

1. From behind a nearby tree, Sinsong uses his magical hand to open the front door while the rest of the party holds ready in case anyone shoots from inside. Nothing happens.
2. Through the open door they see the ground floor: scattered grain sacks, the millstone still grinding, and a pulley system rising to a second level. The mill looks empty; the smoke is coming from upstairs.
3. Eguzkilore enters first; Mia, Nyx, and Amidamaru follow him in quickly, spreading across the ground floor. Sinsong and Nuberroca stay by the doorway.
4. With the party committed inside, the ambush is revealed: a volley of spears and arrows comes down from concealed attackers in the rafters. Two of the throws crit on Eguzkilore, dropping him unconscious. Nyx is hit and badly hurt.
5. Immediately after, ten cultists drop from the rafters into melee, five wearing keep-guard livery.
6. A livery cultist drives a spear under Nyx's armpit, a heavy hit, but she holds.
7. One of the cultists in keep-guard livery steals Eguzkilore's short sword off his unconscious body.
8. Amidamaru casts a cone of flame from his mouth that engulfs four cultists, being careful to spare Nyx from the conflagration. Three cultists die outright in the fire.
9. Mialee shoots from behind a crate, fails the attack, and retreats to safety outdoors.
10. Nuberroca rages forward and engages two livery cultists at the doorway, missing with both attacks. As the cultists land their hits, a *blue glow* runs across each cut and closes it partway.
11. Nyx stabilizes Eguzkilore and downs a healing potion to recover. She's back in fighting shape.
12. Sinsong tries to cast Fire Bolt at one of the cultists, but the spell fumbles, fizzling and sparking in his hand without ever leaving.
13. Recovering quickly from his disappointment, Sinsong leans into his cultist robe disguise: he hugs Amidamaru as if *"arresting"* him, selling the moment to the nearby ambushers. The deception lands hard, and none of the nearby cultists break through it.
14. A livery cultist hits Nyx with his spear, dropping her unconscious. Amidamaru, unaware of his downed friend, attacks the cultist beside him with knives.
15. Sinsong uses his magic to stabilize the halfling.

## 4 - Looting the cultists

## 6 - The light of Elturel
_~19:00?, 30 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. Forcing the horses again, the party pushes to reach Scornubel that same day rather than camp on the road again. ## 8 - Four figures

## 9 - The ambush turned

1. ## 12 - Push to Elturel
_~19:00, 1 Uktar 1489 DR_

1. Rather than camping on the road, the party decides to force the pace to make Elturel that same night — an extra three hours of hard riding beyond the day's march.

## 8 - The trolls come

12. Nyx picks target after target with the longbow, settling into a rhythm — one shot, step, pick again — and chews through the badly-wounded trolls one by one; early in the fight she puts one on the ground with a particularly devastating opening shot. Nuberroca lays a hand on Amidamaru's shoulder; something fierce passes between them and Amidamaru steadies. Mialee slips an arrow in from the flank where the troll cannot see her. Eguzkilore follows up with a shot straight into a downed troll to make sure it does not get up.

3. Amidamaru lays a healing on the wounded; Paco politely refuses, telling him he is holding up. Meanwhile Mialee moves to search the bodies of the guards they had marked as cultists, finding some spare coin but nothing useful.
4. By the fire, Amidamaru and Nuberroca argue over the credit — Amidamaru claims every troll kill; Nuberroca insists he speared each one and never saw them get back up.
