### Knowledge gained
- **The Companion.** The Companion appeared over Elturel forty-seven years ago and ended a vampire plague; popular tradition ties it to mayor *Thavius Kreeg*.
- **Elturel's two-order rule.** Order of the Companion and Order of the Hellriders share the two-suns emblem and jointly run the city since the Companion arose.
- **Baldur's Gate contact.** Merchant **Ackyn Selebon** works in the north of the city, near the Black Dragon Gate.
