## 1 - The interrogation that gave nothing
_~11:00?, 11 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. In the strongroom, over the hour and a half since the yard quieted, Nuberroca has worked the two prisoners without a hand laid on them — what they were doing at the roadhouse, why the boy's legs twisted mid-flight, why they follow the Cult of the Dragon, how they knew **Bog Luck**, what was in the strongroom, why they were spying, and who **Sarek** is to them [Td:28-37]. The two give nothing — *"no dijeron una puta mierda"* [Td:39]. He notes they wear no cult tattoos, no pendants, no cult signs; only a small mark of **Chauntea** that **Lathar** carries [Td:41, Td:685-686].
2. Sinsong asks for a moment alone with each prisoner [Td:43]. He kindles a small flame in his hand and shows it to Lathar; Lathar looks at the Chauntea mark and answers that he will not change his faith *"aunque me vaya la vida en ello"* [Td:687-693]. **Dem** by this time is crying, catatonic, pleading only to be let go [Td:684 ?ord, Td:693-694].
3. Mialee comes in on Sinsong's wordless signal, two blades out. Sinsong says low: *"corta las piernas"* [Td:699-707]. Dem goes to pieces [Td:709]. Sinsong eases off — *"parece que son civiles, de verdad"* [Td:710]. Nyx agrees: *"tiene toda la pinta de que son civiles, inocentes"* [Td:718-720].

## 2 - The yard turns

1. An hour and a half later, scraping noises outside [Td:46-48]. Eguzkilore slips to the doorway: one of the carnomorphs is in the yard, hunched over one of the piled bodies — rat-sized, a knot of broken bone and fur that cracks on the stones with every step [Td:62-71, Td:268-276]. He puts an arrow into it and a second as it convulses and dies [Td:138-152]. More come over the fence — three, four of the small ones scrambling under a cart [Td:170-193, Td:287-294].
2. Pottery breaks on the roof overhead [Td:345-352]. A larger one — dwarf-sized, winged, built of the same stitched bone — plummets from above and lands flat on Mialee, pinning her [Td:430-443]. She twists her blades up to catch its jaws and keeps the teeth off her [Td:443-446].
3. Mialee cuts loose and backs off, calling for cover [Td:450-456]. The wings tear after Eguzkilore and the claw tears open a seam in his chest that barely bleeds — but something inside it begins to grind, a dull inner fury that drops him into a sudden, dragging weariness and will try to swell after every fight he lives through [Td:497-515].
4. Sinsong throws a bolt of fire at the big one and misses, calls Mialee into the strongroom, drops a ward on Eguzkilore [Td:462, Td:544-554, Td:581-589]. Nyx puts her mark on the Metagorger and the next arrow goes clean through — the thing drops like a sack of broken bone, three shafts standing out of its back [Td:594-603]. Eguzkilore steadies himself long enough to clamp down on the bleed inside and holds [Td:604-606].

## 3 - The haul

1. The yard quiet, the party splits. Eguzkilore turns out to have had Bog Luck's keys on him since yesterday without saying so; Nuberroca takes the ring, works through two until the right one turns, and goes into Bog Luck's room with Nyx [Td:744-765]. Sinsong, Eguzkilore and Kaelthar stay in the strongroom and warehouse and start taking everything on the shelves at once [Td:862-905].
2. In Bog Luck's room, the account books and ledgers look clean — names, wages, deposit-and-return columns, the inventory of the strongroom [Td:769-775, Td:921-934]. The books on the shelf are a surprise: philosophy and natural history, out of place for a half-orc innkeeper [Td:770-771]. Nuberroca takes two of the natural-history volumes [Td:789-794]. Nyx and Nuberroca take the room apart — mattresses, drawers, false bottoms, walls, floor, ceiling — and come away with nothing [Td:852-963]. Sinsong, drifting between rooms during the search, pockets a stash of forty gold without announcing it [Td:842 ?ord, Td:3168-3174].
3. In the strongroom, Sinsong lifts a chest and triggers a trap — the second click after the first, a green cloud boiling out — and the three in the chamber (Sinsong, Mialee, Kaelthar) all catch a lungful [Td:874-895]. Eguzkilore had stepped outside for air before the lid came up [Td:897-900]. All three cough through; the chamber reeks of acid, and Sinsong's old burn-tattoo on his back flares at the heat of it [Td:886-895].
4. Someone realises the two prisoners were left with no one minding them for an hour or more. Dem is gone. Lathar is still chained, still there [Td:981-995]. The warehouse's bags and backpacks come up next; the party pile the lot [Td:1013-1016].

## 4 - What Lathar knows

1. Nyx, piecing together what Lathar and Dem have — and haven't — said against the visions she and Mialee had the night before, says it out loud: the **lizardfolk** of the swamp are not merely passing through this country, they *collaborate* with the Cult — they are the ones carrying the cargo the party has been chasing [Td:1003-1010].

## 5 - Dem walks

1. Nyx unties the boy. Nuberroca asks where he lives — Waterdeep, he answers, and he will wait for the next caravan [Td:1058-1081]. Nuberroca gives him food from the cult packs and ten pieces of silver; sees the boy's eyes and adds another ten [Td:1097-1108]. He offers Dem a place as his squire for a wage — Dem hesitates, then shakes his head: *"me dan ustedes mucho miedo"* [Td:1110-1117]. He takes the coin and runs for the stables [Td:1136-1148, Td:1166-1192].

## 6 - The yard dismissed

1. Nuberroca puts his shoulder to the upstairs door, which was wedged shut from inside, and finds the rest of the roadhouse's people in a corner with kitchen knives — the cook **Grisel** out front with a carving blade, a makeshift barricade of chairs and benches [Td:1159-1165]. Kaelthar steps in open-handed; the party tells them Bog Luck's crew were the thieves, the proof is in the room they were guarding [Td:1176-1180, Td:1211-1214].
2. Nuberroca, blunt, offers Grisel first blood — walks up unarmed and lets the cook stab him to see what a carving fork can do; the point lands clean at the throat and does not scratch him [Td:1225-1234]. He walks it back and warns them to defend themselves not from the party but from what else is out there [Td:1230-1232], and leaves them with a line he turns over for a moment: *"si mis dioses me hubiesen dado el don de la palabra seguiría hablando con vosotros, pero como no es así, os dejamos vivos"* [Td:1232-1235]. The party passes a hat around: a piece of gold each toward getting the civilians somewhere else [Td:1251-1263].

## 7 - The rope goes down
_~15:00, 11 Hammer_

1. Mialee suggests a long rest, back in the yard — the tunnel can wait for the morning [Td:1284-1298]. Nyx heads to the trapdoor to run a rope while the rest settle. The shaft drops in a steep ramp, pitch-black beyond ten feet, carved rough and winding north-west [Td:1265-1281 ?ord, Td:3165-3166].
2. The galloping comes before anyone is laid out — hooves at speed on the road, fists on the main gate, five or six voices on the other side [Td:1319-1337]. The party moves for the tunnel. Nyx is still on the rope [Td:1343-1347].

## 8 - Sarek breaks the door

1. ***[EPIC]*** The main door explodes inward and **Sarek** is through, snarling: *"os teníamos que haber matado cuando tuvimos ocasión, sabíamos que erais unos putos asesinos"* [Td:1349-1357]. Sinsong, poised and waiting, throws a bolt of fire square into his face — the hit takes him off his feet, and as he tries to rise his ankle folds under him and will not hold [Td:1358-1372].
2. Mialee drops a sheet of fog across the doorway behind the first wave, dense enough to swallow Kaelthar whole, and shouts the party into the tunnel [Td:1424-1442]. One of the cultist guards rises on a working of flight, drifting ten feet above the mist to spot Nuberroca over the inner rail — Nuberroca and Nyx both open on him as he clears the cover and leave him reeling, twenty-one points of shot and halberd between them [Td:1518-1566].

## 9 - The tide at the door

1. The yard fills — guards and spellcasters, Sarek still on the ground. Nuberroca wades in with the halberd, guard down and striking without care for the opening it gives, and cuts down **Bael** and **Xod** on the first pass; **Reurig** takes the follow-up as he closes [Td:1584-1649].
2. Mialee lays a poisoned sliver in behind Sarek's ribs — the sickness takes [Td:1687-1704] — and sends a floating, unseen hand at his throat to pluck the amulet off him mid-chant [Td:1712-1735]. He catches the cord in his palm before she can snap it, and the hand slides away [Td:1731-1735].

## 10 - Who believes in Sinsong

1. Sinsong stops in the middle of the yard, thinking of the ring of guardians he had been about to call down, and remembers to put the question he had forgotten at the start: *"¿quiénes creéis en Sinsong?"* [Td:1900-1915]. Nuberroca raises a hand — *"tengo una lucecita adentro"* — more guilt than conviction [Td:1923-1933]. Kaelthar follows: *"ésta es mi última vez que te doy mi aprobación"* [Td:1921-1922]. Nyx last — potential, not divinity [Td:1937-1949]. Three. Sinsong tallies and decides it is not enough — *"o funciona entero o no funciona"* [Td:1957] — holds the slot and steps away from the ring unmade, out of reach [Td:1952-1970].

## 11 - The oil and the guardians

1. Rounds pass without the ring. The cultist on the ceiling sings down a line of fire that catches Nuberroca across the shoulder, a searing stripe the length of his back [Td:1972-1991]. **Ibis** opens Nuberroca's side with a long sword and the goliath is wearing wounds [Td:2015-2021].
2. Sinsong tries the invocation a second time and this time the ring blazes around his head and reaches out — Sarek shakes it off, two of the others crumple inside the brightness [Td:2210-2240].
3. ***[EPIC]*** Kaelthar hefts a canister of lamp oil out of his pack, slings it the length of the yard, and bursts it over Sarek's shoulders — Mialee calls out the moment and both halves of the throw land clean [Td:2378-2410]. Sarek is soaked [Td:2401-2410]; from here on any flame that touches him will cost him more than the hit itself [Td:2420-2424].
4. Mialee's arrow finishes **Zephur** in the same breath [Td:2491-2502]. She sends an unseen hand across the yard to pull the strongroom door shut behind the party, then drops into the shaft and takes the first stretch of rope at a run [Td:2505-2510].

## 12 - The halberd clears

1. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca comes off the fog line with the halberd horizontal and shears into **Nefion** — the hit lands and the divine fire in him flares black at the strike, the follow-through taking **Isaiash** at his side. The second swing kills Nefion where he stands and the floating blade hanging over him winks out [Td:2602-2648].

## 13 - The pile at the trapdoor

1. The shaft bottlenecks. Nyx and Sinsong go down first and land one on top of the other at the bottom [Td:2529-2534]. Kaelthar steps on the mist and reappears at the far doorway, a hard charge carrying him the rest of the way through, and the trapdoor comes down behind him [Td:2109-2130 ?ord, Td:2755-2757].
2. Earlier in the pass Nefion had hit Nuberroca and set a floating blade on him in the same breath — the conjured sword took the goliath hard across the shoulder, the nerves in his left arm burning out and his readiness to answer blows with it [Td:2559-2572]. Now a cultist with reach drops through the trapdoor onto Kaelthar — a crushing landing with the sword, and Kaelthar's left arm goes the same way Nuberroca's did [Td:2787-2800].
3. The trapdoor opens again from above and **Jyn**'s conjured sword reaches down the shaft to take the fingers of Kaelthar's left hand apart — Kaelthar drops what he holds, folds unconscious, and slides the rest of the way down the rope to the tunnel floor [Td:2864-2879].

## 14 - The wall of fire and the exit

1. Nuberroca scoops Kaelthar up off the stones and runs — thirty feet, then another thirty on the dash, the dragonborn on his shoulder [Td:2893-2910]. He sets Kaelthar down long enough to work a healer's kit over him and stop the bleeding before the ground takes him, then shoulders him again [Td:3066-3079].
2. ***[EPIC]*** Nyx turns on the stairs and puts a shot clean through one of the pursuers who had come down the rope — the arrow goes in to the fletching and he drops where he hung on the line [Td:3002-3005]. Sarek has taken enough to fall by then; Sinsong throws a bolt of fire onto his oil-soaked body in the tunnel mouth, the oil catches full, and a five-foot wall of flame drops across the entrance, cooking anyone who tries to cross [Td:3028-3036]. **Isaiash** from the lip of the trapdoor roars after them: *"os encontraremos y no pararemos hasta daros caza, malditos asesinos"* [Td:3118-3125].

## 15 - The dark and the speech
_~16:15?, 11 Hammer_

1. The pursuit falls off. The tunnel runs crude and serpentine, cut by rough tools and claws, no light at the far end though an hour has passed and daylight should have reached a mouth by now [Td:3162-3167]. Sinsong wakes Kaelthar with a word of healing and the dragonborn relights the ward of faith around himself for a lamp, then settles on a simple glow laid against his talisman instead to spare the stronger working [Td:3143-3160 ?ord].
2. Sinsong gathers the party in the dark and lays out what he had held back: forty pieces of gold he had turned up in Bog Luck's room, eight for each of them [Td:3168-3174]. *"Me gustaría que empezaseis a confiar en mí"* [Td:3170]. Nuberroca puts a hand on his shoulder: *"Cabezabombilla, estos días has luchado bien. Creo que tienes madera de campeón"* [Td:3178-3181]. Sinsong, quieter: *"Gracias, Nuberroca, sé que eso significa mucho para ti"* [Td:3181]. The party moves on into the dark, Nuberroca carrying Kaelthar, no sound of pursuit behind [Td:3184-3188].
