## 4. Events

1. Mialee lifts a pendant from Trepsin's pyre and consoles Sohey; the goblin then tears north at an unnatural speed.
2. ~14:00 — Sohey returns their stolen gear, magical spectacles and a magical pen; Mialee asks for proof and a delay.
3. Sinsong's healing exposes Eguzkilore's magic-rejecting affliction — blisters bloom wherever the magic touches his skin.
4. ~15:00 — The Nashkag totem is delivered, Nuberroca duels Sohey as farewell, and rum and a sealed ceramic jar change hands.
5. ~16:00 — The party treks three snowed-over hours; Nyx navigates by her own broken-branch markers back to the lodge.
6. ~19:00 — Talis greets them armed, Kushpia at her shoulder and two mercenaries in the hall; weapons stay drawn.
7. ~19:30 — **Ambush sprung** — Talis falls to her own wasp swarm; Kushpia and Sorvic killed; Nuberroca reclaims *Dragongleam*.
8. A troll crashes through the east wing, killing Fenrir; Sinsong's fey guardians scythe eleven kobolds silently; Wessic falls.
9. **Three trolls converge** — one is killed and burned; Kaelthar drops shielding Sinsong; Fenwick emerges with armed kitchen staff.
