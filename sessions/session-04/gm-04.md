2. Sinsong's recruitment speech in the infirmary lands Miguel, a wounded guard with no family, as guide and lookout.
3. Nesim Waladra finds the party with Leosin Erlanthar's torn silver-dragon pendant and asks them to recover the missing Harper monk.
4. ~14:30 — Party leaves Greenest south on the raiders' trail, revising the attacker count to 400–500 from the prints of humans, kobolds, and drakes.
5. ~16:30 — Stragglers' camp scouted from the rocks — Mialee lifts the cultists' four swords under cover of a Minor Illusion.
6. Stragglers ambushed — three cultists killed, one taken alive, the kobolds scattered with several fleeing uphill.
7. Captured cultist refuses to break under interrogation — Miguel escorts him north to Greenest so Escobert can try with proper tools.
8. ~18:00 — Defile ambush sprung from the ridges by a fresh squad with two acolytes and a veteran, forcing the party to retreat a mile back and go to ground as a horn-call alerts the camp ahead.
