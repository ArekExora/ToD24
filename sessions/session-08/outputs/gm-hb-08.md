### Events
- Drake bursts from the corridor at the back of the barracks; killed in one quick exchange.
- Frulam's quarters searched — cult notes, map, and a book on dragons taken from the bookshelf.
- Sinsong falls through a concealed trapdoor; catches the rope, climbs back up, reports a dragon-statue chamber below.
- Winged kobolds surge up the shaft. Eguzkilore is netted and goes down; Sinsong stabilises him with *Spare the Dying*, then a Healing Word brings him back to his feet.
- Shaft barricaded with the writing table; party descends the rope. Drake fight at the foot of the shaft — cleared.
- **Tiamat's chamber** — five-headed dragon statue emerging from a volcano; small wooden chest by its base. Striking the chest triggers an acid trap from the carved black-dragon mouths; party fares well but the dragon book is ruined.
- Hatchery approach: kobolds and two drakes; cleared.
- Behind the iron gate: three dragon eggs in straw. Two cracked open with the greatsword — half-formed hatchlings die on the floor.
- A cave column reveals itself as a **Roper** and drags the dead hatchlings off to eat.
- Roper climbs over the iron gate from the ceiling; grapples Nyx and bites Nuberroca, tentacle needles draining the goliath's strength.
- **Sinsong binds the Roper with sleep.** Party retreats up the rope with the wooden chest; Roper left slumped.
