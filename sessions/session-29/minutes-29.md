## 1 - The Boar's tavern
_~15:00, 29 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. In the plaza the guards haul away the dissolved man and the square fills with patrols, but they have no interest in the party.
2. Nuberroca makes for the tavern where the **Company of the Boar**'s leader, **Jorath**, was seen. He is inside holding court with a cluster of merchants.
3. The goliath teleports behind him and pins him before the man can turn, murmuring into his ear — *"piensa bien tus próximas acciones, pequeña rata."*
4. Blood drained from his face, Jorath turns it into theatre at once — he presents the party as the greatest heroes, the slayers of monsters, the saviours of the Company of the Boar; and then swears that **Khorvalis** fell beside these men defending a caravan.
5. From afar Sinsong yells: *"Los fieles de Sinsong."*
6. Nuberroca interjects a new banner — the heroes of Mansken — and Jorath takes it into his patter.
7. Over and over, Jorath begs to be set down and pleads — *let them be famous among the watch too, call them in, now, please*; Nuberroca cuts him off, the watch already knows them.
8. The goliath turns to Eguzkilore and tells him to put an arrow in the man if he tries anything.
9. Jorath presses the pitch — the Company and the Heroes together could do more than apart, these heroes almost better than the Company themselves; merchants, a joint contract to the road is yours.
10. Eguzkilore slides to the bar and asks after Jorath and his companions; the keeper confirms a guy in armour and one in fine clothes have been in and out these days, Jorath the most constant of the three.
11. Pressed on why the Company abandoned the caravan they were protecting, Jorath spins the excuse — a merchant had paid them for an urgent delivery that could not wait.
12. Nyx pulls Nuberroca aside and asks him to let the man live — the cretin is cheating people, strip him of the scammed coin and bruise his face.
13. A city guard, **Wadding**, drifts in with his companion and asks if there is any issue; Sinsong answers him — friends greeting friends, nothing to see — and the guard shrugs it off and goes.
14. Jorath drives it further — he and the Company have *almost* matched the party's deeds, two dragons slain in a village to the south, basilisks, a young dragon — pitches a joint contract with the merchants for 200 gold, the whole room watching.
15. The goliath sets Jorath back on his feet, mutters a farewell at his ear, and steps away to the bar for a beer; Nyx and Sinsong stay near enough to watch him work the room.
