# Session 29 {{float:"right" 2025-11-30}}
{wide}

## 1 - The tortle leaves the road
_~14:30?, 29 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. Still in the Waterdeep square where **Elodin** cut the air and vanished, the party takes stock. The combat ended with the elf immobilised and gone through a thin white line, a spell none of them placed save that the word *"Kal Ort Por"* carried through it — the same phrase **Azbara** had used on the Daggerford road [Td:3-9].
2. **Amidamaru** calls it there. The tortle has family on the road looking for a place safe from the dragons, three months out at least, maybe four, and he will go to them; sending-stone word can reach him if the party has urgent magic to ask of him [Td:11-24]. He walks away from the square; the party's mage is gone.
3. The square is thick with guards now. The dead man Elodin melted into the stones in the fight is a Waterdeep uniform, and the patrols along the walls have doubled — not pressing the party, just walking [Td:27-37].

## 2 - A rogue in the tavern

1. Mialee makes for *the tavern of the boar* on a hunch; the Compañía del Jabalí told them weeks ago it was their place [Td:39-44].
2. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca does not wait. He clouds a step across the common room and lands at the back of a man he picks out at a crowded table, one hand on the man's shoulder, the other across the throat. The blue arms go round the neck before the man can turn [Td:56-73]. *"Piensa bien tus próximas acciones, pequeña Renata"* [Td:76].
3. The man is **Jorath Vexundar**, the Compañía rogue who had sold them out at the fungal fields — the fiesta boys the party have not forgotten. He goes white, surrenders his weight, and begins at once to spin — the big legend from Greenest, the heroes who closed up the dragons, the dear friends he has been talking up all week to the merchants gathered round him for a Baldur's Gate contract [Td:77-102, Td:160-181].
4. Nyx watches it from the door and reads the shape of the con. She crosses to the barman and lays a gold piece on the counter — *"estoy buscando a este y a sus amigos, ¿los conoces?"* [Td:198-199]. The barman takes the coin and gives the straight answer: Jorath has been there a few days, two companions in and out — one in armour, one in elegant clothes; the big-eater legend has been Jorath's steady pitch [Td:203-211].
5. A guard pushes in on the murmur; Sinsong crosses, speaks the small blessing of a spell under his breath, and talks the guard into a nothing — a minor scene, friends greeting friends, nobody being held against his will [Td:329-347]. The guard shrugs it off and goes.

## 3 - Nyx tells Nuberroca to put him down

1. In a side table Nyx takes Nuberroca aside — the barbarian still carries Jorath by the arm [Td:296-320]. The plan is not a tavern brawl. Let the rogue finish his pitch, let the merchants put the gold on the table, and take the purse off him when he leaves the room; return it to the merchants or keep what feels right as the price of the trouble [Td:306-336].
2. Nuberroca accepts and lets Jorath go on his word; the barbarian plants himself at a wall with a beer and watches [Td:402-411]. Jorath goes back to his table and resumes the show, the price now floated at 200 gold for both companies to ride the Baldur's Gate run that very night [Td:370-378].

## 4 - A window and a chase
_~15:30?_

1. Jorath, thirsty, calls for the barman's attention without paying and climbs the stairs toward the toilets, saying his throat is dry [Td:549-561]. Nyx goes up behind him; Kaelthar takes the stairs; Nuberroca stays below [Td:562-576].
2. From the landing Nyx hears a shutter bang open and no footstep after; the door is locked. She breaks it with her shoulder on the second try and finds the window swinging and the man gone over the sill, climbing down the back wall [Td:609-635, Td:657-662]. She throws herself after him [Td:670].
3. Down in the common room Nuberroca, confused on the chain of the thing, picks up a nearby beggar off a bench like a sack of potatoes and carries him toward the door — *comida, comida* — the barman draws a sword from under the bar and jumps across to intercept, taking a moment to blink at the goliath holding a confused man under one arm. Sinsong grapples the barman, the beggar is set down with an apology, and nobody loses a limb to the misunderstanding [Td:634-669, Td:689-702].
4. On the street Nyx takes the pursuit; Kaelthar runs up on his wolf at double her stride; Sinsong follows after setting the barman down; Nuberroca stays at the tavern with his beer and the confused beggar; Mialee is still at the orphanage [Td:691-702, Td:772-786, Td:836-838, Td:859-868]. Jorath limps from the drop and does not get far [Td:671-674]; Nyx overhauls him and lays hands on him [Td:789-801].
5. He draws a short sword and gashes her forearm when she takes his wrist — the strike is for release, not kill [Td:802-808]. She holds. The others close in.

## 5 - A guard is called

1. Rifling the man's pockets Sinsong lifts a smooth sphere he does not recognise and a purse heavier than a thief's should be; the Compañía insignia catches a thumb, loose coppers [Td:870-881, Td:903-910]. Jorath bargains hard — twenty gold if they will let him walk — *"nunca os he hecho daño, nunca os he atacado, por favor, dejadme en paz"* [Td:886-892]; Nyx will not stake her name on the deal [Td:924-933].
2. Sinsong closes it with a trick: a Waterdeep guard insignia from Elodin's earlier work in the square passes from Sinsong's hand to Nyx [Td:516-517 ?ord, Td:882]; the dwarf drops a word of sleep over Jorath and the rogue folds where he stands, the insignia slipped into his pocket while he goes under [Td:874, Td:920, Td:928-942]. *"Yo estoy buscando un guardia"* [Td:943] — Nyx walks the first patrol down the lane to the body on the stones [Td:946]. Sinsong steps in with the story — the man took a Waterdeep insignia off a dead guard, he saw it himself [Td:954, Td:957-958]. The guard searches, finds the planted insignia, cuffs the limp rogue, and marches him off [Td:960-968].
3. The barman, still back at the tavern, leans out of the broken window and bellows at the empty street about his door; nobody answers him and he gives up on it [Td:972-978]. Nyx permits herself a quiet satisfaction [Td:978].

## 6 - Rooftop watchers
_~16:30?_

1. The party trickles together on the street — Nuberroca walking back from his beer with his head tilted at the eaves, convinced an *amigo* in the city has warned him to watch the roofs; Mialee drifting in from the north with word from her people [Td:987-1000, Td:1011].
2. Mialee carries it: her little birds at the orphanage put a watcher on a rooftop during the fight with Elodin earlier that day, and the word on the street is that someone is paying good coin to put the party in the ground. No name, no face. Watch the roofs [Td:1017-1030].
3. Nuberroca mentions he is short a weapon — the elf cut his lance in two in the square. Mialee produces the great sword she walked out of the arrest circle in the aftermath of that fight, no longer carrying the black light but still a well-made length of steel, and hands it to him; the goliath takes it and sets it on his shoulder [Td:1037-1055].

## 7 - At the Plaza del Pueblo
_~17:00?_

1. The party moves to the market square to spend what little coin they have — herbalist, potions, armoury, jeweller, all under one plaza [Td:1112-1116]. Nuberroca sells Elodin's sword for 25 gold and buys a lance for 2, keeping the balance; the pike would have been eighteen pounds and the breastplate he eyes is sixty gold he does not have [Td:1193-1270].
2. Mialee buys one greater and two basic healing potions for 300 gold [Td:1332-1334]; Kaelthar counts his purse and buys nothing — *"pobres como ratas, sois aquí todos"* [Td:1375-1377]. The talk turns to a diamond for revivify, 300 gold the minimum and the jeweller only carries 100-piece and 1000-piece cuts — split a thousand and perhaps three of three hundred come out, perhaps not [Td:1382-1456].
3. Mialee demonstrates her honest trade in open air. She closes her palm, murmurs a small working, and two pieces of platinum lift out of Sinsong's pouch and settle into Nuberroca's — *redistribuyo la riqueza*, she says, straight-faced, while the jeweller looks on [Td:1510-1527]. Nobody but the party sees the hand that carried them [Td:1515-1521].
4. Nuberroca lays a Cult-of-the-Dragon medallion on the counter and reads the jeweller's face when the dragon figure crosses it — nothing comes back, no spark of knowledge; the elegant piece is worth 25 gold to the man, the worn one worthless [Td:1633-1640]. Sinsong tries his own black stone from the Far Hills on the counter; the jeweller turns it over, calls it nothing he knows, and hands it back [Td:1619-1622]. Mialee reaches for a word of suggestion to put a crack in the man and the jeweller catches the shape of the spell forming — *"por favor, en mi tienda de magia no"* — and asks them to leave [Td:1591-1602].

## 8 - Three errands at dusk
_~18:00?_

1. The party splits for the two hours of daylight remaining [Td:1699-1711]. Mialee and Sinsong to the tavern tables for dice; Nuberroca and Kaelthar to the fighting-pits for prize-fights; Nyx and Eguzkilore to the north quarter to look for the cult.
2. At the tables Mialee plays the first game straight and wins twenty gold off a guard; the second she wins again, the third she loses to a veteran who sits down with fifty gold in front of him. She clears a touch over fifty, more than she brought [Td:1757-1795, Td:1860-1893].
3. At the ring Kaelthar steps into a strength contest against a thin, wiry elf and takes the loss, his supporters down ten gold apiece [Td:1865-1893]. Nuberroca goes next, strikes true, and leaves the elf on the straw with a broken rib [Td:1896-1927]; the house pays him twenty-five gold for the fight and five for the manner of it. Mialee, Sinsong, and Kaelthar each take seventy-five back on the bet [Td:1927-1937].
4. North of the plaza Nyx and Eguzkilore walk the Neverwinter-caravan quarter — Lord Neverember's crest on half the wagons, the long rebuild of the road to Neverwinter underway and crews hiring from every corner [Td:1972-1990]. Nyx catches sight of the stolen wagon in a fenced private lot off the main road, well-watched; Eguzkilore stays to keep an eye on it while Nyx goes back to pull the rest in [Td:2000-2007, Td:2092-2104].

## 9 - A diamond, cut hard

1. The group pools everything at the jeweller's before closing — the twelve-hundred of mixed coin, Nuberroca's lapis lazuli, Mialee's small gems, a 25-gold bottle of fine wine Mialee drops on the counter when her gems come up short [Td:2032-2048]. The jeweller takes the thousand and two hundred for the labour and promises the diamond cut by morning [Td:2051-2057].
2. The split comes out at two diamonds of 400 gold apiece and 200 gold worth of diamond dust — no third stone of 300, the labour did what the labour could [Td:2059-2068]. Sinsong takes custody; the diamonds live in his belt-pouch, since he is the only cleric at the table who can call them to work [Td:2062-2064, Td:2134-2143].

## 10 - A bolt from the dark
_~22:00?_

1. ***[EPIC]*** The five of them — Eguzkilore still at the wagon-watch — are back on the street talking out where to sleep when the first bolt comes out of the dark. It takes Nuberroca through the shoulder and the burn in it is not steel — an unfamiliar venom that reaches deep [Td:2178-2185, Td:2412]. A second bolt follows at the throat; the goliath drops in the street without seeing his attacker [Td:2199-2203]. A third skids off and plants in Kaelthar's wolf, which is standing beside him [Td:2215-2219].
2. The shooter is on a rooftop eighty feet west in the lightless lane, and nobody at ground level can place him [Td:2206-2219, Td:2264-2267]. Kaelthar closes the ground to Nuberroca, lays his hands on the chest wound, and brings him back [Td:2224-2232]. Sinsong moves forward and casts a compulsion across the dark — *approach* — the dark figure resists it once, folds the second time, and begins to come down [Td:2334-2343].
3. The attacker drops to the street and crosses in a half-crouch; Mialee waits in cover with her short bow strung, the arrowhead smeared in her kit's own poison, and takes him from behind as he passes under her lane — the venom catches at the wound but he fights off the worst of it and keeps moving [Td:2359-2378].
4. Kaelthar meets him in close with a thrown javelin that bites and a second javelin that goes wide [Td:2386-2408]; Nyx looses twice from the corner — one wide, one in — and slips back into cover [Td:2413-2419]. Mialee comes at him from the opposite side; the second arrow lands clean through the ribs and the follow-up stab from the shadow opens him badly across the chest [Td:2420-2440].
5. He gathers himself, breaks off, and makes it back up onto the roof the way he came — out of reach before the next arrow could meet him [Td:2487-2497]. Kaelthar lifts into the air after him on draconic wings and finds the pursuit heavy, barely clearing twenty feet [Td:2501-2529].
6. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee crosses the ground on the short blink her elven lineage affords her at level five, climbs the near wall at a run, and looses a last arrow across the gap in the dark — the shaft takes him where he crouches and finishes him [Td:2551-2601]. Nuberroca drops the small javelin and shield, takes up his new lance, and clouds himself the thirty feet to the rooftop in a step [Td:2603-2621]. He drags the body down and lays it in the street. The dead man's gear is a cheap crossbow, broken in the fall, and a short sword glistening with the green viscous poison that put Nuberroca down [Td:2632-2635]. Mialee brushes the blade bare-handed and takes a bad dose of it herself — the shaking of a bad fever before she can wipe the hand clean [Td:2636-2642].

## 11 - The guard takes the body

1. Nuberroca walks the corpse to a passing guard and lays it at his feet [Td:2659-2670]. The guard crouches, reads the venom on the blade, pockets it as evidence of the price of such work, and blows a short on his whistle; two more patrols close in and the three of them haul the body off between them [Td:2681-2699].
2. Nuberroca presses — *si por lo que sea encontráis más información acerca de asesinos* [Td:2700] — and the guard offers nothing he does not already have. *Si supiésemos dónde contratarlos estarían todos arrestados* [Td:2712]. The dead man's purse carried thirty-seven gold and no paper [Td:2719-2727].
3. The gold pays the bed. Three gold a night each at the tavern and the party takes six rooms; Mialee rides the first watch at the wagon in the north quarter rather than sleep [Td:2738-2771]. The night passes without further knock on the door [Td:2774].
