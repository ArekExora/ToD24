## 1 - Arrival in the snow
_~12:40?, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party steps off the teleport circle and the humid castle air of Naerytar is replaced in a single breath by a cold, snowbound mountain in heavy rain [T:70-74], the ground slick where snow meets water [T:173]. The GM calls the area lightly obscured [T:78-79]. Downslope through the pines, ~500m away, stands a stone-and-timber house large enough to be a hunter's lodge for wealthy clients, not a trapper's hut [T:181-187], its ground floor stone and the upper storeys timber [T:188]. The path toward it bifurcates at ~50 ft, left and right [T:189-191]. Fresh boot-tracks ~20 min old lead down [T:206].
2. The arrival circle is a near-twin of the one in Naerytar [T:175-176], with two large carved monoliths standing just in front [T:177-180]. Behind the matching flanking stones at the two path-forks sit two more circles — three in all [T:245-263]. **Kaelthar** reads the Draconic script on the stones but the words make no sense in Draconic [T:208-212]; **Amidamaru** places them as *Loross*, the tongue of an ancient magical empire [T:229-232]. From his half-translation he recognises *marsh* and *frog-croak* on the party's own arrival circle [T:235-240], *snow-lands* on one of the others [T:247-251], and *red sun* on the third [T:252]. The glyphs name destinations.
3. Only the party's own circle shows fresh tracks [T:266]. Amidamaru warns everyone to spread out — he does not want another acid sphere from the toads catching the group clumped [T:273-276] — and Nyx argues for shelter and warmth first [T:279-282]. Amidamaru proposes a warded shelter ritual: ten minutes to raise, eight hours of invisible protection [T:293-294], [T:533-534]. He steps into the trees and begins [T:338-339].

## 2 - The troll and the children
_~13:03, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Two minutes into the ritual [T:342-343], **Nyx** catches a fight to the east — two bands at it, not hunting [T:321-325] — with a weapon-noise she places as the gunpowder-like bolts the party had stripped off bandits on the road weeks earlier [T:314-317], and the cries of something being driven [T:325]. The sound moves toward the party [T:334-335].
2. **Nuberroca** climbs a tall pine to see further [T:390-391]. Through the pines and rain he picks out a hulking brute with moss-like skin dragging two small struggling forms down the path toward the lodge [T:396-399], the children clearly alive and losing [T:400-401]. Nearer, something trailing up the slope resolves into a troll, badly wounded, cut and burned, crude feather-fletched arrows still in him, dark blood on his own hide and bright red on his hands and claws [T:416-424]; Nuberroca names it a troll on sight [T:425]. ***[EPIC]*** From the pine Nyx on her near-perfect perception watches the mossy brute reach the lodge grounds and bind one of the children while he takes the other by chest and arm — and *pulls* [T:477-482], and even at that distance she hears the child's shriek as the arm comes free [T:483].
3. The troll pauses, dazed and bleeding, close enough to catch the party — but they are well-hidden under the pines and he blunders past [T:432-434], [T:459-461]. Fire-bolt-like bursting projectiles crack open on his hide from unseen hunters in the woods, searing patches that do not close while the sword-cuts seal over as he moves [T:426-428], [T:463-466]. He kneels, rises, keeps going [T:467-470]. A last bursting bolt catches his neck at the tree-line and he drops and does not rise [T:504-507]. Nyx argues furiously for the party to intervene — people are being slaughtered down there [T:485-488], [T:515-517] — but Nuberroca is at half, Eguzkilore worse, and the rest no better: no-one will go without a full night's rest first [T:489-500].
4. In the last minute of the ritual, several unseen somethings slip up through the brush and brush the group as they pass; three of the party are touched [T:567-579]. Nyx finds the rope stripped from her pack [T:584-585]; Eguzkilore the canteen cut from his [T:586]; **Nuberroca** the wrapped crocodile meat lifted from the top of his [T:595-596]. Nuberroca shrugs it off — one bite feeds him a week [T:599], [T:601]. The shelter completes a minute later and the party steps inside [T:602-605].

## 3 - The night in the warded shelter

1. Inside, dry and warm, out of the rain and out of sight of what moves outside [T:547-549], [T:605-606]. **Sinsong** steps up to the group out of the long awkward quiet and offers an unscripted apology — *"la culpa ha sido mía. Lo siento mucho, chicos… creo que esto no va de mí"* — and asks the party to let him stand with them from here on out [T:655-674]. Nyx answers with her own correction: too many of them keep peeling off alone, and when the lone one is in trouble no-one knows where to look for them [T:675-682].
2. Nyx shows Sinsong the long sword-cut across her chest — a serious wound that will not close with sleep alone [T:781-784]. The GM lays out the ways to clear it: deep healing beyond her maximum, a restoration spell, or careful field-medicine with a healer's kit [T:818-820]. **Nuberroca** opens his pack, chews a packet of herbs down into a paste, spits it into the chest-wound and packs it in by hand [T:876-878] — with Sinsong whispering guidance over his shoulder [T:851-853] and Nyx steadying him [T:826-830], his treatment lands cleanly [T:870-874]. Nyx shakes off the barbarism and allows that in her tribe the field-medicine goes the same way [T:879-881].
3. Amidamaru takes the two books Eguzkilore brought from the castle and sits down to identify them, a half-hour apiece [T:942-943], [T:973]. The first is the spellbook of a wizard named **Hothars**, written in warded and awkward script that takes the full half-hour to break open [T:947-950]. The second, by one **Keneus**, is heavily annotated with uneasy marginalia and crude illustrations — not a good man, Amidamaru judges, but a wizard all the same [T:982-987]. He pockets both and lies down to sleep [T:988-990].
4. Partway through the second book Eguzkilore picks out two pairs of eyes among the brush — small, yellow, close to the ground [T:1005-1012]. Two goblins creep up to the shelter wall, touch it, probe with a dagger, cannot find a way through [T:1024-1030] — and one carries Nyx's stolen rope coiled on his belt [T:1016-1017]. A third joins a little later [T:1038-1041], and after about ten minutes all three drift off [T:1042-1043]. From the lodge, every so often, distant screams carry up to the party in the first hour of the night [T:1047]. **Mialee** uses the quiet of her last four hours to work scavenged shafts into roughly nineteen crude arrows for Eguzkilore's quiver [T:1209-1211].

1. The shelter dissolves on its own clock [T:1085-1088]. ## 6 - Tharn opens up

Mialee tells him there is no going back [T:2413-2414].
2. Mialee presses him on the lodge, the castle, the cult. He has never seen this place [T:2429-2430]; he came up from Carnath and from Waterdeep and had been three months cooking in Naerytar when the party arrived [T:2449-2452]. He names **Rezmir** — *la señora Rezmir* — the *Wyrmspeaker*, the one who speaks for the dragons [T:2566-2568], and admits she was in the castle most of the time he was there [T:2558], [T:2570-2572].
