## 1 - A compass in the hand
_~09:00?, 2 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Before the caravan rolls, Nyx takes a short rest to finish identifying the smooth sphere she has been turning over in her hand [Td:1-10]. The mote of light inside holds its line no matter how she tilts the glass — a compass, pointing one way and one way only [Td:14-15]. She slips it onto her person and keeps it [Td:17].
2. Nuberroca makes a point to the party before they move out: the ogres from the day before threw trunks like twigs — keep distance if more come [Td:28-31]. Kaelthar heads off the wrong way out of camp and has to be turned round, pointed north [Td:34].

## 2 - On the caravan line
_~mid-morning, 2 Hammer_

1. The caravan rides in file — four workers and two guards to each wagon, the party counting for the guards of their own [Td:122-125]. The one they suspect of hiding the stolen cult cargo sits in the middle of the file [Td:117-120]. **Ardred Briferhew**, a lean sergeant with the look of a man who gives orders by habit, rides at the head; the caravan has no formal leader but the guards defer to him [Td:177-183].
2. Ardred asks the party plain whether they mean to be known as the Greenest heroes or ride anonymous. Nyx and Eguzkilore keep their heads down; Nuberroca and Kaelthar wave the name [Td:140-170]. Those who kept quiet pass; those who did not get looked at by any drivers who had cause to look [Td:473-479].
3. Walking the line to feel out the drivers, Nyx catches the face of the man on the suspect wagon — **Dillian** [Td:186-199]. She slips to Mia under her breath that he belonged to the cultist camp back at Greenest [Td:286-295].
4. The party trades it round quietly. Nuberroca argues for *an adaptive, escalating strategy — upward in violence* [Td:306-307]; Eguzkilore and Sinsong push for watch-and-follow and let the cargo lead them [Td:303-305, Td:322-327]. Nothing is settled; the plan holds at "shadow Dillian, do not spook" for now [Td:285].
5. Ardred lets drop that at Carnath Roadhouse the party will meet **Bog Luck**, the semi-orc tavernkeeper — big as Nuberroca and known for a fist-match. The guards are already betting on who wins [Td:246 ?ord, Td:468-473].

## 3 - A berry for everyone
_~late morning, 2 Hammer_

1. Nyx walks a Goodberry round to each of the party, pressing one into each palm — a quiet handoff, the day's revival stock [Td:328-333].
2. **Craith**, one of the sergeant's guards, drifts over asking for food — he has hunger like the rest [Td:334-335]. Nyx digs a bird egg she had saved for months out of her pack and gives it to him [Td:346-348]. Craith beams and tucks it away — *"Lo guardaré para la feria"* [Td:349-351]. Nothing of it then, but Craith marks it.

## 4 - Amidamaru's quiver
_~mid-afternoon, 2 Hammer_

1. ***[EPIC]*** Riding on, Nyx realises she has a quiver on her hip that is not hers — Amidamaru's, with a sealed note tucked in among the arrows [Td:373-385]. She reads it apart from the others. The note is a goodbye [Td:389-391]: Amidamaru means to fight the cult his own way, not by following at the party's heel, and he has gone [Td:390-391]. She has been carrying it since the morning Amidamaru walked out of Waterdeep without a word [Td:388].
2. Nyx takes stock of what the quiver carries and distributes [Td:392-399]. The pearl Mia had once lent Amidamaru goes back to Mia — *"Creo que era tuya, un préstamo que le hiciste"* [Td:393-395]. Three platinum coins go to Eguzkilore — *"Pensaba que no las iba a recuperar ya"* [Td:398-402]. Two unidentified metallic spheres, each with a small button, stay with Nyx; without triggering them she cannot work out what they do [Td:415-422].
3. Nyx keeps the quiver itself. *"Quiero quedarme el de Amidamaru por si algún día vuelve para dárselo"* [Td:440-441].

## 5 - Shapes behind the trees
_~nightfall, 2 Hammer_

1. The day draws down to dusk and the party catches movement in the trees ahead — large shapes trying to hide behind trunks too narrow for them, badly [Td:487-490]. Three or four are visible; more are not [Td:490].
2. Sinsong leans in, taps Nuberroca's sword, and lays a conjured spark on it so there will be light when it is wanted [Td:502-507]. The weapon brightens — and so does Sinsong's bald head, close enough to catch the same working [Td:574-577]. The surprise is gone; the guards see what is coming [Td:524-525].
3. Four ogres charge from the fore of the trees, with orcs behind them [Td:528-529]. Ardred takes a knee, lays a crossbow bolt into the first one with a fluid gesture [Td:600-606]. Nuberroca enters his rage before the ogres cross the road [Td:530].
4. A fifth ogre jogs in from the back of the woods [Td:681-684]. Mialee throws a wall of fog across the party's flank and dashes behind it, sending Echo above the mist so she can see through the bat what she cannot see through the fog [Td:689-725].
5. Nyx keeps the near ogre marked and lays a venomed shaft into it from range [Td:618-635 ?ord]; the poison turns the giant to tear at itself [Td:997-998]. A second arrow catches it in the throat, and the thing crumples trying to pull it free [Td:1007-1014]. First kill of the fight.
6. Kaelthar closes in on **Fenrir**, his summoned wolf, and comes in behind Nuberroca. He brings a javelin down with the dragon's roar carrying through the strike — the point drives straight into the near ogre's open mouth, a critical [Td:794-824]. One round further on, a returning javelin takes Fenrir through and the wolf dissipates into smoke [Td:951-964].
7. Sinsong plants his back behind a trunk and reaches across the field with a word of command aimed at two ogres by Nuberroca — *"huir"* [Td:1133-1137]. One shakes it off; the other turns to flee [Td:1136-1139].

## 6 - The halberd and the head
_~nightfall, 2 Hammer_

1. Nuberroca sets the butt of his spear in the ground, pins it with a boot, and waits. One ogre charges him and runs itself clean through, out the other side [Td:1037-1041].
2. ***[EPIC]*** He takes the next with the halberd across the same arc — his dark fury pulling tendrils up his veins and the wounds on his chest closing under his own skin as the swing lands [Td:860-868 ?ord].
3. On the fleeing ogre, Nuberroca does not wait. A reaction catches the thing turning, and it falls with his spear buried in its back [Td:1296-1302].
4. Eguzkilore drops to a knee behind cover and sends a hail of arrows into the sixth ogre as it closes [Td:1225-1248]; the creature kneels, bristling with shafts, and begins trying to rise [Td:933-934 ?ord].
5. Across the melee, Mialee closes on a flanking ogre with her scimitars — the first strike cuts into a crit [Td:1305-1324], then she slips free of the ogre's reach in a cutting feint and vanishes behind a trunk [Td:1326-1339].
6. ***[EPIC]*** Ardred ends it. He runs the length of the road, calls the guards into position with their crossbows, and vaults the last ogre — the first stroke rocks it on its heels, the second catches it ducking and the head comes off clean onto the road [Td:1531-1540]. He wipes the blade on the ogre's hide and sheathes it in one motion [Td:1538-1540].
7. Six ogres dead on the road, no deaths among the caravan [Td:1540-1554]. Two guards badly wounded — **Jarth** cut deep, **Isaiash** lighter but hit — the rest with scrapes [Td:1546-1549]. The ogres carry only trophies from past killings: a bashed guard's helmet, a tin cup; nothing worth taking [Td:1626-1633]. Ardred claps Nuberroca on the shoulder and promises the first round of beer at Carnath Roadhouse on him [Td:1572-1577].

## 7 - Creo en ti, Sinsong
_~dusk, 2 Hammer_

1. Sinsong gathers the wounded guards close around him before the caravan moves off. He will heal, he says, but they have to raise their hands and call it out — *"Creo en ti, Sinsong"* [Td:1646-1662]. The guards, confused and bleeding, do it. Jarth and Isaiash fold in; Craith plants himself at the back [Td:1667-1672].
2. ***[EPIC]*** The light rises off Sinsong's bald head as he goes to Goku-pose and the healing lands [Td:1670-1685]. On the first pass Isaiash stabilises, Jarth bleeds to a stop; a second pass brings Jarth back further and Craith comes up whole and radiant, declaring himself a new convert on the spot [Td:1693-1706]. Craith asks whether he can swap his standing-stone for a place among Sinsong's faithful; Sinsong accepts [Td:1704-1710].
3. Mia steps in and tries to monetise the moment — the healing costs two gold [Td:1727-1731]. Craith reaches into his purse and comes up with four electrum. Sinsong refuses the coin: *"No quiero tu dinero, Craith, solo con tu adoración me sirve"* [Td:1732-1733]. Sinsong's long-cold honour notches up a point in the moment [Td:1737-1743].
4. Nuberroca hoists Sinsong by the collar like a child and sets him back down. *"Esta tarde lo has hecho bien, esta mañana no tanto. A partir de ahora te llamaré Sinsong el apósito"* [Td:1711-1721 ?ord]. Kaelthar, from a few paces back: *"El tiritas"* [Td:1722].
5. Ardred, watching, remarks it is good to have a healer along for the road and gives the order to move — they will push to reach the night camp before the light is gone entirely [Td:1746-1753].

## 8 - A sending into the dark
_~night, 2 Hammer_

1. The caravan reaches the rest zone late. Ardred lays the watches: Mialee and Sinsong first, then Isaiash and Craith, then Nuberroca and Nyx, Eguzkilore last [Td:1759-1762].
2. Before his head goes to the saddle-pack, Sinsong sends a whisper out across the road and the night — a working aimed at Amidamaru, somewhere beyond reach. *"Amidamaru, ¿dónde te has metido? Aquí se te echa en falta, te echamos de menos. Vuelve pronto"* [Td:1773-1783]. The party then sleeps.

## 9 - Dillian knows the goliath
_morning, 3 Hammer_

1. Nuberroca wakes with his veins their usual colour, the dark tracery gone [Td:1784-1786]. The caravan is already moving. He drops back along the line and asks Nyx under his breath whether the suspect driver survived the night; he did [Td:1799-1803].
2. Mia reaches Nyx by sending across the wagons — Dillian has recognised Nuberroca, pins him to the fight against Langdedrosa at Greenest. Be careful: he knows who the goliath is [Td:1894-1902]. Nyx passes the warning along [Td:1902-1903].
3. Nuberroca's answer is to lean further into it. He plants himself at the head of the caravan and begins to recite his old killings aloud — *"¿Te acuerdas de ese combate, Nyx? ¿Te acuerdas de cuántos matamos en la cueva?"* [Td:1905-1911] — mugging through the morning. Ardred watches him from a few paces behind, half-smiling, half not; Isaiash asks if he is well [Td:1913-1920].

## 10 - The moon in the sphere
_through 3 Hammer_

1. On the march, Nuberroca pulls out a small smooth sphere he had picked up off the ground after the ambush and shows it to Nyx [Td:1818-1822]. It is nothing like the compass — motes of light inside form a semicircle beside a dark edge, as if a sun or moon were caught mid-rise [Td:1836-1842, Td:1861-1868]. When held, the shape faces the holder [Td:1844-1845].
2. The party passes it hand to hand through lunch — Nyx, Sinsong, Mia all taking turns, each watching the semicircle present itself fresh to whoever holds it [Td:1857-1875, Td:1961-1971]. Dillian casts furtive looks at the party across the fire; the conversation at his side goes quiet whenever Nuberroca passes close [Td:1978-1983].
3. Dinner is boiled legumes and a half-passed strip of meat; the party watches a round of darts for coppers at the night camp [Td:1984, Td:2015-2017]. The watches run Nuberroca and Mialee first, Sinsong and Nyx second, Craith and Isaiash third, Sarek and Jarth last — Ardred, as ever, pulls none [Td:2117-2123]. Before sleeping, Sinsong lays a day-long ward of iron under the skin on Nuberroca, Kaelthar, and himself [Td:2202-2204].
4. Mia pulls the thread the party could not: the picture matches the moon's phase. Nuberroca holds the sphere up in the dusk — the semicircle inside is the moon in the sky, waning as the real moon is waning [Td:2081-2092, Td:2222-2234]. By morning the sphere shows a full disc; the sun is up [Td:2219-2229, Td:2293-2298]. A clock, by moon and sun.
5. Ardred rolls them out at 8:30 on the morning of 4 Hammer 1490 DR after a frugal breakfast; the caravan is on the road again before 9 [Td:2302-2304].
