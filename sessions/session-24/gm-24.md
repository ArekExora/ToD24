## 4. Events

1. Caravan breaks camp at Barlon's and rolls north; the Innevar sisters join the convoy.
2. ~13:00, 15 Nightal — Lunch halt at The Way Inn; a cultist crate cracks open briefly revealing jewellery.
3. Over four days on the road the Innevar sisters work every cart in the caravan.
4. ~13:40, 21 Nightal — Sabotaged wheel nearly drops Nuberroca's cart in the Delimbiyr pass; the pins were pulled overnight.
5. Kaelthar attacks a cultist cart and Nuberroca beheads a cultist guard.
6. Mialee's fog cloaks the fight; Kaelthar's dragonfire kills two cultists inside it.
7. The caravan sides with the cultists; Sulesdeg and Eldkin close on Nuberroca and Kaelthar with iron cuffs.
8. Kaelthar goes down and Amidamaru heals him; the lead cultist's bolt punches into Nuberroca.
