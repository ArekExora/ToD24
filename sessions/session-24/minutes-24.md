## 1 - Rolling out
_~12:00, 13 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The caravan breaks camp at noon after two days laying over in sight of **Barlon**'s tavern — tents struck wet, cold provisions stowed back on the carts, the beasts walked into their harnesses under a low Nightal cloud that has been spitting rain off and on since dawn. **Lord Zelan** crosses the yard for his lunch at the common room with his guards in a loose knot around him; they pass the convoy still jesting at its expense, with the tiny blue sage back inside his lamp. The hooded figure glimpsed upstairs has never come down in all this time — not for meat, not for water, not for a breath of fresh air.
2. The sisters **Arietta** and **Zelina** come with the convoy at the last of the mustering — the merchants made the room for them on one of the carts when the tavern had refused them a bed. Arietta rides silent on the left side of the cart with her eyes on the road ahead and a blanket drawn to her chin; Zelina sits beside her and does the talking for them both — a quiet word for the driver who had taken them aboard, a nod for the party across the gap.
3. The convoy rolls out in its usual order: the three cultist carts at the head, the merchants' wagons through the middle, and two traveller carts at the tail — one of them **Darla** and **Gus**'s cart, Nuberroca's self-assigned post since the Trollclaws. The goliath takes his place at the near wheel without a word and stays there as the line stretches out onto the Trade Way, the tavern shrinking at his back.
4. Sinsong is away ahead of the caravan on his own trail; he had cut north off the main road days earlier after the **Company of the Boar**, intending to catch them before Waterdeep. The party does not expect him back on the main road before they reach the city themselves.

## 2 - Spilled cargo
_~13:00, 15 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. Two day's march after leaving Barlon's tavern, the convoy reaches **The Way Inn** — the Trade Way's hardened mid-stretch waypoint, a walled compound on the road's western side atop a grassy plateau. Three bridges lead up to gatehouses the size of small keeps, each armed with catapults; the inn stands stone and tile-roofed inside the walls under a lookout tower. The gates are shut and the compound closed to travellers while the renovation works are under way, so the convoy pulls off the road under the walls for a lunch halt — food out of the wagons, beasts unhitched to graze.
2. As one of the cultist carts rolls off the road into its halt position, a wheel bumps over a stone and jolts a crate loose from its lashings; the crate falls and cracks open on the ground, and the lid comes up long enough to show a spill of wrapped jewellery — items of worked gold padded in wool — before one of the cultists sweeps a cloak across the whole crate in a single motion and closes the spill back out of sight.
3. Mialee is across the gap first — she steps in from the nearest cart with her hands open at her hips, offering to help settle the lashings. The cultists wave her off hard: one plants a palm on the cloak atop the crate, the other puts a shoulder between her and it, and the refusal lands twice over, hands and voice — *we have it, we have it, step back*. Amidamaru closes from the other side a heartbeat later, kinder-voiced and open-handed, and meets the same wall — the two cultists shift to face him without lifting their weight from the crate, and the second *no* lands harder than the first.
4. Nuberroca drifts over a stride later on the same pretence — a loose strap on his own cart, he says, wanting a word on how the tie-downs had failed. Two more cultists step out from the next cart with their hands on their belts to make the count on their side even, and the words come at him before his hand gets within reach of the cloak — *they have it, they do not need help here*. The goliath holds the eye of the lead cult driver for a long heartbeat before he gives ground.
5. With the party backed off the cultists tie the cloak down over the whole crate and lash the broken lid under it so no second glance can find the join. They stay posted around the cart through the rest of the halt, and when the convoy hitches up and rolls on by afternoon the lead driver's eyes do not leave the party's carts until the Way Inn's walls fall behind them.

## 3 - The long road north

1. The road climbs further north bordering the Misty Forest and, in the distance, they can glimpse the mountains near the Delimbiyr river. The path is clear and the weather bearable, and they advance without incident for several days.
2. Across those mornings the two sisters work the carts one by one — a greeting, a question, a drawn-out thread of family and road and where-are-you-from with everyone who answers them. By the fourth day they have been through every merchant, every guard, every traveller who will sit with them.
3. Nuberroca spends the leg riding ahead of the three cultist carts and dropping back behind them in turns, making a point of being where they can see him. He finds the cult drivers' eyes on him more than once. One of them — the one driving the lead cart — holds the look longer than the others and does not break it.
4. At each night's camp the convoy rings up tight — merchant and traveller carts settling within a few paces of each other — and the party sits down to dice, bread and small beer with the merchants and the gnome Radecere, always looking for gambling; the sisters sit on the edge of these fires and listen.

## 4 - Into the pass
_~14:00, 21 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The mountain pass of the Delimbiyr river is a treacherous one, a thin snaking gravel path with steep cliffs on one side and the roaring waters far below on the other. The convoy threads it single-file, the drivers setting the brakes a notch tighter and the guards closing up to the wheels.
2. On the thinner stretch of the pass the cart where Nuberroca is riding lurches into a lean; a rear wheel is loose on its axle and the whole cart tips toward the drop. Ahead on the pass, the lead cultist driver has turned in his seat to watch over his shoulder.
3. The goliath jumps with feline reflexes and sets his back under the frame of the wagon, holding it there with his own weight and the strength of his arms while Anton, who was walking in front of the cart, pulls the horses to help take the cart to safety.
4. The near guards rush to hold the axle with the barbarian, and between them they walk the cart back off the shoulder and down the pass to the wide cleared ground beyond, where the rest of the caravan has drawn up to wait out the single-file stretch.
5. With the cart secured, and Darla conforted by Gus, Amidamaru goes under the axle. The nails that pin the wheel into its socket are not there — not sheared, not broken, pulled clean out of the wood by someone who had the time to work them.
6. The tortle straightens from the wheel and tells the party his conclussion on the findings: the sabotage is deliberate, and the wagon had been in sight of the caravan all night.

## 5 - First blows

1. Kaelthar does not wait for the party to settle a plan. The cultists are a few places up the row and he reads the sabotage as their work — he summons his draconic wings and takes to the air across the caravan, flying over toward the cultist wagons.
2. Over the lead cultist cart he pulls the wings and drops onto it with all his weight — the fall meant as a strike. The landing goes heavy and ugly, crashing across the frame rather than onto the bed, but the wood opens under him where he lands.
3. The cultists around the broken wagon have their blades drawn before Kaelthar is back on his feet. They close in on their side of the cart, weapons up and voices raised, shouting curses and foul names at the dragonborn.
4. Nuberroca goes after Kaelthar on foot. He crosses the gap between the carts in a rush, striking the first cultist guard who turns to meet him, and takes the man's head clean from his shoulders in a single stroke. The swing was meant to stop the man, not to end him — but a goliath's weight behind a halberd's blade was more than the cultist's slight frame could hold.
5. Mialee reads the opening and takes it. She lays a thick fog across the three cultist carts — guards and party alike lose their lines of sight through the cloud — and sends her unseen hand into the nearest crate while readying her scimitar.
6. The lid comes up. Inside is the cargo the party has been chasing since Greenest: chalices, religious icons, bags of small gems and jewellery stripped from statues. She lets the lid back down softly and focuses on the combat.
7. Amidamaru holds back from the fight, a healing spell at the ready instead of a blade — he will tend, not strike. Eguzkilore stays at the fringe beside him, out of Mialee's cloud: the caravan guards are the ones he has stood watches with across the trip, and he is not going to put himself on the wrong side of them by an unconfirmed suspicion.

## 6 - The caravan turns

1. The caravan's guards do not wait for an order. Those at distance raise crossbows and bows across the length of the convoy and loose at legs rather than through chests — the first exchanges go in low, aiming for a wounding stop and not a kill. The air across the fog thickens with shafts and no one at the carts can read who any one of them is aiming at.
2. The last cultist still on his feet cuts across the shouting with his own version — the party are robbers, they have come to rob the caravan, they have killed his companions. He stays with the line through the whole fight and keeps saying it after, so the caravan hears nothing else from his side.
3. **Sulesdeg** and **Eldkin** come across at the two biggest shapes in the fight — the goliath and the dragonborn — with their hands open rather than their weapons drawn, going for a grapple and a pair of iron cuffs each. Eldkin reaches for Kaelthar beside the broken wagon; Sulesdeg steps in on Nuberroca with his hands empty.
4. Nuberroca has his new polearm already set out ahead of him from the first strike; Sulesdeg comes into the point of it on the way in and takes the halberd through the shoulder before his hands close. The two of them hold a long heartbeat at the end of the shaft, and then Sulesdeg draws his sword. Eldkin and the rest of the caravan guards drop the grapple and pull swords with him.

## 7 - Fire in the cloud

1. Kaelthar is now surrounded on three sides between cultists and caravan guards and the blows close over him in a single pass; he goes down in the dirt beside the broken wagon. Amidamaru breaks from his own cart and crosses the fight to lay a healing spell into the paladin's chest before the ring closes over him; Kaelthar comes back up to his feet in time to meet the next swing.
2. Kaelthar draws breath and empties a cone of dragonfire across the two cultists in front of him. The fog over the carts flashes red from the inside with the heat of it, and the screaming comes a moment behind — the two of them go down burning in the open and the cloud thins around them.
3. Mialee has not left the fog. She works through it with her hand on an unseen weight and her mouth on a soft word, and two more cultists drop to the stones beside their burning companions without seeing what put them there.
4. Eguzkilore crosses in from the fringe when the ring closes on Kaelthar. A flat-of-blade stroke across the back of the nearest cultist's neck leaves one more laid out alive beside the two burning dead.
5. **Paco** — the caravan guard Amidamaru had always said he liked — falls in the press and dies on the road beside the cult dead. From the party's side of the fight it is not clear who put him down or from which side the blow came.

## 8 - The last cover

1. Sulesdeg closes on Nuberroca now that his sword is out and the caravan guards are drawn up on their line. The last cultist on the roof of a wagon still has his crossbow at his shoulder and he puts the bolt through at Nuberroca where the goliath is working between the carts.
2. Nuberroca pulls back around the shoulder of the nearest wagon and sets his line there — the wagon's full bulk between him and the roof, the pass's rock wall at his back.
3. The crossbow bolt finds the gap at the lip of the cover and lands hard in his side — he holds on his feet, but the shaft is in him and bleeding, and Sulesdeg is a stride away with his sword out.
