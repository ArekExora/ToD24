## 2 - The long road north
_12 → 20 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The road from Barlon's climbs out of the lowlands and bends north through broken foothills toward the Trade Way's mountain stretch. The caravan averages a day's march without incident, and nine of them pass under the wheels before the convoy picks up the thread of the real climb [plan L.70-72] [player Richy B].
2. Across those mornings the two sisters work the carts one by one — a greeting, a question, a drawn-out thread of family and road and where-are-you-from with everyone who answers them. By the fourth day they have been through every merchant, every guard, every traveller who will sit for them [GM].
3. Nuberroca spends the leg riding ahead of the three cultist carts and dropping back behind them in turns, making a point of being where they can see him. He finds the cult drivers' eyes on him more than once. One of them — the one who will sit the rearmost wagon at the pass — holds the look longer than the others and does not break it [player Richy B].
4. The party sits down to dice, bread and small beer at each stop with Lai, Edhelri, Noohar's brother Selvek, and the gnome Radecere; the sisters sit on the edge of these fires and listen. No one carries a Sending from Sinsong across the leg [GM]. The nights pass cold and uneventful [player Richy A].

## 3 - Into the pass
_~mid-morning, 21 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. On the ninth morning the road climbs into a narrow mountain pass between the stones — a ribbon of road with the cliff on one shoulder and the drop on the other [GM] [player Alfonso A] [player Alfonso B]. The carts have to walk through single-file; the drivers set the brakes a notch tighter and the guards close up to the wheels.
2. On a difficult stretch of the pass the cart Nuberroca is riding alongside — the travelling family's wagon — lurches into a lean; a rear wheel is loose on its axle and the whole cart tips toward the drop [GM] [player Alfonso A] [player Alfonso B]. Nuberroca sets his back under the frame of the wagon and holds it there with his own weight and the strength of his arms until the near guards can get beneath the axle with him; between them they walk the cart back off the shoulder and set it down on open ground beyond the stretch [player Alfonso A] [player Alfonso B].
3. Amidamaru goes under the axle at the stop. The nails that pin the wheel into its socket are not there — not sheared, not broken, pulled clean out of the metal by someone who had the time to work them [GM] [player Alfonso A]. He straightens from the wheel and tells the party what the wagon has to tell them: the sabotage is deliberate, and the wagon had been in sight of the caravan all night [derived:GM+S21:9].
