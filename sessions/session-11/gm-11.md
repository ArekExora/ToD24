## 7. Knowledge gained

1. **Light of Elturel.** Ancestral device set by Elturel's rulers; keeps the hordes of the dead from the region; has shone ~25 years.
2. **Red dragon sighted north.** A village north of Scornubel was hit alongside bandits by a red dragon — a different colour from the blue seen at Greenest.
3. **No draconid caravans on the Trade Way.** No merchant in Scornubel has seen carts drawn by giant lizards or draconids; giant lizards are known to lizardfolk of the northern marshes.
4. **Order of the Gauntlet in Elturel.** Military order supporting the city guard, well-funded, based at the *A Pair of Black Antlers* tavern; leader **Ontharr Frume**, paladin, named by the guard as one of the best warriors alive.
5. **Chionthar preferred by boat.** Most travellers to Elturel come down the Chionthar by boat; road is slower and less safe.
