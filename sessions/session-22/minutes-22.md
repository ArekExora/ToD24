## 1 - A head in the road
_~12:00, 27 Uktar 1489 DR_

1. Nyx rides ahead of the carts to settle what the painted thing at the crossroads actually is [player:Alfonso], and at close range it resolves not as a stone at all but as a man's head — a human buried up to the neck in the middle of the roadway, the word *"Oathbreaker"* scrawled across his forehead in red paint, unconscious but still breathing [module p.53] [GM].
2. The caravan to a man wants to leave him where he lies — the man surely deserved whatever got him there [player:Alfonso] — but the party overrules them, and Nuberroca sets to the digging himself, slow work with his hands and the butt of his lance. Amidamaru waits at the side with water and healing, and when the hole is deep enough and the first touch lands the man comes round with mud still thick in his beard [GM] [player:Pablo].
3. As the dirt comes off his shoulders a tattoo surfaces on bare skin — a harp within a crescent moon, which Nyx reads at a glance and keeps to herself for now [player:Alfonso]. Clothes come out of one of the packs; the man is dressed and settled in the back of a trailing wagon [player:Alfonso].
4. He gives his name as **Carlon Amoffel** and tells, first for the merchants' benefit, a broken-engagement story — the oath he broke was a promise to marry a woman whose father and brothers all turned out to be bandits, and this is their vengeance [module p.53]. The merchants mostly nod and look relieved.
5. At the next rest stop Nyx draws Amoffel aside with Mialee at her shoulder, and between them they tell him they are Harpers themselves [player:Alfonso] [player:Richy] — at which the cover falls away. He had been riding infiltrated with a cultist caravan a few days ahead of this one when they uncovered him, and the cultists convinced the merchants he was passing intel to bandits, so they buried him on the road to let providence decide his fate [player:Alfonso] [module p.54].
6. The hunt is the same one — the stolen loot moving north — and the name **Leosin** is already in his mouth before anyone offers it [player:Alfonso]. He names **Vauser** too, a half-orc Harper contact in Waterdeep: muscular, red-haired, a scar running across his face, reachable at *The Broken Jar* when the party gets that far [GM].
7. Amoffel takes a place in one of the back wagons [player:Alfonso]. The party briefs him of their situation, the three carts at the front of the caravan are the cultists, and the loot has been identified. Then they ask for his help against them [player:Pablo], he agrees, and from here he rides with the caravan as a third Harper on the trail [player:Pablo] [GM].
8. The following days go by without any remarkable event as the caravan progresses through the Trade Way.

## 3 - The Feast of the Moon
_~evening, Feast of the Moon 1489 DR_

1. At nightfall the caravan halts for the Feast of the Moon — the first bite of winter on the air, the last day of Uktar closing against the first of Nightal [GM].
2. **Eldkin** keeps her own rite apart from the main fire, silent and alone, a dwarven Commemoration of the Fallen for names she carries — though none of them land as names the party knows [GM].
3. The main fire belongs to Shiallia: a feast around the circle, warm cider in hands, caravan voices rising in old songs across the ring [GM]. Off to one side a short Lliira observance has gone up as well, a weapon laid flat on the ground under a scatter of flowers and chanted over quietly [GM]. The whole party sit the feast, but keep to themselves at the Ritual of Remembrance; the stories told are other travellers' stories [GM].

## 4 - A griffon flies off
_1 Nightal → 10 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The road rolls on through Nightal's deepening cold; the stretch takes ten days with nothing worth the breath of a retelling [GM].
2. One morning Targutai goes wild in the harness-line — jaws on a carthorse's neck, hard enough to open skin, then another horse, then another [GM] [player:Pablo]. Amidamaru snaps a quick working over the nearest carthorse and the beast vanishes from sight, out of reach of the griffon's eye; the trick holds, and that horse walks the rest of the morning unseen and unharmed [player:Pablo].
3. Sinsong puts himself between the griffon and the carts and talks him down, barely holding him before he can turn on another mount [player:Pablo]. Once the griffon has settled, Amidamaru works along the harness-line tending the wounded horses, while the merchants give the party dark looks the rest of the day [player:Pablo].
4. Later in the stretch the griffon is quiet for a span, and then without warning takes to the air and does not come back [GM].

## 5 - No room at the inn
_~19:00?, 11 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. A miserable wet-and-freezing day closes at a large inn with the walls of a castle rising at its back [module p.54] [GM]. Two women are already outside under the awning, drenched and out of options — twin sisters **Arietta** and **Zelina Innevar**, bound for Waterdeep themselves and turned away from the door [plan L.57] [module p.54] [GM]. Eguzkilore draws alongside them with a general greeting and the sisters pick him up with a cordial flirtation, Nyx joining the circle and Amoffel standing with them under the awning; between them they lean straight into questions — where from, where to, who else on the caravan, who is paired with whom — until they have every detail they can get [GM].
2. Nuberroca stops at the awning long enough for the sisters to catch his eye before Mialee pulls him through the door, the two of them the only ones of the party to go inside [player:Alfonso] [WA Sergio 2025-09-21 20:07] [GM]. On the table between them sits a strange **lamp** that holds a tiny humanoid figure of soft blue light, two or three inches high, blurry at the edges and apparently content inside [GM].

When Nuberroca asks about the stables anyway, the lead of the three at the table calls over, loud enough for Barlon to repeat the line he was told to pass on: *"nuestros caballos son muy exigentes con quién comparten espacio; nos hemos tenido que reservar los establos también, para su tranquilidad"* [module p.54] [S23:L44-46].
4. The three laugh at them, drop the next remark at Barlon's expense, and settle back to their table and the lamp [S23:L220-223] [GM]. The noble himself has not been seen since Nuberroca entered, and nobody in the room is expecting to see him tonight — he is upstairs in a private room and has not come down all evening [GM].
5. Nuberroca stands his ground with Mialee at his shoulder; Barlon moves between them pleading, while the three at the table watch pointedly bored and ready. The session cuts there [GM] [S23:L47-48].
