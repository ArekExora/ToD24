## 1 - A head in the road
_~12:00, 27 Uktar 1489 DR_

1. Nyx rides ahead of the carts to settle what the painted thing at the crossroads actually is, and at close range it resolves not as a stone at all but as a man's head — a human buried up to the neck in the middle of the roadway, the word *"Oathbreaker"* scrawled across his forehead in red paint, unconscious but still breathing.
2. The caravan to a man wants to leave him where he lies — the man surely deserved whatever got him there — but the party overrules them, and Nuberroca sets to the digging himself, slow work with his hands and the butt of his lance. Amidamaru waits at the side with water and healing, and when the hole is deep enough and the first touch lands the man comes round with mud still thick in his beard.
3. As the dirt comes off his shoulders a tattoo surfaces on bare skin — a harp within a crescent moon, which Nyx reads at a glance and keeps to herself for now. Clothes come out of one of the packs; the man is dressed and settled in the back of a trailing wagon.
4. He gives his name as **Carlon Amoffel** and tells, first for the merchants' benefit, a broken-engagement story — the oath he broke was a promise to marry a woman whose father and brothers all turned out to be bandits, and who had expected him, once the marriage was sealed, to come into the trade with them. When he refused them — the oath and the trade both — the brothers tracked him down on the road and planted him here in answer. The merchants mostly nod and look relieved.
5. Off to one side of the halt, away from the circle around Amoffel, Sinsong is at **Targutai**'s flank. The griffon is hitched into one of the carts in place of a missing horse, and the arrangement has been getting worse by the day — Targutai would sooner eat the horse next to him than pull alongside it, and the only thing keeping him in the traces is Sinsong's steady handling, a hand on the harness and every trick he has for a difficult beast. When that falls short he lays a word of command on him — *hold* — and the halt passes without blood on the harness, barely.
6. At the next rest stop Nyx draws Amoffel aside with Mialee at her shoulder, and they identify as Harpers themselves — at which the cover falls away. He had been riding infiltrated with a caravan a few days ahead of this one — a merchant convoy with cultists hidden among the merchants — and when the cultists made him, they fed the merchants the story that he was passing intel to bandits; the merchants, unwilling to kill him outright, left him buried in the road to let providence decide his fate.
7. The hunt is the same one — the stolen loot moving north — and the name **Leosin** is already in his mouth before anyone offers it. He names **Vauser** too, a half-orc Harper contact in Waterdeep: muscular, red-haired, a scar running across his face, reachable at *The Broken Jar* when the party gets that far.
8. Amoffel takes a place in one of the back wagons. The party briefs him of their situation, the three carts at the front of the caravan are the cultists, and the loot has been identified. Then they ask for his help against them, he agrees, and from here he rides with the caravan as a third Harper on the trail.
9. The following days go by without any remarkable event as the caravan progresses through the Trade Way.

## 2 - Dragonspear Castle
_16:00, 30 Uktar 1489 DR_

1. By the afternoon of the last day of Uktar the road turns west around a long shallow rise, and the ruins come into view — a hulk of blackened stone standing in a sea of grass, towers broken down, walls staved in, a low mist clinging to the ground around the foundations even under the midday sun.
2. **Eldkin** rides up out of the caravan's middle to Nyx's side, as if the sight of the stones has pulled her forward out of the line, and names the place quietly, for Nyx alone to hear — **Dragonspear Castle**. A dwarven fortress once, she says, raised by one of her own.
3. She tells Nyx what she will. It had been built by a dwarf called **Daeros Dragonspear**, long before her grandfathers were born, with an artefact weapon set at its heart that gave the castle its name. For a time it stood as a hold of her people on the High Moor — her kin in the halls and dwarven runes cut deep into the stone above the gate.
4. Then it fell — to fiends, she says. They came up from somewhere under the stones. Her kin bled for the hold of the place twice over, and in the end the castle was ruined and nothing the dwarves had set in it was ever recovered.
5. Today it sits as the party sees it — mist on the ground that does not clear, no smoke from any hearth, no voice of any watch — and the merchants steer the carts a touch wider as they pass. Eldkin does not look back as the road carries on, and Nyx does not press her for more.

## 3 - The golden stag
_20:00, 30 Uktar 1489 DR_

1. Later that day the road rises past a stretch of open ground, and late sunlight falls on the hillside where a herd of deer grazes in the grass. Among them stands a single stag whose coat catches the light like beaten gold, antlers like platinum. The caravan halts at the sight.
2. Half the guards reach straight for their bows — that hide would pay a season, meat for a week, a trophy worth a year of road — the other half hold back, hands raised: a creature coloured like that is not game, it is omen, and bringing it down will bring worse down behind it.
3. As the archers start running toward the hill, Nyx throws a cloud of fog across the slope, dense and low; the hunters lose line of sight and the stag within it, and in the thick of the mist they scatter in different directions after a glimpse that is no longer there.
4. Behind the mist the party divides. Nyx has caught the stag's trail at the far side of the fog and goes after it; Mialee and Nuberroca go with her. Sinsong, Eguzkilore, and Amidamaru stay with the carts to settle the hunters when they come back empty-handed.
5. The chase runs harder than any deer should, and the three of them follow at a long pace through the brush. Somewhere in the ground where the trail turns in on itself Nuberroca loses the track and falls behind; Nyx and Mialee come on alone at last to a moss-grown, ivy-draped ruin in a wooded hollow — a ring of fallen stones, roofless, the trees crowded close against the walls.
6. The stag is already standing there when they arrive, light still on his coat though the sun has fallen below the trees. He turns at the stones and speaks — Sylvan, carrying across the clearing. Nyx takes it in as a Fey-speaker. Mialee hears it through a spell for the understanding of tongues.
7. Behind the shape of the stag both of them feel the hand of **Silvanus** — god of wild places, of what grows and keeps watch. Neither of them speaks the name aloud. Both of them lower their bows.
8. ***[EPIC]*** The stag confirms they are on the right track, and leaves the prophecy on them: ***"Follow the river of gold until you reach the castle in the sky. Not all will survive..."***
9. When the voice goes silent a longbow is lying at their feet — a deer head carved into the grip, its antlers forming the bow itself. The stag fades into the trees between one breath and the next. Nyx takes up the bow.
10. In the silence of the clearing the two of them stand a long moment with the prophecy still in the air between them. Then the last of the daylight goes out of the trees and they turn back the way they came, to find Nuberroca on the back-trail and the caravan still halted on the road.

## 4 - Diverse rites
_~09:00, Feast of the Moon 1489 DR_

1. The Feast of the Moon falls between the last day of Uktar and the first of Nightal — a day set apart from the months, and from the road. By mid-morning the carts have drawn up to a roadside tavern still within sight of Dragonspear Castle to the east, and halt there for the full day.
2. The hours pass easily enough: horses rested in the stable, harness mended, bread and salt-pork taken under the awning. By late afternoon the sun hangs low to the west and the first bite of winter has come in on the air; lamps are lit in the common room as the day thins.
3. **Eldkin** keeps her own rite out in the tavern yard, apart from the warm room, at a small fire of her own under the open sky — a dwarven Commemoration of the Fallen. Nyx comes out and sits with her, and the names Eldkin speaks under her breath are no longer only her private grief. **Daeros Dragonspear** she names first, raiser of the castle on the High Moor. Then the names of her kin who held the hold in the two sieges and did not come out of it — a dozen names and more, one after another, until the small fire between them runs down to coals.
4. Inside the tavern the main fire belongs to Shiallia: a feast built at the hearth, warm cider in hands, caravan voices and the tavern's own folk rising together in old songs across the room.
5. In one corner a short Lliira observance has gone up as well — a weapon laid flat on the ground under a scatter of flowers, chanted over quietly.
6. The rest of the party take their places at the long table and the hearth, warming hands and passing the cider, but stay outside the Ritual of Remembrance; the stories told that night belong to other travellers.

## 5 - A griffon flies off
_~08:00, 1 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The road rolls on through Nightal's deepening cold; the stretch takes ten days with nothing worth the breath of a retelling [GM].
2. One morning Targutai goes wild in the harness-line — jaws on a carthorse's neck, hard enough to open skin, then another horse, then another [GM] [player:Pablo]. Amidamaru snaps a quick working over the nearest carthorse and the beast vanishes from sight, out of reach of the griffon's eye; the trick holds, and that horse walks the rest of the morning unseen and unharmed [player:Pablo].
3. Sinsong puts himself between the griffon and the carts and talks him down, barely holding him before he can turn on another mount [player:Pablo]. Once the griffon has settled, Amidamaru works along the harness-line tending the wounded horses, while the merchants give the party dark looks the rest of the day [player:Pablo].
4. Later in the stretch the griffon is quiet for a span, and then without warning takes to the air and does not come back [GM].

## 6 - No room at the inn
_~19:00?, 11 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. A miserable wet-and-freezing day closes at a large inn with the walls of a castle rising at its back [module p.54] [GM]. Two women are already outside under the awning, drenched and out of options — twin sisters **Arietta** and **Zelina Innevar**, bound for Waterdeep themselves and turned away from the door [plan L.57] [module p.54] [GM]. Eguzkilore draws alongside them with a general greeting and the sisters pick him up with a cordial flirtation, Nyx joining the circle and Amoffel standing with them under the awning; between them they lean straight into questions — where from, where to, who else on the caravan, who is paired with whom — until they have every detail they can get [GM].
2. Nuberroca stops at the awning long enough for the sisters to catch his eye before Mialee pulls him through the door, the two of them the only ones of the party to go inside [player:Alfonso] [WA Sergio 2025-09-21 20:07] [GM]. The common room is empty but for three well-dressed men at the one lit table, swords across their laps and the build of men who use them, one of the three plainly carrying the voice [GM]. On the table between them sits a strange **lamp** that holds a tiny humanoid figure of soft blue light, two or three inches high, blurry at the edges and apparently content inside [GM].
3. **Barlon** the innkeeper bows into the exchange, polite and apologetic: the noble upstairs has paid for the whole inn — the common room, every room, every stall in the stable [module p.54] [GM]. When Nuberroca asks about the stables anyway, the lead of the three at the table calls over, loud enough for Barlon to repeat the line he was told to pass on: *"nuestros caballos son muy exigentes con quién comparten espacio; nos hemos tenido que reservar los establos también, para su tranquilidad"* [module p.54] [S23:L44-46].
4. The three laugh at them, drop the next remark at Barlon's expense, and settle back to their table and the lamp [S23:L220-223] [GM]. The noble himself has not been seen since Nuberroca entered, and nobody in the room is expecting to see him tonight — he is upstairs in a private room and has not come down all evening [GM].
5. Nuberroca stands his ground with Mialee at his shoulder; Barlon moves between them pleading, while the three at the table watch pointedly bored and ready. The session cuts there [GM] [S23:L47-48].
