## 1 - Amidamaru at Nyx's side
_~12:31, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The GM opens exactly where the previous session ended [T:6-14] — Nuberroca in the north-west writing room with two cultists left standing, and Nyx on the flagstones outside where Atascaburras's stray bite had dropped her a heartbeat earlier [T:101-105]. Nuberroca closes on the first cultist with the halberd, opens his throat, and the swing carries clean on into the second man's shoulder — both drop before the round ends [T:118-125]. He sweeps the papers off the table into his pack, brushing coin and trinket aside to free the sheets — the gold is not what he came for [T:134-138], [T:320-323].
2. Amidamaru drops to Nyx's flank, lays himself against her, whispers words no-one present recognises, and a soft red light spreads under his hands over the arrow wounds; Nyx breathes, and wakes conscious [T:189-200 ?ord]. Amidamaru cannot say afterwards what he did, and mid-turn Sinsong asks him — *"¿Qué has hecho para curar a Nyx? Quiero hacer lo mismo con Kaelthar"* — before the fight cuts back in and he sets the answer aside for later [T:363-370].
3. Sinsong loads first Nyx and then Kaelthar onto Atascaburras — three riders is too many, the lizard falls to half his speed under the load [T:263-291 ?ord].

## 2 - The toad-mage's bell and the acid sphere

1. The wizard-toad climbs into the room from the upper stair, chants at Amidamaru, and rings a small bell at his ear; Amidamaru's save fails badly and his eyes roll back — he pitches face-first onto the flagstones, unconscious [T:376-394]. Nuberroca, further west, sees the mage framed in the doorway from the north-east passage [T:401-412].
2. The mage then drops a vitriolic sphere on the party's clumped position at the base of the stair — an acid cloud bursts open and covers everyone in it [T:571-586]. Nuberroca, Eguzkilore, and Sinsong throw themselves clear; Mialee only clears by burning her stored inspiration; Atascaburras eats the full brunt; Kaelthar, unconscious, cannot dodge [T:598-611]. Nyx makes hers on the GM's ruling [T:606-611].
3. ***[EPIC]*** Eguzkilore breaks toward the acid-drenched Kaelthar, works his sleeve into the coating over the paladin's chest and — with a stroke of impossible cleverness — strips the acid off before the second-round burn triggers. Kaelthar's life is saved [T:706-722].
4. Atascaburras trembles in place before Sinsong reaches him and the acid wounds close of their own — no visible cause [T:733-737]. Sinsong then lays a Balm of Peace across the group [T:738-745]: the healing reaches Nyx, Atascaburras, and Eguzkilore of the acid burn, but does not touch Kaelthar and passes Nuberroca by without effect [T:766-782].

## 3 - Nuberroca's halberd and Sinsong's prayer

1. Sinsong retreats with Atascaburras — the lizard carrying Nyx and Kaelthar — clearing the wall behind Nuberroca [T:801-814]; he drops down the far side and pulls into cover in the yard on foot [T:824-843].
2. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca charges the wizard-toad down the stair, drives the halberd home so hard the point breaks out through the mage's back, and Mialee steps in from behind with her dagger to hold the body in place while Nuberroca pushes it off the shaft. The mage is dead before he hits the stones [T:864-891]. Nuberroca takes the mage's staff: the protuberances at the tip are coated in a green slime that matches the bullywug secretions [T:896-903].
3. Kaelthar's breathing fails behind them; Mialee tries medicine on the paladin and fails, then Eguzkilore tries and fails too [T:930-960]. ***[EPIC]*** Sinsong drops to his knees over Kaelthar and speaks an unrehearsed prayer to a god he cannot name — *"Dios, el que sea… Ilumínanos, por favor"* — and lays his last healing on the paladin's chest. Kaelthar wakes, on his feet [T:1119-1145].
4. Kaelthar equips the Thayan greatsword recovered from S52 in place of his longsword and takes position covering the north door [T:1160-1189]. He still carries the cursed cuts left from the regenerating Thayan [T:1168-1170].
5. Nuberroca, moments later, feeds crocodile flesh to still-unconscious Amidamaru — the tortle's starving reflex closes his beak around the meat and he chews a bite without waking [T:1352-1356].

## 4 - Atascaburras infected

1. Atascaburras suddenly turns on his rider — springs off Sinsong's line and lunges for Mialee. His eyes are bloodshot, and a red shadow behind them she has not seen before; the bite misses [T:1029-1033]. Mialee reads the signs on a close look — the bloody eyes and red shadow [T:1053-1058], and the day's refusal of food she had noticed but not connected [T:1063-1067] — and names it aloud as **carnomorph infection** [T:1069-1072].
2. Eguzkilore holds out a ration to the lizard; Atascaburras sniffs it and settles, docile again for the moment [T:1101-1112].
3. Nuberroca opens the south door onto the lizardfolk stables — empty; the mounts are all south in the fighting [T:1196-1203]. He drags Amidamaru along with him as he moves [T:1215-1218]. Mialee, meanwhile, works through a smithy on another floor and gathers fifteen crude arrows off the bench for Eguzkilore's quiver [T:1240-1253]; upstairs she spots crates carrying the same brand-marks as those the party had seen on **Namocel**'s caravan and in the cultists' house [T:1478-1484]. From the window Sinsong spots the swarm of small **bonelicher** masses moving through the eastern brush toward the castle [T:1297-1303 ?ord].
4. Nuberroca pushes through the next south door into a nest of giant centipedes; he braces behind Amidamaru's shell for cover and swings the toad-mage's staff at them — the green slime crusted on the protuberances does far more damage than the wood alone, and he crushes several to a paste in successive hits [T:1698-1732], [T:1885-1899]. Between passes the party warn him about the slime — Nuberroca concedes and pitches the staff aside, re-equipping his own halberd [T:2049-2069].

## 5 - The dragonborn's quarters

1. Mialee climbs to the recently-furnished draconic quarters higher in the tower — clean new furniture, two tall onyx dragon statues too heavy to move flanking the interior door [T:1755-1780], an armour stand carrying scale mail sized for a dragonborn, a wardrobe of elegant clothes [T:2296-2320]. On a low table she finds a smaller statuette carved from real dragon scales [T:2091-2098 ?ord]; when she nudges its base with her rapier the whole piece tips and shatters on the floor — no trap, only brittle craftsmanship [T:2115-2133]. She names the room's tenant aloud — a black-scaled dragonborn [T:2326-2331].
2. Mialee opens the north wardrobe from the doorway with her mage-hand [T:2381-2395]; the moment the door swings, an acid cloud bursts out of the wardrobe [T:2397-2399] and catches her and Kaelthar (who had followed her in) [T:2408-2417]. Whatever was inside the wardrobe dissolves with the vapour [T:2418-2421].
3. In the meantime Nuberroca reaches the kitchens carrying Amidamaru and finds two cooks kneeling beside Mialee and Kaelthar, both of them unconscious and stretched along the wall. The cooks raise their hands: a lizardman had ordered them to take the two bodies down here and had gone back upstairs alone [T:2593-2607]. Nuberroca goes through Mialee's pockets for healing potions and comes out with four common and one greater [T:2653-2665]; he pours the first potion into Kaelthar and the second into Mialee, and both come around [T:2666-2682].
4. Kaelthar tells the group he saw a dwarven woman standing over him during his unconsciousness — she had laid her hand on his chest, closed her fist, and pulled *something* out of him he had not known he was carrying. He had woken emotionally lighter, a peace inside he had not felt in a long time [T:2696-2709]. It is the second such vision in as many sessions ([S52 minutes §2.3](../session-52/minutes-52.md)).
5. **Tharn** the dwarf cook offers beer in place of a fight; Nuberroca accepts, and Tharn takes them into the bodega and pours jars for whoever will drink [T:2716-2745]. Kaelthar summons his wolf **Fenris** out of empty air in the pantry — Tharn drops the milk jar he is pouring for Mialee, panics at the wolf rising up from the stones, and bolts up the stairs [T:2749-2752]. Nuberroca calls him back on the offer of survival and Tharn agrees to come along rather than stay [T:2826-2832]. The party stocks about twenty rations from the pantry on the way through [T:2758-2774 ?ord].

## 6 - Down to the caves

1. From the top-floor stair Sinsong watches six cultists file down past him toward the yard — and a **Metagorger** answers by climbing through the window; the cultists turn and rush it, opening a window to slip past unseen [T:2996-3006]. The party regroups in the sotherly cave — old bullywug ground, walls holed by insect action, half-devoured corpses on the floor [T:3170-3176].
2. Atascaburras, still ridden by Sinsong, jerks and bites at his rider — Nuberroca catches the lizard by the neck in time and takes the worst of the strike off [T:3072-3079 ?ord]. The eyes bleed red for a heartbeat and then clear [T:3079-3091]. Sinsong tries to send him up the stair to fight; the lizard lunges at him a second time and Nuberroca has to catch him again [T:3094-3098].
3. A Metagorger comes down the stair after them; Nuberroca's halberd shears two of its front bone-legs off and it pitches forward [T:3345-3362]. Kaelthar cuts in with the Thayan greatsword and Fenris takes a second bite on the same pass [T:3316-3345 ?ord]. Mialee's arrow finishes it [T:3395-3400]. Sinsong burns the corpse to ashes with a firebolt so no second carnomorph erupts from it [T:3408-3409].
4. A second Metagorger — bathed in acid — enters from the east and starts dissolving the corpse-litter as it advances [T:3427-3445]. Nuberroca strikes it once with a spear; the strike splashes acid back onto him, his armour begins to melt, and the spear's steel dulls under the vapour [T:3438-3446]. He withdraws north [T:3448-3454].
5. Sinsong points Atascaburras at the acid-carnomorph and gives him the order to fight [T:3450-3451]; the party runs north to the far cave. From the south they hear the lizard's roars and the wet sound of gelatine — Atascaburras dies down there fighting the second carnomorph, buying them the time to escape [derived:T:3483-3486+T:3489-3491].
6. Amidamaru wakes at last as the party's flight slows past the scorched corpses of the toad-mage's guardians; he pulls to his feet and runs after them [T:3506-3509].

## 7 - Anton's paper and the Blythefield circle
_~13:00?, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. In the north cave the party stops to read the papers Nuberroca lifted from the writing room. Mialee shares one — an *informe de Anton* — that names Anton as the man who murdered Carlos Namocel on the caravan, and that lays out Anton's intent to let the Thayans do the party's dirty work: *"El grupo de Anton llega, son violentos, no estrategas. Usad eso, dejad que maten a los tallanos"* [T:3586-3597]. Another paper turns the massacre at Karnaz into a story about Sinsong personally leading the killing of civilians [T:3606-3607].
2. On the north cave floor is a large magical drawing Sinsong recognises through Arcana as some kind of teleport pattern he does not know how to trigger [T:3559-3567 ?ord]. Amidamaru, coming back to himself, confirms it — teleport, needs a key word [T:3656-3664]. Nuberroca tries words at random — first *Melon*, then *Blythefield* — the second word wakes the circle [T:3667-3675].
3. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee, Nuberroca, and Sinsong vanish first; Eguzkilore, Nyx, and Amidamaru step onto the circle after them (Tharn beside Eguzkilore) and vanish too. The party appears standing on an identical circle high in snowy mountains — the humid castle air replaced with sharp cold, pine forest all round, and a long house of several storeys at the far end of a footpath across the clearing [T:3676-3702]. The GM closes on the threshold [T:3703]. The party levels up to 6 [T:3729-3730].
