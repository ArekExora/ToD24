# Session 45 {{float:"right" 2026-05-01}}
{wide}

## 1 - At the moat
_~15:00, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party is regrouped outside the moat around the crocodile carcasses from the earlier fight — Nuberroca still bloody from working one open with his hands [T:39, 82]. Mialee has found the tunnel-mouth that connects the moat to the castle interior; after several wrong turns she has the correct path, and the party has held short of committing to go through [T:35-36 ?ord].
2. They settle on the plan: slip through the tunnel, find and kill the bullywug leader, and leave lizardfolk sign on the body to spark a revolt between frogs and lizardfolk [T:177-178]. Nuberroca offers to fashion a necklace of crocodile teeth and leave it as a lure; freshly-pulled teeth would obviously look freshly pulled, so he rubs them dirty against his own arse to age them [T:120-128].
3. Eguzkilore has grown a step surer on his feet — ground that would mire the others passes under him clean, useful in a marsh where every step is a slog [T:147-153].

## 2 - Amidamaru returns

1. As Mialee turns to scout the tunnel, Amidamaru appears behind Nuberroca, warning the party not to do anything stupid [T:311-314].
2. He explains he was contacted a week ago by a woman he could not identify, who told him the party was in danger; he came as fast as he could, but strange creatures on the road forced him to circle wide before he could find them [T:334].
3. The scouting question is settled: Mialee and Amidamaru will slip through the tunnel first while the rest wait; the sending stone will signal the others when the way is clear [T:367-368].
4. Nyx is still visibly torn from the crocodile attacks — the same wounds Nuberroca carries [T:412-415] — and Amidamaru casts a small healing on both of them before they regroup [T:428].
5. They set a weighted guide-rope tied to a shore-stake, to steer the others through the flooded tunnel once Mialee's signal comes [T:457-462].

## 3 - Through the flooded tunnel

1. Mialee and Amidamaru submerge and swim the flooded tunnel — twenty feet across the moat pool, then ten, thirty, forty, and eighty more feet of near-straight passage, turning right at the first fork, right at the second, left at the third [T:479, 482-483].
2. They surface into near-total darkness. Amidamaru pulls out a magic-lit pebble to see, but Mialee stops him — the party has to pass unseen, there may be enemies — and he pockets it, setting Munin the owl on his shoulder to borrow its night-sight instead [T:500-503, 511, 571].
3. What little light there is comes from luminescent green fungus clinging to the rocks [T:539-540]. The air is heavy, wet, and full of the sound of running water, dripping, and constant frog-croaking on every side [T:572, 733-736].
4. Mialee scouts a chamber to their right, hugging the shadows. A tiny lagoon inside is packed with tadpoles — smaller than the giant ones the party has been fighting outside — and two bullywugs stand over them, tending the brood [T:628-634]. She calls them *"las mamás bullywugs"* [T:634].
5. She signals through the sending stone: the entrance chamber is clear, two bullywugs to the right, come through [T:675-678].

## 4 - The rest of the party comes through

1. Nyx slots into the crossing: the party goes in order — Nyx leading, Eguzkilore second, Nuberroca and Kaelthar behind — using the guide-rope through the last stretch where their breath runs short [T:700-704, 709-715].
2. Kaelthar has left his heavy armour on the outer shore — the mission is stealth and the plate would splash and clang [T:222-231 ?ord]. The rest are already soaked to the bone.
3. Nyx hands Amidamaru back one of his quivers — empty now; she says she ran through his arrows quickly in the last fight [T:753-761].
4. Amidamaru's return prompts an arrow count: Nyx has burned through her stock in the crocodile fight and only has one left [T:761-764]. Once they share out what everyone carries, each archer ends up with eighteen apiece [T:775-779]. Kaelthar remembers that the lizardfolk stripped his javelins from him before the castle, so he has nothing to throw [T:784].
5. Eguzkilore weaves a soft field over the group as they move, the water sound around Nuberroca fading unnaturally as he steps clear of the pool [T:866-868]. Anyone within his aura moves silently, though Nuberroca and Kaelthar need to stay close to keep the effect [T:838-846].

## 5 - The nursery guards

1. The party creeps toward the two bullywugs. Kaelthar takes the front without armour [T:970-971], Nyx behind him, Nuberroca groping through the near-blackness by touch, tapping Eguzkilore's shoulder to stay oriented [T:1023-1032].
2. The bullywugs turn towards them — they've heard something. They mutter to each other in low voices, weapons raised, still not quite locating the intruders [T:1039-1044].
3. Kaelthar swings first — the first cut lands hard, flame searing from the blade, and the second drives in with divine fire [T:1292-1340]; the bullywug he engaged answers with a pair of hard strikes [T:1064-1066]. Nuberroca reaches through the dark, swinging his halberd where the sound comes from, and connects for two heavy blows [T:1066-1156].
4. Mialee slides into a flanking position and looses her first shot from the shadows; it finds the first bullywug and drops it on the spot [T:1219-1239].
5. Seeing its partner dead, the surviving bullywug shrieks a sharp warning that carries into the caverns — danger, they are being attacked — and swings back at Kaelthar, its blade catching his unprotected ribs twice for a heavy wound, the edge slick with a stinging venom [T:1285, 1340-1380].
6. Nuberroca circles into its blind side and cleaves through with a savage halberd stroke, dropping the second one [T:1367-1370]; the room falls back into wet silence except for the tadpoles thrashing.
7. Kaelthar is bleeding badly, his skin already welting from the poison, and drops to one knee. He closes the worst of the wounds with a laying-on of hands, though the venom-burn stays [T:1413-1437].

## 6 - Lights in the dark

1. Mialee sees small round lights approaching from the far end of the water — motes of pale magical light drifting in slow arcs [T:1496-1500, 1569].
2. The party listens. Nuberroca picks up more splashing from deeper in the caves; the frog-croaking that has been constant since they arrived starts to thin, then stops in patches — *"este silencio me inquieta"* [T:1508-1526].
3. Amidamaru names it at once: it is a wizard's scouting light, and whoever cast it is coming to see what tripped the alarm [T:1634].
4. The party debates: retreat down the tunnel, hide in the tadpole chamber, or push forward. Amidamaru argues that a party of six can't slip past unseen — some should feign a retreat while one or two try to pass hidden [T:1586-1589 ?ord].
5. They fold back into the nursery cave. Amidamaru wedges himself into the water and pulls into his shell like a stone on the bottom [T:1667-1671]. Nuberroca stays in the light, sword and shield in hand [T:1908, 1918-1926]. Mialee presses to the wall in what little dimness she can find [T:2179-2185]. Everyone tries to go unseen — for once, hoping to blend into the shadows rather than stand out [T:1641-1644].
6. Nuberroca hears a rhythmic chant from the water past a small island [T:1685-1688, 1992], then a rush of croaking as one of the lights fixes on a frog that has spotted him and starts calling — the frog knows exactly where he is even without light [T:1726-1735].

## 7 - Acid from the mist

1. From the small island in the water a green mote of light sputters, then swells to the size of a head as it flies at the party — the wizard's throw. It bursts among them and covers the chamber in acid [T:1992-2005]. The walls give no cover; everyone but Amidamaru turns their body against it in time [T:2035, 2047]. Amidamaru, caught in his shell, takes the full lingering burn [T:2050-2054, 2343-2344].
2. The fight erupts. Waves of giant frogs start hurling themselves out of the water at Nuberroca — first one, then more, leaping impossible distances to land on him and clamp jaws [T:1842-1845 ?ord, 2528, 2622].
3. Eguzkilore and Nyx loose arrows into whatever the passing motes of light briefly reveal [T:1797-1830 ?ord, 1843-1858].
4. Nuberroca digs in and calls on the fury of his gods — the older wounds on him close visibly as he fights [T:1929-1937].
5. Kaelthar advances into the mess without armour. A wave of giant-frog bites and a bullywug champion's follow-up strike drop him in a single exchange [T:2617-2641]; Kaelthar falls to the flooded stones, unconscious.
6. Nuberroca reaches Kaelthar and tips one of Nyx's berries into his mouth [T:2754]; Kaelthar comes back to the edge of consciousness and shuffles aside on foot — a walking step is all that grace affords him in that moment [T:2799-2817].
7. A moment later Nuberroca himself vanishes in a step of grey vapour and reappears five feet under the water, holding his breath in the dark [T:2841-2853].
8. The frogs stop chasing Nuberroca and turn on Nyx — five at once, tearing at her, until one clamps onto her and swallows her down [T:2660-2668 ?ord, 2740].
9. Nyx cuts her way out from inside — she drives her shortsword through the frog's belly and crawls free [GM].
10. ***[EPIC]*** From out of the mist the frog-mage closes on Amidamaru and lands a devastating strike — a venom-slick blade at close quarters that finds the joint and dislocates his elbow [T:2988-2999, 3001]. He drops — his first grievous wound [T:2993-2995].
11. Kaelthar steps across the chamber in a flash of grey vapour and reappears beside Eguzkilore, out of the frog pack's reach [T:3255-3266].
12. Eguzkilore reaches the fallen Amidamaru and slips one of Nyx's berries into his mouth [T:3556-3565]. Amidamaru rises, barely clinging on.

## 8 - Killing the mage

1. Mialee throws a fog cloud over the fight — a twenty-foot sphere that blinds everyone inside, party and frog-mage both [T:2926, 3061-3063]. Amidamaru presses close and cuts the mage with a thunder-charged rapier strike [T:3408-3425].
2. Eguzkilore holds his bow at the edge of the mist. Mialee shouts him a bearing through it — *open to the left* — and he moves those few steps sideways until he can see the mage's silhouette [T:3049-3067].
3. ***[EPIC]*** He looses. A perfect shot through the mist finds the mage; Eguzkilore passes the credit back to Mialee — *"el mérito es de Mialee, me ha avisado"* [T:3070-3081]. The mage is left on the brink.
4. A moment later Nyx puts a second arrow through the mage — the shot finds him even across the chamber — and cuts the tendon in one leg, leaving him prone and dragging himself toward the water [T:3163-3220]. Later in the fight she finishes him with a final shot to the back; the mage sinks, jerking, into the water [T:3587-3591].
5. As the mage dies the scouting motes wink out all at once. Eguzkilore takes the cue: if the lights are gone, the toad is no longer alive [T:3594-3600].
6. ***[EPIC]*** Nyx is caught a second time by another giant frog — pulled fully into the throat as it starts to leave the chamber with her [T:3951-3963, 4181-4182]. Amidamaru and Eguzkilore break off and chase; Amidamaru's rapier bites in from one side [T:4157-4165] and Eguzkilore drops the frog with a close shot [T:4197-4207] — Nyx crawls out barely alive [T:4204].
7. The remaining bullywugs, blind in the same fog Mialee raised, blunder into each other and take swings at empty air [T:3531 ?ord, 4173]. Kaelthar, with nothing to throw, breathes fire through the mist and catches one frog outright — Nuberroca, caught at the edge of the cone, has his beard singed down the front [T:3835-3873].
8. Nuberroca hits a bullywug champion with a reckless swing [T:3690-3712], though another catches him from behind moments later for a heavy blow [T:3941-3948]; Mialee cuts into another giant frog from the shadows with paired daggers [T:4136-4147].
9. The last of the pack breaks and dives into the water. Mialee runs it down and takes it with a final shot as it tries to escape — it was the one wounded at the beginning, trying all along to survive [T:4247-4254].
10. When the noise stops the party stands amid a mess of frog carcasses and dead bullywugs. The wizard's body lies in the acid-slick where he fell; his venom-staff was broken in the fight and lost to the acid [GM]. The chamber, and the way back to the moat, is theirs for the moment.
