## 1 - The late arrival
_~19:00, 17 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Outside in the snow, Amidamaru has sat out the whole first act of the ambush — keeping **Trepsin**'s mossy cloak away from the lodge so no one would mark him for the butcher's companion, and now satisfied the cloth carries no magic [T:111-114].
2. As he closes on the house at a run, his eye catches on the glint of nearly five silver in stray coppers along the floorboards inside the window — rolled into the corner from someone's robbery earlier in the hall — and he scoops them into his belt as he goes past [T:72-77 ?ord].
3. ***[EPIC]*** Amidamaru drops the cloak in the snow, unslings a backpack into the branches so his shell can breathe, calls his bladesong, and dashes the full ninety feet to the lodge; at the window he lowers his horn and crashes shell-first through the glass into the antechamber, finding his footing among the dead [T:150-234].
4. Eguzkilore pulls off the wall, puts two arrows into the troll still half-stuck at the breach, and catches a second wind as the shaft leaves the bow [T:238-268].
5. Sinsong, half-frozen from the earlier cold blast, finds his voice through the murder-hole wall — shouts for help, swears the trolls have cut Talis down and the staff must come save them — a panicked lie the kitchen crew cannot quite credit, but it buys a full beat of hesitation [T:285-340].
6. The same breath weaves a bond of warding light on Kaelthar, Nyx and Mialee — the three stir out of unconsciousness, though Nyx and Mialee remain welded to the ice [T:287-329].
7. Mialee strains against the ice with everything the cold has left her, and the ice wins [T:341-349].

## 2 - Pendants in the kitchen
_~19:05, 17 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The troll at the window drags its dead companion aside to make room for itself, swings once at Eguzkilore and bites only stone [T:351-366]. Its brother on the goliath pins Nuberroca by the wrist, catching a claw in the barbarian's grip ready to be run through on the next swing [T:367-379].
2. Amidamaru pushes forward through the hall and comes face to face with **Morrigan** — the cook who had watched them at the door before, kitchen rag thrown aside, boiled leather visible under the cloth and a pendant shaped like a dragon's eye at her throat [T:400-413].
3. She refuses him passage. He stabs with the rapier anyway, non-lethal — the thrust slips between her ribs and perforates a lung — and Morrigan screams across the hall that it is the party who are the attackers, turning the whole kitchen staff on him [T:415-453].
4. Nuberroca drives the magical spear through the pinned troll's flank with his god's fury behind it, works a second thrust past its guard, and butts it back with the shaft; the beast is left standing only on habit [T:461-500].
5. Kaelthar heaves himself upright beside the stair, carries his longsword into the staggering troll in a straight overhead swing, and fells it where it stands — the east wing falls quiet behind the broken wall [T:504-514].

## 3 - The dragon-eye pendants
_~19:10, 17 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Seeing Amidamaru's blade already in Morrigan's ribs, the kitchen staff defend themselves. Morrigan grips her pendant, mutters a few words, and a spectral sword flickers into being behind the turtle and strikes at his back from behind the ring of cooks that keeps him from turning [T:516-524].
2. From the opposite flank **Oberyn** brings his sword down on the unconscious Nyx in a cold, clean stroke; she stops twitching and he throws himself atop her to bind her mouth. The cultists are striking to subdue the whole way through — the lodge wants the party alive [T:525-534].
3. **Fenwick** confirms Talis dead at a glance — triturated — and leaps across the hall to the frozen Mialee. Another clean strike lands; the wound bites deep enough to leave her something she will carry for days [T:534-545].
4. **Lysa** crosses from the kitchen door past Nuberroca, her own pendant in hand, eyes up at Sinsong on the stair; the *ven aquí* she murmurs is for the cleric, and his will slips under it before he can set his feet [T:544-575].
5. Eguzkilore sheathes his bow for a sword he was not ready to swing — the troll's guard slaps the blade out of his grip on the draw and spins it across the planks away from him [T:559-565].

6. Sinsong, still on the floor behind the stair and half out of his fighting mind, crawls his way open to the first door, meets a kobold's face on the other side, and quietly shuts it again [T:569-575]. Half for terror, half for the ache of the cold still in his bones, he burns himself down to call up his circle of fey guardians at a working he cannot usually reach — Nuberroca snaps at him to save the spells for healing instead and he refuses: *"voy a hacer lo que me salga de los huevos"* [T:578-620].
7. The cleric then opens the next door and finds **Tharn**, the one cook they had always liked, calmly filleting meat. Tharn hears the shouting, drops his knife, and ambles toward the fray: *"¡hombre, Sinsong! ¿qué pasa? que hay mucho ruido"* [T:667-681]. Sinsong tries to send him off with a polite lie, but Tharn walks straight into the aura of fey — the spirits open his face as he passes, and he goes down clutching his own throat before he understands [T:680-711].

## 4 - The halberd and the headless cook
_~19:15, 17 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The troll inside the antechamber regenerates, finds Eguzkilore disarmed on the planks, and opens his throat with both claws; the elf slides down the stones gurgling [T:714-729].
2. Nuberroca charges Lysa down the hall calling for her death. The first thrust of the spear she turns on her blade; the second drives through her dress and rips the cloth open to show cultist armour beneath, and the shaft-strike that follows rolls her sideways [T:741-790].
3. Amidamaru stops trying to push past Morrigan and shoves her off balance; the jolt shakes the magic out of her and the spectral sword dissolves mid-swing [T:802-826]. He slips out of the ring the way he came and vaults back through a window at the far wall [T:827-855].
4. Kaelthar stumbles past the stair, takes up his longsword with both hands as best he knows, and buries it through Lysa's skull — he has to brace his knee against the wall to pull free [T:862-883].
5. Morrigan's next spectral sword glides at Kaelthar's shoulder and the magic in it falters mid-swing — the blade passes through his plate without finding purchase [T:908-918]. Then Fenwick seizes his own pendant and cants a few words at Nuberroca; the goliath shakes the compulsion off clean [T:919-934]. Oberyn tries the same trick a heartbeat later — this time the barbarian's will slips — until the **Pendant of Iron Will** at his throat begins to glow and he tears the hold loose on a second breath [T:935-955].
6. Between the swings the party hears the cultists arguing through the far door — one of them is holding out longer than he should, someone ought to activate the defences. They do not know how [T:956-962].

## 5 - Pants down in the kitchen

2. The troll shoulders back into the hall through the ruined frame and rains two claws on Kaelthar; the dragonborn drops again, limp against the stair [T:998-1022].
3. Nuberroca disengages across the floorboards and barges into the kitchen — demands to know what the cleric is doing on his knees — then grabs him by the collar, hoists him bodily, and marches him back to the hall with the pants still around his boots [T:1066-1103].
4. Set down in the circle of fey, Sinsong keeps the guardians on the troll from a crouch while Nuberroca turns back to the halberd-work [T:1098-1114]. 6. The cultists spring their retreat. Morrigan hoists the unconscious Nyx over her shoulder; Oberyn drags Mialee away; Fenwick takes up Talis's corpse and closes the heavy door behind them with a word to the troll: *acabad con ellos* [T:1192-1224].

## 10 - The cellar

1. In the cellar they find three captives, chained and hooded. The first is a dwarf with a face broken to pulp and fingernails torn out; the second a human woman less wrecked but still bruised; the third a man in city clothes, untouched [T:3164-3174]. Nuberroca cuts chains before he asks questions.
2. The dwarf laughs with the breath he has left when he hears Talis is dead — that bitch deserved to die, he says [T:3200-3209]. The woman is **Miresella** of Baldur's Gate, a caravan-walker who had turned aside at the lodge and been snared by a four-armed troll in a mossy cloak [T:3245-3261]. Nuberroca turns and gestures at Amidamaru — the turtle had carried that same cloak in before the fight, now dropped in the snow outside — and tells her the butcher is already dead; her eyes open very wide [T:3252-3262].
3. The man in the citizen's clothes is **Caemon** — a rank-and-file monk-brother of Amaunator from Hillsfar on pilgrimage to the chapel at **Parnast**, picked off the road by Talis and kept because his faith would not break [T:3286-3298]. Nuberroca asks after the second sun said to have hung above Elturel; Caemon calls it *the Gift of Amaunator*, a shield his order holds against the undead, and says his own cloth is too thin to call its light [T:3309-3346].
4. Nuberroca asks for a boon against the restless dead of his northern village; Caemon lays a hand on the barbarian's face and leaves a sliver of divine favour in it, nothing more [T:3347-3356]. Eguzkilore asks for help with his magic-rejecting condition; the priest reaches for a mild cure on him and the elf screams and drops in a heap on the stones as fresh welts bloom down his skin [T:3366-3391].
5. Caemon heals Nuberroca a little for the trouble, bows, and asks to be let go to sleep in a real bed somewhere upstairs; Miresella goes with him, picking a room without a corpse in it [T:3395-3421]. They give the dwarf's name as **Craggnor** — none of them know anything more of the cult than the names *Talis* and *Varram* [T:3425-3433].

## 11 - Craggnor's fire

1. The party put a halberd under his chin and demand names. He stonewalls, insists he knows nothing, sneers at Nuberroca's threats, keeps the composure of a soldier used to his own pain [T:3511-3542].
2. Mialee catches her breath as the others leave and takes the dossier out — Talis's own hand pegs Craggnor as **Varram the White**'s personal lieutenant and friend, with a bitter curse in the margin [T:3585-3594].
3. Sinsong wraps the barbarian in a sigil of close companionship, urging him to lean into his strength, and Nuberroca puts his face an inch from the dwarf's and squeezes his skull with both palms [T:3634-3660]. Craggnor's jaw gives out — he wants to know what they want [T:3659-3665].
4. Mialee asks about Varram. Craggnor confirms the name: *Varram el Blanco*, a wyrmspeaker — one of those who holds parley with the dragons themselves — and insists he does not know where the man is or what work his cult does at Parnast [T:3666-3680].
5. Nuberroca demands more and shoves the cult's dragon-insignia down the dwarf's throat; Craggnor's eyes light from inside, and a line of fire unspools from his chest into the barbarian's — a searing blast — and Nuberroca goes down smoking [T:3773-3795]. Craggnor burns his own bindings loose in the same breath [T:3796-3798].
6. Mialee sinks her blade into him under a draught of her new poison; Craggnor shrugs her off and hits her with three bare-knuckle blows that put her on the stones [T:3797-3871]. Sinsong calls to Amidamaru to intercept the next strike; the turtle steps between Mialee and the dwarf and takes a single crushing punch that drops him where he stands [T:3864-3905].
7. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee finishes it — a blade across Craggnor's neck as he reaches for his insignia again `?` — and the dwarf dies on the cellar floor. Sinsong crawls between the fallen and lays his hand on each of them in turn; the three breathe again, stable, before the session closes [T:3906-3921].
