# Session 7 {{float:"right" 2025-03-16}}
{wide}

## 1 - Lightning returns
_~15:00, 25 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. The corridor fight continues. **Frulam Mondath** stands in the chamber beyond with **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** and berserkers behind her, while at the doorway Nuberroca is still in his battle-fury with a sliver of life left in him. At the back of the corridor Eguzkilore carries the unconscious Sinsong, who is still bleeding out, and Amidamaru — having pulled back from the line — has turned back into the tunnel.
2. Nuberroca dissolves into a wisp of cloud and reforms at the back of the corridor, reaches for his pouch of bandages to staunch Sinsong's bleeding, then realises he can pour a healing potion directly down the dwarf's throat instead. The potion brings Sinsong back to consciousness, still in Eguzkilore's arms.
3. Nyx steps back from the doorway, murmurs some strange words, and pulls a tangle of vines up from the corridor stones — wide enough to take in all the cult fighters at the front. Every one of them tears clear as the creepers reach, but the floor is now a wrestling mat of green underfoot, and the cultists will have difficulty moving.
4. With three cultists pressing forward and the rest of the party at his back, Amidamaru plants himself in the corridor mouth, braced for any blow that comes: the tortle cannot run without leaving everyone else exposed.
5. **Frulam conjures a spectral blade beside her**; the blade pares at Mialee and misses, and the priestess advances toward the doorway.
6. Mialee leans out and looses an arrow at Frulam, then slips back along the wall — the priestess does not see her go.
7. Langdedrosa, lightning gathered in his throat once more, fixes the corridor again and advances towards the heroes.
8. The half-dragon breathes a clean white line down the passage, catching everyone in the corridor in single file. Sinsong, shielded by the rocks, avoids the direct hit; the rest of the party all twist clear of the worst, but the line still rakes them — Nuberroca and Mialee (still hidden along the wall) weather it, while **Eguzkilore, Nyx, Amidamaru and Sinsong all collapse to the stones, unconscious**.
9. From the chamber beyond, Mia hears a low roar: *"No sé dónde estás, pero te buscaré y te cazaré."*
10. ***[EPIC]*** Eguzkilore, lying on his back bleeding out, fights death off with a sudden surge; his eyes flutter open and he pushes himself up. Between the unconscious Sinsong and the unconscious Nyx beside him he picks Nyx — smaller and easier to carry — gathers the halfling under one arm, and sprints for the cave mouth.

## 2 - Hauling the wounded

1. Turning to cover the retreat, Eguzkilore digs deep for a second burst of effort and looses a bow shot at the nearest pursuing kobold — the arrow goes wide — and patches himself up moments later by sheer force of will.
2. The cultists push down the corridor regardless. The tangle of vines still bites: a berserker forces through the green and only just clears the corridor; **Frulam Mondath** follows hard on his heels.
3. Inside the cave Nuberroca moves to recover the unconscious Sinsong, dropping the bundle of plates and household debris he had hauled out of the camp earlier that afternoon to lighten his load before lifting the cleric.
4. He considers the cave wall — could a goliath swing collapse the corridor on top of the cult? Nuberroca judges the task too far beyond him and lets it go.
5. The goliath throws a hand axe at the nearest kobold instead: the axe takes the creature in the chest and drops it on the stone. A second axe from his other hand clips another kobold — the blow lands but the creature stays standing.
6. Behind him a berserker reaches the unconscious **Amidamaru** in the corridor and pins him to the stone. The tortle is held in place, not finished.
7. From her hiding spot Mialee fires twice at a kobold and both shots go wide; she slips back along the wall, lost from sight again.
8. Langdedrosa sprints the length of the corridor in one motion, his eyes on Nuberroca: *"tú eres mío."*
9. At the doorway two kobolds catch Eguzkilore as he runs through with Nyx in his arms — both blows land, one with crushing force. The borrowed vitality Nuberroca had poured into the elf moments before holds him on his feet where the hit should have dropped him.
10. In Nuberroca's arms Sinsong stirs back to consciousness. The goliath presses a finger to the dwarf's lips and tells him *"tranquilo, Cabezabombilla"*; on contact the bald head *flares with a soft glow*.
11. Sinsong, half-awake, weaves a protective binding on the goliath who is carrying him — a tether that shares part of Nuberroca's wounds with the dwarf.
12. Nuberroca looks **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** in the eye across the corridor and answers him with the line the half-dragon left him with the day before: *"Acabaré contigo, pero no hoy. Todavía no soy lo suficientemente fuerte."*
13. The goliath then grips the pendant at his throat, invokes the world tree — *"ayúdanos, gran árbol del mundo"* — and burns the last of his resolve so the wisp of cloud takes Sinsong with him.

14. He lands further down the corridor and brings his greataxe down on a kobold in a wild, open swing, cleaving the creature apart in a single stroke. At the same moment a surge of borrowed vitality pours across the corridor from Nuberroca into Eguzkilore at the doorway.
15. As they push into the entrance cave, **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** emerges from the corridor mouth behind them — visibly furious, lightning crackling at his throat once more. The half-dragon is winding up a second breath but has not yet released it.
16. From Nuberroca's shoulder Sinsong whispers a word of healing over Nyx; the halfling, still in Eguzkilore's arms, comes awake at the doorway.
17. They flee in earnest. Nuberroca catches a parting blow on his way out — his battle-fury blunting it, a faint blue glow knitting the gash half-closed as he moves — then sprints through the cave mouth with Sinsong over his shoulder.
18. Outside the cave the five still on their feet are clear of the corridor. Still pinned by the berserker, **Amidamaru is left behind**.

## 3 - Fog cloud and flight

1. The party debates direction between the tents as it moves. Eguzkilore argues for breaking out through the camp's main exit; Sinsong calls for north; the group commits to north and falls in behind him.
2. The cave's kobold guards stagger out into the daylight, blink at the sun, and turn straight back inside — but the berserkers and **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** push out after the party, closing to about sixty feet behind.
3. Nyx turns mid-stride, fixes her will on the casting, and drops a fog cloud between them and the pursuers. The berserkers run straight into the wall of vapour and lose the trail — the cloud buys the party the gap they need. Through the murk they hear voices searching in tongues they do not understand.
4. The party threads behind the abandoned tents toward the rim instead of through the gate, the white cliff-face above them visible from the camp.
5. With the chase broken for the moment Sinsong calls them in close and binds three of the party in a shared blessing — a steady channel that lends each a small magical hand for the next ten minutes.
6. After a while the party watches from cover as the berserkers come back through the fog, glance about, and turn back toward the cave. **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** and the two berserkers retreat — Amidamaru still inside — and the party is loose in the bowl with nobody on their trail.

## 4 - Rest and return

1. The group debates whether to rest or push back in at once. Eguzkilore worries the longer they wait the worse Amidamaru's odds; Sinsong wants to wait until dawn; Nyx points out the kobolds will not change their habits whether the party rests or not.
2. They settle on leaving the bowl entirely. Mialee remembers caves in the slope above the camp from the rim recon two days back; the party climbs up out of the valley basin and finds a hide that keeps the cave entrance in view.
3. Nyx uses her last spell of the day to conjure berries for the party's rations; Sinsong sits down to keep copying the spell — the new scroll work he had started in Greenest.
4. **Watches are split.** Eguzkilore takes the first four hours; Mia takes the second four. The other four sleep eight hours straight.
5. Sinsong, after the watch is set, says aloud what he wants everyone to remember: *"me gustaría que apreciaseis que os he salvado a todos."* Mialee answers him: *"un minuto de silencio por los que no han podido salir de la cueva."*
6. The long rest passes without incident. The party wakes after the eight hours; the camp below them is still dark — Nyx wondering aloud whether the kobolds keep an inverted cycle and might be the ones asleep now.
7. After some arguments about the approach, the party settles on a plan: Nyx and Mialee will move ahead as a stealthy vanguard, while Sinsong, Eguzkilore and Nuberroca follow some distance behind in case they are needed.
8. They cross the camp under cover of the tents. The cave entrance is empty — no Langdedrosa, no Frulam, no guards.
9. Nyx sends a magical message in the direction they last saw Amidamaru — *"Amidamaru, are you there?"* After a long beat the answer comes back: he is in the room where they captured the drunkard; he is hurt but only a little; he is unarmed and stripped of his gear. Three kobolds are around him; he does not know where Langdedrosa or Frulam are.

## 5 - The cave skirmish
_~00:00, 26 Marpenoth 1489 DR_

1. Some kobolds at the entrance spot them as they advance. Eguzkilore readies a bow shot at the kobold he can see; Mialee drops the first kobold with a single arrow, then steadies her aim for a measured follow-up. Sinsong steps out and hurls a bolt of fire at a second kobold across the chamber, dropping it noisily on the stone.
2. Nyx readies a shot for the first enemy in range, and Nuberroca readies a swing for any attacker that closes on him. The cave is dim, and the corridor turns create blind spots.
3. A kobold creeps into Nyx's line of sight; she lands a clean shot and drops it dead at her feet. Mialee conjures an illusory double of Nyx at a column further forward as a decoy for the kobolds to track, and Sinsong's second bolt of fire punches straight through its centre and into the kobold past it; the illusion shimmers and reforms behind the bolt.
4. A berserker rounds the column from the bend and fumbles into Nuberroca's space, axe still in hand — and trips flat before he can swing. Nuberroca lifts his greataxe over the sprawled man and brings it down with crushing force, splitting the berserker's axe in half and leaving him trembling on the stone, still alive. Mialee notes the barbarian has been disarmed.
5. The downed berserker hauls himself up, slashes at Nuberroca with what is left of his weapon in a reckless lunge, then shouts the party's positions back into the cave — three on the right, two on the left, spread out.
6. Moments later they clear the first wave. As the tripped berserker tries to break away, Nuberroca's axe catches him mid-pivot and drops him. Eguzkilore and Nyx pick off the last kobolds at the bend while Sinsong refreshes the shared blessing over three of the party. Sounds of fresh reinforcements coming down the passage carry from the dark. The five push to the next column and reform behind it; Mialee slips into hiding, leaving her illusory double of Nyx still standing at the original angle as a static decoy. Nuberroca scoops a fallen crossbow off the stone and readies a shot for whatever comes next.
7. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee repositions around the column to flank — and the move takes her right past **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath**, hidden behind it. She had been moving unseen until that step, but it puts her squarely in his line of sight; the half-dragon steps out and catches her twice in the same beat with the sword. She had not searched the chamber and never saw him coming.
8. The party throws everything at Langdedrosa at once. Mialee slips back, repositions, and snaps off a bow shot of her own — the arrow lands, but the column blunts the shot. Sinsong conjures a sword of pale spirit-light beside the half-dragon; he cannot see Langdedrosa directly, but the blade strikes on his behalf and lands a clean opening hit. Sinsong follows with a bolt of fire that bites through the column's cover with searing force. The half-dragon is visibly bloodied before he can counter-attack.
9. Langdedrosa's lightning breath catches the back of the chamber; Mialee, Nyx and Sinsong ride out the worst of it. Sinsong, struck hard, lets his focus slip — the spirit-blade dissolves in the air.
10. Nyx fires back from cover and her arrow finds him — a clean, hard shot — *"Toma, hijo de puta."* The half-dragon is visibly down to a sliver. Mialee uncaps a healing potion and drinks it all down, the brew settling in deep, then ducks behind a column.
11. Langdedrosa's breath gathers again at his throat. He squares up and lines a second white line down the chamber, voice carrying ahead of it: *"no vais a salir ni uno con vida de aquí."* **Eguzkilore** is caught full, **Nuberroca** partially — a wash of pale blue light running over the scorched line on the goliath's side as the worst of the breath bleeds off; two kobolds in the path of the breath drop dead.

## 6 - Cyanwrath falls

1. With Eguzkilore reeling from the breath and Nuberroca bleeding from his own share of it, a fresh berserker steps out from behind the columns and drives his blade into the goliath's back — a faint blue glow chasing the steel and pulling the wound shut behind it. The kobolds press in around Nuberroca; only his rage is keeping him on his feet.
2. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca calls **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** out by name: *"Langdedrosa, ¿así es como pelea un guerrero, escondiéndose detrás de rayos y atacando a los caídos? Si tienes honor, deja de jugar con presas fáciles y enfréntate a un verdadero titán. Ven a por mí, demuestra que no eres un lagarto cobarde."* The goliath dissolves into a cloud and reforms at the half-dragon's flank, bringing the greataxe down twice from behind. Both blows land squarely, and the half-dragon visibly buckles under them.
3. Eguzkilore tries to follow up with a shot, drawing on his last reserves of clarity and grit to stay upright at the wall. Mialee ducks back into cover behind the column.
4. Sinsong steps forward and calls a beam of celestial light down on Langdedrosa — but with companions in the way the beam cannot be aimed safely. It catches Eguzkilore in the side as the elf is gripping his scimitar, mid-swing at Langdedrosa, leaving him visibly wrecked and staring back at the cleric.

5. **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** recovers and rounds on Nuberroca with a sneer: *"¿Cómo puedes atreverte a retarme a un duelo cuando sois cinco atacándome a mi?"* He lines another lightning breath squarely on the goliath against the wall and lets it go; the breath rakes him, but a flicker of pale blue runs over the worst of the burn before it can sink in.
6. Nyx, with the bow at full reach, fires the killing shot. The arrow takes Langdedrosa as he stands and he goes down on one knee, then to the stone. He looks across at Nuberroca and answers him — *"no eres más que un maldito cobarde que necesita de muchos compañeros para abatirme"* — and falls. Nuberroca answers back as the half-dragon hits the stone: *"Muere bastardo rodeado de tus kobolds, perro."*
7. With the half-dragon down, Eguzkilore turns to the remaining berserker — the one that had been hitting Nuberroca from behind — and tries to talk him out of the fight: *"esta no es tu guerra, márchate y no te haremos nada."* The words land well and the berserker visibly hesitates, but the kobolds are still shooting and Nyx is hitting him from a distance; he ultimately decides to keep fighting, drives Eguzkilore down to a knee, and is then cut down by the elf himself seconds later.
8. **Langdedrosa is alive.** Sinsong reaches the half-dragon where he fell and finds him still breathing — unconscious, but his pulse holds where many others would have stopped. Nuberroca produces his rope and binds him fast before he can recover.
9. **The party loots the bodies.** Langdedrosa carried a spear, a longsword and a handful of curios — a glowing worm, a ball of grease, a piece of tree bark — plus a small purse of silver and copper. Nuberroca remarks on the glowing worm and the dwarf's bald head together. Mialee pulls a ring off one of the cult bodies, and Nuberroca claims a map off one of the kobolds.
10. Amidamaru, hearing Nyx's voice up the passage, **pulls free of his bindings**, finds his gear back in the cell, cuts past two kobolds with his claws on the way out, and meets the halfling in the corridor with word that his stretch is clear and the party can move through. Nyx hugs him in the dark; Sinsong checks him over for damage.
11. With **Amidamaru recovered** and the cave quiet, Nuberroca turns back to the half-dragon — but when he checks for breath there is none. **Langdedrosa has died** where he lay, the wounds too deep for recovery.
