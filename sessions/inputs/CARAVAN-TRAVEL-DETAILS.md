# Caravan travel details — spans S13 through end-of-caravan arc

**Purpose:** canonical planning document for the merchant caravan the party joins in Baldur's Gate (contracts signed S14) and rides north to Waterdeep. Holds the mission brief, the planned day-by-day travel timeline, the caravan roster, and the transcript → canonical name map used during `/draft` sweeps. Delete this file when the caravan arc closes.

**S13 status:** unblocked. S13 only touches `Ackyn Selebon` (Order-of-the-Gauntlet contact in Baldur's Gate — canonical form set; no NPC file until named on-camera). The caravan proper enters play in S15.

**NPC file policy:** create `campaign/npcs/<name>.md` only when the NPC is named on-camera in a session. Roster info below is authoritative when the file is eventually created.

## Mission

- **Objective:** find out where the cultist contingent's treasure is going and what they plan to do with it.
- **Payout:** 50 gp each on arrival to Waterdeep.

## Waterdeep on arrival

- **La jarra rota** — tavern in Waterdeep with the Harper contact.
- **Vauser** — half-orc, muscular, scar across his face, red hair. Harper contact in Waterdeep.
- **Golden stag prophecy** (delivered on Uktar 30th, referenced in Nightal 8th event): *"Seguid el río de oro hasta el castillo en el cielo. No todos lo lograréis…"* — *"Follow the golden river to the castle in the sky. Not all of you will make it…"*
- **Departure**: caravan leaves Waterdeep on 30 Nightal, arriving at next destination on 9 Hammer.

## Caravan roster (10 carts)

3 cultist carts (6 guards and 3 drivers), 5 merchant carts, 2 traveller carts.

### Merchants (5 carts, 4 named + Oyn Evenmor joining Uktar 11th)

- **Lai Angesstun** — Male Gold Dwarf. Hauls scented cooking oil and perfumes from Amn for Waterdeep aristocrats. Talks about money constantly; won't buy anyone else a drink or a roasted turnip for the whole trip. 1 cart. **Guards: Kaelthar (10 gp), Nyx (8 gp), Eguzkilore (5 gp), Eldkin.**
- **Edhelri Lewel** — Female Moon Elf. Hauls exotic wood from the Jungle of Chult for Waterdeep master carpenters. Impatient with people, exacting about her wagon, doting on her animals. 2 carts. **Guards: Mialee (10 gp), Nuberroca (8 gp).**
- **Noohar Serelim** — Male Moon Elf. Hauls exquisite wooden carvings made by Cormyr elves. Extraordinarily articulate; talks constantly. Travels with his mute brother **Selvek** (communicates only through sign language). 1 cart. **Guards: Amidamaru (10 gp).**
- **Beyd Sechepol** — Male Half-Elf. Hauls ale and beer, selling it en route to the other travellers. Diplomatic, defuses arguments, but careless with gear and horses (friction with animal-lovers). 1 cart. **Guard: Sulesdeg.**
- **Oyn Evenmor** *(joins Uktar 11th at Stranded)* — human male, trades in exotic birds.

### Caravan-employed guards (party-external)

- **Eldkin Agetul** — Female Shield Dwarf. Veteran of this route, waves her experience in others' faces. Perfectionist. Paired with Lai's cart.
- **Sulesdeg the Pole** — Male Human. From the Shaar; 7'5" tall. His tribal name means "tall as a lodge pole"; on the Sword Coast he is just "the Pole". Quiet, but people listen. Paired with Beyd's cart.

### Travellers (2 carts)

- **Radecere Perethun** — Male Rock Gnome. Destination unknown. Eats alone, seldom speaks, rides in the back staring wistfully. Comes alive at gambling.
- One family cart + one random-traveller cart. Occupants TBD in-play.

### Adventuring party joining Uktar 12th (El reposo en el camino, additional cart)

- **Eldrin Kaldur** — noble; trades in exotic meats that are starting to worm; uses magic to keep them cold.
- **Jaxen Korrith** — knight.
- **Thalor Cheston** — wizard.

### Joining at Daggerford (Nightal 22nd)

- **Janma** — pays unwanted attention to the party from Nightal 23rd onward.
- **Azbara** — joins alongside Janma.
