## 1 - The tower's second floor
_~07:40, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The GM opens on the tower's second floor at 07:39, 16 Hammer 1490 DR — Nyx, Eguzkilore, and Mialee alone below; Amidamaru and Nuberroca stay up on the top floor, out of the way of the quiet-hands work the three below are about to try [T:17-21], [T:159-165], [T:642-643]. The room they are in has doors leading out to a room to the north and to a room to the west; the floorboards under their feet are quieter than boards this old should be [T:352-360], [T:263-268].
2. Nyx works the doors in turn, checking each frame for a trip-line, a marker, anything to say the hinge is watched, and finds none [T:229-252], [T:317-321]. The first door is going to creak; she dabs a small amount of oil into the hinge before pulling it open [T:271-291]. The room behind is empty, its floor unmaintained enough to crick a little away from the walkway — dust on the boards, debris where it fell [T:299-336].
3. The second door has fresh grease on its hinges — the party's fingers come away marked — and opens onto a similar empty room; the walkway is quiet under boot, the perimeter debris still where it dropped [T:319-336]. Neither room has anything hidden.
4. The third door opens onto a longer chamber — a table with a dead lamp at its centre, a scrap-pile of broken armour and old weapons piled along the northwest wall, and in the southwest corner a vertical stone cylinder rising through the ceiling: the top of a chimney [T:376-393]. Nyx puts her hand to the stone — it is warm; something is being burnt below [T:390-392].
5. Working through the scrap-pile with careful hands to keep it quiet, the party finds an oiled cloth wrapped around a bundle of short-sword blades — no hilts, no crossguards, but freshly forged and in perfect condition. Three or four more bundles under it hold dagger and spear blades of the same craft [T:441-475]. Not master-work but functional — someone in the castle is running a small smithy up here [T:463-475]. Eguzkilore takes two short-sword blades and a dagger blade, Nyx pockets two dagger blades, and the rest they leave where they lie [T:498-508].

## 2 - The rope descent

1. The party settles on a descent from the third room's window rather than a walk down through the noisy floor below [T:544-598]. Twenty-five feet to the ground; the rope threads down at a slight angle to clear the window of the floor below [T:590-594], [T:685-687]. Nyx and Mialee brace on the rope from inside the window; Eguzkilore drops first.

Tompu comes into the trees slowly, his face rearranging as he catches up with the situation [T:895-909].
2. Nyx asks whether Pharblex is still alive. Tompu confirms it, and the news does not disappoint him — *"Pharblex es muy grande y muy fuerte. A nuestro gran líder lo mató de un solo golpe"* [T:927-929]. **Suncaller**, the lizardfolk chief, is who he means by *nuestro gran líder* [T:951-954].
3. Nyx pushes on what they need to prove they have won the fight when they do — Tompu's answer is short: *"necesitamos la prueba de que habéis acabado con él"*, the head if they can carry it, or the fish-mouth hat Pharblex wears [T:1013-1024]. Tompu is asked to wait and bring Snapjaw to the trees when he does arrive [T:1063-1078].

## 5 - The insect chamber
_~09:35, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The stairs from the pool cavern climb into a chamber the party hasn't reached before — the floor and walls are honeycombed with small holes, and the loose earth on the boards is *moving* [T:1530-1550]. Mialee reads it correctly at once — a nest [T:1551-1555]. The passage forward runs the length of the chamber; there is no other path.
2. Eguzkilore leads and the swarm wakes [T:1600-1611]. Centipede-shaped things pour up his legs and bite through the leggings; the first opportunity attack goes wide, the second catches him with a mouthful of bite that stops him where he stands [T:1611-1637]. Mialee dashes across after him to pull attention off; two more swarms and their long worm-forms slap at her, one landing as a crit and a second normal — she twists half of the crit away with her rogue's reflex [T:1735-1755]. A follow-up round of swarm attacks catches Eguzkilore with a poisoned bite that leaves his hands trembling on the bow [T:1717-1765].
3. Nyx works with the party through the swarm, hiding behind Eguzkilore where the halfling's small stature lets her disappear inside his shadow, and holds her arrow for the shots that matter [T:1866-1878]. The poison in Eguzkilore's blood clears at the end of the round [T:1938-1948]. Eguzkilore breathes through the wound, a moment's second-breath discipline that closes the worst of the acid [T:1935-1955]. Mialee disengages back along the pool wall and hides in her own shadow [T:1825-1856].
4. Two full-grown bullywugs amble up the corridor to investigate the noise — one advances, one holds back, calling in the language none of the party speaks [T:1892-1908]. The insect swarm that had circled Mialee finds the advancing frog first and starts eating it in earnest [T:2019-2023]. From her hide, Mialee palms a spectral hand full of the bugs and dumps them over the bullywug's back, feeding the swarm [T:2033-2045].
5. The advancing bullywug, half-eaten and desperate, tries to spring past — bounces off Mialee in the dark and, out of turn actions, stops mid-jump [T:2249-2266]. The second, taking three arrows in the back over the round, dashes down the corridor at full stride and bounds away [T:2267-2277]. The party fires as a coordinated volley — Nyx twice, the first arrow with advantage from surprise and a burst of ranger's ambush damage on the shot, the second follow-up without advantage; Mialee's arrow into the same alley with a slow-strike that stumbles the frog mid-jump; Eguzkilore closing on the half-eaten one from behind with dagger and driving it down close to death [T:2183-2287]. Then, on a single-turn surge of effort, Eguzkilore drops the dagger, unslings the bow, and wheels for the fleeing frog eighty-five feet down the corridor — the arrow takes it clean in the throat mid-jump and the bullywug drops felled on the wet floor [T:2288-2340]. The insect swarm finishes the half-eaten one where it lay [derived:T:2019-2023+T:2249-2266].

## 7 - The volley from the crane ledge

The party spends a long stretch at the ledge weighing options — an ambush across the drop, a rush of the wizard, a volley from cover — and settle on the last: three arrows into the wizard from the top, all together, then break back to the ledge behind [T:2820-3040].
2. ***[EPIC]*** All three fire at once, on the same beat, with advantage from surprise [T:3043-3048]. Nyx's first arrow — the head of the shot marked with a burst of ranger's ambush damage — goes into the wizard's shoulder for near-crit damage [T:3033-3057]. Mialee's shot into the same wound follows and drives her sneak-strike through it [T:3059-3062]. Eguzkilore's arrow lands as a critical, the redraw for damage goes deeper still, and one point above what would have been enough finishes the wizard on the spot [T:3066-3083]. The wizard falls; the frogs around him haven't moved a muscle yet.
3. Eguzkilore's second shot of the volley — a critical on the roll again — wounds another bullywug but the shot uses one of the improvised arrows and doesn't finish him [T:3094-3110]. ## 8 - The cultist wizard and Nyx's disguise

2. The nearest cultist wizard answers with a hissing green sphere lobbed onto the party's ledge — placed so Nyx sits at the edge of the burst, the others in the centre [T:3427-3456]. Mialee, Nyx, and Eguzkilore twist for the save; two make it, Eguzkilore fails and drops to the ledge unconscious, his skin still smoking under the acid [T:3455-3472]. At the top of his next turn the burn will eat another slice off him — his death-save streak will start with an automatic fail from the acid [T:3480-3484].
3. Nyx sprints across and presses one of her berries into Eguzkilore's mouth as an action — carrying it to another mouth needs the full turn, not a bonus [T:3492-3510]. His death-save had come up a three: he stabilises on the berry to a single point of life, and the acid stops eating the wound [T:3506-3510]. Mialee lays a bank of fog between the party and the wizard; the fog settles thick and white across the ledge [T:3513-3541].
4. A bullywug warrior finds Nyx through the fog by sound, comes up on her with disadvantage, and lands a hard bite that leaves her badly wounded [T:3549-3557]. Nyx is not going to outlast another exchange like that at this range.
5. Nyx casts an illusion of another skin on herself — the shape of one of the small bullywugs she had seen earlier in the corridor, a companion of the ones now lying dead, no one alive to say otherwise [T:3595-3614].
6. ***[EPIC]*** On the sapos' turn one crashes out of the fog toward Eguzkilore, unable to see the ranger through the mist [T:3638-3646]. Eguzkilore's bow was already up and readied; his reaction shot goes off with disadvantage from the close range and rolls a nineteen and a twenty on the two dice, and by the party's house rule for double-critical outcomes on advantage or disadvantage, the arrow is the killing blow the moment it leaves the string [T:3647-3673]. The bullywug takes it through the throat mid-croak and falls onto the ranger [T:3669-3673].
7. ***[EPIC]*** Nyx walks toward the wizard as if lost, then vaults from the ledge to the ground below with an acrobatic drop — advantage from surprise on the strike, her short blade lit with a second burst of ambush damage on the follow-through — a crit-and-critical that leaves the bullywug warrior heavily wounded and reeling, though not down yet [T:3720-3775].
8. Mialee follows Nyx off the ledge but the wizard sees her mid-air and side-steps her landing; she lands hard, twists an ankle on the wet floor, and her concentration on the fog fails through on the same breath — the fog thins to nothing [T:3835-3944]. She is back on her feet inside two heartbeats but bruised. In the follow-through, her sneak-strike finishes the bullywug warrior Nyx had wounded on the way down [T:3899-3911].

## 9 - Pharblex and the paralysis cliffhanger
_~10:00?, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. **Pharblex** enters the chamber — considerably larger than the rest of the bullywugs, waddling in a low bow-legged rock, a fish-mouth hat pulled down over his head, and his voice grating orders in the bullywug tongue [T:4132-4145]. Two smaller bullywugs flank him.
2. Pharblex is temporarily blinded — the GM's ruling holds only for a round or two — and moves forward palpating each dead bullywug body on the floor to check for players [T:4162-4172]. He reaches Nyx, still in her illusion, and touches her shoulder. Nyx's performance to hold the impersonation comes up mid — not enough — and Pharblex hesitates, one hand still on her [T:4173-4183].
3. Nyx takes his hand as if she is guiding him toward the party's line and casts Faerie Fire around them — Pharblex, both flanking bullywugs, and the cultist wizard behind, all four in the ring [T:4186-4224]. Every one of them fails the save; the four figures around her light up in a soft dim glow that the party can see through and the frogs cannot dim [T:4210-4225].
4. Mialee lays a second bank of fog between herself and Pharblex to cover her stance [T:4256-4283]. Pharblex answers with a low bass croak that swells to a roar of thunder across Nyx's position; Nyx saves and catches half of it, and the sound carries deep into the castle above [T:4409-4432].
5. Pharblex closes on Nyx directly. His first swing is wide in the fog; the second bites hard, and a follow-up ability lands on top of it — Nyx crashes to the floor at zero, and her Faerie Fire winks out with her concentration [T:4435-4451]. Her illusion holds, since it is not on her concentration [T:4453-4463], [T:4491-4494].
6. Mialee races to Nyx from the fog and administers one of the healing potions she has been carrying since Baldur's Gate — a full pour into the mouth of an unconscious ally is a rolled draw rather than the max the potion would give her, but it is enough to bring Nyx around and back on her feet [T:4488-4557]. Nyx sits up and settles her breath [T:4560-4567].
7. ***[EPIC]*** As a follow — bonus action, on a spent heroic inspiration to let the spectral hand carry the weight — Mialee sends her invisible hand across the room with one of the ceramic spheres she took from a chest at Carnath Roadhouse and breaks it against Pharblex [T:4560-4595]. The paralysing effect closes around him; he fails both saves in succession [T:4597-4603]. Pharblex freezes mid-step, held where he stands. The session closes on him held there in the fog, the small bullywugs scrambling around him, and the three of them — Nyx healed but bloodied, Eguzkilore at one, Mialee limping — deciding what to do about a paralysed frog king [T:4604-4620].
