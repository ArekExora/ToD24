## 1 - Island camp in the Mere
_~09:00?, 14 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party camps on a muddy islet in the marsh, boats pulled onto the shore and the lizardfolk guides *Tompu*, *Chikonaui*, *Axid*, and *Nenetl* resting beside them [Td:70-78]. Mosquitoes swarm the air; birdsong is strangely thin [Td:71, Td:162-165].
2. Mialee lifts a sealed glass vessel from among the floating debris at the water's edge [Td:72]. The moment her fingers close on it she feels a faint magic clinging to the glass, though she cannot say what it does [Td:73-75]. She stows it unidentified.
3. Over the next hour Mialee cuts and binds a bundle of twenty rough arrows from the dead wood of the islet [Td:93-106]; she sets aside fifteen for Nyx [Td:453-464].
4. Nuberroca sits on the shore with the longsword taken from a fallen enemy, draws it from the sheath, taps it against his spear for sparks, listens for the sound of the metal, tests the balance [Td:283-285]. It is lighter than steel of its length should be, perfectly balanced, cutting almost of its own will [Td:286-289]; he reads it for what it is — a magic blade [Td:290].
5. Nyx studies one of the three pulp-cored flasks the party has been carrying — small bottles with liquid and a thumb-latch [Td:270 ?ord, Td:303-308]. She compares the fluid against her own healing potions and the resemblance is too close to be chance [Td:316-318]. She warns the others these are no safe draught — *"son un poco peligrosas"* [Td:321].
6. Nyx edges up to Nuberroca's tamed giant lizard **Juancho** to try to befriend him; the beast is half-tamed and tolerates her hand once Nuberroca gives her the word [Td:137-141 ?ord]. Mialee's own lizard **Atascaburras** stays near her through the morning.
7. Mialee asks Nuberroca why he has taken Sinsong's mark; Nuberroca answers that Sinsong has shown himself a worthy warrior across many days, and that since the dwarf of the lamp [Td:186] and the vision of his tribe fallen to the undead [Td:187], a new energy flows beside his anger — bright, not black [Td:188-191]. He wonders aloud whether Sinsong might one day climb into the firmament of his tribe's gods, and whether he himself might follow [Td:200-212].
8. Eguzkilore sits apart, still blinded from the previous days' fighting [Td:328-329]. Nyx rolls up her sleeves, rinses her hands in the brown water [Td:380-381], and with Nuberroca steadying him works a careful medicine over the ruined eyes [Td:384-387]. Eguzkilore blinks open, pupils flickering in the morning light — his sight returns [Td:387-398].
9. Eguzkilore then shares what his sharper ear caught from the lizardfolk's murmured talk during the night: they fear the *devoradores de cadáveres* — the Carnomorphs — are abroad in this marsh as well [Td:399-406]. That, he suggests, is why the birds have gone quiet.
10. With Eguzkilore healed and the arrows bound, the party stows gear and pushes the boats back into the water [Td:409].

## 2 - Through the silent reaches
_~11:00?, 14 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party rows north between banks of reeds and rotting vegetation [Td:471-473]; the lizardfolk swim alongside the boats, soundless in the water [Td:474]. In the long stretches of silence the hair stands up on the back of the neck [Td:475].
2. The lizardfolk grow jumpy, scanning every clump of grass and reed on the water [Td:476-478, Td:482-483]. Kaelthar translates the one word repeated under their breath — *"coatl"*, the walking grasses — and relays their warning to steer the boats clear of the larger thickets [Td:511-517, Td:555-560].
3. The party pulls into single file and bends the course rightward, giving the thickets a wide berth [Td:565-574].
4. ***[EPIC]*** Across the quiet water Nyx catches a thread of human voices to the east — Common, not frog [Td:602, Td:604-606]. She points through the reeds and reaches with a thought-voice to whoever is there: *"Soy una amiga y solo quería saber que no hay peligro"* [Td:673-698]. A startled voice answers in her head — a man, in Common — asking who she is [Td:689-695]. She tells him she is only passing through, bound for the castle; he replies *"seguid con la misión y alabad así a Tiamat"* [Td:704]. She returns the greeting in kind — *"alabad así a Tiamat"* — and goes silent [Td:705-707]. To Mialee she murmurs that these are cultists, and that nothing more must be said [Td:709-710].
5. The party rows on, boats low in the water, the lizardfolk still skirting crocodiles under the surface as if they were old business [Td:717].
6. Two more hours of silent water follow [Td:719]; they eat in the boats rather than put ashore [Td:720-724].

## 3 - The crocodile ambush

1. Nyx and Nuberroca are the first to see the wake-lines converging on the boats from four or five quarters at once [Td:739-742]. Nuberroca sets his halberd across his knees and waits [Td:753-754]. The lizardfolk still see nothing [Td:742].
2. Eight crocodiles break the water around the boats — young ones, mid-sized, and one plainly larger than the rest [Td:755-757]. The lizardfolk flinch but hold their places; they had feared something worse [Td:768].
3. Two crocodiles clamp on Juancho from either side and begin to drag him under [Td:808-823]; others take runs at the boats, and one bites through the hull of Nuberroca's craft, water pouring through the hole [Td:844-851].
4. Eguzkilore's longbow drops a crocodile from the boat's edge with two shafts into its flank [Td:853-864]; the lizardfolk hurl the last of their javelins between them, scoring a hit here and there before their quivers run dry [Td:841-842, Td:934-938, Td:1046].
5. Nuberroca swings into the crocodile pinning Juancho — a mighty cut opens the beast nearly in two [Td:895-900]. Its blood sprays caustic across the water; Juancho and Tompu catch the spatter and burn where it touches, Nuberroca himself scorched by the same spray [Td:914-918]. Juancho sinks beneath the red water [Td:921-923].
6. Mialee's bow sends an arrow straight down a crocodile's throat — out at the base of the neck — and it rolls dying under the boats [Td:960-966].
7. The great crocodile heaves itself against Nuberroca's hull and locks its jaws on his leg, hauling him flat on the deck and lashing him with its tail [Td:997-999, Td:1012-1019]. Nyx empties her last three arrows into it, each shaft drawn to the same mark on the big crocodile's flank [Td:1026-1042]; Eguzkilore's longbow lands a telling first shot and still the thing holds Nuberroca [Td:1050-1053].
8. Then the water parts behind the great crocodile — Juancho surfaces, alive after all, and tears into its flank [Td:1125-1129]. Tompu, taking courage, leaps up with the steel scimitar the party gave him and scores a cut across the beast's ribs [Td:1135-1140].
9. ***[EPIC]*** Still pinned under the great crocodile's jaws, Nuberroca lights up in white eyes and a flash of cloud [Td:1090-1095 ?ord]; he reappears on the beast's back and drives his spear two-handed down into the base of the skull [Td:1096-1107]. A second thrust from above lands close behind the first [Td:1109-1116]. He springs back to the boat, and the shot that finishes the big crocodile is Mialee's [Td:1121, Td:1141-1148]. The body sinks.
10. Nuberroca drags the carcass alongside with the longsword, carves a slab of belly meat free [Td:1157-1165, Td:1176-1181]. As he cuts, the wound at the hide's edge begins to knit before his eyes [Td:1182-1184]; the thing is not entirely dead. The flesh grows back and the crocodile heaves alive again, biting him off the hull [Td:1185-1200].
11. ***[EPIC]*** Eguzkilore's next shot catches the beast in the eye and pins through the skull; the body goes still at last and begins to sink [Td:1219-1224]. Nuberroca saws the head free before it goes down and lifts it dripping onto the boat [Td:1226-1234].
12. Sinsong, bald head glowing softly, kneels at the broken hull and knits the splintered wood back with mending until the boat sits proud of the water again, battered but afloat [Td:1241-1247].
13. Nuberroca works four long teeth from the severed head for keepsakes [Td:1292-1293]. Mialee calls dibs on a necklace; Nyx on a belt, and boots besides [Td:1277-1280].
14. Through Kaelthar the party asks the lizardfolk if the crocodiles of this marsh all come back the way this one did; the answer is no, they have never seen such a thing [Td:1264-1267 ?ord].

## 4 - A camp in sight of the castle
_~19:00?, 14 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party rows another two hours; the towers of a half-ruined castle rise above the trees to the north, stone fallen in places, no watchmen visible on the broken battlements [Td:1304-1310]. Tompu urges them to put ashore for the night and finish the approach at dawn [Td:1305].
2. They pull the boats onto a muddy islet and arrange them in a shallow horseshoe between the water and the stone-flecked ground — a makeshift wall against anything coming from the water [Td:1326-1333]. Nuberroca argues for a fire; Nyx points out the castle walls can see them from here and reminds him the party is here to go unseen [Td:1340-1343].
3. Nyx offers a lesser restoration to the worst-wounded; Nuberroca accepts [Td:1375-1384]. Mialee comes over asking plaintively for one of her own and gets a second [Td:1385-1396].
4. Nyx hands out one of her berries to each companion and keeps five for herself [Td:1472-1476].
5. The party agrees to sleep in the beached boats to stay out of the mud [Td:1354-1359 ?ord]. Mialee and Eguzkilore, needing less sleep, will share four hours of watches with Nuberroca and Sinsong and then with Kaelthar [Td:1411-1416, Td:1443-1447].
6. Nuberroca tries to warn Tompu with hand-signs about the danger from the water; what Tompu hears instead is that Nuberroca means to kill him in his sleep. He scrambles back, Kaelthar laughs and translates, Nuberroca stammers *"no, yo no, el agua, el agua"*, and Kaelthar settles the lizardfolk with *"el agua no mata"* [Td:1454-1458].
7. Nuberroca kneels beside Juancho before turning in, strokes his flank, and feeds him a strip of crocodile meat — *"Pensé que te habías muerto"* [Td:1420-1424 ?ord]. The lizard preens under his hand like a strange dog [Td:1430-1431].

## 5 - The Carnomorph assault
_~22:00?, 14 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Half an hour into the first watch Mialee catches a small splash from the water; bone over mud, a sound she has learned to recognise [Td:1508-1513]. A hunched, grass-smeared figure crawls out of the shallows and lunges at her [Td:1530]. She drives her short sword through it and shouts the warning that wakes the camp — *"¡Carnomorfos!"* [Td:1542-1548].
2. Nuberroca is on his feet in a breath, armour unbuckled from the night [Td:1655-1660]; a Boneleecher's teeth catch him with a savage bite before he can plant himself, but his fury blunts half the damage [Td:1562-1572].
3. A faster, lighter figure the size of a javelin flickers across the mud and takes up a flanking position behind the camp — a Boneleecher of a different build, quicker than the rest [Td:1635-1640].
4. Nuberroca swings into the Boneleechers, radiant light flashing off the halberd with each stroke [Td:1600-1614 ?ord]; one of them collapses as the light touches it.
5. ***[EPIC]*** Then the reeds themselves rise from the water — a tangled, hulking mass of weed and dead grass taller than Nuberroca, limbs within limbs [Td:1664-1669]. The lizardfolk begin to shriek — *"¡Coatl! ¡Coatl! ¡Coatl!"* — and bolt in every direction [Td:1675-1678]. Nenetl runs; Axid runs; Chikonaui tries and takes a Boneleecher's bite as he scrambles [Td:1676-1680].
6. Mialee pivots and cuts two of the smaller carnomorphs — a flick of the shortsword from behind, then the offhand scimitar — then springs up onto Atascaburras's back and lets the lizard carry her to the far side of the barricade [Td:1693-1719].
7. The Metagorger's whip-vines come lashing out across the camp [Td:1830-1838]. Eguzkilore is dragged bodily forward and spat back again [Td:1839-1884]. Two more whips reach for Nuberroca and miss — one overshoots and strikes the Metagorger itself [Td:1846-1872].
8. A Boneleecher without armour bites Eguzkilore where he lies and he yelps [Td:1799-1801 ?ord].
9. ***[EPIC]*** The whip-vine comes down on Nuberroca again and this time it strikes true. A spark flares as it bites, Nuberroca falls, and the thing hauls him five feet closer to its maw [Td:2219-2224].
10. Nyx has no spells left that will touch the big one. She lifts one of the healing-potion grenades, closes her eyes, and lobs it straight down onto the fallen Nuberroca [Td:2266-2294]. Green smoke billows up around him; the wounds knit, and he comes back to consciousness, bloody but alive [Td:2294-2302].
11. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee is dragged into the Metagorger's body — swallowed, blind, pinned inside the churning mass of weed [Td:2007-2013 ?ord]. Each heartbeat the inside of the thing eats at her [Td:2063-2066]. Her half-cast step of light fades before it can bear her out [Td:2030-2055]; she drops into unconsciousness inside the Metagorger and slips closer to death with each heartbeat, twice over [Td:2063-2066, Td:2396].
12. Nenetl is bitten down in the mud nearby and does not get up [Td:2023-2027].
13. Juancho fights at Nuberroca's side, striking where the barbarian directs him [Td:2149-2153, Td:2372-2375]. The Metagorger's next pass drops him; this time he does not come back [Td:2404-2407, Td:2554-2555].
14. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca steps into the Metagorger's reach, enters his fury [Td:2337-2338], forces his arm into the mass of weed and tears Mialee free in a single heave [Td:2355-2358]. He pitches her over to Nyx's side of the barricade [Td:2363-2366].
15. Eguzkilore pulls clear of the thing in front of him, sprints to the limp Mialee and presses one of Nyx's berries into her mouth; she comes around at a thread of life [Td:2385-2397].
16. Nuberroca lays into a Boneleecher behind the barricade; the next stroke of the radiant halberd drops it [Td:2448-2462].
17. Nyx empties her remaining arrows into the Metagorger, every shaft drawn to the same glowing mark [Td:2068-2082 ?ord, Td:2435-2444]; the last arrow goes in and the weed-body shudders, slumps, and sloughs apart into a wet heap of half-rotten plants [Td:2444-2445].
18. In the handful of exchanges that follow the last standing Boneleecher is thrown down [Td:2520-2530].

## 6 - After the Coatl

1. The surviving lizardfolk — Axid, Tompu, and the shaken Chikonaui — crawl back out of the reeds, mobbing one another to tend wounds [Td:2532-2534]. Nuberroca climbs onto the pile of torn weed, plants his spear and shouts across to them *"¡No corráis, cobardes! ¡Somos muy fuertes!"* for Kaelthar to translate [Td:2536-2540]. In the lizardfolk's answering talk the party catches the one word *publicus* and the tone — they are speaking of being free, at last, of the bullywugs who have cowed them [Td:2541-2545]. The lizardfolk recover a d6 arrows apiece from the churned mud [Td:2546].
2. Nuberroca goes to Juancho's body, kneels, holds him. He puts a strip of crocodile meat to the dead jaws and gets no answer [Td:2554-2563].
3. Tompu's mood turns. He draws his scimitar and gestures across his wounded companions, then across Nuberroca and Eguzkilore — all those who were bitten [Td:2574-2583]. Through Kaelthar he tells the party that the bitten must die before the infection takes the village [Td:2580-2584].
4. Nuberroca and Nyx push back; they have been bitten many times and are still standing, and all four hands are needed to reach the castle [Td:2585-2587, Td:2594-2596]. Tompu stares at Chikonaui.
5. After a long silence Chikonaui speaks quietly. He can feel something moving in him. He thinks he is infected [Td:2599-2601]. Axid steps back from him at once; Tompu lifts the blade [Td:2602-2604].
6. Nuberroca puts himself between Tompu and the lizardfolk — bind him for the night, he argues; if he lives till morning he is clean, and if not the blade is still there [Td:2605-2612]. Kaelthar translates. Tompu spits on the ground and relents [Td:2613-2619].
7. Chikonaui is bound to a stump at the edge of the camp; the party and the surviving lizardfolk take what rest they can, Sinsong going round once more with cure wounds for the worst-wounded [Td:2634-2680].

## 7 - Night exodus
_~21:00?, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. In the grey light the party wakes to two things. To the south, moving across the water: an ogre-sized shape with its skin inside-out, ribs curling the wrong way, flesh dropping from its sides as it walks; around it seven or eight Metagorgers, and the water boils with the shadows of as many more under it [Td:2683-2684].
2. And beside the camp — the stump stands empty, the ropes cut, and Chikonaui lies on his back with his own spear through his chest, a pair of ragged wings unfolding wet from his shoulder blades [Td:2689-2696].
3. The party pulls the boats off the sand, drives into the water, and begins to row for everything it is worth [Td:2699-2707].
