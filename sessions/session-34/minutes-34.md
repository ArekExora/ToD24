## 1 - Two stones on a cairn
_~06:30, 10 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party wakes early at the roadhouse [Td:7-8]. Over the first bowl of the morning they trade what they remember of yesterday's work; Eguzkilore and Mialee between them earn the day's small favours for recalling it best [Td:4-57].
2. Eguzkilore wanders out past the yard and his eye catches on a small cairn of walker-stones. On top where there should be one stone there are two, very alike. He touches them and feels at once that they are magical, though not what they do [Td:70-85].
3. He walks the pair back inside and offers them to Sinsong. Sinsong pockets them without ceremony: *"Pesas un poco, Sinsong."* *"Pues las putas piedras estas."* [Td:88-109].

## 2 - Breakfast and the rats that were not rats
_~07:00?, 10 Hammer_

1. Over the morning's thin fare Nuberroca brings the newly-arrived Kaelthar up on the previous day's run — the owlbear, the carnomorphs that boiled up out of the ground, the ration-haul they brought back and the civilian whose leg was savaged while they were trying to sleep. He mentions in passing that between him and Sinsong they stabilised the wounded man before turning in, and allows that he was already asleep for most of it [Td:124-141].
2. The common room is heavier than the day before. **Eoman**, the worker Sinsong healed, sits hunched over his bowl with the look of a man newly reminded of his own skin [Td:233-238].
3. Nuberroca approaches **Bog Luck**, who breaks off his conversation the moment the goliath sits down. Bog Luck calls the night *"una especie de animal, algunos animales, especie de ratas extrañas o algún monstruo de la marisma"* — the sentries, he says, handled it. He is grateful for Eoman's care and will have the man off work for the day [Td:252-262].
4. The barman grumbles for his part — the rats were worse last night than any night before, hammering the walls, as though in heat. Nuberroca and Sinsong press him in jest: Sinsong insists his extermination was professional. The barman shrugs and keeps wiping his glasses [Td:316-336].
5. Clark arrives, reads out the day's work-roster, and names the party's six as the escort: Kaelthar, Nyx, Nuberroca, Mialee, Sinsong, Eguzkilore. Half an hour, then march. The night-rest was supposed to buy them another day off, but *"hay que agilizar"* — some of the workers mean to leave in a day or two [Td:338-355].

## 3 - At the marsh-edge
_~09:00?, 10 Hammer_

1. An hour and a half's walk brings them to firm ground within smell of the marsh [Td:412-420]. The workers scatter to begin surveying the roadbed [Td:422-424]; the party take the escort [Td:426]. Nyx and Kaelthar peel off to the far side out of sight [Td:437-438]. Eight hours ahead of them, with nothing yet moving [Td:427-429].
2. Each of them picks a station. Mialee patrols; Eguzkilore keeps a crossbow across his knees under cover; Sinsong readies a fire-spell for the first ten minutes and then gives it up to flick pebbles [Td:430-436].
3. One of the workers — **Dillian** — keeps looking at Nuberroca. Eguzkilore loops the long way through the camp to come alongside the goliath without drawing eyes and whispers that the man is watching him too long [Td:481-502].

## 4 - A warning shot
_~10:30?, 10 Hammer_

1. Nuberroca drifts within line of Dillian. The man's two companions evaporate the moment he arrives, leaving Dillian pretending to measure a trunk [Td:503-517].
2. Later Nuberroca puts a crossbow bolt into the tree inches from the man's head. Dillian flinches and goes back to work with a visibly steadier hand [Td:524-530].
3. Sinsong crosses to Nuberroca when the next lull comes. *"Nuestro objetivo es que aguanten con vida y lanzándole virotes no suelen aguantar con vida."* Nuberroca shrugs — *"me ha salido de dentro"* — and promises not to shoot allies. For now [Td:538-549].

## 5 - A spine from the bush
_~15:00, 10 Hammer_

1. The afternoon stretches. One of the small bone-things erupts from the brush at Dillian's elbow; Nuberroca is on it in a step with the halberd and pins the creature to the ground through its tangle of broken limbs [Td:626-635].
2. Nuberroca uses the moment. He puts a hand on Dillian's shoulder — *"¿por qué me miras tanto?"* — and asks under his breath who told the man to watch him [Td:641-660]. Dillian stammers that he was told the goliath was dangerous and needed watching [Td:788-791]. Two of his co-workers arrive at the sound of his earlier scream [Td:667-670]; Nuberroca waves off their concern with the dead thing on the point of his spear, and walks Dillian off alone *"para ayudarle a relajarse"* [Td:671-704].
3. Pressed harder [Td:732-738], Dillian names the man who gave the order. Nuberroca leans close for the whisper: *"Sarek"* [Td:739-740]. From the edge of his eye Nuberroca marks that Dillian's two friends are watching his every step [Td:741-743], and that something in the standing water nearby is moving [Td:744-747].

## 6 - The ambush in the shallows
_~15:30?, 10 Hammer_

1. More of the small carnomorphs — Boneleechers in their new skins — burst from the ground around them. Nuberroca cuts the first down as it closes [Td:750-761].
2. From the pools behind them a larger thing hauls itself up: four legs under a mass of muscle and tendon with no proper skin on it, antennae and extra ears and eyes scattered down its back, and eight eyes in a face like an insect's. A smaller sibling scuttles clear of the water beside it. Something larger comes up a moment behind: armoured in bone-plates like an armadillo crossed with a beetle, trailing fluid that may be marsh-water or may be its own [Td:810-836].
3. Dillian is catatonic in Nuberroca's grip [Td:708-713 ?ord]. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca gathers what he has left — his once-a-day cloud-step and a hoarded piece of inspiration — and burns both at once [Td:904-926] to vanish into mist with the man held hard against him and reappear thirty feet closer to the party [Td:934-956]. Dillian, dropped on his feet beside the civilians, vomits once and sprints for his companions [Td:957-959].
4. Sinsong's bald head lights up and a ring of radiant angels forms around him; he names the civilians, the workers, and the party as under its protection, and the swamp-things that step inside are torn by the light [Td:1000-1021]. Mialee ducks into the body of the surveying crew and makes herself invisible among them [Td:1037-1046].
5. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca sets on the eye-covered Metagorger with the halberd and lands two full-force strikes in succession, cutting antennae and splitting half its eyes before it has a chance to answer. Blood runs out of it from every seam, but the thing is still standing [Td:966-989]. Eguzkilore puts an arrow between the armoured one's bone-plates for the first good hit of the fight [Td:884-897 ?ord].
6. The armoured one bounds across the ground and tries to reach the party. Eguzkilore disengages, binds a wound closed with the breath he still has, and gets clear [Td:1142-1163]. One of the Metagorgers pivots in mid-charge, snatches up the body of a fallen Boneleecher and swallows it whole; its wounds close as it eats [Td:1300-1310].
7. Mialee's shot drops a leg off the eye-covered one and leaves it staggering [Td:1240-1247]. A small one springs at Nuberroca and impales itself on his spear [Td:1256-1261]. Nuberroca's next pass takes down a small one, carries through on the backswing into another, and the third stroke lands on the armoured one [Td:1360-1373]. The last big one folds under the halberd, armour-plates and all [Td:1430-1433]. He turns to call the scattering civilians back around him [Td:1434-1437].

## 7 - Dillian is dead
_~16:30, 10 Hammer_

1. In the lull after the fight a voice screams — Dillian's, cut short [Td:1302]. By the time the party gets to him he is dead [Td:1391-1392].
2. Mialee searches the body and recovers a small purse [Td:1466]. She later hands it off at five pieces of gold and twenty coppers [Td:1519].
3. Sinsong goes to Dillian's two friends, **Jyn** and **Xod**, and asks what they would have done with the body. Jyn speaks for both: they barely knew him, and the risk was understood when they signed on. If he does not rise again, that will do [Td:1489-1495].
4. Nuberroca cuts in with the fact that Dillian spoke to him before the end, and that he knows what Dillian said [Td:1496-1500]. Jyn's face shuts — *"no creo que la incompetencia de tus compañeros sea culpa nuestra"* [Td:1550] — and he announces that he will not keep working for the pittance they are paid, and goes [Td:1551-1553].
5. Mialee hands Jyn Dillian's purse, as from the dead man's own kin [Td:1519]. Jyn takes it [Td:1550].
6. Sinsong, having worked out that the man who sent Dillian is of a piece with somebody from an older session, bolts in a panic — *"es el Rodri, me está siguiendo. El Odir viene a por nosotros, hay que huir de aquí"* — and makes for the trees. Mialee, Nuberroca and Eguzkilore chase him down and talk him back [Td:1531-1548].
7. By the time the party collects itself the civilians have scattered. About half, under Jyn, are walking back toward the roadhouse without them. The rest cluster around the party [Td:1575-1602].
8. An hour on, Mialee goes back to look at the body on her way out. Dillian is gone. Nothing drags away through the mud — it has simply been taken [Td:1627-1643].

## 8 - Back under the eaves
_~18:00, 10 Hammer_

1. The party returns to Carnath with the workers an hour behind the first group. The common room looks at them as they come in [Td:1657-1662].
2. Nuberroca is loud about the day's work from the door. **Isaiash**, one of the gate-guards, snaps back from behind the counter: *"ahí está la carretera para Waterdeep. No sé qué hacéis aquí."* Nuberroca invites him to perform an obscene favour; Isaiash answers in kind, lowering his voice when he realises the room is thin [Td:1670-1687].
3. Sinsong sits down to beers in the near-empty bar [Td:1697-1703] and ends up at the counter with **Jonan**, one of the escort, grousing about workers who do not work [Td:1713-1727].
4. Mialee sees Jyn and the returning workers go straight to **Ardred**, the caravan's registrar, who hears them out and immediately pulls out his book to record it [Td:1727-1730].
5. Nuberroca sits in on a knot of workers — Jarth, Runo, Reurig — and the man he has come looking for, **Sarek** [Td:1948-1958]. Prodded, Sarek names himself a soldier who follows orders [Td:1990-1992]. Nuberroca pushes him to show the insignia of his cult [Td:1994-1996]; Sarek's companion goes for his weapon and Sarek stops him with a flat *"mañana, aún no"* [Td:2003-2004].
6. ***[EPIC]*** Sarek turns on Nuberroca and warns him in a voice that reaches no further than the two of them: *"andaos con ojo porque estáis creando muchos enemigos, y no sois tan fuertes como creéis."* And walks off [Td:2004-2009].
7. Nuberroca takes the warning to Mialee first [Td:2060-2062] and then straight to Bog Luck [Td:2083-2088]. Bog Luck professes surprise — Sarek is not the sort — and promises to speak to him [Td:2091-2094].

## 9 - A ransacked room, a locked door
_~19:00?, 10 Hammer_

1. Upstairs the party finds the room turned over. Nothing is missing; they carry everything on them. Someone was looking for something and left no mark of what [Td:2108-2127].
2. Sinsong, hidden in a corner of the common hallway [Td:2071-2080 ?ord], watches **Anton** — the cultist who stole the party's cart on the road from Waterdeep — slip out of Bog Luck's locked office and back in again [Td:2298-2317]. He brings the news to the party in their room [Td:2298-2302].
3. Nuberroca lays out Sarek's threat [Td:2322-2330]; Sinsong lays out Anton going in and out of a door only Bog Luck should hold the key to [Td:2301-2304]. The two pieces fit — Bog Luck is in on it, or he is being used [Td:2308-2317].
4. Sinsong palms one of the stones Eguzkilore gave him in the morning and speaks into it [Td:2384-2385]; Mialee's answering voice comes through the mate stone in her hand [Td:2386-2390]. The two stones are a pair of **Sending Stones**, good for one exchange a day [Td:2400-2408].
5. The plan writes itself. Mialee will pick the lock on Bog Luck's office under cover of a distraction; Nuberroca will keep Bog Luck himself occupied over supper; Sinsong will plant himself in the yard with his view of the office door [Td:2367-2379 ?ord].

## 10 - What the ledger said
_~20:00?, 10 Hammer_

1. Mialee reaches the door with her floating invisible hand on it, picks the lock on the first try, and slips in. Light inside gives her away for a heartbeat and she pulls the door almost closed behind her [Td:2454-2466].
2. The room is spartan and immaculate: a desk with an empty top but for an inkwell and a pouch of sand, a bookshelf of philosophy and natural history worn with use, and under the bed a trunk whose lid is not locked. Inside the trunk is a Waterdeep guard-officer's uniform with full regalia, and black clothing that could be a cultist's and could as easily not be [Td:2491-2501, Td:2674-2686].
3. On the shelf is a ledger. Entries of valuables kept there carry two ticks apiece — except the most recent, which carry only one. Beside each entry is a name and a small gilt mark of a dwarf smoking a pipe. Among the one-tick entries sit *"dos esferas de detención"* — the pair she lifted from the inner storeroom chest the night of her S33 scouting — and she adds the second tick to the entry with the inkwell, folding her own theft back into the ledger. A twelve-platinum bag plus a cheque under the name *Ardred* takes a tick as well, and she blots the ink. The trunk, the uniform, and the shelf go back exactly as they stood [Td:2505-2534, Td:2686-2688].
4. In the yard Sinsong is lifting his voice — *"¡socorro! ¡socorro ya!"* — to put eyes on him. Isaiash and **Jarth** come at a half-run; Jarth takes the pantomime for what it is and goes straight for the office door. Sinsong has roped Anton to the ground with Command and cords between the two shouts; Isaiash, when he arrives, cuts the ropes without a word [Td:2466-2478 ?ord, Td:2546-2583].
5. Anton snaps upright and puts a rapier through Sinsong's ribs [Td:2649-2650] — the blade goes in between the bones *"sin demasiada complicación"* [Td:2669-2669] — and Sinsong drops [Td:2667-2673].

## 11 - The brawl in the yard
_~20:30?, 10 Hammer_

1. Sarek closes on Eguzkilore and tries to pin him; Eguzkilore slips free [Td:2628-2638]. Jarth comes at him from the other side and gets the hold [Td:2641-2646]. Eguzkilore wrestles free of Jarth on his own next move and catches him in a counter-hold [Td:3012-3024].
2. Nuberroca, down from Bog Luck's table on the first shout, grapples Jarth back and tears him away from Eguzkilore [Td:2777-2821]. He opens with a full-force halberd stroke under the chin and a cleaving follow-through that catches Sarek on the backswing [Td:2949-2961].
3. Mialee seals the office door shut from inside using her floating hand on the lock-plate, bolts out past the melee, and comes around behind Jarth with the shortsword in reverse grip — a sneaking strike where he cannot see her coming [Td:2831-2843, Td:3108-3123].
4. ***[EPIC]*** Jarth, bleeding, drops his sword long enough to pull a talisman from his collar and chant a line Nuberroca does not recognise. The goliath's limbs lock on him; the halberd falls out of his grip and his fury snuffs out at once [Td:2965-2985]. The next sword-stroke that lands on him lands clean through the open guard, and takes the breath out of him [Td:2987-2993].
5. Isaiash shelves his loyalty to the house and switches sides, dragging Sinsong's body clear while shouting that the party started it, then setting arrows against Eguzkilore and Mialee from the near rail [Td:3075-3081].
6. Mialee drops Jarth on her next pass with the shortsword in both hands — non-lethal to the end, barely a flicker of breath left in him [Td:3108-3124]. Eguzkilore puts his bow aside for a scimitar and takes two careful cuts at Sarek, slowing him but not dropping him [Td:3140-3158].
7. **Bog Luck** fills the office doorway with his semi-orc bulk — *"¿qué cojones está pasando aquí?"* — and reads the yard in a glance: Sinsong bleeding on the stones, Isaiash tending him, three of his guards and three of the escort upright with weapons out. He steps in between with hands open, not even drawing [Td:3067-3075 ?ord, Td:3159-3171].

## 12 - Under watch
_~21:00, 10 Hammer_

1. On Bog Luck's order every weapon in the yard lowers at the same time. The guards collect themselves; the party collects itself [Td:3203-3209].
2. Bog Luck sends the party upstairs under watch. *"Son las 9 de la noche en ambos sitios"* — too late to sort it tonight; tomorrow he will have answers from all of them [Td:3209-3214].
3. On the way up Nuberroca makes the one offering he can — he warns Bog Luck that his guests have been in the room and that Sarek has already told the goliath he will not be alive to see noon. Bog Luck's face goes still at the word *"revuelto"*, and he says nothing [Td:3216-3221].
4. The night passes without further disturbance. The long rest comes with it, though not the full of its usual benefit — Sinsong wakes the goliath mid-rest to half his vitality and no more, and the escort lies down knowing exactly what the morning will bring [Td:3255-3265].
