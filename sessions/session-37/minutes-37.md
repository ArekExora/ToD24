## 1 - Rest in the tunnel
_~16:30, 11 Hammer? 1490 DR_

1. The party sits roughly 240 ft into the tunnel, bruised and spent [Td:282], with pursuers somewhere behind them [Td:181]. In the dark Nyx's foot catches something metallic — a short pipe-shaped object, no tobacco smell, no clear purpose yet [Td:177-180].
2. Nuberroca hands Nyx a spare length of rope to replace the one Nyx lost on the way in [Td:182-188].
3. The party decides to camp in the tunnel rather than press on wounded [Td:207-213]. Mialee argues for pushing forward [Td:260-263], but Nuberroca insists on resting — half the party is heavily hurt and there is no proper healer in the group with Sinsong away [Td:275, 279].
4. Nyx places her warding spell behind the party, audible, triggering if anyone comes within sixty feet [Td:287-290, 296-298, 302-305].
5. Mialee breathes incense and summons Echo [Td:293-299]. She sends the bat eighty feet ahead down the tunnel to watch, with instructions to come running if it sees anything [Td:317-331].
6. Nyx distributes a palm of her berries to the party — four to Mialee to shave one day from a lingering grave wound, one to Eguzkilore for the last of his bruises [Td:426-430].
7. Nuberroca works a day off one of his own grave wounds through the long rest with his Healer's Kit [Td:463-468, 500-515].
8. Over the rest Nyx fiddles with the strange pipe. Up close it has no tobacco at all, and when she sets it to her ear she can hear noticeably farther than her own ear reaches — a listening horn, not a pipe [Td:476-482].
9. Eight hours pass. Neither the alarm nor Echo stirs; no one comes down the tunnel [Td:457-460 ?ord].

## 2 - Through the tunnel to the marsh
_~00:30?, 12 Hammer? 1490 DR_

1. Nuberroca argues against open flame in the enclosed tunnel [Td:540-541], but there is no other way for the party to see together, and Mialee suggests Sinsong's bald head would have done nicely were he here [Td:536-547]. Nuberroca ends up walking point with a lit torch while the others ring him at a distance [Td:590-600].
2. Over the hours the walls shift under Nuberroca's torchlight — carved stone softens into packed earth, then earth braced with woven willow holding the roof and walls together [Td:549-551]. The air thickens into damp and some stretches of wall drip [Td:552-553].
3. After roughly three hours there is still no light ahead; after four the party sees an arch of pale, steady light at the tunnel's end — not the flicker of torches, something constant [Td:555-559].
4. Mialee sends Echo out through the arch to listen and look. The bat reports the marsh beyond — a boggy flat with scattered trees, no one in sight on the near ground [Td:560-575].
5. The party emerges into the **Mere of Dead Men** [Td:571].

## 3 - Following the webbed tracks
_~05:00?, 12 Hammer? 1490 DR_

1. Half an hour out of the tunnel the dawn is coming on grey [Td:681]. A path winds out ahead of them, clearly marked with hatchets on trees and strips of cloth tied to branches [Td:602-603].
2. In the muddy stretches above the water the party can see fresh webbed footprints [Td:603]. Nuberroca and Nyx study them — unfamiliar but, from the tunnel's context, likely **lizardfolk** [Td:608-609, 644].
3. Nyx reminds Nuberroca that she and Mialee both saw lizardfolk in their visions — these are the same tracks their dreams showed [Td:646-648].
4. The marsh is foul and slow going — bugs rising from every pool, mud dragging at every step [Td:682-685]. The party follows the trail cautiously.
5. Nyx's ear catches something ahead: splashing, several bodies, moving as quietly as they know how [Td:694-697]. She signs the others to halt in a soldier's gesture and slips forward alone to scout [Td:698-700].

## 4 - The lizardfolk ambush
_~05:15?, 12 Hammer? 1490 DR_

1. Nyx tries to hide in a thicket. The lie of her hiding is one of the worst she can remember, and she knows at once that at least one of them has seen her [Td:716-724].
2. Looking back from the thicket she counts around seventeen — most about human height but a touch shorter; two or three the size of a tall man; one of them very large indeed, nearly Nuberroca's size, and it is the big one that has her [Td:744-749, 951-953].
3. Behind her Nuberroca steps apart from the others, into the torchlight alone, and starts to laugh like a madman at nothing — bait for the ambush [Td:701-705 ?ord].
4. ***[EPIC]*** Nyx draws, breathes, and sends an arrow into the big one's shoulder from cover — a hard hit, enough that the party can see him flinch and set [Td:808, 826-827].
5. Nuberroca strikes the flat of his torch against his shield and calls out in the dark: *"No nos acechéis criaturas. Si queréis pelear, salid a por ella. Si no, hablad."* [Td:752-753 ?ord].
6. Nyx whispers a word to Nuberroca across the gap — the biggest one is now his to meet [Td:751 ?ord].
7. The lizardfolk carry clubs set with small stones and a brace of javelins each; none has thrown yet [Td:884-886, 894].
8. Nuberroca produces a strip of travel-ration and a gold piece and holds them out to the nearest advancing lizardfolk [Td:909-917]. The lizardfolk hesitates, lowers its javelin a fraction, and approaches with its shield-arm forward [Td:915-920]. The others watch and seem to be weighing their choices [Td:916].

## 5 - Battle in the marsh
_~05:20?, 12 Hammer? 1490 DR_

1. One of the lizardfolk breaks the quiet and hurls a javelin at Nyx [Td:1013-1015]. She twists, draws her bow in the same motion and sends an arrow back along its path — a critical shot into the thrower's belly, a near-killing wound [Td:1024-1041]. The lizardfolk crumples, dragging himself behind the trunk and cursing Nyx in his own tongue: *matadla, que me ha dejado de la mierda* — only the big one and Kaelthar catch his words [Td:1339-1341].
2. Kaelthar enters the fight behind Nuberroca's cover [Td:1047], a shimmer of warding turning the first javelins thrown at him [Td:1211-1215, 1297-1301].
3. One among the lizardfolk raises brambles out of the ground — thorny vines that whip across the party's footing, blood for every step they try through [Td:1225-1230]. The marsh they stand on becomes a trap.
4. ***[EPIC]*** A javelin crits into Nyx's left arm; blood goes fast and the shock leaves her stunned where she stands [Td:1458-1472]. 
5. Mialee throws a bank of thick fog across her own ground, then steps into the cloud herself and sees her targets through Echo's blind-sight [Td:1543-1573].
6. A critical hit lands on Nuberroca's right leg — a burn across the flesh that will leave him more vulnerable to fire from now on [Td:1735-1738].
7. Nuberroca drops his shield and spear, pulls free his **halberd**, and sets to work with its longer reach [Td:1761-1764].
8. The lizardfolk caster, seeing Mialee's fog still standing, rains **Hail of Stone** down through the whole column — a pillar of falling stone sixty feet tall [Td:1842-1853]. Everyone inside takes it hard: Nyx drops to the ground [Td:1853, 1873-1874]; Mialee survives with slivers [Td:1864-1865]; Echo is pulverised in the air [Td:1866-1870].
9. Mialee, through the fog, drives a scimitar into a lizardfolk's flank — a stab in from his blind side [Td:1914-1917]. 
10. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee uses her *mage hand* to tip a healing potion down Nyx's throat — Nyx stirs on the ground, half conscious but seeing [Td:1921-1931, 1965-1967].
11. ***[EPIC]*** The chief draws a steel sword — the only worked metal among the band [Td:1611-1612 ?ord] — and takes two shots at Kaelthar's back. The second lands critical and drops Kaelthar unconscious; the chief pulls the kill at the last moment and the dragonborn stays alive [Td:1978-1996, 2015-2022].
12. The chief shouts an order only Kaelthar half-catches, and the next blows against the downed PCs come softer — the lizardfolk pull their strikes to *daño no letal*, binding the fallen instead of killing them [Td:2041-2056, 2089].
13. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca invokes his cloud-stride and vanishes from the ring — he reappears outside the lizardfolk circle, free of the surround [Td:2103-2105]. The lizardfolk flinch at the magic [Td:2105].
14. Nuberroca lowers his halberd and holds out his hands: *"Efectivamente somos buenos guerreros y todavía tenemos más trucos, pero este combate lo habéis ganado vosotros."* [Td:2106-2109].
15. The caster speaks and the brambles melt back into the ground — the fight is called [Td:2113-2114].

## 6 - The chief's bargain
_~05:30, 12 Hammer? 1490 DR_

1. The chief steps forward, keeping a careful distance from Nuberroca's reach, and asks whether he is the party's leader [Td:2121-2122]. Nuberroca answers that he is not — but that he often ends up speaking for them [Td:2124].
2. Nuberroca offers five gold and then another thirty silver as reparation. The chief accepts the coin and tells Nuberroca he looks a reasonable, intelligent man [Td:2142-2153].
3. The bound party is brought water that steadies them for talking [Td:2162]. Nuberroca explains the party had come up through a tunnel after a fight at a roadhouse on the far side — and later asks the chief straight whether they themselves run with the Cult of the Dragon [Td:2157-2201].
4. The chief answers in broken Common: *"A veces hay gente que merece morir. El culto hace lo que cree correcto. Aunque a veces tenga aliados malos."* [Td:2205-2209]. He plants the cult as neighbour, not kin.
5. The chief explains why he wants the party's blades: another kin of the marsh — the **bullywugs** — killed his lizardfolk leader by a stab in the back, and the bullywugs outnumber his band [Td:2290-2302].
6. His offer: kill the bullywugs' leader for us. Safe passage through the marsh can be talked about when the thing is done [Td:2303-2305]. Nuberroca accepts the terms [Td:2306-2308]; Nyx nods the same from where she sits [Td:2309].
7. The chief adds that the bullywugs are more numerous than his own band — the party will have to be *más inteligentes* than they were against lizardfolk if they expect to walk out of the bullywug nest [Td:2314-2317].
8. Kaelthar, awake and bound, pushes the chief in what Draconic he can muster — a kick to the leg and then a boot in the ribs for his trouble [Td:2311-2321 ?ord].
9. The lizardfolk mark Mialee for a magic-user by her fog and leave her bound; the same goes for Sinsong-in-absentia, by Nuberroca's accounting of the party [Td:2366-2370].
10. The chief sends the bulk of his band off to patrol the marsh and keeps a handful with him to guard the party [Td:2373], then at last introduces himself: **Snapjaw** [Td:2383]. Draconic comes to him naturally; his Common is broken — *"yo Snapjaw, vosotros fuertes, matad bullybooks"* [Td:2385].
11. Snapjaw commits to travelling the next leg beside the party, his group to follow when summoned [Td:2409-2412].
12. The sky over the mere is just beginning to pale when Snapjaw calls the day's work done — ~05:30 by now — information on the bullywugs' nest and leader is to be exchanged while the party rests the rest of the morning out [Td:2358 ?ord, 2413-2419].
