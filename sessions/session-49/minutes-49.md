## 1 - Pharblex held, and the fog goes silent
_~09:50, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The session opens where session 48 ended — **Pharblex** held mid-step by the paralysing sphere Mialee had broken against him at the close of the last fight [T:10-15], the small bullywugs that flanked him and the cultist wizard behind him already dead [T:33-35]. Nyx lies on her back where she went down and Mialee's potion brought her back to a single point of life [T:23-25], [T:38]. Mialee's second bank of fog still lies white across the ledge — nobody outside her line of sight has a picture of the field [T:26-29]. Eguzkilore stands inside the mist with a giant frog directly in front of him; at least a pair of warriors are somewhere further in [T:33-36], [T:46].
2. Mialee opens on Pharblex — at point-blank range and against the frog's held stance the sword bites deep as a crit, a rogue's cut riding through on the same swing [T:49-55]. A follow-up with the off-hand lands as well [T:64-66]; she cuts to hide behind cover before the frogs can find her [T:69-73].
3. In answer the giant frog snaps at Eguzkilore in the fog [T:74-75]; another warrior comes up on Nyx palpating in the dark and, between his blindness and hers, misses the swing wide [T:76-82]. From the south corridor shouts carry — bullywugs calling out, coming fast [T:92-96].
4. Eguzkilore draws on the giant frog in front of him — the sapo had been left at a single point of life somewhere in the earlier fight and drops on the arrow [T:104-109]. He would have stepped back to the fog but stays on the line to hear what is coming down the corridor [T:110-121].
5. Pharblex fails his end-of-turn save [T:117-128] — GM lets the whole party's fate ride on that single roll, and the paralysis holds [T:126].
6. ***[EPIC]*** Nyx puts her Hunter's Mark on Pharblex [T:163-171] and, blind through the fog, fires twice into his approximate position [T:171-176]. Both arrows find him — the first at the shoulder, the frog's held stance opening him to a crit as the arrow buries deep [T:177-181]; the second follows it in for another crit at the same range [T:184-197]. On Nyx's late catch the GM lets an ambusher's opening burst fold into the first shot [T:187-193]. Pharblex takes both without a sound; somewhere in the fog the soft glow Nyx had marked him with at the fight's opening winks out, but no one at the ledge can see through the mist to know it [T:198-199], [derived:T:198+T:340-342].
7. Mialee sees only that Pharblex is *"sigue quieto"* [T:202] and asks herself whether killing him would drop the paralysis [T:203]. She strikes again — the sword bites deep into the held frog for another crit, a rogue's follow-up cut riding through [T:216-234] — then, in the same breath and with a small step to reach one of the smaller frogs beside her, drives an off-hand cut into it and a sneak-strike behind; she hears it fall behind her as she withdraws to hide behind Pharblex himself [T:235-247].
8. The nearer warrior, homed in by the arrows, finds Nyx and lands both swings — small damage but she must hold her illusion through them [T:248-254]. She holds through the impact — then the GM realises the frog-skin illusion did not need holding in the first place, and it stays regardless [T:255-262].

## 2 - The champion in the fog
_~09:55, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. From the south passage the shouts have become the sound of a bullywug champion moving forward with warriors behind him [T:279-282]. Eguzkilore pushes three paces further into the mist and walks straight into a bullywug guerrero — the two of them touching in the dark [T:284-296]. He attacks blind; the strike lands with the dagger [T:297], and a second cut digs deeper [T:298-306]. When he wrenches the dagger out the sapo falls, the blade the only thing holding it up [T:307-309].
2. The GM tells Nyx everything around her is now dead [T:310-313]; through the fog she cannot see it, and she sets herself ready for the first fresh enemy to step clear [T:315-321]. Mialee lets the fog drop, and the mist clears from the ledge [T:323-332]; where Pharblex had stood there is only him on the floor and the corpses of his guard [T:332-341]. Eguzkilore, closest to Pharblex inside the fog, realises the frog's soft glow had gone out at some point in the exchange [T:340-342].
3. From the south passage two bullywug warriors arrive first, with a bigger sapo — a champion — coming up behind them [T:373-380], [T:456-459]. Mialee fires from cover — sneak-strike into the leading warrior [T:377-382] — warns the others down the line and re-hides [T:388-395]. The warrior tries to run through her; her turn-out swing misses [T:397-409].
4. Nyx's arrow, ready since the fog dropped, goes into the leading warrior from the darkness — a slow-strike sinks in for a solid wound [T:417-421]. Eguzkilore's first arrow catches the same warrior and drops him where he stood [T:422-429]; his second, into the champion at range and against cover, drives clean in [T:430-446]. Nyx moves her Hunter's Mark across to the champion [T:466-470], puts a slow-strike arrow into him for a thin wound [T:479-486], and a follow-up shot out of the dark finishes him [T:487-490]. The passage goes quiet; only tadpoles croaking in the birthing chamber [T:493-494].

## 3 - Pharblex's head
_~10:00, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Mialee asks whether they can carry Pharblex out whole [T:346-350]. GM notes his gear survived the fight — the leather armour is filthy and shot through with arrows and cuts, and beyond it he carried nothing but the fish-mouth hat over his face [T:352-360], [T:364-366].
2. Nyx draws her short blade, gets to it, and separates the head — grim, wet work [T:366-368], [T:495-498]. Eguzkilore keeps watch with the bow while it happens, ready for whatever might still come down the corridor [T:499-500].
3. They pull five arrows from the dead and split them — three to Nyx, two to Eguzkilore [T:504-512]. Mialee takes Pharblex's head off Nyx's hands and packs it beside the heart she keeps in her bag [T:523-528]; the GM logs the head at three kilos [T:530].
4. The three cultist wizards' bodies from the earlier volley yield nothing — no staff survived the arrows and the wet floor; only their component pouches are salvageable [T:541-551].
5. Session had opened at 09:50; between the two rounds and the decapitation and the loot-search ten minutes have crossed — the clock now reads 10:00 [T:553-555]. The cave is otherwise quiet; only frogs and running water from the birthing chamber [T:555].

## 4 - The frog-shrine and the muddy library
_~10:05, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The GM lays out the room for Nyx, who cannot see the screen from her seat — the ledge behind them where they had come down the crane, and three passages ahead: the tadpole cave to the south they already know, and two west, one climbing and one level [T:558-570]. Mialee points at the climbing passage — a soft purple glow up the stairs and what could be lamps beyond it [T:572-577].
2. Eguzkilore's judgement is to fall back down the passage the last bullywugs came from — he thinks it is the safer route, and he is badly banged up [T:579-582], [T:601]. Nyx suggests they carry the trophy out first and come back with the others [T:593-596]. Mialee, greedy for the rest of it, wants the last rooms [T:1005]; she wins the argument and they push west and up the stairs, all three in stealth [T:604-624].
3. At the top of the stairs is a low shrine — carved rocks on the walls in the shape of clumsy frogs, a soft purple glow emanating from the eye-stones [T:627-634]. Mialee reads the symbols: pieces of Ghaunadaur, the god of the oozes and slimes [T:655-658], and of Shar, goddess of shadows [T:658], mixed together as if someone had taken pieces of two religions without knowing what either was — a crooked cross and a crescent moon, side by side [T:659-662]. The eye-glows are a luminous stone, not gems [T:663-667].
4. A second chamber opens off the shrine — mud-covered floor and walls, a table with candles, and along the wall half a shelf of what were once beautifully-bound books [T:675-683]. On the table are the books the sapo had been reading with muddy hands — magic books, ruined beyond any cantrip repair [T:685-705]. Along the shelf a few volumes are still elegant and still intact [T:697-699].

## 5 - The spore-trap
_~10:15, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. In the middle of the room sits a closed chest [T:694-696]. Mialee has learned from the last chest that opened on her — she steps back to the doorway and sends Eguzkilore and Nyx into the corridor behind her while she works [T:711-724]. Her spectral hand can turn the lock but does not have the pull the lid needs [T:733-751]; she goes in in person, careful, and prises it up with a dagger [T:748-753].
2. As she opens it, six ceramic pots drop from the ceiling — a well-hidden trap [T:756-763]. Four hold; two crack on the boards and the room begins to fill with a pale spore-cloud [T:759-763]. Inside the chest are two more elegant magic books, untouched [T:754].
3. Mialee fails the save [T:765-771]. The spores take her mind; where the party stood at the door she now sees two giant bullywugs staring at her, sure with the certainty of a hallucination that the only way to survive them is to get underwater [T:778-784]. Wide pupils, yelling in a language no one at the table speaks, she drops under the table [T:793-796], then bolts for the corridor [T:817-830].
4. Nyx puts her hands out low, palms open — no threat, quiet — to keep Mialee from spooking any further [T:797-799]. Eguzkilore holds his position at the corridor mouth, watching the room and the chest [T:812-814]. Between the three of them Mialee runs at a hallucinated frog and bolts past both of them; her speed is more than theirs and she leaves them behind [T:822-830].
5. She reaches the birthing chamber pool and hides under the surface with only her eyes above the water [T:836-838]. Nyx follows at her back, arrives at the pool-edge, and pretends to look somewhere else so Mialee will hold in the water [T:838-843].

## 6 - The corpses in the water, and Mialee's return
_~10:20, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. From the pool-edge Nyx sees several bullywug warrior corpses on the rocks and floating in the shallows — none of them the party's kills [T:843-844], [T:850-853]. The wizards from the earlier fight are entirely missing from the pile, and the warriors she can see have all been killed by blunt force — no cuts, no bites [T:986-991]. It does not match how any of the party fights [T:993-994]. She keeps her distance from Mialee, going through the motions of hunting for her [T:857-861].
2. Meanwhile Eguzkilore recovers the chest — the two elegant magic books from inside, and the four intact spore-pots from the floor, all wrapped tight in his pack against water and cracking [T:867-884]. The rest of the room is only broken pot-shards and mud [T:877-879].
3. Nyx sends a whispered message across to Mialee — her voice in Mialee's head, still Nyx's own [T:910-915]. Mialee, in her hallucination, hears the frog speaking to her; Nyx narrates the small motions of her body — sitting down, opening a hand, walking a few paces at a time — as proof it is Nyx and not a bullywug [T:917-932]. Mialee: *"No eres tú, tú estás dentro del sapo"* [T:929-931]. After ten minutes the spores wear off; Mialee comes out asking for water and one of Nyx's berries [T:933-942].
4. Nyx warns her on the way back — *"la avaricia rompe el saco"* [T:1002] — Mialee's greed drove them to the chest. Mialee, half-recovered and undeterred, points at the last unexplored passage — the west-and-north one they had not walked [T:995-997], [T:1010]. Nyx follows the pull; Eguzkilore behind [T:1010-1013].

## 7 - The conjuration slab, and out
_~10:35, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The last chamber is thick with the same fog they climbed down through on the crane — dense enough that Nyx is barely visible even to those next to her; Eguzkilore, the tallest of them, is the only one whose head clears the cloud [T:1013-1017]. Mialee casts Detect Magic and picks up a conjuration aura at the room's centre [T:1021-1031], [T:1061].
2. She blows softly on the fog with a handful of air, clears a patch, and beneath it lies a slab of stone carved with a star and a ring of unknown symbols [T:1036-1043]. Roughly fifteen feet by fifteen feet, big enough that Mialee's step onto it does not trigger any mechanism she can feel [T:1044-1052], [T:1140-1142]. She reaches for the arcane knowledge she has and cannot get further than *"magia poderosa"* — powerful magic, but no more [T:1085-1106].
3. Nyx wants to sketch the pattern for Amidamaru to read later [T:1107-1108]. Between the fog to clear and the level of detail she wants, the GM warns her that a full drawing would take nearly an hour; they settle on a rough sketch in ten minutes, on the back of an old letter Nyx had carried [T:1113-1143]. Eguzkilore hangs back at the corridor mouth in cover; Mialee helps Nyx clear enough fog to get outlines down [T:1135-1144].
4. Party takes the water tunnel out, the way they had come in — all three roll high on stealth [T:1163-1177]. Between the swim and the walk they arrive at the tree-line where Amidamaru and Nuberroca were left, at 10:45 [T:1188-1192]. They stop there in cover, holding the reunion until the others are up to speed [T:1197-1204]. Session closes on Nyx's summary — they killed Pharblex and they are still alive [T:1219].
