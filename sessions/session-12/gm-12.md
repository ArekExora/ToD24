## 7. Knowledge gained

1. **The Cult of the Dragon serves Tiamat.** The cult is named for Tiamat, goddess of chromatic dragons.
2. **At least five dragons confirmed.** The blue seen at Greenest, a red working alongside it in the north, either the same red or additional reds striking other cities, and a white.
3. **The alliance against the cult.** Order of the Gauntlet, Harpers, Lords' Alliance, Emerald Enclave — all working to break the cult.
4. **Red Wizards may be stirring.** Ontharr's guess — if Nuberroca's home is near Thay's border, undead activity fits Red Wizard patterns.
