### NPCs
- **Ontharr Frume** — Duels Nuberroca, recruits him, briefs cult intel.
- **Leosin Erlanthar** — recruits Nyx to the Harpers.

### Knowledge gained
- **The Cult of the Dragon serves Tiamat.** The cult is named for Tiamat, goddess of chromatic dragons.
- **At least five dragons confirmed.** The blue seen at Greenest, a red working alongside it in the north, either the same red or additional reds striking other cities, and a white.
- **The alliance against the cult.** Order of the Gauntlet, Harpers, Lords' Alliance, Emerald Enclave — all working to break the cult.
- **Red Wizards may be stirring.** Ontharr's guess — if Nuberroca's home is near Thay's border, undead activity fits Red Wizard patterns.

### Status changes
- **Nuberroca joins the Order of the Gauntlet.** Signed in; sworn to law, order, and defence of the innocent.
- **Nyx joins the Harpers.** Signed in; receives the **Harpers pendant** — leather cord, harp pierced by a sword.

### Loose ends
- **Arthax** — bandit boss who ordered Amidamaru's kidnap; identity, location, and motive unknown.
