## 1 - Dawn on the road
_~09:30?, 29 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The caravan picks itself up on the road to Waterdeep [Td:1-10]. **Antón** has gone with their wagon and the horses have only just been brought in; the two prisoners are dead in their bindings; the merchants are intact and want to move. The bodies will be left on the pile the party has already been dragging behind them [Td:21-24]. Mialee levels a long glare at **Miguel** and **Fenwir**, the two Greenest guards who had been talking the day before about her stealing the cart; neither answers it [Td:13-18].
2. Nobody has horses to chase the stolen wagon with [Td:50]. Amidamaru sends his familiar **Munin** forward along the road to find where the wagon goes [Td:73-82]; when the gates are in sight later the bird is nowhere above the carts queued at the arch [Td:589-590].
3. On the move again, the party rakes over the plan — deliver the chests to the Harpers in Waterdeep, press north to the roadhouse at **Carnath** [Td:140-165]. Mialee fumbles trying to recover the handler's name — *"Eloísi... Erosin"* — the elf monk Janma had named in S27 [Td:181-186].

## 2 - A quiet word with Nyx

1. Half-way to the gates Amidamaru pulls Nyx aside from the carts for a moment [Td:308-311]. He asks after her; she waves it off; he lays a hand on her and heals the last of the beating she took in the chest [Td:315-316]. She protests — he has already cast [Td:317-321].
2. He pours the whole thing out to her — *"¿Qué hacemos siguiendo esta caravana? ... Teníamos una misión anterior muy tranquila, en paz, y venimos a meternos en algo que secuestran, nos torturan, estamos a punto de morir día tras día"* [Td:332-336]. He calls the rest of them *"una manga de inadaptados sociales"* — *"uno se cree Maradona, el otro un pitufo con esteroides, el otro un T-Rex que sabe hablar"* [Td:337-338]. His brothers are off looking for a place safe from the dragons; he could be with them, not here [Td:342-346].
3. Nyx holds her ground quietly. They have helped many; a barbarian village was saved; the road has their blood on it but it is still the right road [Td:348-358]. She will not rest easy knowing the cult is still at the party's back [Td:371-374]. She hands him an inspiration for the honesty of the ask [Td:370].

## 3 - A hooded rider ahead of the gates
_~11:30?_

1. The gates of Waterdeep come up a mile and a half out — four or five carts queued at the arch, guards on the walls and movement along the battlements [Td:383-385]. A single lone rider on a big black destrier waits a few hundred paces short of the arch, perpendicular to the road, watching the caravan come [Td:385-386, Td:420].
2. Sinsong picks the shape out at distance by something he recognises in it — *"Nuberroca, es que no vamos a ser capaces. Ni a tontazos."* [Td:449-456]. As the carts close in, the others make him out: hooded, red-cloaked, flame-sigils and other arcane marks on the cloth that none of them can place [Td:654-659].
3. Sinsong asks to be hidden before the elf's eye lands on him. Amidamaru lays a hand on his shoulder and the dwarf disappears — the spell will hold about an hour [Td:456-467]. Sinsong climbs down into the family cart and pulls the blanket over himself [Td:466-467, Td:615-616]; Amidamaru whispers across his mind — *"no sólo quédate callado, sino quédate quieto"* [Td:526-529].
4. Nuberroca, who is told only that they are hiding Sinsong, loops a cord round his own chest and takes Amidamaru up onto his back as if the tortle were his next *bebé*, replacing the dead cultist from the night before [Td:555-562]. The merchants look once and look away — the fools' legend has grown that much [Td:563-568].
5. **Baird** calls the guard-fees out along the road while they walk — Kaelthar five, Nyx ten, Eguzkilore eight, Mialee fifteen, Amidamaru ten; Nuberroca gratis, a joke the barbarian lets stand [Td:596-603]. Kaelthar's wolf is hooded under cloth and his own hood pulled up — the civil quarter does not love a dragonborn or a loose beast [Td:625-644].

## 4 - Elodin's tale
_~12:00?_

1. The rider lets them close. He pulls the hood back — an elf; eyes injected red to the whites; froth at the corner of his mouth. *"A vosotros os conozco. ¿Dónde está Sinsong? Llevo esperando. En Waterdeep nadie le ha visto. Ni dijisteis que estaba aquí."* [Td:671-679]. Mialee buys them a day — the dwarf will meet them tomorrow in the plaza [Td:680-687].
2. He falls in alongside the carts: *"Vale, iré con vosotros"* [Td:688]. Pressed on why — *"Tengo que matarle. Es un ser vago, manipulador. Él condenó a Blandea y muchos de ellos están petrificados por su culpa."* [Td:706-713].
3. He gives his tale as they walk. A dark stone turned up in a mine in the Far Hills; the villagers who touched it sickened and slowly turned to stone [Td:719-730]. By his telling a madman convinced Sinsong to use the stone, and Sinsong turned nearly the whole of his village into stone himself [Td:731-734]. Elodin was turned to stone when he came after; a *patrona* he met in the dark cut him a deal and brought him back — the price is Sinsong's blood for the people's lives [Td:739-744]. *"Se ganó mi confianza y fingió ser mi amigo y cuando bajé la guardia me intentó asesinar."* [Td:742-743]. If this ends in a sword he will take the party with him: *"vosotros seréis los que pagáis sus errores."* [Td:755-757].
4. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca enters rage and levels the long polearm at the elf's face [Td:758-760]. The elf's sword lights with a wrapped black light and takes the shaft in two across the middle [Td:762-764]. Elodin keeps the point against him: *"si me buscáis, me encontráis"* [Td:765]. Nuberroca grips the broken shaft back: *"con amenazas no funcionamos aquí, elfo"* [Td:766].
5. Mialee reaches for whatever magic hangs on him — a mage's armour and the sword itself magical [Td:770-786]. The party catches breath on the move, enough that Nuberroca pulls a few points back from the earlier bruising [Td:792-798].

## 5 - A spoken command
_~12:30?_

1. Elodin spends a spell on Nuberroca at a glance. *"Cálmate, escúchame y acompáñame a la plaza hasta que aparezca Sinsong."* [Td:873-879, Td:893]. Nuberroca cannot shake the pull of it; the rage goes out of him and he walks on meekly, a half-zombie led by the elf's command [Td:884-905].
2. Mialee is the only one at the carts who sees the spell cast [Td:902-905]. The others see only that the barbarian stopped in his tracks and walks beside the elf like a servant [Td:905-907].
3. Mialee reads it and does not force the fight — the elf plainly has more than they could trade blows for in the open [Td:946-953]. She tells Nyx the plan: let Nuberroca walk with the elf to the plaza and bring him the beer up there, keep eyes on him the whole way [Td:949-957].

## 6 - Through the arch

1. Guards at the arch stop the first merchants and ask their business [Td:971-984]. Mialee gives them **Nyx**'s name — *"Soy Nyx, somos una caravana de comerciantes desde Baldur's Gate"* [Td:977-981]. Guards count the carts, do not press, wave them in [Td:994-998].
2. Eguzkilore stops at Darla's cart on the way through for the two large empty chests that had held her and the real Nyx [Td:1003-1025]. A guard asks what is in them; Eguzkilore gives the only honest answer available: *"no, están vacíos"* [Td:1022-1023]. The guard half-helps, half-shrugs, and waves him through [Td:1030-1033].
3. Inside the arch Janma presses up to Mialee one more time — the gold on the cart is for the Harpers and she does not trust Mialee to hand it off herself; she will walk with the cart to see the drop made [Td:1035-1053]. Mialee agrees to witnesses.
4. The caravan dissolves at the gate — each merchant collects what is owed and goes [Td:1054-1056].

## 10 - The elf slips the net

1. The sergeant cuffs Elodin, drops a net over him, and props him enough to stabilise but no further [Td:2317-2320]. Mialee walks the elf's great sword out of the arrest circle with an unseen hand along the ground [Td:2373-2383].
2. The elf comes round long enough to curse Sinsong and mutter a prayer to a name none of them catch, in a tongue none of them know, asking help in finishing the dwarf [Td:2362-2367].
3. ***[EPIC]*** He speaks three more words — *"Kal Ort Por"* — a thin white line opens in the air before him, swallows him whole, and closes behind him [Td:2487-2494]. The sergeant stares at the empty net on the ground [Td:2500-2502]. The party has seen that exit once already — the Red Wizard **Azbara Jos** had used it to leave the camp on the Daggerford road [Td:2496-2499].
4. The sergeant turns on them. A man has been killed in his square today, and the elf is the one he wants for it [Td:2559-2588]. Nuberroca accepts the question and tells him what he saw of Elodin — an elf, strong, hunting the dwarf by his cart; said he was his patron's man and would have the dwarf's blood [Td:2559-2574]. The sergeant takes the sword and takes the dead man and goes [Td:2581-2588].

## 11 - Far Hills

1. The guards leave the plaza. The party gathers in a side-street. Sinsong, down from the roof at last, opens the cloth bundle at his belt and unwraps a small dark stone — enough that the others can see, no further; nobody is to touch it. Fifteen days to turn to stone, no cure known. Half his village is as it was when he left [Td:2650-2663].
2. He gives the long version at last. The stone was turned up in a mine in the **Far Hills**. His master went to find a cure and touched the stone himself. Sinsong rode to **Candlekeep** to read; he rode to the elf hold at **Myth Drannor** and took the hospitality of a wizard called **Elodin**; between them they found a plant said to lift the curse. Elodin touched it experimenting and began to petrify himself. The next night Sinsong saw a figure in the dark cutting a deal with Elodin for his life. Elodin woke the next day cured — eyes red to the whites — and lit the room with a crack of purple energy as Sinsong fled. The brand on Sinsong's bald scalp dates from that night [Td:2664-2673].
3. Nyx admits she had searched his pack months ago, seen the stone, and so believed him. She did not touch it [Td:2675-2690].
4. Nuberroca takes a pinch of grit from his belt — the *arenilla* of Sinsong's own stone, the one he had destroyed under earlier watch — and lets it fall through his fingers like salt. *"Lo importante es conservar los amigos, Sinsong."* [Td:2706-2712]. Sinsong hands Kaelthar a platinum for the save in the fight and holds the thanks back from Nuberroca [Td:2602-2614 ?ord].
