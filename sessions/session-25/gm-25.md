# Session 25 — GM event log

## 1. Summary

1. **Caravan turned** — Order of the Gauntlet reveal and Mialee's "Heroes of Mansken" ruse swing the merchants to the party.
2. **Destination named** — Carnath Roadhouse past Waterdeep; cargo is for **Rezmir** the Black.
3. **Throats cut in the night** — Two bound captives killed cleanly on Nyx's watch; no witness.
4. **Sinsong hunted** — Elf tracker **Elodin** rides in asking after a dwarf with a tree-tattoo nape.

## 4. Events

1. Pass fight ends — lead cultist captured; Mialee's Heroes of Mansken ruse holds the caravan undecided.
2. Chests confirm Greenest loot; Nuberroca's Gauntlet reveal turns the caravan toward Waterdeep delivery.
3. Papers name Carnath Roadhouse as destination; wagon rebuilt, prisoners sorted.
4. ~16:00 — Interrogations name **Rezmir** as the black Lady Dragon; mercenary released; corpses cremated at dusk.
5. Long rest. Cold camp off the road; watch rotation set badly.
6. Two bound cultists found with throats cut at dawn; only one captive left alive.
7. ~13:00, 22 Nightal — Daggerford arrival; errands run; prisoner paraded; three old bandit prisoners cut loose.
8. Long rest. In Daggerford.
9. ~09:00, 23 Nightal — Caravan rolls out: Innevar sisters gone, two new strangers aboard; elf **Elodin** rides in seeking Sinsong.
