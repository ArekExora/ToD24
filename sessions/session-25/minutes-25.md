## 1 - Fight in the pass
_~14:00?, 21 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The caravan ambush is already running from the top of the session — the sabotaged wheel in the pass [Td:8-9], Kaelthar's dragon-flight onto the lead cultist wagon [Td:10], Nuberroca's polearm through the first guard [Td:19], Mialee's fog cloud over the carts [Td:25], chalices already bloodied inside the chests [Td:30], three cultists unconscious [Td:26]; Kaelthar has dropped once and Amidamaru brought him round [Td:44]. Sinsong is off-screen north chasing the Company of the Boar [Td:72-75]; Nyx is at the table but not yet in the fight herself [Td:80].
2. Nuberroca flashes a short arc behind the near wagon for cover [Td:77]. **Sulesdeg** — the caravan's captain, who tried to arrest him unarmed before the fight tipped over into bloodshed [Td:99-102] — now has his sword out and is swinging to kill; the standing cultist works the surviving caravan guards against the party, shouting that these adventurers are robbers who have murdered his companions [Td:108-110]. A crossbow bolt catches Nuberroca at the lip of his cover [Td:113-118].
3. Amidamaru reads Sulesdeg coming through to finish Kaelthar on the ground and steps across to catch the captain's wrist — a hand on the arm, *"please, bear with me,"* no blow [Td:188-191]. Sulesdeg pushes through regardless; he throws himself across Kaelthar, pins him down [Td:192-194], and snaps a pair of irons on his wrists [Td:217]. Kaelthar tries to answer with a tail-strike from under the knee but the angle is wrong and it goes nowhere [Td:250, Td:277].
4. **Eldkin** — the dwarven guard who travels with **Lai** [Td:278-280, Td:348] — advances on Nuberroca with a second pair of irons in her hand. Nuberroca swings as she enters his reach but the strike skates past her [Td:282, Td:313]. He swaps the old polearm for a halberd at his hip in the breath between swings — a longer reach and heavier dice [Td:289-299].
5. Mialee has climbed up on one of the carts. She throws down a convincing-looking parchment — an illusion of a mandate sealed with the gauntlet of **Ontharr** — and calls out that they are the Heroes of Mansken on a sanctioned mission against the raiders of Greenest and Greenfields [Td:128-144 ?ord]. Her unseen hand works the lid of the nearest chest to show the plunder; the lid comes up on rations [Td:144-164]. The lead cultist takes the opening — *"they're liars, they're deceiving you with illusions"* [Td:166-169] — and the caravan guards hold, undecided [Td:170-173].
6. Mialee moves on the lead cultist and takes him to the ground in a grapple [Td:329-338]; she sets a dagger to his throat and tells him to confess — a hard enough look at him that he visibly flinches, afraid [Td:435-443]. Her unseen hand lifts the lid of the other chest while she speaks — jewellery, chalices, religious icons in precious metal [Td:454-460].
7. On the other flank Nyx goes to Lai and asks her to come help calm the fight [Td:347-357], then crosses behind Eldkin and takes her to the ground — Nuberroca steps in to hold her from the other side and the pair of them get the grapple cleanly [Td:359-375]. Nuberroca unspools the rope out of his pack and winds Eldkin flat on the stones [Td:400-408]; Nyx murmurs that it will not be long, soft words close by her ear [Td:409-412].

## 2 - The chests, the pendant and a corpse count

1. The fight ends with the lead cultist pinned under Mialee's dagger and Eldkin on her face in the dirt. Sulesdeg finally comes off Kaelthar, lifts him up but keeps him in hand [Td:488-490], then unlocks the irons on his wrists [Td:505], pushes past him without a word, and goes to Lai to swear quietly at her about what has just happened [Td:506-509]. On the way past, Amidamaru closes on him and lays a hand on his back — the life goes out of the captain's eyes for a long breath and comes back, and he keeps walking as furious as before [Td:500-504].
2. **Beyd**'s instruction is that the party should prove the goods are what they say [Td:570-572]. Sulesdeg sets his sword into the lid of one of the chests and splits it open; what spills is what the party has been saying — chalices, religious icons in precious metal, bags of stones prised from the sockets of temple statues [Td:582-586], a few pieces smeared with old blood [Td:593-597]. The four lead merchants — **Lai**, **Edhelri**, **Noohar**, and Beyd — pull apart from the rest and talk it over [Td:538, Td:600-606].
3. Beyd's first proposal, overheard, is to bury the cultists on the road and share the plunder among the surviving merchants and guards [Td:605-606]. Amidamaru cuts in — the gold has to go back to the villages it was taken from, through the Order of the Gauntlet; Nyx steps up behind him with the same line — they have tracked these cultists all the way from Greenest and will not see the loot broken up at the roadside [Td:607-625].
4. ***[EPIC]*** As Amidamaru's words carry — *the Order of the Gauntlet* — Nuberroca stands tall and lets his frame open to full goliath size behind him; he hauls the furs off his shoulders and shows the great fist blazoned on the breastplate underneath [Td:629-633].
5. Nuberroca then rounds on the merchants — they ran when the Company of the Boar had fallen upon the caravan, left the party holding the line, and now they want to split the plunder of the dead between them [Td:640-643]. Mialee opens the door a crack to gifting the humble family a coin out of the general stock, but no more than that [Td:634-646]. Lai sides with the party against the carve-up — pack the chests into two carts, abandon the third for parts, run the whole load to Waterdeep, and let the city's justice resolve the ownership [Td:654-655].
6. Amidamaru goes through the dead cultists' pockets. There is nothing of note on them beyond their arms, except that one of them is wearing a dragon pendant — the full-dragon style the party has pulled off cult bodies before, not the kobold version [Td:511-528 ?ord]. He pockets it. Searching the wagons for papers he turns up a note that reads *the destination is Carnath*; the party learns the name is a roadhouse along the road past Waterdeep [Td:712-721]. Mialee adds that she has *Castle Naerytar* down in her own notes from somewhere earlier, though no one in the party can place where [Td:725-740]. Amidamaru also prises three or four rubies and small stones from the loot for his own scroll work [Td:684-692].
7. The practical work takes the rest of the daylight. Amidamaru patches the broken family wagon out of parts cannibalised from the one being abandoned [Td:681, Td:751-752]. Nuberroca brings the humble family forward — **Gus** and **Darla**, with **Anton** travelling with them — and moves their wagon to third position so he can keep eyes on both the family and the cultists at once [Td:1603-1614]. **Paco**, a caravan guard who fell in the fight, is laid out at one side; Amidamaru closes his eyes and covers him with a blanket [Td:530], then shapes a small glowing stone to mark where he lies [Td:1644-1652]. Nuberroca — still large from the breastplate moment — heaps the three cultist dead in a pile and sets the surviving suspect propped against one of the corpses, *"we'll talk later, criminal"* [Td:659-668]. The three cultist dead are cremated with Kaelthar's dragon-breath, so nothing comes back out of the mountain at night [Td:1635-1640].

## 3 - A cultist in the ground
_~17:00?, 21 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The party splits into two groups to work on the four live captives. Nuberroca, Mialee, and Eguzkilore take the one Mialee had on the ground — the one the cultists had followed into the fight — out past the carts [Td:826-868]; Amidamaru, Kaelthar, and Nyx take the other three to the opposite side [Td:869-912].
2. Eguzkilore digs a pit and the three of them put the lead cultist in it up to the neck, blindfolded with a strip torn off his own shirt [Td:925-942]. Nuberroca lies down face to face with him and asks whether he would rather speak now and die with honour or die like the rat he is [Td:972-976]. The cultist's answer comes level: *"Tiamat's kingdom will come soon, and my sacrifices will not be in vain. I have no fear of death"* [Td:989-991].
3. Nuberroca lets the fury take him so he can lean on the thing with his strength instead of his words, and leans in harder — but the cultist's fanaticism holds against fear and intimidation both; the man answers only with the long speech of his order — the world will be a better place under the dragons [Td:1017-1038, Td:1049-1052]. Nuberroca plants the halberd a few inches from the cultist's face: *"you are this close to your goddess, right now"* [Td:1041-1045]. The cultist does not break.
4. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee sets a small wooden chest down on the ground beside the cultist's head and conjures an illusion out of it — four scorpions, clambering over the rim and walking toward his bound-eyed face [Td:1350-1360]. Nyx, as the illusion carries them in, lays a sound of her own under it — the dry rasp of claws on gravel, so close the cultist feels it on his cheek [Td:1344, Td:1356]. The cultist breaks. The caravan's destination was always to cross Waterdeep and go on a little further to the Carnath Roadhouse [Td:1365-1366]; the goods were for the Lady Dragon [Td:1373-1375], whose colour he gives as black [Td:1387].

## 4 - The pendant trick

1. On the other side of the camp Amidamaru hangs the cultists' dragon pendant around his own neck, slaps the three awake, and casts a sway across all three of them at once; two shake it off, the third — the sergeant — settles into looking at Amidamaru like a friend [Td:1089-1092]. Amidamaru leans into the role, pendant forward, and asks the sergeant who he is supposed to meet at the Carnath Roadhouse [Td:1125-1137].
2. The sergeant has nothing to give him. He is not a cultist at all — a mercenary hired out of Baldur's Gate to run with the caravan as far as Waterdeep [Td:1170-1183], no questions asked about who paid him or what the cargo was [Td:1183-1189]. He has watched his companions die, he does not want to go the same way, and he asks only to be released and allowed to walk to the next town on his own feet [Td:1197-1216]. The sense of him sits sincere; the party lets him go [Td:1210-1214].
3. Nyx takes the last of the three aside and asks him in a low voice what he can offer for his life [Td:1505-1527]. His answers come back the same as his companion's — the goods are for the Lady Dragon [Td:1537] — and he adds a name for her the cultist chokes out as something close to **Rezmir**, colour black [Td:1537-1542]. She asks whether they are guarding sacred eggs for the lady; the cultist tells her no, he would consider that an enormous honour [Td:1551-1557]. She slaps him once across the face and leaves it there [Td:1571-1574].

## 5 - First light and a cut throat
_~05:00?, 22 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The caravan agrees, with some reluctance, to keep moving with the party; they can tell from the way the merchants look at them now that something has shifted [Td:1660-1665]. The convoy is too far behind to reach the next town before dark and makes a cold camp off the road halfway in [Td:1683-1686]. The watch is organised badly — Amidamaru first, Nyx second, Eguzkilore for the long end, Nuberroca and Mialee not actually on the rota at all because the decanting of hours did not get worked through properly [Td:1715-1717, Td:1981-1992].
2. The party wakes to find the cultists tied behind the carts dead in their bindings — throats cut, cleanly done, only one of the three left alive [Td:1749-1752, Td:1783-1788, Td:1796-1797]. The survivor is woken by the discovery and panics at the sight of his dead companions; Nuberroca backhands him demanding what he saw and the man swears, shaking, that he knows nothing [Td:1783-1792]. Nuberroca puts his old road training on the bodies and reads the cold at about five hours earlier — Nyx's watch, by the counting-back [Td:1946-1955].
3. Nyx walks the ground around the cart as the camp wakes. She can read the trace of someone who came up on the carts well before her patrol passed, and the patrol tracks that walked across those tracks afterwards — her own, going by — but the ground is bare stone and the trail does not carry beyond that [Td:1864-1872]. Kaelthar casts his sense out for any undead or celestial lingering nearby and finds nothing [Td:1824-1829]. Nobody saw anything on any of the four watches.
4. Mialee goes around the camp while people are breaking fast, moving five feet above the ground, looking for blood on anyone's clothes [Td:1893]. She finds a smear on the sole of Eguzkilore's shoe — a single mark, nowhere else on him [Td:1894-1898]. He claims he must have stepped in it while looking at the body. The sense of him sits uncertain; she lets it rest [Td:1902-1909].

## 6 - Daggerford
_~09:00?, 22 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The caravan makes Daggerford four hours in [Td:2035], where the merchants settle to lose the day in town and set out again the next morning [Td:2050-2062]. Nuberroca has the surviving cultist tied against his chest facing outwards all the way in — like the Inca babies in a sling, so the prisoner has to look at everything Nuberroca looks at — and keeps him there into the tavern, drinking in his face [Td:2103-2124, Td:2131-2137].
2. The errands move quickly. Eguzkilore buys two dozen arrows at the general stall; Amidamaru fills Mialee's order for ten blocks of incense and takes a few extra for his own work; Nuberroca asks after rope and Nyx hands him hers off her belt — she has plenty [Td:2087-2103, Td:2158-2167]. Amidamaru also asks after a smith in town for a commission he intends to settle by message [Td:2214-2217].
3. Nyx passes Eguzkilore the magical longbow she has carried since the forest god crossed their path [GM, Td:2176-2204] and takes his ordinary longbow in exchange; neither of them makes a fuss about it, the trade is just quietly done over a drink.

## 7 - The elf at the gate
_~09:00?, 23 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. In the morning the caravan goes out light. The noble with his mage and his guard are stopping in Daggerford for their own business [Td:2229-2231]; the hired sergeant from the night before has walked off before the caravan rolled out [Td:2231-2232]; and the two sisters — **Arietta** and **Zelina** [GM] — are gone. Nobody saw them leave [Td:2277]. Nobody can say when they last did [Td:2294-2300]. Nuberroca jokes that it might have been the sisters who did the cultist in the night [Td:2301-2302].
2. There are also two new faces in the caravan where the departures leave gaps. A small gnome woman with knives sewn into the folds of her clothes who moves fluidly and watches the party — Nuberroca particularly, with his strapped prisoner — with open attention [Td:2234, Td:2257-2263]. And a man dressed in black, hooded, who speaks to no one and travels alone [Td:2240-2244].
3. ***[EPIC]*** As the carts are rolling out an elf comes down the road toward them at a hard gallop. He is tattooed on the face and has knives threaded through the stitching of his clothing — not drawn, kept — and asks the Daggerford traffic quickly as he rides, person by person, about a dwarf [Td:2323-2328]. He stops at Lai and Eldkin: *"I'm looking for a dwarf like you. He has a mark here — a tree tattooed on the nape of his neck"* [Td:2334-2335]. He turns to the great blue goliath with the human bound to his chest and hesitates [Td:2337-2339].
4. Nuberroca lifts the strapped prisoner by way of answer: *"a dwarf? This is one!"* The elf answers flatly, *"no, that's a human. And you are strange,"* and then, *"I'm looking for a dwarf. He thinks he's a god. He calls himself Sinsong"* [Td:2340-2351]. The name catches. Nuberroca grins and shows a scar: *"this scar — he healed it for me."* The elf: *"and didn't he give it to you first? He's a bastard"* [Td:2355-2360]. Nuberroca invites him to ride with the caravan: Sinsong, last seen on the back of a chicken, will probably turn up [Td:2362-2365]. The elf wheels his horse away: if the party finds him, tell him **Elodin** is looking for him [Td:2367-2374]. He rides out at the gallop he came in on, and the caravan follows the road north.
