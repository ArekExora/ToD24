# Session 40 - BACKTRACKED {{float:"right" 2026-02-22}}
{wide}

## 1 - Morning on the dead island
_~08:00, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party has slept on a small half-dry island in the swamp beside the mound of carnomorph corpses they killed overnight. **Chikonaui** lies dead on the ground beside them, one hand loose, throat cut with his own blade; a half-formed wing sprouting from his back.
2. From the south, **several Metagorgers** push across the water toward the island. Behind them walks a figure bigger than Nuberroca — arms set the wrong way, ribs opened backwards, a tangle of bone and muscle with huge claws — a **Shambler**. The water around them seethes with smaller bulges — **Boneleechers** surfacing.
3. Nuberroca walks back to **Juancho**, his lizard mount killed the night before, and gathers a ragged bouquet from the swamp weeds. He lays the flowers across the lizard's head and tells him he was a brave mount who could carry his weight. Noticing a twitch, he presses one of Nyx's berries into Juancho's mouth; Sinsong lays both hands on the lizard and Juancho half-rises, slow but alive.
4. The party loads into the lizardfolk boats, leaving the corpses of **Nenetl** and Chikonaui on the island. Juancho is lifted onto one boat, **Atascaburras** swims alongside.
5. Sinsong moves between the boats laying a hand on each of them in turn — party and lizardfolk alike — his bald head shining bright; he tells them they can now walk over the water. The spell lasts for an hour but Sinsong keeps renewing it as they travel.
6. Half an hour out the carnomorphs reach the island but do not pursue — they stop over the corpses and start to feed. From a distance the party watches the creatures eat, grow and evolve into new shapes.
7. During the two hours' travel Nuberroca works on reducing the pain of his grave wounds, with the help of Nyx's berries he manages it.

## 2 - The castle in view
_~10:00, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The castle rises from the swamp as they close — stone, large, old, its wooden roofs mostly staved in and most of the windows either broken or boarded over. A moat rings the whole site. From the west they can distinguish three groups of buildings around it: a stockaded enclosure to the south, two large buildings in the centre outside the walls, and a cluster of mud-and-filth hovels to the left.
2. Closing on the landing they realise what the patrols mean: **bullywugs and lizardfolk are both working here, together**. Nobody raises an alarm as the boats pull in — nobody seems surprised at them arriving at all.
3. Sinsong and Eguzkilore pull cultist robes from the pack and shrug them on.

## 3 - At the shore

1. Sinsong steps off the boat and walks north across the open water toward a corpse half-sunk by the shore. The bullywugs and lizardfolk in sight pay the party little mind — but the dwarf walking on water turns every head on the quay.
2. Nuberroca asks Atzi and Tompu what the mixed camp means — _"how is it that there are lizardfolk here working with the frogs?"_ The two of them answer that it is just as they had already warned him: in Naerytar, lizardfolk and bullywugs coexist, and that is exactly why they wanted the bullywugs gone. Atzi and Tompu themselves will fight; the rest of the clan might not — they obey **Dralmorrer**, and above him **Rezmir**, _the dragon lady_.
3. Nuberroca presses: if the party kills the bullywugs, will Rezmir not come down on the clan? Yes — _"so we have to finish quickly and restore order before she finds out and gets angry"_. And if the party kills Rezmir instead? _"Rezmir es buena"_ — the lizardfolk will not help against her. Nuberroca argues it anyway: if she is strong and the party kills her, then the party is stronger still. The lizardfolk reply that it would make them evil.
4. Nuberroca asks for three plans with a requirement: _"uno de los planes tiene que ser violento"_. Sinsong will slip in alone, Nyx will take Atzi and Tompu through the lizardfolk camp to look for sympathisers. The last plan — violence — is held in reserve.

## 4 - The woman at the fence

1. Eguzkilore spots a figure moving at the stockade line to the south. Nuberroca rides Juancho down with the elf behind him and the lizardfolk circling, and they find a woman crouched against the sticks of the enclosure — elf-cut ears, pale skin, Eguzkilore's height, dressed like a court jester in patchwork of too many colours and a smile from ear to ear. She says she is watching for a way into the castle.
2. Nuberroca introduces himself with chest-flexing and ridiculous flirtation; the woman answers in kind, the colourful patches rising on her chest. She is here because her mother said her father is in the castle — _"bald, dressed often in red"_ — and she has come to find him. The warhammer she carries weighs only five pounds; she balances it on her finger when Nuberroca asks and admits she rarely swings it — her friends usually fight for her.
3. She asks if she can come with them. Nuberroca and Eguzkilore accept, provisional — _"if you don't catch up and we are not fighting, fine; if we are killing things, join in"_. She slips off to fetch her friends from the forest to the south.

## 5 - Nyx surveys the pavilions

1. Nyx takes Atzi and Tompu into the lizardfolk camp. The clean cane pavilions belong to the lizardfolk; the filth-and-stick hovels above them are the bullywugs'. She tucks her Harpers pendant deeper under the cloak and lets the grime of the road sell her as another unimportant adventurer.
2. Three lizardfolk at the pavilions wave the group off and go back inside their huts. Two words come through the exchange — _explorer_ for Nyx's group, _guards_ for the lizardfolk themselves. The lizardfolk here speak **Draconic**, not Common. Nyx breaks off to find Kaelthar to translate.
3. Meanwhile Sinsong, briefly stopped by the lizardfolk guards after his water-walk and watched by curious bullywugs, is let go — unable to understand him, they move away and go back to their huts.

## 6 - The Sinsong stone

1. The woman — **Harley** — rejoins Nuberroca and Eguzkilore at the fence. Sinsong presses a Sinsong stone into her hand, _"for membership you need this,"_ and lays his other hand on her shoulder, bare skin to bare skin. Nuberroca steps between them and Eguzkilore so the ranger does not see, and warns Sinsong with a half-smile — _"cuidado, Sinsong, en mi aldea matamos por menos"_.
2. Harley presses on the party's size: she asks whether they are only four. Sinsong gestures at the empty air behind them and tells her the party is as many as her own friends. She says she likes it and goes to fetch them. The moment she is out of hearing Nuberroca turns to the others and tells them it is a trap; Sinsong says he will bolt the moment she says to wait.

## 7 - The first scream
_~10:30, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Nuberroca rides Juancho into the large-lizards' corral; the other mounts spook and he hides Juancho to one side, then approaches the keeper alone. He asks about buying a lizard — _"yo comprar uno. Oro"_ — and the keeper offers a straight trade in metal weapons, mounts for arms. Nuberroca shows a short sword as a sample; the keeper freezes at the blade, fascinated, and reaches for it with hands up. Mialee notes aloud that she has one just like it; the keeper fixates on hers.
2. The clan hands no weapons away for mere crocodile teeth, Tompu translating — _"we're not risking our lives for trinkets"_.
3. A scream cuts out of the castle — a death-scream in a human voice. Tompu is still with the party; Nuberroca turns on him and tells him to go back and press the lizardfolk again.
4. Tompu returns with the news that this time the lizardfolk shouted him down as a traitor and talked about reporting him to Dralmorrer. Pressed, Tompu admits he carries orders from Dralmorrer and from _the dragon lady_ — and that those orders include obeying the bullywugs. _"We're going to have to go to the last plan"_.

## 8 - Harley's friends

1. Harley returns with her friends — and the party catches on at twenty paces that they all walk _wrong_, limping like puppets with slack strings. Kaelthar is back with Nyx and the lizardfolk now, the only translator the group has.
2. ***[EPIC]*** Harley names them for Nuberroca one by one — Mili, Pili, Leo, Gilgamesh, Tintón, Nisong, Romanum, Rodolfo, and lastly Paco, picked up on the road here. Nuberroca says only the ones with flesh on them are his sort; the rest can earn his liking by saving his life. Harley takes no offence.
3. Nuberroca pulls Nyx and Mialee aside and gives them a standing order: _"si salen lagartos de este corral, asesinadlos"_.

## 9 - The gate

1. A second death-scream reaches them from inside the castle — this one a man's voice fading. Tompu says it is probably a prisoner — bullywugs and lizardfolk here do not kill each other, and four prisoners were brought in recently.
2. Nyx and Mialee sneak up to the castle gate. The outer door is a battered wooden pair; the right leaf is wedged dead, the left can be shoved open; above them an old iron portcullis hangs so rusted a lay reader could believe it ready to drop by its own weight. Mialee cracks the left door, puts her head in, says _"hola"_ to a hall of bullywugs who ignore her, and pulls it shut again.
3. Sinsong glides down from above the walls and lands beside Nyx and Mialee at the gate; two bullywugs lean out of the shooting slits above and knock on them to peer further out. The three slip into the bushes and the bullywugs lose them. A crocodile breaks the moat beside them going for the frogs on the wall and sinks back under.
4. A third scream reaches them, this one a woman, from inside the castle.
5. From the gatehouse a bullywug sage with a two-handed staff steps out, lays a hand on the thickest bush, and starts to chant — seconds later the branches peel open and the three of them stand suddenly in daylight with no cover left.
6. The sage barks an order and bullywug guards sweep out of the gate to form up around him. Nyx tries to walk away calmly; nobody stops her. Mialee makes a point of bowing — the sage looks at her with open contempt and keeps giving orders.

## 10 - Teach me to fly

1. The sage grabs Sinsong by the hand and demands, in a flat impatient voice, to be taught to fly at once. Sinsong plays — training takes time, the first step is this stone, he presses a Sinsong stone into the sage's palm. The sage squints at it, checks whether it is a magic focus, decides it is only a stone — but Sinsong insists it is sacred.
2. The cleric states the ritual needs a quiet room and asks for a tour; the sage refuses to take him into _the magic room_, which _warm-blooded can't enter_ — but there is a chamber upstairs where the two of them will not be disturbed. Sinsong adds that he will need Nuberroca along — _"he is a loyal follower, and as the future god I am, I need people who believe in me for this"_. The sage shrugs and leads them in.
3. Harley's undead follow at a wave; the bullywug guards at the hall part for them more than for anyone else.

## 11 - Ambush on the drum
_~11:00, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The upper chamber is open to the sky — an old stone platform heaped with piles of fist-sized stones for throwing, and in the centre a wide skin drum used to call the castle to arms. The sage walks Sinsong to the middle and tells him to begin.
2. ***[EPIC]*** Sinsong lies face down — hands so, like this. Lie on your belly. _"Mía, ataca"_.
3. Mialee's blade comes up with a poison on it; Nuberroca comes in reckless, polearm up, and strikes. The sage lurches to his feet, swings the staff down at Sinsong still lying on his belly, and misses, then throws a ray from his hand that goes wide as well. Behind him the bullywugs already in the gatehouse start up the stairs.

## 12 - The drum sounds

1. Sinsong steps up onto the drum and sets a sphere of radiant light around himself — picking Harley into it and leaving the undead out. He stomps the drum to raise the castle's alarm — the sage ordered it earlier but never got to drum it himself.
2. Nyx catches the moving bullywug champion mid-stride with an arrow, then closes on the sage and strikes him down without killing him. Mialee double-checks that nobody is dying; the sage is already finished — he slumps alive next to the drum.
3. Harley stations one of her undead — Paco — next to her and sends the rest down the stairs to meet the bullywugs climbing up.

## 13 - The waves on the stairs

1. Bullywugs launch themselves from the ground floor straight through the arrow slits in enormous leaps — their jump carries them clean through — while the lizardfolk below start throwing javelins up through the same slits. Eguzkilore and Mialee hold the inside stairs; Nyx covers the slits; Nuberroca holds the lower landing with Harley and her remaining skeletons.
2. Nyx runs out of arrows. Mialee pulls ten out of her own quiver and sends them floating across the room with a conjured hand to Nyx. Harley commands the rest of her undead to _keep cleaning frogs_, which they understand.
3. In wave after wave the party drops the bullywug sage's underlings — rapiers, javelins, bite-and-grab — and Nuberroca swaps his polearm for a crossbow when the stairs clog. Through the arrow slit they can see the bullywug compound outside emptying — some thirty of them moving across the ground toward the gate.

## 14 - The green bomb

1. A first bullywug mage casts a green sphere of gas and acid among the fighters on the lower floor. Harley's zombie takes the full dose; most of the bullywug skirmishers around it fall.
2. A second mage leaps straight through one of the slits onto the platform and tries another ray from his staff. Nuberroca wades in, polearm swinging, and lands two swings; the mage falls to Eguzkilore's follow-up shot moments later.
3. A third mage further back casts the green-bomb spell again, this time overlapping his own side's first mage; the party dodges most of the acid, the first mage does not and drops; a smear of the acid clings to Eguzkilore and keeps eating at him over the next moments. Harley cuts off one more casting mid-syllable with a counter of her own — the mage stares at his staff in confusion.
4. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee calls over the lip of the battlement in broken common to the lizardfolk below: _"¡muerte a los Bullywugs! ¡muerte a Pharblex!"_ — the only words of it they understand being _bullywug_ and _Pharblex_. Tompu, who has drifted back into the lizardfolk crowd below, finds his own appeal to the clan carrying farther from the moment the shout lands.
