## 1 - Amidamaru's rage
_~20:00, 25 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. With the combat still raging around her, Mialee casts an arcane message, north-east towards the family wagon, and the answer comes from somewhere inside. She dashes into that wagon, slips behind the family's trunks, and prises the biggest chest open: **Darla** is curled inside, unresponsive, immobile.
2. Nyx will not drop the act. She shouts at the party that their false companion will not survive if they harm her. The false Darla slams two heavy blows into Nuberroca.
3. Bound to obey Sinsong's Command from before, the halfling pushes toward him and passes within reach of Eguzkilore — he takes the opening, grapples her forcefully, then punches her in the face, demanding to know where she has their friend.
4. Amidamaru goes to her with a healing hand on the forehead to keep her upright, then — eyes closed — presses the muzzle of his pistol against her leg and fires.
5. The halfling screams in pain, but holds the lie: she IS Nyx, there is a fake Nyx out there impersonating her, and she will not say where unless they let her go. Behind her, Sinsong holds the spirits he had conjured around himself, and when the halfling walks through them she is torn open again.
6. Mialee works at the second big chest with her unseen hand — the lock gives quietly — and finds the real **Nyx** inside: curled, half-dressed, barely reacting. She gives her a sip of water; Nyx stirs but does not come round.
7. Nuberroca swings recklessly on Darla, dropping her unconscious, then flares across the camp in a step of cloud to come down beside the false Nyx and strikes at her too.
8. The doppelganger strikes a brutal counter-blow back — a long tear across the barbarian's front. **Enki**, the cultist strapped to his chest, takes the worst of it; the man's head opens, brains and blood on Nuberroca's own front.
9. Nyx, still bleeding, pleads: *"dejadme en paz, malditos bastardos, yo solo quiero vivir"*. Kaelthar reaches out with his will and the halfling goes rigid, paralysed where she stands.
10. ***[EPIC]*** After hearing that Mialee found Nyx, Amidamaru puts the pistol to the doppelganger's forehead, and fires at point-blank. Her head bursts. The halfling shape loses hold and swells into a loose, human-sized form, with dark-blue skin, features unformed, the clothes straining at the seams.

## 2 - Two drugged women

1. Amidamaru lifts the real Nyx out of the chest and carries her outside.
2. Kaelthar picks Darla up, marks her against mistaken identity, and carries her out separately on his wolf.
3. Neither woman has any visible wound; both read as drugged, not poisoned.
4. Kaelthar lays hands on Nyx to remove poisons from her body, but she has none.
5. Sinsong offers a prayer that *might* reach her — he needs the faith of the whole group — and asks that the party accept his stones and his cause before he tries. The party will not, and the offer dies there.
6. Mialee stands over Nyx and reaches for any magic held on her, finding none.
7. She whispers across into Nyx's mind and asks what happened: the halfling replies that she noted a prick, could not move after that, and that whoever held her has been injecting her every day.
8. Mialee tells the others and explains that the only cure she can see is waiting it out.
9. With the false Darla clamped in shackles, Mialee wipes the dead doppelganger clean with her magic [Td:633-634 ?ord] and retrieves Nyx's belongings to give to her when she is awake enough to take them, among the halfling's stuff, she finds an odd thimble ending in a sharp point.
10. The question of **Gus** comes up. Nuberroca wants him raised, Kaelthar offers to protect the body from decay until a priest can be found, and the party weighs the price of calling him back. Nuberroca lets the idea go when the cost sits poorly against a stranger's debt.
11. Mialee takes Gus's ring and clothes to keep for Darla before his body goes to the ground.
12. The camp is shaken. People are gathered around the body of the doppelganger and the merchants have run out of questions once they see it.
13. The caravan sends dinner and beer across to the party in thanks, more open-handed than usual, and **Beyd** stands them to the drinks himself.
14. The watches are posted before retreating to the bedrolls.

## 3 - A knife in the dark
_~08:00, 26 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. By morning Darla's shackles are empty on the ground; not forced, simply slipped. 
2. By the chest wagon lies **Carlon** the Harper, dead on the ground, a single puncture in the back from behind. The party recalls that the doppelganger worked with its fists; this is someone else.
3. The question of who was on watch snaps around the camp — Mialee is first to voice it, *"Eguzkilore, again?"*, and the elf bristles that he was watching.
4. **Eldkin** comes over and examines the body — the man was leaning into the treasure wagon when it was done — then turns her attention openly to Mialee and the short sword hanging from her belt.
5. **Miguel** and **Fenwir** are already saying it aloud to the other guards: the elf stabbed Carlon to keep the treasure; these adventurers won't hand a coin to the **Order of the Gauntlet**. Mialee works the crowd but finds the murmur unled — nobody is directing it.
6. Nuberroca, Eguzkilore, and Kaelthar go to track the doppelganger helped by Kaelthar's wolf.
7. Beside where Darla's shackles lay Eguzkilore picks up tiny prints — gnome or halfling — that four or five paces further become full human prints running for the trees [Td:926-937 ?ord]. They follow into the wood but Nuberroca cannot hold the trail and they lose it.
8. Nuberroca comes back furious. Mialee points him at **Miguel** for spreading that he killed Carlon.
9. Nuberroca goes at him, but Miguel — scared — denies it, the small-blade wound could not have been a greatsword-carrying goliath.
10. Mialee pivots: not Miguel, **Fenwir**. Nuberroca is at Fenwir's face next — but Fenwir flips the story: the guards suspect *Mialee*, her short sword fits the wound, and she has turned the goliath on them to peel him off her own trail.
11. The hooded man who travels alone — until this moment silent — speaks up for the first time: if there are no witnesses, let the gods take vengeance on the guilty.
12. Nyx, able to move but still in the drug's hold, adds that she is alive because Mialee spotted the doppelganger. The guards drop it and resume their posts.
13. On the way back to his wagon Kaelthar passes the two tied captives — **Cheko** and **Mane** — and his wolf's tail swats them in the face as he goes.
14. Carlon is buried by the roadside before the wheels roll. The caravan starts moving towards Waterdeep, and the day on the road passes without incident.

## 4 - Jamna the Harper
_~08:00, 27 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The next morning the caravan has Waterdeep in sight on the horizon, about two days from the gates.
2. The party argues what to do now that no cultists remain to follow — Mialee proposes posing as cultists and running the carts the rest of the way themselves, but a goliath, a tortle, and a dragonborn make poor cover for a cult caravan.
3. Over breakfast Mialee goes forward to **Jamna** — the small gnome from Daggerford — and apologises for Sinsong's suspicion the day before. Jamna remembers her and asks how she can help.
4. Jamna lays it out: they have different bosses but the same objectives. She was embedded in the caravan to pick out the Cult of the Dragon operators, and the party has already done that work for her.
5. About Naerytar — she has heard it may be in the Mere of Dead Men, from an older reference tying the castle to the Order of the Astrologers' holdings there.
6. Jamna assumes the party is Order of the Gauntlet; Nyx names only Nuberroca and Kaelthar for the Gauntlet, and Nuberroca pulls his best attempt at a smile.
7. Jamna cycles through options, lands on Harpers; Mialee's wink confirms, and Jamna withdraws her opening line — same organisation, different cells. She confirms **Leosin** as the common handler; Mialee's read is that Jamna is being straight with them.
8. The plan: when the caravan reaches Waterdeep in two days, Jamna will take the chests on to Leosin and the Order of the Gauntlet with a positive report of the party.
9. The party should go straight for the north gate and head on to the **Carnath Roadhouse** — a roadhouse on the way to Neverwinter which Jamna believes is the cultists' handover point, and which is seeing new foot traffic because **Lord Neverember** is rebuilding Neverwinter after a catastrophe.
10. Her sources have not been able to confirm the handover directly. Beyond Carnath they always lose the thread.

## 5 - A pocketed heart
_~14:00, 27 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The party's attention lands on the hooded figure from Daggerford — the one who had spoken for them the day before.
2. Nyx walks the caravan apologising to the merchants about her strange behaviour the previous days — it had not been her, she tells them.
3. Using the crowd as cover, Mialee works an ethereal hand into the hooded man's pocket from afar.
4. The pocket holds an insane weight of coins. Below the coins she finds something fist-sized, soft, that gives under her fingers; a small pouch with pellets inside; and two folded scraps of parchment.
5. She lifts one of the parchments on the hand up over the crowd. The man is beginning to notice.
6. Mialee cleans the hand off when it comes back. They whisper across to Nyx about the fist-sized thing: it is a heart — too big for a halfling, perhaps human, perhaps pig.
7. They go to Jamna with what they have. She shares her knowledge: Red Wizards of Thay are usually bald and tattooed, and the daggers they carry leave wounds no magic will close. She cannot place the heart.
8. If this really is **Azbara Jos** — a name the party has never heard — then he is one of the most powerful wizards alive, and the party is to be very careful of him.
9. Nuberroca presses the group for a reason to attack if they are not sure he is the villain; the party does not commit.

## 6 - The blue lamp legacy
_~19:00, 27 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. Later on the road Nuberroca pulls Nyx, Mialee, and Eguzkilore aside, out of Jamna's earshot, and tells them a secret.
2. Since **Lord Zelan's** blue lamp magic hurt his head and showed him the vision of his village turned undead, something has been changing in him.
3. ***[EPIC]*** He opens his fist. The veins across his hand turn from blue to black; a dark halo of smoke peels off the knuckles. He bends and brushes a wildflower by the roadside with the back of his hand and the flower blackens, withers, and crumbles.
4. He tells them he does not know what it is. Something guides his anger now, and when Sinsong once put a note on his back something inside him pushed out and tried to tear it off.
5. Mialee pats him on the shoulder and feels him for any magic held or lingering — nothing.
6. Eguzkilore tells Nuberroca that when they reach Waterdeep he should have someone look at it.
7. Nuberroca agrees and names the darkness in himself as something he does not like.
8. The next morning, the caravan keeps moving, and the day on the road passes without major events.
9. That night, with Waterdeep in sight and only a few hours march left to cover, the caravan prepares to sleep.

## 7 - Torch in the fog
_~06:00, 29 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. Before dawn breaks, while Nyx and Eguzkilore have the watch, a torch comes toward the camp through a thick fog.
2. Eguzkilore reads the weather — the coming days are to be clear, which makes the fog around them stranger.
3. He steps out of the arc of light and calls out, for the last time, who goes there. He cannot see anything beyond the torch itself.
4. Nyx takes a ray of cold in the back from the direction of the torch and sees roughly where it came from.
5. Eguzkilore wheels and runs for the sleeping camp — a long, hard dash to the sleepers to shake them awake.
6. On his way through the camp he passes two bodies on the ground — no time to stop and look closer.
7. Nyx casts a spell to mark the torch holder with fey light, allowing them to know the shape in the fog.
8. The mage throws three ribbons of fire at Nyx — all three find their mark, leaving the halfling struggling.
9. Mialee climbs up onto Nuberroca's shoulder to get a line on the mage.
10. Nyx fires back — her shot was well aimed, but the arrow rebounds off a wall of light at the mage's chest.
11. Nuberroca steps out from behind a cart, flashes across the camp in a step of cloud to land in front of him, and his first swing meets a magical barrier and slides off.
12. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca gives in to the dark aura. The blue lines along his body darken; black smoke runs down the lance; the point drives in and Azbara Jos grasps at his own chest, shrieking.
13. The wizard holds himself upright, meets Nuberroca's eye, and tells them they are stronger than he thought, but they have more enemies than they know; **Rezmir** will hear of this, and he will see them again.
14. A plane-cut opens between them like a strip of millimetre film, closes a breath later, and Azbara is gone.

## 8 - Anton vanishes
_~06:30, 29 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. Back in the camp, the two bodies Eguzkilore had passed in the dark turn out to be the captive guards Cheko and Mane — knifed in their bindings, out of sheer sadism.
2. They are found a step from where they had been sleeping, as if they had moved before they were killed.
3. One of the wagons carrying the stolen treasure is gone, and all the horses have been cut loose and spooked.
4. **Anton** — the man Nuberroca had been protecting — is gone with the wagon.
5. The whole caravan — guards, merchants, party — turns out to run down the scattered horses, and after three hours they have most of them back, the stragglers coming in through the morning.
6. The lost wagon has kept to the road — forward toward Waterdeep's gates (four hours off) or back the way they came, the party cannot tell.
