## 1 - The doppelganger unmasked
_~09:00?, 25 Nightal 1489 DR?_

1. The combat is already running — someone (**Mialee**, by what the group later confirms) had already thrown a whisper across the camp to the real Nyx, and the halfling in front of them had not been the one who answered [Td:261-272]. Amidamaru casts his own whisper now, north-east towards the family wagon, and the real Nyx answers from somewhere inside [Td:3-16]. He dashes into that wagon, slips behind the family's trunks [Td:17-21], and prises the biggest chest open: **Darla** is curled inside, unresponsive, immobile [Td:51-56].
2. The fake Nyx will not drop the act. She shouts at the party that *"vuestra falsa compañera no sobrevivirá si me hacéis daño"* [Td:164-167] and turns her bow on Nuberroca, who is out of rage and takes two shots clean [Td:85-89, Td:183-186]. Eguzkilore crosses the camp, catches her bodily and drops her speed to zero, then punches her in the face: *"¿dónde estáis?"* [Td:107-137, Td:144-146].
3. Amidamaru goes to her with a healing hand on the forehead to keep her upright [Td:170-174], then — eyes closed — presses the muzzle of his pistol against her leg and fires [Td:176-177]. She holds the lie: *"yo soy Nyx, hay una Nyx falsa que está intentando suplantarme, que no os diré dónde está, porque si no me dejáis escapar"* [Td:179-180]. Behind her, Sinsong holds the spirits he had conjured around himself, and the fake Nyx walks through them and tears open again [Td:237-240].
4. Mialee works at the second big chest with her unseen hand — the lock gives quietly — and finds the real **Nyx** inside: curled, half-dressed, barely reacting [Td:298-325]. Amidamaru gives her a sip of water; she stirs but does not come round [Td:314-321].
5. Nuberroca swings on the fake Nyx recklessly [Td:337-343], then flares across the camp in a step of cloud to come down beside the real Nyx and strike at her double again [Td:344-345]. The doppelganger carries a brutal counter-blow back into him — a long tear across his shoulder — and he spends the cultist strapped to his chest as a shield; the man's head opens, brains and blood on Nuberroca's own front [Td:373-398]. The fake Nyx, still bleeding, pleads: *"dejadme en paz, malditos bastardos, yo solo quiero vivir"* [Td:429].
6. Kaelthar reaches out with his will and the fake Nyx goes rigid, paralysed where she stands [Td:434-453]. Amidamaru closes the gap, puts the pistol to her forehead, and fires at point-blank [Td:460-469].
7. ***[EPIC]*** Her head bursts [Td:492-493]. The halfling shape loses hold and swells into a loose, human-sized form, blue-skinned and dark-blue, features unformed, the clothes straining at the seams [Td:494-509].

## 2 - Two drugged women

1. Amidamaru lifts the real Nyx out of the chest and carries her outside into the air [Td:600-601]. Eguzkilore picks Darla up on his wolf and takes her out separately, bound against mistaken identity [Td:617-623]. Neither woman has any visible wound [Td:536-539]; both read as drugged, not poisoned [Td:316, Td:553-556].
2. Kaelthar lays hands on Nyx but at his level the gift only lifts poison, which is not what she has [Td:549-556]. Sinsong offers a prayer that *might* reach her — he needs the faith of the whole group — and asks that the party accept his stones and his cause before he tries [Td:596-598, Td:712-715]. The party will not; the offer dies there [Td:720-726].
3. Amidamaru stands over Nyx and feels for any magic held on her — nothing [Td:749-750]. He whispers across to her mind and asks: she tells him she noted a prick, could not move after that, and that whoever held her has been injecting her every day [Td:739-745]. He names the drug a daily dose at about twenty-four hours and tells the others the only cure he can see is waiting it out [Td:747-751].
4. Darla is clamped in shackles [Td:762-768]. Amidamaru wipes the dead doppelganger clean with a flick of will [Td:633-634 ?ord], pockets a short sword, a longbow (Eguzkilore's old one, back from her), armour and clothes, and gives the lot to Nyx when she is awake enough to take it [Td:645-651].
5. The camp is shaken. Nobody is cooking — people are gathered around the body of the doppelganger — and the merchants have run out of questions once they see it [Td:768-782]. The caravan sends dinner and beer across to the party in thanks, more open-handed than usual [Td:784-787], and **Baird** stands them to the drinks himself [Td:875]. The watches are posted [Td:807-813]; the night passes without further event and the party take a long rest at dawn [Td:874-883]. Nyx is still deep in the drug's hold — exhausted and only slowly coming back; each night will peel one layer off [Td:886-890].

## 3 - A knife in Carlon's back
_~08:00?, 26 Nightal 1489 DR_

Murder most foul -> This should happen the 27th

1. By morning Darla's shackles are empty on the ground — not forced, simply slipped — the group has clamped a shape-changer in an iron that opens for any shape she likes [Td:896-901]. And by the chest wagon lies **Carlon** the harpist, dead on the ground, a single short-sword puncture in the back from behind [Td:902-905]. The doppelganger worked with its fists [Td:904-905]; this is someone else.
2. **Elfking** comes over and examines the body — the man was leaning into the treasure wagon when it was done — then turns his attention openly to Mialee and the short sword hanging from her belt [Td:947-957]. Miguel and Fenwir are already saying it aloud to the other guards: these adventurers are not going to hand the loot over, they killed Carlon to keep it [Td:974-979, Td:997-1007]. Amidamaru works the crowd and reads the murmur as unled — nobody is directing it [Td:1041-1043].
3. Nuberroca, Eguzkilore, and Kaelthar take the wolf and go to track the doppelganger. Beside where Darla's shackles lay Nuberroca picks up tiny prints — gnome- or halfling-sized — that four or five paces further become full human prints heading for the trees [Td:926-937 ?ord]. They follow into the wood but Nuberroca cannot hold the trail and they lose it [Td:1047-1051].
4. Nuberroca comes back furious. He confronts Miguel first, finds he has the wrong man, apologises, and goes to **Fenwir** instead — fists in his face, demanding what rumours he is spreading [Td:1064-1082]. Nothing shifts.
5. The hooded man who travels alone — until this moment silent — speaks up for the first time: if there are no witnesses, let the gods take vengeance on the guilty [Td:1088-1092]. The guards drop it and resume their posts [Td:1101-1104]. On the way back to his wagon Eguzkilore passes the two tied captives — **Checo** and **Mane** — and the wolf's tail swats them in the face as he goes [Td:1116-1121].
6. Nuberroca puts the whole thing to rest from the box of his cart: *"vuestro intelecto y vuestras palabras no sirven para resolver los problemas"* [Td:1105]; they will keep moving to Waterdeep [Td:1106].

## 4 - Janma the Harper
_~07:00?, 27 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The day on the road passes without incident; by next morning the caravan has Waterdeep in sight on the horizon, about two days from the gates [Td:1136-1138]. On the road, Amidamaru goes forward to the small gnome woman from Daggerford — the Harper the party will come to know as **Janma** — and introduces himself over breakfast [Td:1213-1218]. He apologises for Sinsong's suspicion the day before [Td:1217-1219]. She accepts the apology — awkwardly, she does not know him — and asks how she can help [Td:1221-1224].
2. She lays it out: *"tenemos diferentes jefes pero mismos objetivos"* [Td:1225-1227]. She was in the caravan to pick out the Cult of the Dragon operators and neutralise them, and the party has already done that work for her [Td:1227-1230].
3. The party confirms Castle Naerytar as the destination Amidamaru overheard the cultist soldiers name [Td:1245-1246]. Janma has heard of it too — she believes it is in the Marismas, the Mere of Dead Men, from an older reference tying it to the Order of the Astrologers' holdings there [Td:1235-1250]. She knows the party belong to the Order of the Gauntlet — all except Mialee and Nyx, who name themselves as Harpers [Td:1256-1261]. She is a Harper herself; different cells of the same organisation [Td:1266-1278].
4. She confirms the handler: *"Si no me confundo, **Leosin** será el que ha hablado"* [Td:1278-1281]. Amidamaru confirms Leosin sent them after this caravan [Td:1282].
5. The plan: when the caravan reaches Waterdeep in two days, Janma will take the chests on to Leosin and the Order of the Gauntlet, with a positive report of the party [Td:1286-1289]. The party should go straight for the north gate and head on to the **Posada de Carnath** — a roadhouse on the road to Neverwinter which Janma believes is the cultists' handover point and which is seeing new foot traffic because *Lord Neverember* is rebuilding it after a catastrophe over the Winter [Td:1291-1306]. She has not been able to confirm it directly; her last cultist caravan — brought in by one *Bael* — was lost before it reached the point [Td:1312-1315]. Beyond Carnath they always lose the thread [Td:1316-1320].

## 5 - The heart in the mage's pocket

1. The party's attention lands on the other hooded figure in the caravan — the one who had spoken for them that morning. Nyx walks the caravan apologising to the merchants about her strange behaviour the previous days — *"no era yo, ¿sabéis?"* — using the crowd as cover [Td:1502-1511], while Mialee works an unseen hand into the hooded man's pocket from the far side [Td:1512, Td:1521-1528]. The pocket holds an insane weight of coins, more than fits in a jacket [Td:1532-1535].
2. Below the coins she finds something fist-sized, soft, that gives under her fingers [Td:1538-1540]; a small pouch with pellets inside [Td:1540-1542]; and two folded scraps of parchment, inked in a script none of them read, more sigil than letter [Td:1583-1587]. She lifts one of the parchments on the hand up over the crowd [Td:1581-1583]. The man is beginning to notice [Td:1580].
3. Mialee cleans the hand off when it comes back [Td:1546-1548]. They whisper across to Nyx about the fist-sized thing: it is a heart — too big for a halfling, perhaps human, perhaps pig [Td:1552-1559].
4. They go to Janma with what they have. She confirms it: Red Wizards of Thay are usually bald and tattooed, and the daggers they carry leave wounds no magic will close [Td:1644-1646]. She cannot place the heart [Td:1649]. If this really is **Azbara Jos** [Td:1676] — a name the party does not catch cleanly — then he is one of the most powerful mages alive, and the party is to be very careful of him [Td:1687-1689]. Nuberroca presses the group for a reason to attack if they are not sure he is the villain; the party does not commit [Td:1691-1694].

## 6 - What the blue dwarf left in Nuberroca

1. Later on the road Nuberroca pulls Nyx, Mialee, and Eguzkilore aside, out of Janma's earshot [Td:1733-1735], and tells them a secret. Since the blue dwarf hurt his head and showed him the vision of the undead walking into his village, something has been changing in him [Td:1737-1746].
2. ***[EPIC]*** He opens his fist. The veins across his hand turn from blue to black [Td:1741]; a dark halo of smoke peels off the knuckles [Td:1742-1743]. He bends and brushes a wildflower by the roadside with the back of his hand and the flower blackens, withers, and crumbles [Td:1797-1798].
3. He tells them he does not know what it is. Something guides his anger now, and when Sinsong once put a note on his back something inside him pushed out and tried to tear it off [Td:1763-1766].
4. Amidamaru pats him on the shoulder and feels him for any magic held or lingering — nothing [Td:1771-1773]. Eguzkilore tells Nuberroca that when they reach Waterdeep he should have someone look at it [Td:1782-1783]. Nuberroca agrees but names the darkness in himself as something he does not like [Td:1784-1785].

## 7 - Torch in the fog
_~22:00?, 28 Nightal 1489 DR_

1. The GM tells the party they have one more day of travel ahead and will reach Waterdeep by the morning of the 29th [Td:1714-1716]. The day passes without major events [Td:1842-1846]. During the night Nyx and Eguzkilore have the watch when a torch comes toward the camp through a cold, thick fog [Td:1847-1868]. Eguzkilore reads the weather — the coming days are to be clear, which makes the fog around them stranger [Td:1866-1874]. He steps out of the arc of light and calls: *"¿quién va ahí por última vez?"* [Td:1849]. He cannot see anything beyond the torch itself [Td:1851-1855].
2. Nyx takes a ray of cold in the back from the direction of the torch and sees roughly where it came from [Td:1890-1900]. Eguzkilore wheels and runs for the sleeping camp — a long, hard dash to the sleepers and a longer one to shake them awake [Td:1909-1915]. Nyx throws down a shimmer of fey light into the fog as a marker and knows now where the mage stands [Td:1930-1940].
3. A black-mantled mage walks out of the fog and throws three ribbons of fire at Nyx — she takes the whole set and nearly goes [Td:1988-1990, Td:2082-2085]. Amidamaru shoves her up onto Nuberroca's shoulder [Td:2095-2097]. Nyx fires back — her shot would have hit, but the arrow rebounds off a wall of light at the mage's chest [Td:2100-2102]. Nuberroca steps out from behind the cart, flashes across the camp in a step of cloud to land in front of him [Td:2115-2123], and his first swing meets a magical barrier and slides off [Td:2125-2134].
4. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca gives the strike over to the dark aura. The blue lines along the shaft of the lance darken; black smoke runs down the wood; the point drives in and the mage grasps at his own chest, burning [Td:2140-2145]. The mage holds himself upright, meets Nuberroca's eye, and says: *"Sois más fuertes de lo que pensaba. Pero no estáis solos, tenéis más enemigos de los que creéis. Rezmir escuchará de esto. Nos vemos más adelante."* [Td:2155-2158]. A plane-cut opens between them like a strip of millimetre film, closes a breath later, and the mage is gone [Td:2165-2170].
