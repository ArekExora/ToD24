## 2 - Two bullywugs in the dark
_~17:10, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Roughly fifty minutes into the rest, Amidamaru — perched on Munin at the ceiling — spots two large bullywugs coming down from the north corridor, still out of sight of the party [T:458-476]. He whispers the warning; if the two reach the corpse-strewn ground they will raise the alarm [T:471-476].
2. The party debates ambushing versus hiding; Nyx points out any sound will draw them regardless [T:477-500]. They agree to ambush from cover and creep forward, Amidamaru wading through the deeper water [T:511-534].
3. Amidamaru surfaces just enough to speak the words of a Sleep spell over the pair. Both bullywugs fail the save and go rigid, incapacitated where they stand for the round [T:562-585]. Amidamaru corrects the GM's uptime — the effect lasts to the end of their next turn, at which point they save again [T:626-629].
4. The party creeps closer while the two are frozen, but the second save comes clean on both — sixteen and twenty, no effect at all [T:626-629]. Amidamaru, undaunted, throws a Minor Illusion of a lizard-scraping to the far side of the chamber to pull their attention away from the water [T:669-696].
5. One bullywug hurls a stone at the phantom sound and the rock plunges into empty water; the other stands very still and listens [T:729-738]. Mialee lofts an Invisible Mage Hand and stirs a small chop in the water in front of the one that stayed put; the bullywug jabs at the ripples with his rapier and voices begin to sound in the corridor beyond [T:815-847].
6. Amidamaru, watching through Munin, sees the second bullywug slip back into the tunnel while the first probes the water. Behind him, more shapes are coming — *"veo que se vienen un montón de enemigos"* — and he dashes to disengage back into deep water, Munin pecking Mialee's head on the way out to hurry her along [T:895-923].

5. Nuberroca reaches the water — *"empieza a borbotear el agua a mi alrededor"* — and drops into the tunnel, Rage already lit [T:1795-1803]. The party follows Mialee's anchored rope out; sixty feet of tunnel past its end lands them in the moat [T:1222-1229].

## 4 - Above ground: smoke on the tower
_~17:17, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party surfaces beside Tompu and Atzi in the shallow moat outside the cave. The GM anchors: 17:17 in-fiction, 15 Hammer 1490 DR [T:1329-1330]. They post the two lizardfolk in front of the tunnel mouth in case a bullywug follows them out; nothing comes [T:1330-1380].
2. Mialee spots eyes in the water at the surface and they vanish — not a bullywug, they think, given the direction, but not something they can identify either [T:1406-1413]. Nuberroca wades in, splashing loudly to draw a response, and gets none — the surviving crocodiles were killed off before the dive [T:1428-1431].
3. Amidamaru sends Munin up a hundred feet over the courtyard to read the castle. Bullywug, lizardfolk, and human patrols move separately across the yard — no mixing, no coordination visible from the air [T:1519-1525]. The wind is *"un viento nunca lo habéis visto antes"* — new, and worth noting [T:1521].
4. A pillar of smoke is rising from the castle's entrance tower — large, controlled, not a hearth-fire [T:1566-1585]. The party works through it: Nuberroca and Amidamaru guess *carnomorfos*, Mialee lands on the likelier answer — the bullywug dead being burned [T:1587-1603].
5. Nuberroca leans on Atzi and Tompu to come inside with them — the goal is a wounded push against the bullywug rank-and-file, not the chief [T:1672-1702]. Atzi baulks: *"Pharblex es muy poderoso"* and killed his own chief, no matter how loudly Nuberroca invokes *the great Nuberroca* or reminds him they slew the crocodile Coatl between them [T:1683-1698]. Nuberroca gets his best offer — the lizardfolk will come as far as the outer chamber and break if the chief himself appears [T:1700-1702].
6. Tompu, on Nuberroca's read, is *"un poco mierdecilla"* and will only get killed inside; Nuberroca sends him back south to the initial corral to mind the mounts, Juancho marked with Nuberroca's purple scarf [T:1705-1716], [T:2071-2094]. Amidamaru hangs a backpack containing two of Nyx's berries from a nearby tree — the party's fallback stash if they come out gearless [T:1719-1735].
7. Nuberroca uses the medicine kit on Mialee's remaining lingering wound and rolls a nine — enough, just — the last-day scar clears [T:1381-1397 ?ord]. The GM tops the party's short-rest healing by ten hitpoints across the board *"por ser generosos"* [T:1401-1405].
8. The party debates waiting overnight versus going straight back in. Eguzkilore votes for a long rest under cover; Mialee argues the bullywugs will re-fortify and the party's edge will bleed out with the light [T:1481-1510]. The vote goes with Mialee — before nightfall, back in [T:1530-1540].

## 5 - The Spike Growth carpet
_~18:15, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Nuberroca dives back in first, Amidamaru right behind him, to probe the tunnel [T:1795-1824]. Nuberroca palps his way along the wall in the dark for the first eighty feet without issue, then a slice opens across his arm from the wall itself — three points of damage on nothing but rock [T:1825-1836].
2. Every five feet the cut repeats. Nuberroca takes another slice and calls the retreat; Amidamaru's own light does nothing to show what is cutting them [T:1841-1856]. They reverse — Nuberroca six points down, Amidamaru three — and the GM stops the counting when the pair are back at the moat mouth [T:1880-1892].
3. Amidamaru takes ten minutes to prepare Detect Magic as a ritual, then goes back in alone with a pebble-light [T:1907-1917]. The last forty feet of the tunnel light up as active magic under the spell — Amidamaru rules it Spike Growth, invisible-to-natural cover cut into the tunnel walls at the caster's own gate [T:1940-1946], [T:2008-2044].
4. Beyond the trap, at the tunnel's inner mouth, Amidamaru sends Munin in briefly to see through his familiar's eyes: one wizard, five bullywug rank-and-file, one frog waiting in the chamber [T:1956-1988]. The chamber has gone pitch — Darkness has been laid over the entrance itself, no longer the Dancing Lights of the earlier round [T:1953-1962].
5. Amidamaru retreats back out, reports the numbers, and his emphatic tactical advice is *"lo primero que hay que hacer es abrirse, separarse, no darle lo que puede que nos tire la magia fuerte"* — spread on entry, do not clump under the wizard's area magic [T:2098-2102]. The party works the plan and files up in order: Nuberroca leads with Cloud's Jaunt in reserve for the wizard, Amidamaru second as tank, Atzi third, Mialee and the ranged behind, Kaelthar rear [T:2100-2131]. In the courtyard beyond the tunnel mouth, the party hears more distant *carnomorfo*-like sounds — the forest is not safe either [T:2046-2057].

7. The GM closes at 19:20 in-fiction, ten more real minutes to walk out of the tunnel. The party owes Tompu the news of Atzi's death, and Pharblex, the bullywug chief, remains untouched deeper in the cave [T:4107-4116].
