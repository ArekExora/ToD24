### Events
- On the road to Greenest — six strangers form a party over a shared meal.
- Approach to Greenest at dusk — town under attack; raiders in the streets, blue dragon overhead.
- Looted townhouse — first contact; mixed kobold and cultist looters put down (some in keep livery).
- ~19:00 — Desperate family — Cuth and three children saved; mother killed before the party closed.
- River-bank skirmish — flanking patrol with two drakes routed.
- ~20:00 — Inside the keep — locked in; Tarbaw and Escobert brief the tunnel mission, hand over the keys.
- The Old Tunnel — spider-nest disaster; Mialee and Nyx downed, Sinsong killed.
- Retreat to the keep — party carries out their dead and unconscious; remaining spiders disengage into the dark.
- ~21:00 — Aftermath — Sinsong resurrected on credit (600 gp); wakes with arachnophobia trauma.
- Short rest. In the keep.
