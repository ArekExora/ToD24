## 1 - The morning hut
_~08:00?, 16 Hammer 1490 DR?_

1. The party wakes inside **Harley**'s dome-shaped hut in the swamp outside **Castle Naerytar**, where they bedded down after the previous night's rescue of **Sinsong** and **Kaelthar** [Td:5-10]. **Mialee** sleeps on through the morning conversation in a corner of the hut [Td:255-258]. Harley opens the day with the cult greeting — *"Alabado sea nuestro Dios"* — and parts of the party answer in kind [Td:5-8].
2. Sinsong announces he spent the night awake while the others slept, running his identification ritual on everything the party is carrying; he reels off the hours worked and declares every item on them identified [Td:21-23], [Td:268-272]. Over the course of the morning he walks each PC's inventory in turn until the pass is clean [Td:349-352].
3. The lizardfolk prisoner **Ketsu**, taken the night before and still held by **Nuberroca**, has to be dealt with before anything else [Td:51], [Td:65]. The party pauses to read his intent — Harley and Sinsong come down firmly against killing him; Nyx and Eguzkilore think it is a bad idea but will defer to the group [Td:51-57]. The ethics volley — *"¿No somos asesinos?"* — tips the vote away from a clean execution [Td:69-70].
4. The compromise is to knock Ketsu out. Nuberroca first clamps a hand over the lizardfolk's mouth to muffle him [Td:65-67], then — on Sinsong's say-so — strikes him on the head and leaves him unconscious in the grass [Td:91-96], [Td:121-122]. Sinsong moves in close, strips the weapon and shield so Ketsu cannot ambush them when he comes to [Td:146-147], and ends up with a pair of small gems from the lizardfolk's belt in his own pocket [Td:148-151].
5. Sinsong turns to **Harley** and resumes his campaign to recruit her to his cult, pressing one of his small pendant stones into her palm and telling her that so long as she wears it she is his friend [Td:164-170], [Td:198-213]. The ritual stumbles on her literal mind: she holds the stone up and asks what faith is and what the stone is for, tries to barter (more stones means more friends), and finally reaches out to rub his bald head affectionately when he turns away in frustration [Td:217-227], [Td:229-232]. He gives up and crosses to Nyx, who confirms with a hand on her chest that she wears his stone equipped [Td:237-242].
6. Through the hut's one-way wall the party can see the swamp outside: Snapjaw's lizardfolk have already moved deeper into the castle; the figures still visible are a patrol group, separate from his [Td:263-265]. Eguzkilore and Nyx take stock of their quivers — five arrows between them [Td:312-316], [Td:672-678]. Harley re-casts her hut so they will have another eight hours of cover [Td:306-311].

## 2 - The strike from inside the hut

1. Harley gets bored and asks who has an idea [Td:362]. Before anyone answers, Sinsong turns to her and says he will attack — *"Me giro y empiezo a hacer un fireball en la mano"* — asking only where to aim [Td:364-365]. Harley waves vaguely north and tells him he is intelligent enough to find a target [Td:366-369].
2. ***[EPIC]*** Sinsong steps to the edge of the dome, points, and a sphere of green light leaves his hand and strikes a lizardfolk sentry out in the swamp [derived:Td:364+Td:374+Td:388]. The attack is a magical ability rather than a spell, so it carries straight through the hut's wall — stones ricochet back into the dome and everyone inside takes a hit of the backlash [Td:389-393], [Td:423-430], [Td:434-436].
3. The dome is now a bright green half-sphere plain against the swamp — *"gracias a esa gilipollez, ahora todo el mundo sabe dónde está vuestra cabaña"* [Td:417].
4. **Mialee** rouses to the chaos [Td:255-258 ?ord], [Td:440]. She slips to the hut's edge, lets fly on a hidden shot, poisons the lizard she catches, and ducks back into cover before anyone can spot her [Td:478-480], [Td:641-651]. Nyx shoots another of the sentries down [Td:482-493]. Harley points a finger at a third and sends out a sound like a struck bell [Td:505-511]. Eguzkilore sends an arrow at the one still standing [Td:515-516].
5. At the alarm the whole patrol surges [Td:517-519]. Within the breath that follows, the castle's **drum** begins to beat again across the water, raising the alarm [Td:731-735]. Sinsong's hope that they had burned the drum last night falls flat — *"han hecho otro"*, they built a new one [Td:736-742].
6. Harley cries out at the pattern — *"Hay que reconocer cómo en seis segundos se moviliza todo el puto castillo"* [Td:745].

## 3 - The flight

1. Sinsong calls out from inside the hut: do they stand and fight, or run? [Td:602]. Eguzkilore votes to run at once [Td:605-606]; Harley agrees that frontal fighting is suicide given the leaders they have not even seen yet [Td:296-298].
2. **Kaelthar** — missing an arm from his earlier captivity, barely able to lift a weapon — is slow to react and finds the fight already on when he turns to it [Td:544-548], [Td:759-764]. He steps outside the hut and summons his wolf steed **Fenrir** (the steed cannot enter the dome) [Td:580-595], ducks back inside and settles for a brief shimmer of protection on himself before stepping out again [Td:565-568].
3. The party scatters. Sinsong dashes south out of the hut [Td:609-614] and Nuberroca falls in behind him [Td:622-624]; Mialee breaks the other way to draw heat [Td:632-633]; Eguzkilore and Nyx push east through the undergrowth [Td:627-629].
4. The lizardfolk clear the fence in a single bound and come after them [Td:518-519 ?ord]; two of them rush the hut and discover they cannot cross its wall, but the rest flow around it [Td:712-715].
5. Realising he can do nothing on the ground, Kaelthar unfurls his draconic wings and lifts straight up into the air, leaving **Fenrir** on the ground as his decoy [Td:1143-1159]. He climbs until he is a vertical line above the swamp — ninety feet up, beyond javelin reach, nothing to swing at [Td:1444-1456].
6. **Mialee** throws down a bank of fog across the chase line and vanishes into it [Td:1181-1183]. **Nyx**, bitten by a leaping lizard [Td:1211], drops into a protective ward Sinsong casts on her at a distance [Td:1113-1127 ?ord] and keeps moving on halved speed through the swamp mud [Td:1198-1200].
7. Harley weaves her sorcery into a cover of invisibility that reaches over both herself and Eguzkilore — one point of her inner spark spent to carry the pair [Td:869-872 ?ord]. The two of them fade from view [Td:874]; the lizards' pursuit slides past them into empty swamp [Td:911-916].

## 4 - Sinsong falls, Mialee taken

1. ***[EPIC]*** A lizardfolk **geomancer** closes the distance on Sinsong, lifts both hands, and fires a bolt of green sickness at the airborne cleric; the magic rolls over Sinsong and he pitches off balance [Td:1411-1416]. He drops the thirty feet he had cleared through the air, hits the swamp ground unconscious, and the fall itself leaves him one slip closer to death [Td:1422-1430].
2. Two lizards pack around the downed cleric, grapple him, and begin to drag him back toward the castle — restrained, unconscious, taken [Td:1619-1625].
3. Mialee, from her hidden fog pocket, reaches an unseen hand into Nyx's belt pouch and fishes a berry out; she slips it between the halfling's teeth and Nyx comes back to one point of life with three berries left [Td:1860-1865].
4. Nyx uses the moment to shape herself into a small lizardfolk, walks forward out of the fog, and begins to pantomime helping the pack hunt the party down [Td:1891-1897], [Td:2001-2013].
5. The pack keeps groping blindly for Mialee without finding her, but at point-blank range even blind strikes land by sheer saturation [Td:1641-1655 ?ord]; she takes several hits and finally calls it — *"Me rindo, no hace falta que me peguéis más"* — and lays down her weapons [Td:1972-1979]. The lizards haul her up between them and carry her back toward the castle gates with the other prisoners [Td:1977-1980], [Td:2092-2094].

## 5 - Nuberroca lost, Kaelthar away

1. Nuberroca stays with the retreat only long enough to drop a leading lizard with his polearm, then another; he keeps the pursuit on himself while the others thin out across the swamp [Td:1735-1745], [Td:1808-1817].
2. ***[EPIC]*** In his last gesture visible to the party, he hurls Sinsong's small faith stone away from himself into the swamp — *"con mi último aliento de vida cojo la piedra de Sinsong y la tiro a toma por culo"* — refusing to let it be taken with him [Td:1830-1831].
3. Nuberroca falls. Four or five of the lizards drop around him before the circle closes; the geomancer-mages hang back and he never gets his axe into them [Td:2173-2181].
4. Kaelthar, still aloft, drifts west with the remaining minutes of his wings until the enemy below stops looking up and the air is empty [Td:1836-1839]. Harley teleports twice more at full invisible-dash to put the chase behind her [Td:1538-1548 ?ord], [Td:2109-2113].

## 6 - Regrouping in the swamp

1. The pursuit scatters. Eguzkilore and Kaelthar spend half an hour making sure they are no longer followed before they circle back toward where they lost each other [Td:2214-2217], [Td:2244-2246]. On their way, the ground is churned with the tracks of every creature from the fight; the only readable prints they can pick out are a lizardfolk's and a woman's, heading east [Td:2297-2300].
2. Nyx, slipping free of the enemy once the search spreads out, picks up Harley's trail and finds her first [Td:2158-2167 ?ord], [Td:2199-2201]. Harley re-raises her hut on the ground where the two women meet so they can sit and breathe and plan [Td:2251-2254].
3. Eguzkilore uses his **Sending Stone** to call Mialee's name across the swamp [Td:2218-2222]. The voice that answers is not Mialee's — it is a calm, educated voice they have never heard before: *"Mialee está en nuestro poder. Si os entregáis ahora, no morirán"* [Td:2222-2227]. He has no words left to answer; the message runs out [Td:2228].
4. The four survivors eventually find the glow of Harley's hut and reconvene — Eguzkilore and Kaelthar guide in on the half-green dome they recognise from the morning [Td:2316-2320]. Kaelthar lays hands on Nyx, and the party takes a short rest before they decide anything [Td:2343-2349], [Td:2375-2378].

## 7 - The plan, and the father

1. Harley tells the others she is going in through the front gate — alone, as a cultist, as the "lost daughter" of a Red Wizard she claims is her father; she describes him as bald, pale-skinned, with a red cloak like her own [Td:2480-2482], [Td:2557-2558]. Sinsong — speaking in Harley's ear by a thread of magic — warns her: *"Harley, yo que tú, no me haría muchas ilusiones con tu padre. Puede que no sea tan majo como esperas que sea"* [Td:2572-2573].
2. Nyx offers the alternative. If Harley hides her from sight and hands her **Mialee's Bag of Holding**, she can creep into the keep, bundle Mialee and Sinsong one at a time into the bag, and throw the bag out of a window to Eguzkilore waiting below — the bag is a pocket dimension, the people inside it should land soft [Td:2515-2528], [Td:2566-2571]. The plan carries two risks only: that she will give herself away by sound, and that the captives will be chained to a wall [Td:2535-2538].
3. The plan is set, and they add Harley's gambit as a decoy. Harley fades out of sight, re-shapes her cloak to match the cultists' robes, and walks for the front gate [Td:2190-2192 ?ord]; Nyx enters through the back, hidden and leaving no trail [Td:2614-2619], [Td:2647-2650].
4. ***[EPIC]*** Harley reaches the gate and begins shouting at the top of her lungs — *"¡Papá! ¡Papá! ¡He venido a casa!"* [Td:2683-2686]. Cultists spill from the keep, surround her at swordpoint, and demand her weapons; she insists she is unarmed and submits [Td:2700-2708], [Td:2772-2782].
5. She picks a human in a red cloak out of the receiving party — a Thayan — and runs at him screaming *"¡Papá!"*, trying to throw her arms around his neck [Td:2765-2771]. He pivots away: *"Yo no tengo hijos"* [Td:2784-2786]. The guards gag her, bind her arms, and set her against the wall with the other captives [Td:2787], [Td:2818-2822].
6. Thrown with the others, Harley sees the state of the room — Mialee and Nuberroca are already here, with dried blood on their hands, and the cultists are already working on Mialee; Sinsong is not in sight [Td:2721-2724 ?ord], [Td:2762-2764].

## 8 - The elf in the kitchen

1. **Nyx**, hidden and leaving no trail, drifts through the keep's back halls silent and unseen [Td:2647-2658]. She finds the kitchen one floor down from the dining room and watches a tiefling and an elf working at a table while a dwarf oversees them [Td:2862-2874].
2. She writes a note in charcoal on the back of a surveillance report she had picked up on an earlier run — *soy una arpista que está intentando rescatar a sus amigos; si me puedes ayudar, nos vemos* — and drops it crumpled beside the elf's chopping board, where it blinks visible as it leaves her unseen hand [Td:2908-2917], [Td:2928-2938]. The elf twitches, feigns a near-miss with his knife — *"casi me corto"* — pockets the note under his apron, and tells the dwarf he needs to fetch flour upstairs [Td:2941-2946].
3. ***[EPIC]*** On the top floor the elf drops to a whisper by the stair rail. Nyx pulls off her Harper amulet and sets it on the table so he can see what she is [Td:2960-2965]. He gives his name as **Haronir**, confirms he is the Harpers' inside man, and tells her the cultists had already started suspecting him before her note arrived — the vigilance report she had thrown at him was about him [Td:2971-2978].
4. Haronir sketches what little he knows for her: no secret passages he has ever found, but there is a sealed cellar beneath the keep that even cultists cannot enter; access is said to be through the dining room in the **northeast tower**, through a well-watched door, and the castle's loot runs down that way [Td:3003-3011]. Beyond that, he is blind — some wings of the castle are reserved for the bullywugs and the lizardfolk alone [Td:3004-3005].
5. By this point four of the party are in cultist hands inside the keep — Mialee, Nuberroca, Sinsong and now Harley — while Nyx walks the castle unseen with a Harper ally, and Eguzkilore and Kaelthar wait out of sight of the walls for a bag that has not come [Td:2879-2881 ?ord], [Td:3011-3017].
