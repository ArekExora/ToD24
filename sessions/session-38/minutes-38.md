# Session 38 {{float:"right" 2026-02-15}}
{wide}

## 1 - The deal takes hold
_~06:00?, 12 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Nuberroca holds **Mialee** in his lap, cord round her wrists, and tells **Snapjaw** they have surrendered and only want rest and to do as asked [Td:16]; Mialee tries to work the ropes free, Nuberroca grips her tighter [Td:132-141].
2. Mialee presses Snapjaw on why the lizardfolk serve the Cult; the answer: *"La gloria es de Tiamat, pero nosotros no rezamos a los dragones"* [Td:27-31], and the bullywugs — not the dragons — are what they want dead [Td:33-35].
3. The lizardfolk name their lord of the castle: *"Dralmorrer"*, *"El castillo en el lago"*, where *"el agua es verde"* [Td:42-52]; Nuberroca mocks the exchange by pissing and showing *"agua amarilla"* while Mialee is still in his lap [Td:72-77].
4. ***[EPIC]*** One lizardfolk takes a shine to **Eguzkilore**'s short sword and offers four silver, a dried bird, and two beads of its own make [Td:112-121]; Sinsong pushes Eguzkilore to accept and quietly pays five gold for the bird [Td:142-150], the lizardfolk around them erupting into a frenzy of dried-bird and crude-collar offers once they see the gold change hands [Td:161-173].
5. Mialee learns from Snapjaw that the *señora dragona* commands the dragonborn chief and that gold flows up to the dragonborn [Td:183-194].
6. Nuberroca trades his pickaxe to one of the big lizardfolk for a pair of "magic" collars; on handling them he realises they are not magical, just village-stall trinkets [Td:195-215].
7. Snapjaw announces the deal is sealed — the lizardfolk patrol will carry on, and **Tompu** will guide the party to the castle where they must kill **Pharblex** [Td:225-230]; *"Pharblex es peligroso, él mató a nuestro líder"* [Td:231]; the lizardfolk return the party's weapons and withdraw [Td:225, Td:243-247], and Nuberroca releases Mialee from his lap with the warning not to pull more tricks in front of these people [Td:249-250].

## 2 - Waking the dragon
_~06:30?, 12 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Sinsong lays his hands on **Kaelthar** — his bald head flaring — and heals him back to his senses [Td:251, 266].
2. Kaelthar comes to raging about his missing weapons and starts for the lizardfolk shouting *"hay que matarlos a todos"* [Td:270-273]; Sinsong drops him with a Sleep spell before he can land a blow [Td:276-284].
3. Nuberroca kneels beside him as he wakes and explains: *"Sinsong te acaba de lanzar un hechizo, como me hacía a mí"* [Td:291], and that the party has agreed to kill the bullywug leader in exchange for release [Td:297-299].
4. The party asks Tompu through Kaelthar for a short rest; Kaelthar reports that Tompu is calling them cowards but will defer to them [Td:308-315].

## 3 - Short rest, long leaving
_~07:00?, 12 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Eguzkilore hands the lizardfolk scimitar to Kaelthar so he has something to swing [Td:339-348], then asks whether he can swap his weapon mastery to the unidentified magical longsword Mialee is carrying before combat [Td:352-375].
2. Nuberroca spends time trying to lash the unidentified magical longsword onto the shaft of a spear for a one-handed polearm, then thinks better of it — the lash-up would shatter at the first thrust and the plan is quietly shelved [Td:390-487], the sword earmarked for Kaelthar instead [Td:489-491].
3. Nuberroca sets out to re-stock his medicine kit by foraging through the marsh with Tompu's help, book in hand; the plants are the right plants but he fumbles the processing — a *natural 1* on his medicine throw — and comes back empty-handed [Td:498-553].

## 4 - Marsh road to Naerytar
_~07:00?, 12 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party asks Tompu where to the castle; he answers in words they do not understand except *"Naerytar"*, which Kaelthar translates as the bullywug castle [Td:524-528].
2. Mialee rides on **Nuberroca**'s shoulders — his pace suits her better than her own [Td:531-534]; formation: Eguzkilore on point with Tompu, Kaelthar in the centre [Td:558-566].
3. Tompu stops them more than once with a hand on Eguzkilore's shoulder — far ahead a crocodile the size of a cart surfaces; the guide carries them wide of it without incident [Td:567-574].
4. After roughly two hours of marsh the party stumbles on a far larger lizardfolk patrol; whether these serve the Cult too the party cannot say and has not asked [Td:575-578].

## 5 - The second patrol
_~09:00?, 12 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Tompu goes ahead and signals the strangers with bird-calls and gutturals the party can barely parse [Td:580-586]; the lizardfolk respond in kind and the leader of the patrol — a bead-draped magic-user with limited common — steps forward [Td:603-608].
2. The leader mutters over one of his trinkets, his eyes light briefly [Td:620-623], and when it fades he addresses them in words they understand: *"vais a matar a los Wollybugs, dice Tompu"* [Td:623-624].
3. He pledges four of his own warriors — **Atzi** the big one and three others — to join the escort [Td:636-640], and names the party's deadline: *"una luna y un sol en llegar. En dos lunas estaremos allí"* [Td:655-659].
4. Mialee buys their loyalty with three daggers to the smaller lizardfolk and a short sword to Atzi [Td:665-685]; the warriors look at her *"admirándote"* in reply [Td:685-686].
5. ***[EPIC]*** Mialee doubles down — she lifts one of **Sinsong**'s painted *piedras* with an unseen hand and lets it drift into the big one's palm with the words *"necesitaréis este objeto mágico"* [Td:686-694]; Nuberroca passes his own stone, then Sinsong hands over three more, promising *"en los descansos cortos voy haciendo más"* [Td:697-702].
6. Sinsong admits in passing that his *piedras* are painted river-pebbles — the first batch was his transformed villagers, these are plain stones with eyes drawn on [Td:704-710].
7. Tompu, bypassed in the gift-giving, pads up with his hand out and sad eyes; Mialee hands him her last scimitar and he lights up [Td:711-719].
8. From here on the party moves at the centre of a lizardfolk escort, Mialee crowning herself *"la señora de los lagartos"* [Td:720-724]; Nuberroca grins *"vamos como los porteadores"* [Td:725-726], the lizardfolk closing ranks round the march.

## 6 - Bullywug patrol
_~11:00?, 12 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. One hour on, Tompu and the escort stop in their tracks, sniffing the air; the word they share needs no translation — *"Bullywugs"* [Td:800-813], and the stench of rot catches the party next [Td:810-812].
2. A patrol of mud-caked frog-men is sweeping the ground straight toward them; the lizardfolk, who go to lengths to keep themselves clean, visibly recoil [Td:816-824].
3. Sinsong draws a steadying bond between Mialee, Eguzkilore and himself to carry them through the bluff [Td:836-862]; Mialee tells Tompu through Kaelthar they are not fighting this patrol — they will pretend to be cultists bound for the castle with the lizardfolk as escort [Td:863-867].
4. Tompu stares and Atzi's lizard-face falls — *"Ibais a matar Bullywugs"* [Td:868-869]; Mialee answers that they will, but Pharblex first [Td:870-874].
5. The bullywugs pass without stopping, pointing at the lizardfolk and laughing something close to it [Td:884-889]; the lizardfolk duck their heads and let it ride [Td:889-890].
6. Kaelthar overhears the lizardfolk muttering afterwards — the party ducking a patrol has them whispering whether this *"libertadores"* are truly going to free them or are just cowards [Td:900].

## 7 - The reed embarcadero
_~late evening, 12 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Darkness closes in and the lizardfolk, who have no night-vision, start struggling with the going; Sinsong takes Tompu's spear, kindles a Light on the blade and gives it back — Tompu drops it in panic the first time until Kaelthar explains it is friendly magic [Td:932-940].
2. By spear-light they push on another two and a half hours and reach a stone platform rising out of the water — three dugout canoes rest on reed decks, with fire-blackened cobbles at the centre [Td:941-947].
3. The lizardfolk head straight for two reed baskets, dig out dried birds and crushed rats and settle to eat; the four new escorts try to barter their meal for coin from Sinsong, who shows them his roast chicken and waves them off [Td:951-957].
4. Sinsong talks the group through a wholesale healing session: with hope kindled over them he moves scar-closing prayer by prayer between Nuberroca, Mialee, Eguzkilore and himself [Td:1006, Td:1019-1072, Td:1098-1105].
5. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca pulls out his medicine kit and Sinsong steadies his hands — between them they close two of Nuberroca's lingering wounds in a single pass [Td:1120-1156].

## 8 - The hunt for mounts
_~morning, 13 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The night-camp mud shows heavy tracks a wide arc round the camp — lizard-feet much broader than Tompu's; the guide names them *"caballos lagarto"* — mounts of a kind, if the party could tame them [Td:1223-1235].
2. Nuberroca scouts for herbs again under Tompu's guidance; this time he returns with a plant that smells right and Sinsong, bald head aglow, works the paste in and closes another grave wound [Td:1267-1292].
3. The party sets out east into the marsh after the tracks; roughly two hours in Tompu points out four lizards *"bastante más grandes que un caballo"* half-hidden in the reeds, feeding [Td:1482-1491].
4. Sinsong edges close with a guiding whisper on his lips, quiet as he can manage, then lets a sleeping prayer roll through the reeds — three of the four lizards topple on the spot [Td:1534-1591, Td:1663]; one, far larger than the rest, stays wide awake and locks on him [Td:1591-1601].
5. The big lizard dashes him down and goes for a bite that falls short as he bolts [Td:1601-1613]; Nuberroca steps in with his spear held crossways, no cut, blocking the beast off while shouting at Eguzkilore to help [Td:1619-1623].
6. Mialee tosses rations wide of the sleeping ones to draw a quiet one toward her, then floats a rope round its neck with an unseen hand and steps up onto its back [Td:1673-1703]; Nuberroca turns to Atzi for help, and with the big one's steadying hand on his shoulder his next approach comes smoother [Td:1627].
7. ***[EPIC]*** The big lizard rips a gash across Eguzkilore's face — the wound soaks him in blood and venom-slick skin — and Eguzkilore is blinded for as long as the wound takes to heal [Td:1843-1858]; Nuberroca kneels beside him with wrapping and marsh herbs trying to steady the damage, but his fingers slip and the quick-fix fails [Td:1894-1906].

## 9 - The bullywug ambush
_~midday, 13 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Croaking rolls up out of the reeds and every lizardfolk whips to the southeast; a swarm of giant toads leaps bodily out of the undergrowth onto **Tayana**, one of the escort lizardfolk, and in seconds she is dead [Td:1913-1917].
2. Behind the leaping shapes a bullywug in feather-collars — their shaman — strides forward, a slick-coated blade already in hand [Td:1920-1921, Td:2006, Td:2012-2016].
3. Tompu bolts [Td:1926-1927]; Mialee presses on with the taming, Sinsong keeps the sleep on the fallen lizards, and Nuberroca swings up onto the lizard he has broken, polearm couched [Td:2093-2098].
4. The bullywug shaman closes on Nuberroca with a venom-slicked blade — the sting of the cut flashes on each hit, but the venom does not take hold [Td:2007-2016].
5. Sinsong calls down a protecting swarm of light — picking friends out, sparing the lizardfolk and the half-tamed mounts; the first toads die under it [Td:2019-2033].
6. ***[EPIC]*** The shaman answers with a wide green blast of his own that catches the lot of them [Td:2127-2144]; Sinsong is caught full in it, loses the swarm, and goes down right there in the mud [Td:2138, Td:2147-2156].
7. Nuberroca, nearly dropped himself but still mounted, slams a bone-crush through the shaman's hand — the staff flies loose and the shaman is forced onto the back foot [Td:2101-2122 ?ord].

## 10 - Berry and bite
_~midday, 13 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Eguzkilore gropes his way through the dark to Sinsong — he cannot see but he can hear him fall — and feeds him one of Nyx's berries; Sinsong comes to [Td:2227-2240].
2. Atzi vaults onto a toad and crushes its skull with his mace, then tears the throat out of a second with a bite [Td:2315-2321]; the surviving toads break and bound for the open water [Td:2322-2329].
3. Nuberroca swings down from his lizard, drops his pack and chases the retreating shaman — three spears thrown, the first catching the shaman between the shoulders and dropping him face-first in the mud [Td:2353-2389]; a second thrown spear catches another frog as the swarm scatters [Td:2389-2399].
4. The lizardfolk gather Tayana's body, strip her collar of feathers, and mark the spot with sticks and stones [Td:2402-2404]; Tompu offers the party a bitter blessing — *"Hemos perdido a un hermano, pero hemos matado muchos bullywugs y tenéis vuestros caballos"* [Td:2405-2407].
5. Nuberroca names his mount **Juancho** and Mialee names hers **Atascaburras** [Td:2423-2426, Td:2517]; the mounts stay nervous but will carry them.
6. Mialee rolls out the mage's blade and corpses — nothing of value except a bag of spell-components useless to a Harper, and a residue on the blade that she pieces together as the mage's own natural venom, sweating off its own skin, dissipating in minutes [Td:2527-2555]; she searches for empty vials to collect it and finds she has none [Td:2554-2578].
7. Sinsong unfolds the sealed message he pulled from **Bog Luck**'s warehouse and tells the group *"creo que era para este momento"* — the content stays close [Td:2585-2593].

## 11 - By canoe to the next camp
_~afternoon, 13 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Big heal between combat and travel: Sinsong lays a wave of peace over the party, rounds the healing off on himself with his bald-head glow again, and the party burns through most of Nyx's remaining berries [Td:2461-2486].
2. The three canoes allow two mochila-carrying passengers each, with the lizardfolk and the tamed mounts swimming alongside; after eight hours on the water the lizardfolk call a halt at a dry island [Td:2650-2721].

## 12 - The pendant of Sinsong's warrior
_~night, 13 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Guard rotation set for the night — Mialee and Eguzkilore pull the watches since elves rest the shortest; Eguzkilore insists his blindness only makes him the best watch they have [Td:2724-2731].
2. More healing before sleep — Sinsong moves through the party closing lingering wounds on Nuberroca, Mialee and Kaelthar; Nuberroca lets his rage carry a healing of its own and closes a wound off his own fury [Td:2743-2800].
3. ***[EPIC]*** Sinsong draws Nuberroca aside and offers him the recipe he found at Bog Luck's warehouse — a cruel rite to validate Nuberroca's faith in Sinsong and bind him as his warrior; Nuberroca presses on what it protects against — mages, red mages, necromancers, undead — and agrees [Td:2866-2889].
4. Sinsong binds Nuberroca's own blood into the stone with three bell-tolls of pain; by the third chime fresh wounds have opened across Nuberroca's body and the stone is reforged — Sinsong lays the newly-minted pendant round his neck, promising a will of iron against mind-magic and a steadiness no mage could shake [Td:2890-2935].
5. Nuberroca prays his thanks — *"Sinsong, soy más fuerte de lo que crees"* — flexes his pectorals and spends the night curled by Juancho getting to know him better [Td:2938-2947, Td:2993].
