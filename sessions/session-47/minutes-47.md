## 1 - Atzi's death and the long rest
_~19:20, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The GM opens on the moat outside the cave; the party has just surfaced, and **Tompu** waits with the two mounts, looking past them for Atzi [T:75-86]. Nyx breaks the news — *"tu compañero no ha salido con vida, lo siento. Cayó antes la maldicción de esas criaturas"* — Atzi had been infected in an earlier bite and hid it [T:87-104]. Tompu, in grief, mutters *"maldito idiota"* — the lizardfolk knew the risks and should have said [T:105-107].
2. Amidamaru asks how the infection spreads and the GM answers what the party already suspected — through the bite [T:108-113]. A quick round of checks: nobody in the party was bitten in the cave; everyone is battered but nothing new is on them [T:113-125].
3. The party pulls back from the tunnel-mouth before the bullywugs can come looking. Nyx leads the search away from the moat and into the trees, and finds a small hollow between rocks with cover enough to camp [T:126-160]. Amidamaru whispers a short prayer over himself and his own wounds start to close [T:130-138].
4. Roughly an hour is spent settling; before sleeping, Nyx spends her remaining slots on Goodberries and slips one to each teammate — the party's day-berries [T:222-266]. Amidamaru takes an hour and ten minutes with his materials to call **Munin** back to him — the owl had died in the last cave [T:270-276].
5. Eguzkilore takes the first watch and, walking his loop through the trees, gathers roughly forty or fifty branches from the forest floor for arrow-shafts [T:335-346]. Partway through the watch Amidamaru catches a large controlled fire burning inside the castle's entrance tower — *"un fuego en el castillo, en la torre, bastante grande de hecho"* — and points it out to Eguzkilore; Nyx confirms it from her own watch corner [T:296-330].
6. The full rest runs ten hours plus the two spent camping, and the party wakes at ~06:15 on **16 Hammer 1490 DR**, notably fresher [T:357-362]. Mialee spends part of her guard fashioning nine improvised arrows from the branches Eguzkilore gathered, and hands them over to him [T:377-388].

## 2 - Back to the tunnel-mouth
_~06:30, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party breaks camp and rides north — Nuberroca on Juancho, the rest on foot — for roughly half an hour back toward the cave [T:406-427]. The ground around the tunnel-mouth is churned; the crocodile corpses they left there yesterday are gone, and the disturbed earth runs to the water's edge and back [T:430-440].
2. The signs read plainly — carnomorphs have come through and eaten, or the corpses themselves have turned [T:441-451]. Amidamaru sends Munin up over the treeline but the owl reports nothing moving [T:457-461].
3. The party debates trying another entry — a breach in the wall on a different side of the castle, spotted on their first sweep and never used [T:463-499]. Amidamaru brings Munin close and sends him to peek through the breach; the owl comes back with large forms inside, three of them, though the count is not clean [T:500-521]. Eguzkilore, in a whisper, tells Amidamaru and Nuberroca that something is watching them from the underbrush [T:522-528].

## 3 - The carnomorph ambush
_~06:35, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. From the north, a rustle of leaves and a skittering of small feet on branches — the party's old acquaintances [T:540-546]. Two ratlike shapes push out of the leaves — patched flesh and mismatched bone, tendons riding wrong across joints, articulations bending where they shouldn't — and two more surface from the water at the edge [T:550-558], [T:626-631]. The party is caught by surprise; the little ones get the first turn.
2. Amidamaru steps into their path and drops into a defensive stance, drawing the pack onto himself instead of the party [T:596-605]. The small ones swing at him and none get through the tortle's guard — three attacks in succession, three misses [T:610-631]. A last shape — dog-sized this time, four bone-limbs with a long neck and a stubby snouted head, plates from birds and other creatures grafted on — pushes out of the water last and closes on Amidamaru with claws and a bite, both fumbling wide [T:642-676].
3. Nuberroca, mounted on Juancho, jabs his spear at the closest small one as it comes into reach and knocks it flat on the mud with the follow-through [T:634-641], [T:783-786]. Nyx, in the middle of the pack, opens with her short blade — the first swing goes wide, but her second bites and the little one crumples where it stood [T:690-716]. Mialee, still out at range, puts an arrow into the one closest to Amidamaru and finishes it in a single shot [T:720-733]. Eguzkilore, engaged in the melee behind, works his dagger from Amidamaru's flank and lands both cuts on another small one [T:735-752].
4. Nuberroca takes his own turn on the big one, swinging open-armed the way he trusts, and pours a wash of dark radiance through the blow — the polearm goes deep, and the butt of the shaft slips wide on the follow-up [T:753-793]. The dog-sized carnomorph closes on Nyx and sinks its teeth in for a bare scratch — the bite carries the sickness with it, but Nyx sets her jaw against the infection and shakes it off clean [T:791-807]. Another of the pack breaks off to Nuberroca and a heavier bite goes home for eight points through his fury; he saves the infection check too [T:826-846].
5. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca's second-round strike carries dark radiance again and the polearm goes deep — the wound flares first as a black orb, then blossoms into pure light before the strike is finished [T:895-916]. He follows with the polearm's butt at the last live one; Eguzkilore, holding a reaction shot, drops it clean the moment it breaks cover [T:929-956]. The five heaps of stitched flesh on the ground were once the crocodiles the party left there the day before.

## 4 - Fire, alarm, and acid from the tower
_~06:40, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Amidamaru works through the crocodile corpses one at a time with a bolt of fire — burning each so the pattern cannot repeat [T:970-987]. On the last one the flame catches broader than he means it to; a plume goes up bright enough that a bullywug at one of the castle windows sees him and starts shouting [T:988-996].
2. The party freezes rather than moves, arguing north versus south under the alarm — Eguzkilore wants a wide loop by the south, Nyx wants the tree-line north [T:998-1017]. Two or three minutes pass with nothing done [T:1200-1201].
3. A hissing green sphere arcs from the tower window and bursts on the ground at their feet, showering the whole cluster in acid [T:1050-1054], [T:1121-1124]. The PCs all twist clear and take half; **Atascaburras** and **Juancho** save badly and take the full brunt [T:1055-1075].
4. Juancho spasms and dies where he falls [T:1080-1083]. Nuberroca hauls the dead lizard up bodily and runs him into the moat, scrubbing the acid off the corpse before the second burn can eat deeper [T:1117-1130]. Nobody moves to help Atascaburras; the second lizard drops in the sun, coated head-to-tail in acid, and lies still [T:1207-1215].

## 5 - Atascaburras rekindled and the flyover
_~06:45, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. ***[EPIC]*** Where Atascaburras lies, the acid burns start to fade instead of eating deeper — the flesh knits, the scales come back new, and a moment later the acid finds fresh skin and eats it away again — a cycle that repeats before the party's eyes without anyone casting a thing [T:1160-1180], [T:1208-1219].
2. Nuberroca opens his book on beasts and works the identification keys against what he's seeing — the species he can name, and lizardfolk of the guard kind are stubborn, but *"algo extraño ha pasado, no ha sido muy natural"* [T:1220-1236]. Juancho, he notes bluntly, does not regenerate; only Atascaburras [T:1244-1250].
3. Amidamaru takes ten quiet minutes to work a magic-sensing ritual and passes the reading slowly over the pair — no active enchantment on Atascaburras, no lingering effect he can read; only the faint pull of Eguzkilore's bow among the party [T:1260-1272].
4. Nyx reminds the group of the lizardfolk pact — they said they would help if the chief were killed [T:1274-1278]. Amidamaru pushes back, tired: they have tried the cave three times, the wizards are strong, and *"por qué se reproducen tanto"* [T:1277-1285].
5. Amidamaru sends Munin a hundred feet over the castle to read the courtyard — giant lizards, lizardfolk, bullywugs, and a few cloaked human figures moving separately, no coordination visible from above [T:1296-1330]. The entry tower has the burn zone at its centre, penned in as if they use it deliberately for burning [T:1327-1329]. The southeast tower is sealed under thick, dense webs — long-abandoned by the look of them [T:1345-1354]. And the central keep-tower has an enormous horizontal cylinder resting almost on its side within it — *"un pepino cojones"* — big enough that Amidamaru points it out to the party by direction [T:1370-1378].

## 6 - Up the abandoned tower
_~06:50, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Nyx asks Mialee if she carries a grappling hook and Mialee produces a full climbing kit — pitons and rope, not a grapple, but usable for a bolted ascent [T:1382-1400]. The GM reads the wall for them: the castle is ruined enough that handholds and creeping plants make an unaided climb feasible, though a bad slip from height would land on the rocks at the moat's edge [T:1404-1407], [T:1467-1470].
2. Nuberroca has another idea. He drops his armour on the ground, takes ten paces of run-up, plants a jump, and lets his Goliath step carry him partway up the tower wall in a puff of cloud [T:1476-1553]. He hangs a moment by his fingers on the stone, calms his grip, and steps again — this time to a window at fifty feet [T:1590-1594].
3. Nuberroca ties off and drops the rope from the window — then Amidamaru's message reaches him in his ear: the windows below are aligned with his, and the bullywugs in the lower rooms will spot the line the moment it swings [T:1596-1635]. Nuberroca hauls the rope back up as two lizardfolk lean half out of the window one floor down, one of them wearing a **red feather** [T:1650-1670].
4. Amidamaru works the problem: Mialee's unseen hand can reach sixty feet and can hold the rope out at an angle from the wall itself — the narrow windows are shallow enough that even half a metre of drift will clear them [T:1751-1858]. Mialee sends the hand up; Nuberroca takes the line at his end and lowers it out at the diagonal [T:1837-1858].
5. From the shadows of the room behind Nuberroca, something moves on the ceiling. He turns and sees a large spider dropping toward him — and lands the first blow himself with the polearm before it can attack, a killing critical that leaves the thing curling into a ball as it falls dead [T:1776-1793]. The room, it turns out, is not as abandoned as the webs suggested.

## 7 - The spider chamber
_~07:00, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The initiative order carries from the killed spider; two more giant spiders emerge from the corners as the party starts climbing. Nyx goes first up the rope, hand-over-hand under Nuberroca's grip [T:1862-1888]. A spider lobs a white sticky bolus down at Nuberroca and he twists just clear of it [T:1965-1979]. A second bolus catches Nyx mid-climb and holds her fast against the rope [T:1983-1988].
2. Nuberroca hammers back at the closer spider with the spear — the strike lands hard and leaves the thing sluggish, its next lunge poorly aimed [T:1989-2038]. Nyx pulls herself up the rope still webbed to it, reaches the window, and cuts herself free of the silk with her scimitar once she's inside [T:2100-2135], [T:2263-2273].
3. ***[EPIC]*** With more spiders webbing the rope and biting his exposed side, Nuberroca deliberately drops one hand from the line — takes the fourteen-point bite for it and lets fury flood the answering swing [T:2043-2074]. He holds all the party's weight one-handed while spider after spider chews at him, and answers each in turn with the polearm [T:2098-2100], [T:2255-2257].
4. Nuberroca's wounds glow blue and knit on their own again, the same slow light the party saw closing him up after the cave — no cast, no source on his sheet [T:2210-2218]. Mialee makes it up on a clean acrobatic run and clears the window [T:2151-2156]. A giant spider closes on Nyx as she pulls into the room and lands a heavy bite for twenty-one points; she drops unconscious inside the tower [T:2314-2325].
5. Mialee dashes across, disengages from the spider on her, and presses one of her own carried berries into Nyx's mouth — Nyx wakes to a single point of life and stays down on the boards [T:2427-2454]. A round later another spider is on Nyx and drops her again; Eguzkilore, only just up the rope, unloads another volley in the same breath and the last spider falls [T:2665-2686].
6. Eguzkilore uses one of his own berries on Nyx and she comes around again [T:2705-2709]. Amidamaru works down the line of the wounded — Eguzkilore, Nyx, Mialee — and where his healing lands a soft red light rises out of the wounds as they close, unlike anything on his sheet [T:2740-2755]. When he reaches Nuberroca, the last few wounds close plainly, none remaining [T:2753-2756].

## 8 - The trap door and voices below
_~07:30, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The chamber they've broken into is the tower's top floor — three storeys under them, thick webs everywhere, and a scatter of animal skeletons across the boards, three or four of them plainly bullywug [T:2717-2740], [T:2811-2821]. The bullywug bones carry no arms or armour; the spider corpses have no venom glands worth extracting either [T:2795-2803].
2. In the middle of the floor, a wooden trap-door held down by three iron nails driven through the seam [T:2804-2810], [T:2932-2934]. Amidamaru sends Munin down through the window and back into the second floor from below — the middle room there is empty and clean, no webs; central chamber, staircase, two doors, one north and one west [T:2830-2851].
3. Mialee runs the trap-door for traps and finds none — the nails are the whole of it [T:2887-2938]. Amidamaru sets Munin to picking at each nail with his beak while the party keeps quiet; the owl has little strength but the work is patient [T:2984-2997]. Nuberroca offers to descend a rope through the outer window and force the door from below; Eguzkilore takes the run instead, wanting to fetch Nuberroca's armour on the way [T:2775-2800 ?ord], [T:3016-3023].
4. Eguzkilore drops through the outer window on Nuberroca's rope, a hush pulled over his passage so his boots swallow into the wood [T:3016-3040]. From the second-floor side he twists each nail loose with his blade one at a time, catching each in his free hand so nothing rings on the boards [T:3040-3075]. The door swings free and the party files down onto the second floor [T:3080-3094].
5. Nyx puts her ear to the wood and listens through to the floor below — perhaps a dozen lizardfolk voices talking amongst themselves, and threading through them a handful of human voices, none of them in Common; a single fragment she can pick out concerns *"una espada"* [T:3094-3108]. Two doors face them from the room, one to the north and one to the west [T:3113-3116].
6. The GM closes at 07:40, 16 Hammer 1490 DR; Kaelthar is still outside in the woods with Atascaburras, and Tompu spent the night somewhere south with the other lizardfolk [T:3132-3144].
