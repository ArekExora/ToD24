# Session 21 — GM event log

## 1. Summary

1. **Horses stolen** — four horses taken by the Company; caravan in trouble to advance.
2. **Peryton ambush** — four heart-eating birds routed by caravan and air rescue.
3. **Sinsong rejoins** — on muzzled griffon Targutai, bartered in Baldur's Gate.
4. **Cult crate cracked** — jewels and coin inside.
5. **Khorvalis dead** — killed by trolls.

## 3. Epic moments

1. [4.4](minutes-21.md#4--the-perytons-dive) — **Nuberroca** jaunts up through the fog and grapples the lead peryton mid-air, dragging it down blind.
2. [5.2](minutes-21.md#5--a-rogue-griffon) — **Sinsong** drops from Targutai into celestial wings, a ring of angelic guardians sweeping the three birds.
3. [5.6](minutes-21.md#5--a-rogue-griffon) — **Amidamaru** arcs a fire sphere that bursts across both fleeing perytons, freeing Nyx.
4. [5.7](minutes-21.md#5--a-rogue-griffon) — **Eguzkilore** vaults into Nyx's falling trajectory and catches her just before impact.
5. [9.3](minutes-21.md#9--broken-crates) — **Nuberroca** jaunts above the cultist cart and shoulders a crate open — jewels and coin inside.

## 4. Events

1. Caravan proceeds north together — carts tied in pairs to cover the four missing horses.
2. New caravan contracts — Nyx and Eguzkilore promoted, Mialee and Amidamaru rehired; Nuberroca shakes down 4 gp.
3. ~16:00 — Peryton ambush and griffon rescue: three birds killed, one escaped, Nyx nearly taken; Sinsong rejoins mid-fight.
4. Sinsong Commands and tries Sleep to keep Nuberroca off Targutai; Nuberroca throws back the stone Sinsong gave him.
5. Long rest. Fenrir summoned as Kaelthar's bonded wolf mount.
6. 18–22 Uktar — Sinsong scouts north on Targutai for the stolen horses, finds only days-old tracks.
7. ~16:30 (23 Uktar) — Nuberroca jaunts into a cult cart and cracks a crate — jewels and coin inside.
8. **Khorvalis's** severed hand and axe found in roadside brush — trolls killed the barbarian.
9. ~12:00 (27 Uktar) — Caravan nears the Trade Way intersection; a moving painted stone sighted.
