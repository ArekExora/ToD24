## 1 - The morning quiz
_~07:00, 11 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Before breakfast the party talks over yesterday's wreckage — what the small bone-things are called, what their mission is, who they suspect [Td:1-30]. Eguzkilore pins down **Metagorger** as the big one; a second answer places **carnomorphs** as the family [Td:9-19]. Suspects: **Anton**, the cultist who stole the cart and knifed Sinsong in the ribs; **Bog Luck**; the guards drawn around him [Td:47-54]. Bog Luck has been complaining about rats in the storeroom for months; the carnomorphs are only days old; the party ties the two to the meteor shower out loud for the first time [Td:88-96].
2. The night before, the party had been ransacked; while straightening the room Nuberroca turns up with new marks on his sheet — things the recent days have left on him [Td:106-110]. Sinsong, lifting a mattress, pulls out **nine pieces of copper** — pathetic for a bribe or a reward [Td:111-116].

## 2 - Nyx's dream
_~07:10?, 11 Hammer_

1. Nyx wakes agitated, breath fast, visibly rattled in her bed [Td:124-144]. She tells a dream she cannot shake: she was somebody like Nuberroca — a goliath, axe in hand, hammer-wielders beside her, marching into a fight with orcs. They lost and she died. She lay dead a while, and then rose hungry and started walking without quite knowing how [Td:148-166].
2. Walking in that half-state she saw **lizardfolk** come up out of the earth, carrying boxes to a castle — not golden, not above a river of piss, a plain castle [Td:168-174]. Then a marsh, two eyes in the dark, a mouth closed on her, and she was flown off into the night [Td:175-177].
3. Sinsong, who studies these things, chews on *"los enanos del Fallow"* a moment — the name sounds familiar but will not place for him [Td:200-217]. He presses Nyx on whether she has ever truly known hunger; she answers that she has, in the lean stretches between settlements, and the party moves on without decoding any of it [Td:181-193].

## 3 - Faith narrowed, half the caravan leaves

1. Sinsong announces his guardians will from here on protect only his faithful, and polls [Td:252-284]. Nyx is with him in friendship but has no faith. Nuberroca is recovering his confidence. 2. Downstairs, three or four workers and a guard are harnessing horses to carts [Td:297-299]. A worker tells Nuberroca plainly: *nos vamos*. One has died, the pay does not cover this; some are staying, most are not [Td:301-307].
3. Nuberroca steps where **Bog Luck** cannot see him and gives **Sarek** a two-handed obscenity — a wave with the open hand, then grabs his crotch with the other [Td:339-343]. Nyx translates sotto voce that in local manners this reads loud [Td:343-344]. Sarek stares him down: *"Hoy no tengo que contenerme. No me busques las vueltas. Porque igual yo me marcho, pero tú no llegas a mañana"* [Td:349-352]. And then, turning back to his cart: *"No se nos ha perdido nada en este Cenagal lleno de escoria como vosotros"* [Td:355-358].
4. Three carts leave in short order — three civilians and a guard each, Sarek among them [Td:433-434, Td:959-962].

## 4 - The empty carts and the quarrel
_~08:00?, 11 Hammer_

1. Nyx throws a trace-less veil over herself and slips out behind the carts [Td:429-432]. She closes on one — none of them trying to be quiet — and sees the bed empty of the cult's cargo, only four or five bags of personal clothes [Td:449-456]. Mialee goes to the next cart in turn and finds the same [Td:486-493]: all three are empty.
2. From the yard the party watches **Bog Luck** confront Sarek on the way out. The exchange runs loud enough to catch: Bog Luck says this is not how it is done, Sarek's work ended when the workers left; Sarek shoots back his work was to deliver the cargo, it has been delivered, and if he stays he will end up killing someone — better to go on time [Td:467-478]. Bog Luck leaves it with a disgusted snort and stalks off; the party catches the sour look on the way past [Td:476-478].

## 5 - The idol in the strongroom

1. Nyx asks Bog Luck about the locked chamber at the back of the house. 2. Sinsong steps into the warehouse and reaches with a working to locate cult idols [Td:539-542 ?ord]. He gets one — about sixty feet to the south, inside the strongroom [Td:555-560]. Only the one, and the spell cannot say whether it is cult-stolen or some panoli's household icon [Td:965-969].

## 6 - The pack
_~08:45?, 11 Hammer_

1. A guard — **Craith** — walks up loud, accusing Nuberroca of saying his face stinks and dropping the rest: before the half of the caravan rode out, they had been telling everyone the party were thieves [Td:722-739]. Craith walks off indignant [Td:740]; the people in the yard now eye the party like they will grab their purses when the party comes near [Td:741].
2. Nuberroca walks over to **Bog Luck** to have it out: *"¿Qué les pasa a tus guardias que me están insultando todo el rato?"* [Td:748-752]. 3. Nuberroca opens the pack to show he has nothing he could have stolen. Bog Luck reaches in. He pulls out a **silver ingot** — *"¿cómo has conseguido esto honradamente?"* [Td:787-793] — and from Nuberroca's deeper inventory, the two **cult talismans** the party took in Greenest off the dead [Td:810-812].
4. Bog Luck's verdict: *"Veo que es cierto que sois ladrones"* [Td:801]. He closes the pack and hands it back [Td:803-805]. The talismans don't read as cultist loot — Nuberroca has been too vocal about standing against the cult for anyone to buy that — so they read as plain theft [Td:816-820].

## 7 - The arson gambit

1. Sinsong, quiet to Nyx and Nuberroca: he has sent a floating hand into the warehouse and set a lantern of fire on the ceiling, roped to drop when the door moves [Td:830-842]. The idea is to force whoever can open the strongroom to come out and act [Td:841-846]. Nuberroca steps back from the door so he is not standing over it when it starts.
2. Smoke begins to seep from the warehouse — a thin ribbon through the roof-boards [Td:905-909]. Nyx, loud and innocent, calls to Bog Luck from where she stands: *"Voxlack, huele a quemado aquí, mira a ver qué pasa"* and holds her ground so he can see it was not her [Td:910-911]. Bog Luck opens up; smoke and dancing light inside; the yard rushes in with buckets from the well [Td:912-918]. Nuberroca draws water and gives it to **Konnor**; Konnor takes it with a strange look [Td:924-925].
3. Ten minutes and the fire is out [Td:926]. Some tools are calcinated; the roof needs repair; nothing structural [Td:926, Td:986-987]. Bog Luck catches Sinsong on the way past and lays the bill out: fifteen or twenty pieces of gold, more if the beams need replacing, charged against the construction company; and the party is not to set foot in the warehouse again [Td:926-989]. A guard plants himself at the broken door to make sure [Td:989].
4. Nuberroca, probing: *"¿Te suena el nombre de Langdedrosa?"* [Td:1153]. Insight comes back empty — Bog Luck shows no flicker [Td:1159-1162]. Nuberroca, aside to Mialee: *"este está en el ajo"* [Td:1163] and walks off. Bog Luck pockets them, thanks him, and asks the party once more to take their things and leave [Td:1188-1191].
