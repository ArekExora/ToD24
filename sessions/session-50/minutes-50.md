## 1 - Berries and a book at the tree line
_~10:45, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The session opens where session 49 ended — Nyx, Mialee, and Eguzkilore coming up out of the water at the tree-line about twenty paces southeast of the castle's southeast tower, mud and blood on all of them and Pharblex's head still in Mialee's bag [T:1-6], [T:37-50]. Nuberroca joins the reunion late from the mounts; his audio cuts out on first try and Mialee has to talk him through the reset before he can hear them [T:60-95].
2. Nuberroca asks Nyx whether she has any berries to spare and how soon she can grow more [T:106-113]. She grows a fresh batch to twelve [T:130-131] and shares them out — six to Nuberroca (one for himself, five for **Juancho**), two to Kaelthar, one to Mialee, three kept for herself [T:141-164]. Nuberroca wraps Juancho's five in a strip of crocodile meat and feeds them into the giant lizard's mouth; Juancho eats it whole and recovers a small measure of hurt [T:175-186]. The wound-glow the party has been reading on Juancho's flank stays lit — GM notes the lizard is still below half life [T:184-186].
3. Nyx signs off for a short rest to top off [T:191-193]. Nuberroca spends the hour with his book of Natural History, asking why the lizards are healing as fast as they are [T:206-215]. The book gives him what it can — the lizards are exceptionally resilient in extreme climates, hardened by fighting prey and each other, taking blows that would kill most creatures their size [T:231-235] — and it does not quite explain what he is watching Juancho recover from [T:236-237]. He palpates both mounts, looking for anything to tell them apart; Juancho carries scars along his flanks and Atascaburras does not, but otherwise he can find no difference between them [T:238-262].

## 2 - The pike and Snapjaw's smile
_~11:55?, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The party walks toward the main gate; about ten minutes in they reach the lizardfolk village and find it in commotion — two groups shouting at each other, pushing but not yet coming to blows [T:277-282], [T:296-305]. Mialee sends **Tompu** a mind-message to come to their old meeting spot [T:297-302]; Kaelthar creeps close enough to eavesdrop through the Draconic and picks up that one side is defending the cult and the other insulting the bullywugs [T:326-352]. Nyx asks who is more numerous; those wearing the red mating-plumes are just barely the larger group [T:353-357].
2. A few minutes later Tompu arrives with **Snapjaw** — three bright red mating-plumes standing high on his crest, and no partner beside them [T:365-375]. Mialee borrows a spear from Nuberroca [T:386-425] and drives Pharblex's head down onto its point — she walks up to Snapjaw and Tompu with the pike held out and both of their eyes bulge out of their sockets [T:427-434]. Tompu starts jumping in place like a small child; Snapjaw beats his chest, then tries something no lizardfolk face is built for — a smile, teeth bared with the effort, done plainly for the party's sake — and takes the pike out of Mialee's hands [T:434-444].
3. ***[EPIC]*** Snapjaw runs the head through the village shouting in Draconic; Kaelthar catches the meaning and translates for the rest — *"está muerto, está muerto, está muerto"* [T:444-446]. The argument stops instantly [T:447]. Every lizardfolk without a red plume peels away; those with red plumes fall in behind Snapjaw [T:450-455]. Mialee, to the tribe's back: *"Pues hemos cumplido. Ahora sois vosotros los que tenéis que acabar con todos los bullywug del pueblo y del castillo"* [T:460-462]. Snapjaw points west; a runner sprints for reinforcements, and the whole troop moves out — reinforced by roughly nine lizardfolk soldiers who had ridden in with Snapjaw [T:459-478].

## 3 - The revolt and the breach
_~12:00?, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. About a minute later the party watches the lizardfolk attack for themselves — an acid ball drops between the bullywug guards and kills three of them where they stand [T:481-484]; stones begin to fall from the sky onto the bullywugs as the lizardfolk casters open up, and the bullywugs pour out to meet them [T:485-489].
2. The party debates whether to fight beside the lizardfolk or slip into the castle from behind while the front line is loud [T:491-509]. Nyx and Mialee argue for the sneak — the cult's attention is elsewhere and they can go for the leaders [T:495-509]. Nuberroca is uneasy about leaving the outnumbered lizardfolk to fend for themselves [T:519-520]; Kaelthar defers, willing to swing either way [T:528-530]. They settle on the back route — the wall breach the party had scouted from the previous incursions [T:522-539].
3. Alarm drums start beating from the castle proper as they approach the breach [T:620-622]. Kaelthar's armour clangs against every stone on the way in [T:604-612]. Once through, distant combat sounds carry from further inside the castle; the party picks two words out of the Draconic drifting across the yard — *weapons* and *wings* — without understanding what they refer to [T:662-666].

## 4 - The drakes in the courtyard
_~12:05?, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. The drakes on this side of the wall tolerate them at the doorway but grow uneasy as they push further into the yard [T:632-634], [T:672-678]. When the party crosses some invisible threshold, one drake breaks for Mialee — the charge overshoots her at a run and passes her clean by, and her opportunity-swing of the scimitar catches its flank as it goes [T:691-714]. Another pins Nyx against the wall; she strikes back at it with her sword [T:701-702], [T:740-760].
2. Nuberroca rides Juancho into range of the drake harrying Nyx and brings the polearm down on it — the shaft cracks its skull against the flagstones, driving the beast's chin into the ground as it dies mid-lunge [T:773-825]. Kaelthar charges the third at a full run — his boot catches on the uneven stone and he goes down hard, and his scimitar snaps between two blocks of the wall where he threw his weight to catch himself [T:836-878]. The drake sees a meal at its feet and bites him where he lies [T:881-889]; Atascaburras dashes in from the wall to draw it off him [T:891-894].
3. Mialee and Nyx pick the two remaining drakes apart between them — sneak-cut and arrow, one after the other — while Nuberroca has Juancho scramble straight up the courtyard wall and back down on the far side of the melee, riding a climbing lizard over the fight [T:914-993]. Last two drakes fall between them [T:1041-1066].

## 6 - The Thayans in the keep
_~12:12?, 16 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Nuberroca kicks the keep tower door open [T:1221-1228]. Two men in heavy armour turn to face him — faces pale and bald under drawn hoods, one word spoken in a language nobody at the table speaks, and both charge [T:1224-1229]. Nuberroca gives in to rage and brings the polearm across [T:1289-1298]; the first swing lands and the second knocks the closer man flat with the shaft [T:1263-1320 ?ord]. The divine energy he tries to layer on top seems to dissipate on impact — a resistance he was not counting on [T:1443-1447].
2. Kaelthar speaks a word that binds the prone Thayan where he lies — the man goes rigid, only his eyes moving [T:1357-1379 ?ord]. Kaelthar reaches for a second word to add a ward on himself, then realises it would break his hold on the first, and holds off [T:1382-1392]. Mialee squeezes past Nuberroca in the doorway and buries her scimitar into the paralysed Thayan — a rogue's cut riding through on the same swing — before slipping back out [T:1394-1413]. Nuberroca layers another crit into the same wounded target with the polearm; the divine energy dissipates again, but the shaft-work still bites hard [T:1421-1477].
3. The Thayan rolls free of the paralysis on his next save; the wounds he took should have felled him, but he holds on [T:1478-1482]. Kaelthar's cut finally drops him where he lies [T:1500-1516]. A second Thayan comes into the fight through a further door thirty feet in [T:1517-1523]; Nuberroca catches him with a solid swing as he crosses the threshold [T:1521-1529]. The Thayan closes on Kaelthar regardless and lands two clean strikes, and Kaelthar drops unconscious under him [T:1527-1533].

## 7 - The retreat

1. Mialee tries to feed one of Nyx's berries into Kaelthar's mouth; the berry does not take, and the wounds do not close [T:1543-1552]. She tries to drag him and cannot — at two hundred and fifty pounds of dragonborn and plate he is more than twice her carrying limit, and she manages only three paces [T:1567-1587]. Nuberroca hauls Kaelthar up onto Juancho for the retreat [T:1644-1657], [T:1783-1786]. Kaelthar keeps failing his rolls against death through the ride — the berry Mialee had put in his mouth never takes [T:1687-1693 ?ord], [T:1852-1856].
2. As they pull back, the party sees the wounds close on one of the pursuing Thayans as he closes on Nyx [T:1710-1713 ?ord]. ***[EPIC]*** He brings the sword down at her in a full cinematic cut and she twists away in the last instant; the blade drives into the wall behind her and sticks there. He wrenches at the pommel to free it and burns his turn, and she runs [T:1707-1717], [T:1746-1758]. She and Mialee swing up onto Atascaburras and Juancho, and the party rides out into cover [T:1721-1774 ?ord], [T:1826-1832].
3. Nuberroca knocks a further Thayan prone with a lance-thrust on the way through [T:1636-1639 ?ord], [T:1809-1813]; a bullywug slips into the water and swims east, but none of them see who [T:1834-1841]. Closing summary: lizardfolk are killing bullywugs at the entrance village, small skirmishes are burning through the courtyard, the party has opened the door to the keep tower, and the Thayans inside have driven them back [T:1873-1877].
