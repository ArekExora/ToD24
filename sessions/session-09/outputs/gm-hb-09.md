- Cave-mouth ambush — three kobolds drop Amidamaru, who is revived with a berry; the party seals the shaft. Nuberroca decapitates Langdedrosa's corpse for a trophy.

- Barracks retaken — five kobolds and a drake roosting on the old beds, all killed.

- ~10:30 — shrine ambush in two kobold waves; Mialee strikes the last dragon egg and fires the nest, but the shell holds; **Eguzkilore is killed** and Amidamaru downed.

- Mialee, disguised as a kobold, triggers the shrine's acid trap, killing most of the kobold reinforcements.
- The party carries Eguzkilore's body out; Sinsong wards it for a ten-day resurrection window.
- On their way out, Amidamaru is dropped again and Mialee is surrounded.
