## 4. Events

1. Cave-mouth ambush — three kobolds drop Amidamaru, who is revived with a berry; the party seals the shaft. Nuberroca decapitates Langdedrosa's corpse for a trophy.
2. Long rest. In the watch-cave, the opened chest yields pearls, gems, and non-magical jewellery.
3. ~09:30 — the escaped Roper ambushes the party at the cave columns and is destroyed by Amidamaru's fire and Nuberroca's greatsword.
4. Barracks retaken — five kobolds and a drake roosting on the old beds, all killed.
5. Nuberroca is healed near-whole; the party descends the rope into the shrine chamber to find kobolds massing.
6. ~10:30 — shrine ambush in two kobold waves; Mialee strikes the last dragon egg and fires the nest, but the shell holds; **Eguzkilore is killed** and Amidamaru downed.
7. Nuberroca traces the reinforcement route into a lower bat-cave, fights off troglodytes, and escapes by Cloud's Jaunt.
8. Mialee, disguised as a kobold, triggers the shrine's acid trap, killing most of the kobold reinforcements.
9. The party carries Eguzkilore's body out; Sinsong wards it for a ten-day resurrection window.
10. On their way out, Amidamaru is dropped again and Mialee is surrounded.
