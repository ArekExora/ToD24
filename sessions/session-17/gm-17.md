1. Morning spent looting bodies and burying the dead; Amidamaru claims the bandits' strange pistols.
2. Interrogated the bandit leader; Nyx extracted the pellet from Nuberroca's shoulder.
3. Took the three prisoners on, bound to the caravan under vigilance.
4. ~14:00 — Forced march to the next camp; uneventful night.
5. ~08:00 (10 Uktar) — Nuberroca collapses with fever; caravan passes into the Fields of the Dead.
6. ~19:30 — Nine will-o-wisps sighted off the camp; four lured off, five engaged.
7. Will-o-wisp fight — Kaelthar downed four times, Amidamaru once, **Munin killed**; holy-water weakness discovered mid-fight.
8. Back at camp — fires doused; Nuberroca shares his tribal lore on wisp feeding behaviour.
9. Party levels up to 5.
