1. Set a cover story as reinforcements for the goblin problem and walk in as a cook's escort.
2. The steward Gastyn admits them to the entry hall; the lady of the house is informed.
3. Mialee's invisible sweep upstairs finds a vaulted hall of four chromatic-dragon tapestries around a gold-thread Tiamat.
4. Sinsong recognises the mounted griffon as his lost Targutai; two flasks are prised from its hollow head.
5. Nuberroca is bathed clean; Sinsong defecates on the lodge doorstep as an offering to any listening god.
6. Sinsong freezes solid trying on the elven armour; Nuberroca thaws him by the hearth.
7. Two gargoyle sentries ambush the entry hall; Sinsong Commands one away before Talis calls both back to their plinths.
8. Audience with Talis the White — ordered to find and exterminate the Nashkag goblin tribe by morning.
9. Hole up under Amidamaru's warded shelter for the night; dawn finds them sleepless but rested.
