## 1 - The fairy and the empty well
_~07:00?, 4 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Eguzkilore steps off from the camp to relieve himself in a bush, and the piss finds a small winged thing hiding inside. The fairy climbs out drenched, cursing him in a language he does not know, and makes a sequence of gestures that are plainly obscene even without translation [Td:1-3]. He feels something go out of him — not any part of his body, something hollow where it should not be [Td:4, Td:35] — and the fairy wings itself invisible and gone [Td:5-7].
2. The curse is a slow bleed on his soul: every time he reaches for magic beyond his basic knack, there is a chance a piece of him goes with the casting. Being no spell-slinger, it will cost him nothing in practice, but it does not lift of its own accord — it is a curse, and it stays [Td:12-29].

## 2 - Backgrounds at the fire
_~07:30?, 4 Hammer_

1. Sinsong proposes that at each day's breakfast one of them share something of their past — a small ritual to know each other better, since the party has been on the road together a while and the backgrounds have barely surfaced [Td:55-61].
2. Sinsong goes first. His village was a mining town inside a cave, and his people struck a black stone deep in a dig — brilliant and heavy and taken for valuable. Within days half the village was stone itself. He has the piece and no idea where to begin looking for the cure [Td:72-85].
3. Mialee comes from Waterdeep. When they first met her she had two orphans in tow, children she had been training for a trade; she keeps up with her master there, took the recovered cart to him, and he intends to use it to raise more spies [Td:91-96].
4. Eguzkilore says plainly he is in it for fame and glory — young still, taking an *Erasmus* of sorts before he has to settle to work in the forests of his tribes [Td:101-102]. Sinsong tries to pitch him on a higher purpose; Eguzkilore declines [Td:103-107].
5. **Ardred** rides in and gives the order: *"vamos a partir"*, dawn-to-night marches, and the reminder that the arrival at Carnath is set for the 9th of Hammer, five days out [Td:110-115].

## 3 - A shower of stars
_~20:00?, 4 Hammer_

1. Eight hours of march bring them to full nightfall. The waning moon still holds more than half its disc, the sky is clear, and above them the stars are streaked — the party sees shooting stars, and most of them running the same way, west to east [Td:129-134].
2. Over the course of dinner the meteors multiply. What began as the odd flare becomes one every five or ten seconds; some burn large enough that the party can see them nearly reach the ground [Td:144-171]. Nuberroca's sun-stone — the sphere he picked up after the ogre ambush — shows a moon on its face that no longer matches the moon above, since the icon is fixed and the real moon has turned [Td:189-195, Td:1601-1608].
3. Ardred is unbothered — in these stars, the old men said, falls the iron that mixes with earth-iron to make blades more permeable to magic, and he would be pleased to see any of it strike nearby [Td:251-256]. The party is less sanguine; Mialee asks whether staying put is safe, Sinsong wants a tree to shelter under [Td:245-248 ?ord, Td:257-258].
4. Mialee, Sinsong, Eguzkilore, and Nyx take the horses to go find a shelter — a cave in the hillside, if the mountain will offer one [Td:287-298].

## 4 - A cave on the hillside
_~22:00?, 4 Hammer_

1. The first cave they find is small — twelve or thirteen could squeeze in — and they push on [Td:325-328]. Half an hour later they come on a larger one: thirty or forty people would fit, though not the wagons or the horses [Td:359-363]. The walls carry old animal-rubs; no beast lives there now, but outside the mouth there is a goat-path with real traffic on it [Td:369-371].
2. At the cave's entrance the ground has a small fresh crater, the width of a closed fist, and a stone sitting in it — porous, black, warm but no longer hot, the earth beneath it not even crystallised [Td:376-389].
3. Mialee crouches to it. With her gloves on she picks it up — a plain weight at the touch, nothing felt through the leather [Td:390-401]. Sinsong, when he sees the colour, tells her to drop it: *"se parece a la piedra de mi pueblo"* [Td:405-411]. She refuses to leave it behind and wraps it in a cloth without touching her skin to it, and stores it [Td:415-420].
4. Eguzkilore volunteers to ride back for the camp while Mialee and Sinsong hold the cave; Nyx goes with him so the whole return party keeps night-vision between them [Td:432-444].

## 5 - Spiders out of the dark
_~22:30?, 4 Hammer_

1. Mialee gathers wood and lays a fire in the cave [Td:457-466]. While it builds she walks the floor and finds a half-eaten mouse — bites smaller than the mouse itself, and more than one [Td:514-526]. Sinsong, picturing a swarm of fire-ants, makes for the mouth of the cave and starts loosing flame at the walls on his way out [Td:536-556].
2. A hairy leg comes out of a hole in the ceiling. Then another. A spider the size of a dog descends on silk, a second shows itself at a nearby hole, and a third surges from a crack in the wall [Td:574-608]. The first spider's strike catches Mialee off-balance and her bow falls from her hand; a web lashes past her and misses [Td:596-614]. A bite catches her and the venom slides off — she rolls with the strike and the fangs find little of her [Td:615-630].
3. ***[EPIC]*** Sinsong plants his feet and calls down a circle of radiant guardians around himself, holding every ally and every creature in the cave outside its reach save the spiders [Td:639-666]. The light rolls out from him and the three spiders take it in full — one dead on the ground, the other two bolting back into their holes [Td:669-686]. Sinsong finishes the dead one with a bolt of fire, to be certain of it [Td:697-700].
4. He keeps the circle up and walks the cave with the light at his heels. Small insects die as he passes; four spider corpses in all drop from the ceiling holes across the length of the cave, and from one of the gaps a slow drip of thousands upon thousands of dead ants [Td:703-734]. He spreads a pair of radiant wings and goes along the ceiling to sweep what he had not reached from the floor [Td:719-724]. By the time the circle runs out, the cave is clean of anything that bites.

## 6 - Mis argumentos narrativos
_~00:00?, 5 Hammer_

1. Eguzkilore reaches the caravan and tells Ardred they have found a cave that would shelter them all; it is half an hour by horse [Td:446-479]. Ardred shrugs — the caravaneers are not frightened and are already turning in. If Eguzkilore can convince them, go ahead. Eguzkilore rolls his best persuasion and makes a hash of it; no one lifts their head from a bedroll [Td:481-501].
2. Kaelthar takes him up on the wolf for the ride back [Td:506-508]. At the cave Mialee shows Kaelthar the swept floor and the burning spider corpses outside; Sinsong, straight-faced, tells Kaelthar that Eguzkilore has caught syphilis — he soaked a fairy from above [Td:810-814]. Eguzkilore cannot heal it: too holy for Sinsong's reach yet [Td:816-826].
3. Mialee asks Eguzkilore where the rest of the camp is. He answers with a straight face: *"Mis argumentos narrativos han sido tan magníficos que se han quedado todos durmiendo ya"* [Td:844-847]. The lie goes down without comment.
4. Later, Sinsong tries sending his voice out across the night — *"Ander, venid a la cueva. Los meteoritos están cayendo cada vez más cerca. Quizás no sobreviváis esta noche"* [Td:875-878]. No answer comes back. Eguzkilore, pointedly: *"Están sobaos. Ya te lo he dicho yo"* [Td:887-889]. The four turn in with Eguzkilore on first watch; by the small hours the meteors have thinned to nothing [Td:894-899].

## 7 - A second morning
_~08:00?, 5 Hammer_

1. Kaelthar wakes them for breakfast [Td:903]. Sinsong takes a turn at the storytelling: in his village his old master taught him brews and spells drawn from the plants that grew in their deep cave, and the master is stone now too. The magic itself, he says, comes from his belief that everyone can be made better [Td:943-947].
2. Mialee's turn. She was a child when the people of Waterdeep found her and took her in, and she means to give back to the city that made her what she is — *"un niño ladrón"* [Td:950-955]. Eguzkilore quietly cinches his pack straps in front of him and shuffles his hand to it [Td:956-957].
3. They ride down and rejoin the caravan on the road. Ardred asks how the night went — the stones fell close but not close enough to hurt; some of the workers wanted to go looking for the pieces to sell, but there was no time [Td:995-1005].

## 8 - Ardred on magic iron
_~13:00?, 5 Hammer_

1. Over the midday halt Mialee presses Ardred on the fallen stones: where would she take one to be understood? Any decent blacksmith in Waterdeep or Neverwinter, he says. The iron that falls from the stars runs stronger than the iron drawn from the earth; mixed, it comes out more permeable to magic. He cannot explain the smith's work — he only ever heard the men who did it talking shop [Td:1037-1050].

## 9 - Birds take flight
_~15:00?, 5 Hammer_

1. The afternoon's march is unnaturally quiet — none of the usual bird-noise, especially from the north. When they begin to doubt there is any life in the woods at all, a flock lifts out of a stand of trees three or four hundred feet off, and the party makes out several orcs moving on the caravan in a very bad attempt at stealth [Td:1066-1094].
2. ***[EPIC]*** Eguzkilore takes the first of them before the orc has crossed any ground — one arrow out of the drawn string, through the eye, dead on the first impact [Td:1097-1109]. His second shot into the next orc goes shallower, through the shoulder, and that one breaks into a sprint toward the party [Td:1110-1117].
3. Ardred stands back from the cart and watches. *"Claramente sois buenos combatientes, no creo que necesitéis nuestra ayuda"* — if that proves wrong he will step in, otherwise it is the party's fight [Td:1154-1157]. He does not step in.
4. Kaelthar presses forward and drops the first orc with a single stroke of the longsword, then steps through the mist to the next tree and cuts the second — the stroke lands but does not drop it [Td:1227-1270]. Sinsong flings a bolt of fire that passes Kaelthar's head and sears a nearby tree, but the winter damp will not take the flame [Td:1282-1284]. Mialee drops behind a trunk, breathing, and sinks an arrow into the second orc's back with the quiet-killing shot that is her trade — a one-stroke kill [Td:1286-1294].
5. The whole business runs some twenty seconds, no caravaner hurt [Td:1297-1298]. Ardred claps a slow applause from the cart. *"Magnífico. Me he quedado sorprendido sobre todo con la habilidad de vuestros arqueros"* [Td:1298-1301].

## 10 - Something ate the orc
_~16:00?, 5 Hammer_

1. When the party goes back to the orc bodies to recover arrows, one of them is gone. The ground where it had fallen is turned up — claw-marks, teeth-marks, a smear of blood — the orc has been torn to pieces and dragged. No tracks lead cleanly away [Td:1389-1393]. Sinsong reaches with a locate working at a thousand feet in every direction and finds nothing [Td:1403-1420].
2. Mialee, half to herself: *"Si antes se removieron las arañas que estábamos quemando y ahora se ha removido un cuerpo así de un orco, ¿nos puede estar siguiendo algún bicho?"* [Td:1399-1400 ?ord]. They press the caravan to move faster [Td:1437-1442].

## 11 - On the other body
_~16:30?, 5 Hammer_

1. At the second orc body Sinsong spots them: two creatures the size of mice, devouring the corpse at a rate no mouth that size should manage — fifteen or twenty seconds and the whole orc would be gone [Td:1452-1458]. They are not mice. The description that comes to the party is a shapeless amalgam of broken bones, raw muscle, and strips of hanging skin; each one different from the other [Td:1459-1462].
2. Sinsong throws fire at the nearer one as the first act of the fight; it reels [Td:1463-1465]. The second does not stop eating — the wounds the party puts into it close as it swallows [Td:1489-1492]. A second bolt from Sinsong and two arrows from Eguzkilore bring the first one down; it twitches twice on the ground and does not move again [Td:1493-1510].
3. Mialee reaches with a floating invisible hand to turn the dead one over, and gets a close look — a mouth lined with mismatched teeth, each tooth sprouting from a different kind of flesh. She vomits [Td:1522-1535].
4. The second creature breaks for the first body with its half-formed pincers; Kaelthar and Eguzkilore work it with arrows and it folds, muscle still twitching, into the dust [Td:1510-1549 ?ord]. Mialee pushes both carcasses off the road with the floating hand and cleans the hand [Td:1552-1556]. Eguzkilore refuses to go near either body to recover his arrows; Sinsong lays fire on the pile and they burn [Td:1559-1572]. The caravan beds down for the night without further incident; during the long rest Mialee turns another hour on the parchment she has been puzzling at and finally makes out its school — abjuration [Td:1612-1638].

## 12 - The horse that was taken
_~20:00?, 6 Hammer_

1. A day's march on, the caravan stops to camp beside something strange: a large depression in the dirt in the middle of the road, a wide dried-up blood-stain, and the horse's tackle — straps, yoke, bits — scored with bite-marks but all accounted for. The horse itself is gone [Td:1641-1646, Td:1659-1663].
2. The blood is coagulated; investigation puts the kill between one and five days back. Something tore a horse down here, pulled the carcass free of the gear, and took the body [Td:1655-1662]. Mialee, flatly: *"Estaré atento porque algo mata caballos por aquí"* [Td:1665-1666].

## 13 - Boneleechers at the fire
_~late night, 6 Hammer_

1. Round the fire they hear the same stone-on-stone they had heard before. Mialee, more awake than the rest, sees a small misshapen thing moving through the brush toward the camp — a kin to the two from the orc body — and alerts the party before climbing onto a cart to take cover [Td:1700-1713].
2. Eguzkilore puts an arrow into the nearest; it rocks the thing on its feet but does not drop it. A second arrow does [Td:1716-1731]. Sinsong tries to put a fear on the creatures and it slides straight off them; a bolt of fire does better [Td:1786-1800]. Nyx moves out of cover and takes one down in close with her blade, then plants a shaft into the one beside Nuberroca with a stroke that could not have been placed better [Td:1810-1819]. Nuberroca steps into his fury and goes for the one on him with the halberd; both swings bite only air [Td:1821-1831].
3. One of the creatures lunges at **Fenrir**, Kaelthar's summoned wolf. A single bite and the beast dissolves into smoke, and the Boneleecher falls backward in the dust — then its bones snap and recompose and it is on its feet again as if the fall had not happened [Td:1844-1857].
4. Another springs for Kaelthar on the ground; a bite catches a fold of exposed flesh and tears it [Td:1869-1876]. He feels the pull of it in him — a sickness beneath the wound that his Dragonborn constitution does not quite throw off [Td:1878-1886]. He gets up swinging; the first stroke of the longsword catches on the stones and he loses the second [Td:1898-1902]. Pinned by three of them, he takes a step through the mist to clear the ring and comes up outside their reach [Td:1904-1908].
5. Sinsong calls down his guardians a second time. The light rolls across the three creatures left standing; all three fall together. *"Limpiado"* [Td:1914-1942].
6. He crosses to Kaelthar and wraps him in an embrace. Kaelthar, patting Sinsong once on the back: *"Estoy bien, amigo gay"* [Td:1949-1952]. Kaelthar, back at the fire a minute later, catching the party's eyes: *"¡Qué joder, ya me habéis llevado a la sífilis, cabrones!"* [Td:1962-1964]. The carcasses go into one pile and Sinsong keeps laying fire on them until they are only ash [Td:1972-1996].

## 14 - El tiritas and the fairy
_~03:00?, 7 Hammer_

1. On the first watch Sinsong turns to Eguzkilore and asks about the fairy. Eguzkilore tells it plain — he was in the one place he should not have been, the fairy spoke in her language, and ever since there is a small hollow in him [Td:2015-2020]. Sinsong proposes a rite of apology on the spot, and asks Eguzkilore to beg the fairy's pardon and give a word of praise for Sinsong himself [Td:2024-2031].
2. Eguzkilore, grudging: *"Pido perdón al hada y gracias a las alabanzas de Sinsong, que vaya todo bien"* [Td:2032, Td:2041-2044]. Sinsong's bald head catches a glow, the hollow in Eguzkilore closes up, and the curse is gone [Td:2046-2051]. *"Lo que sea que te pasase ya no hace falta"* [Td:2047-2051].

## 15 - The Metagorger at dawn
_~05:20, 7 Hammer_

1. On the second watch, in the blue before sunrise, Mialee and Kaelthar hear a sound they have heard before — the hollow click of walking bone — louder now, mixed with the chittering screams of the creatures from the orc body. They move out on foot to look [Td:2082-2097].
2. ***[EPIC]*** What Mialee sees between the trees is three to six of the small bone-things fighting over each other — not quarrelling, eating. One is growing. In some fifteen seconds it has gained enough mass to crush and swallow a second and then a third; a fourth tries to flee. The thing that stands up from that meal has four legs with bone sticking through, two pincer-arms where none were before, and a new mouth large enough to use them — then it bounds after the one that fled, catches it, and tears it in half [Td:2100-2122].
3. Mialee slips back and wakes the camp [Td:2126-2130]. The Metagorger comes on at a surprising sprint, and Kaelthar meets its charge inside its reach — a stroke of the longsword takes a pincer off at the joint. The pincer goes into the thing's own mouth and the stump begins to grow back as it swallows [Td:2142-2158].
4. The first bite that comes for Kaelthar misses; the remaining pincer scrapes a weak score down his armour and finds nothing underneath [Td:2167-2171]. Sinsong, pushing up without his armour on, throws fire at the thing and lays a shimmer of warding across himself and Kaelthar in the same breath [Td:2190-2211]. Kaelthar hits it twice, the second with a divine burn under the blade [Td:2214-2224]. Mialee, caught without her bow (dropped from the fright of it), scrambles for it and plants an arrow once she has her hands on it [Td:2229-2230]. Kaelthar, driven: *"Me estoy coronando"* [Td:2258].
5. Two more arrows from Eguzkilore, another bolt from Sinsong, and Kaelthar brings the sword down a last time — the Metagorger folds, deformed, into the dust [Td:2237-2266 ?ord].
6. Sinsong lays hands on Kaelthar and the gash from the bite closes [Td:2268-2271]. A last spasm from the carcass brushes Kaelthar's armour and does not break it; Sinsong puts fire to it and the tree next to it, and the thing burns with the smell of hot chemistry rather than hot flesh [Td:2272-2280]. Mialee, flat: *"¿Cuánto falta para la taberna?"* [Td:2283-2284].

## 16 - Tracks in the mud
_~day, 7 Hammer_

1. Before the party makes a last long rest, Sinsong reaches out to feel for the cart that was stolen from them in Waterdeep. He finds it — in the caravan, with them, right there [Td:2370-2378].
2. The rain comes down hard; the horses slow, the cart-wheels catch in the mud, and the party's boots pick up a stubborn skin of it. The rain's one gift is the tracking. Line after line of small holes along the road match the little bone-things from the fire; longer, lonelier trails match the larger one. At one spot the party finds three sets of the small holes together, and nearby a different print — a half-skeletal foot the size of a tall man's — moving in a group with two of the larger prints. A third kind they have not met [Td:2393-2403].

## 17 - Carnath Roadhouse
_~12:00, 9 Hammer_

1. The last night in camp passes without attack; the heavily armoured ones sleep in their armour both nights running — none the worse for it in the morning [Td:2351-2371, Td:2409-2412]. In the morning the road brings them in sight of a large two-level building that is pointed out as Carnath [Td:2414-2417].
2. At the gate is a semi-orc nearly Nuberroca's size — **Bog Luck**, the one Ardred had told them of back on the first day of the march. The yard is already crowded with their caravan's wagons, going in one by one to be unloaded into the hall; the ground has frozen overnight and the goods will not sit out [Td:2444-2466].
