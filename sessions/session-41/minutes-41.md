## 1 - Round ten on the drum
_~11:00, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Nyx looses a last arrow into the bullywug follower who had been chasing them up the stairs about the true faith, and finishes him on the stones [Td:249-250].
2. Eguzkilore pulls on the longbow, holds a breath, and takes a jumping bullywug clean through the eye at the top of its leap over Nuberroca; the body folds against the parapet [Td:252-273].
3. Mialee leans over the stone lip and shouts in the broken common she has — _"los Bullywugs han revelado contra el culto y contra Tiamat"_ — and tries to pass it off as a lizardfolk truth. The lizards below hear enough to suspect her, no more [Td:280-290].
4. She draws the stones back around her and lays a fog across the drum platform; the drum and the party vanish into the cloud [Td:287-293].

## 2 - Nuberroca at the stairhead

1. Three bullywug lizard-shamans in feathered gear gesture up from the ground; the stone around the platform lifts and ripples. A wave of grinding earth catches Harley, Paco the skeleton and Eguzkilore. Harley drops under the hit; Paco goes down with her and stays down; Eguzkilore holds his footing on the stones [Td:425-464].
2. Nuberroca comes in reckless, polearm swinging, and splits a leaping bullywug champion in two with the first swing; both halves drop behind him and he's bathed in it [Td:362-365 ?ord, Td:489-494].
3. The second swing could kill the lizard beside him. Instead the goliath drives the polearm into the wall, strikes his own chest, and roars _"¡Snapjaw!"_ at the lizard before swinging back and killing the frog beside it instead [Td:493-500]. The spared lizard, unknown to the party, is a Snapjaw loyalist [Td:510-512].
4. ***[EPIC]*** Sinsong, in the ring of harrying light that turns around him on the drum, hurls a bolt of flame into the drum skin from an open hand; the hide catches and begins to smoulder from the edge [Td:513-515].

## 3 - The lizardfolk hold the body

1. More bullywug champions reach the platform. Three champions and a mage press Nuberroca at once, all swinging with advantage because he is surrounded; every attack lands and he falls under the weight of them [Td:781-803, Td:811-814].
2. One of the bigger lizardfolk sprawls across the goliath's chest, keeping the bullywugs off him; the lizards want him alive [Td:816-818, Td:1428-1430].
3. Sinsong, still in melee contact with the goliath, lays a curing touch on him on his reaction; Nuberroca gasps up to a single point of life and does not rise [Td:875-883].
4. The spared Snapjaw-loyal lizard does not make it through the next round — he lies dead on the stones nearby, a quiet casualty of the room [Td:1167-1169].

## 4 - Kaelthar on the wing
_~11:15?, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Kaelthar arrives late to the fight through the front gate. The lizardfolk pressing on Tompu are not pressing hard but will not join; Tompu is taking shoves and open hands and not an outright beating [Td:928-933].
2. Kaelthar steps between them and speaks to the lizards directly, in Draconic — his first language and theirs — a short piece on common enemies and refuges Tompu is being refused [Td:950-971]. The words land further than they should, and the lizards shift on their feet, hesitant, half-dubious [Td:1007-1015].
3. ***[EPIC]*** On the back half of his first turn he unfurls dragonborn wings, launches off the ground, and climbs straight at the second-floor tower window — _"a tomar por culo"_. The giant lizards can scale walls; they will not catch him on the climb and know it [Td:1046-1072].
4. Below him the lizardfolk guard begins to drift away from Tompu toward the tower door, drawn by the fight [Td:1076].

## 5 - The rope above the fog

1. Nyx, invisible inside Mialee's drifting fog, cuts a ten-foot length from her sixty-foot rope with the short sword and raises a Rope Trick straight up through the cloud. She climbs through the slit on the last of her turn and the opening closes behind her [Td:1188-1224].
2. Mialee's fog loses its anchor the moment Nyx passes into the dimensional pocket — concentration cannot cross the border, and the cloud rolls off the platform as it fades [Td:1308-1313].
3. Nuberroca comes to and finds himself free to shift in the breath of the recovery; he uses his species jaunt and reappears beside the tower window [Td:1131-1145 ?ord].
4. The first thing he does from the parapet is roar — _"¡Snapjaw!"_ — out across the castle grounds [Td:1163-1169].

## 6 - The twin acid spheres
_~11:30?, 15 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. A new flight of bullywug mages reaches the tower. The first throws a sphere of green acid over Nuberroca and Kaelthar; the acid rolls over both of them and both are caught in the full of it. Nuberroca drops where he stands [Td:1364-1380].
2. The second mage plants the same spell in the same place. Kaelthar pushes a second-chance moment and shrugs most of the acid off; Nuberroca, unconscious on the stones, takes the dose again at full and his life flickers nearer the edge [Td:1381-1416].
3. A third mage reaches the platform with a running jump, swings a bogged staff into Kaelthar for a heavy blow, and drops the dragonborn to the floor; the lizardfolk around them keep hitting without killing — the order from below is still _"vivos"_ [Td:1422-1430].
4. From her Rope Trick slit, Nyx drops back down the rope, slides across the smoke, and feeds one of her berries to Nuberroca where he lies. He breathes again [Td:1443-1449].

## 7 - Up the rope

1. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca comes to with Nyx on top of him. He pulls her up, runs to the rope with her and drops her at the base so she can climb back into the pocket [Td:1753-1760]. Then he comes back across the stones for Kaelthar — grips him by the tail and drags him to the rope [derived:Td:1795-1818].
2. Nuberroca stamps a foot and shrinks himself to Medium so the carrying weight stops slowing him [Td:1680-1693 ?ord], then climbs the rope with Kaelthar in tow; both reach the pocket at the top [Td:1829-1838].
3. Sinsong does not make it. The lizards press him from all sides inside his still-turning ring of light and begin to drag him off the drum by the hips. Mialee, climbing, calls down to him by his nickname — _"Cabezabombilla"_ — before anyone else notices he is being taken [Td:1864-1876].
4. Mialee pulls the rope up behind her last of all and the slit closes [Td:1875-1883].

## 8 - The elf and the bald man

1. From the Rope Trick slit the party watches the surviving defenders stack the bullywug and lizard corpses on the still-smouldering drum. The elegant elf Dralmorrer gives orders in the courtyard, chewing out the bullywugs for the carnage; a bullywug mage finishes the dissolution with a last acid sphere over the pile [Td:1912-1917, Td:1957-1961].
2. Harley sits silently beside them and cries as her undead burn down to grease on the drum [Td:1963-1967].
3. Beside Dralmorrer a bald man in red robes with four hooded bodyguards steps into the courtyard — pale faces, half-visible tattoos under the hoods. Mialee recognises him from the caravan on the road [Td:1952-1977 ?ord].
4. The bald man looks straight up at the Rope Trick slit, meets eyes with the party, and gives a thin smile; a cultist runs off at his word. Below him more cultists are quietly forming a circle under the slit; they know where the party is and are reading for the blast [Td:1991-2022].

## 9 - The bag of holding

1. Harley opens her Bag of Holding and tells the group to climb in. Mialee, Nyx and Eguzkilore fit — Eguzkilore just — and vanish into the pocket. Nuberroca at eight feet is too big by far, and Kaelthar is only almost small enough [Td:2032-2074].
2. Harley puts invisibility on both Nuberroca and Kaelthar at once with a single casting, then on herself; the Bag goes into Nuberroca's hand [Td:2092-2109].
3. Harley climbs into the Bag with the others, carrying it with her; the three outside are left invisible and holding the pocket [Td:2102-2109].
4. Kaelthar, still invisible, lays a curing touch on the goliath and gives him enough to stand; then takes the rest of his lay-on-hands pool back into his own wounds [Td:2165-2186].

## 10 - The dimension collapses

1. The Rope Trick hour runs out. The floor of the dimension vanishes under their feet; everyone inside drops the ten feet to the stones below — a bruising fall but no worse [Td:2218-2219].
2. Nuberroca cushions his landing with his species jaunt and reappears at the tower window, still invisible, before the mages can pick him out of the air [Td:2199-2203 ?ord, Td:2219-2222].
3. Kaelthar flashes to the same spot in a plume of mist and lands beside the goliath. The casting gives him away — his invisibility snaps the moment the spell forms, where Nuberroca's species jaunt had kept the goliath hidden, and the cultists on the ramparts see him at once [Td:2203-2223].
4. The dragonclaw and his bodyguards swing on the newly-visible dragonborn and land the first hit from the ramparts [Td:2224-2225].

## 11 - Command, chain, and the forest

1. One of the hooded bodyguards speaks a word of command at Kaelthar; the first word slides off him. A second bodyguard follows with the same word, and this one takes. Kaelthar begins to walk toward them against his will [Td:2256-2275].
2. He gets as far as the melee ring and takes the opening swings; the first blow lands cleanly with him pinned on all sides, the next drops him to a single point and he falls unconscious. The cultists seize him before Nuberroca can turn [Td:2371-2402].
3. Nuberroca, still invisible, drops over the parapet for the fall and lands near Juancho and Atascaburras, who have come back up the path for him. He runs ninety feet a turn until the trees cover him and the castle is out of sight behind him [Td:2297-2307 ?ord, Td:2406-2410].
4. In a clearing beyond the forest edge he tips the Bag of Holding out onto the ground like a sack of marbles; the three inside spill out gasping, cold and half-asphyxiated — barely a minute of air left between them [Td:2410-2411, Td:2353-2365].

## 12 - The smithy

1. In the castle, concurrent with the forest escape above, Sinsong wakes to being slapped awake by cultists. His armour, weapon and staff are stripped and piled a few feet away, under guard; Kaelthar's gear is in the same pile. Across the room the dragonborn is unconscious and almost naked, gagged, bleeding but stable — someone has attended his wounds [Tdi:3-6].
2. The room is an old smithy, poorly kept, with many piles of half-finished weapons at the walls. Dralmorrer stands elegant in the middle, keeping his distance so the blood won't reach him; three lizardfolk guards and two cultists hold the two prisoners; near the door stand Azbara Jos — bald, tattooed, red-robed — and two pale hooded men in red with armour and tattoos showing under the hoods [Tdi:7-16].
3. Dralmorrer tells Sinsong not to try to lie; their friend by the door will know. He wants names, abilities, spells, and objectives [Tdi:16-18].
4. Sinsong tells him he is Sinsong, from a mountain dwarf village half of which has been turned to stone, that he has come looking for a cure, and that the dragonborn — _"Kaelthar o algo así"_ — is a travelling companion he barely knows [Tdi:23-31]. The lie-check against the pale guard fails; Sinsong has not sold it [Tdi:33-36].

## 13 - The Red Wizard's questions

1. Azbara Jos crosses the room without quite touching Sinsong and demands to know which spells he uses, how he flew above the drum, how he summoned the creatures that attacked for him [Tdi:97-101].
2. Sinsong tries the gambit from the drum. Flying, he explains, is a procedure of discipline — lie face down and shout _"Mía, ataca"_ at the right moment [Tdi:99-105]. Azbara does not laugh — _"parece que piensas que esto es un juego"_ [Tdi:106-107]. Sinsong admits to the ring of harrying light and the fire-bolt by name, and admits he could not re-raise them now without his focus [Tdi:111-129].
3. Dralmorrer presses him on the lizardfolk ally. Sinsong gives up Tompu's name. Dralmorrer turns and speaks a short phrase in a language Sinsong doesn't know to the lizardfolk guard beside him; the single word _Tompu_ comes through [Tdi:151-157]. The guard confirms Tompu is one of their own fort's lizardfolk — a traitor [Tdi:158-162]. Dralmorrer drives a knife into Sinsong's hand for the lie — Tompu had told the party only that the lizardfolk here were being pressed by the bullywugs [Tdi:163-169].
4. Pressed on their objectives, Sinsong tells him they're looking for friends; one of Dralmorrer's bodyguards dislocates Sinsong's elbow for the evasiveness [Tdi:193-204]. Pressed again on who sent them — the Harpers? the Order of the Gauntlet? — Sinsong points at the gagged Kaelthar: _"el dragón... el Lao Gigante"_ [Tdi:233-249].

## 14 - Pharblex demands blood

1. Pharblex steps into the smithy — a bullywug mage half again the size of any the party has seen, in a crude mitre with necklaces of bones, feathers and dead bugs. He is flanked by two mages and three champions and comes straight at Sinsong with a dagger [Tdi:283-288].
2. Dralmorrer stops him by the wrist. The argument begins at a shout: the party have killed something like thirty-three of Pharblex's bullywugs across the morning, and he wants both their heads. Dralmorrer refuses, and Azbara watches disappointed from the back of the room, as if the whole exchange is beneath him [Tdi:289-294].
3. While they argue, Dralmorrer gives an order to the cultist holding Sinsong — mark them. The cultist takes a dragon-shaped pendant from his neck, picks it up with the smithy's own tongs, and lays it in the forge coals to heat [Tdi:294-296].
4. The argument runs some ten minutes. In the middle of it a cultist arrives with a quiet word; Dralmorrer orders Pharblex out, and the bullywug chief, surprised to be overruled, leaves [Tdi:300-304].

## 15 - Anton of the road

1. Anton — the man the party last saw with the caravan on the road — walks in and sees Sinsong tied. He explodes immediately — _"¡Cabrón! ¡Chicas puñaladas me pegaste!"_ — and lunges, until the Thayan guard puts a knife to his throat [Tdi:309-314].
2. Anton has a short private word with Dralmorrer. He tells him the party killed all his companions on the road. He will nonetheless speak for them: once the interrogation is done, Dralmorrer should let them go [Tdi:321-325].
3. On his way out, Anton turns and smiles at Azbara — _"Salvo que los necesites para tus armas"_ [Tdi:326-327]. Sinsong catches the word — weapons — and starts to ask; the next cultist's palm closes his mouth hard; here the questions come from one side only [Tdi:328-336].

## 16 - Tompu's blood on the blade

1. The smithy door opens again and two cultists drag a bound lizardfolk inside. Dralmorrer looks across to Sinsong: _"¿Este es el Tompu del que hablabas?"_ Sinsong confirms him [Tdi:337-343].
2. Sinsong plays friendly, hoping Tompu's half-connections can save them — _"menos mal que estás aquí"_ — but Tompu is terrified and will not meet his eyes [Tdi:343-348].
3. Dralmorrer walks over to Tompu and tells him, with the elvish quiet that doesn't need volume, how deeply he is disappointed in him — _"con todos los esfuerzos que he hecho para que vosotros recobréis vuestra antigua gloria"_ — then turns to one of the Thayan guards and says only _"todo tuyo"_ [Tdi:353-355].
4. ***[EPIC]*** The guard takes a half-finished blade from a nearby pile — one of the better-made among the bad ones, still unquenched — and sinks it into Tompu's chest. The blade drinks the blood as it enters. Tompu's scream travels through the smithy walls — the same scream the scouts outside will hear [Tdi:356-365]. The guard pulls the blade clear only when the drink is done, then takes Tompu's head and the body is dragged away [Tdi:363-365].

## 17 - The mark of Tiamat

1. ***[EPIC]*** The cultist with the forge tongs lifts the pendant out of the coals — the dragon shape red-hot in the fire — and presses it hard into Sinsong's forehead. The brand holds for a long second before he lifts it off. The mark of Tiamat sits on the Peace cleric's brow [Tdi:366-368].
2. He crosses the room and does the same to Kaelthar; the brand wakes the dragonborn mid-press, screaming into his gag [Tdi:369-371].
3. Sinsong goes under from the pain of the burn — the interrogation continues with Kaelthar alone for another twenty minutes which Sinsong will not remember [Tdi:371-385].

## 18 - A hut in the trees
_~13:00, 15 Hammer 1490 DR?_

1. In a clearing well south of the castle Harley kneels in the dirt, lays down a small crystal orb and a careful ring of stones, and casts her own fuller version of a shelter-dome over them. A ten-minute ritual leaves a transparent semi-sphere around the five of them, opaque from outside, moved as she moves. She tints it to vanish into the forest green [Td:2546-2585].
2. Eguzkilore has no proficiency to craft arrows; Mialee has the Scribe's hands for it. In the hour of the short rest she pulls wood from the forest and makes him sixteen improvised arrows — poor, blunt, but shootable [Td:2619-2680].
3. Nuberroca lies down and sleeps where he stops; Mialee and Nyx argue it is still morning and he will not be moved [Td:2615-2650 ?ord].

## 19 - The scouts and the breach

1. Nyx and Mialee move out together along the forest edge toward the castle. The scream from the castle, when it comes, is a lizardfolk voice — Eguzkilore makes the species through the trees [Td:3002-3003]. Nuberroca immediately guesses — Tompu [Td:3003].
2. On the north-east side of the castle wall Mialee finds a breach in the stones, a possible back way in, though the moat is still in the way of it. They mark it and keep moving [Td:2861-2882 ?ord].
3. From a line of sight on the primary tower, Mialee throws a sending-whisper at the window she can see and reaches first Kaelthar, then Sinsong. Both answer. They are on the first floor; they are watched; they can speak only if they can hide their lips [Td:2758-2810 ?ord].
4. Kaelthar reports what he sees in the smithy: the bald man, two to four Thayan-looking clerics, three lizardfolk guards, Dralmorrer and two bodyguards behind him, and someone else by a door he can't fully see [Td:2792-2799].

## 20 - The council in the trees

1. The scouts rejoin the hut and tell what they have. There is a breach but there is also the moat; the back door is not a free door [Td:2932-2938]. From the trees they can see smoke rising from the first tower — someone, now, is setting fires inside the castle [Td:3030-3037]. Mialee tries another whisper at Kaelthar and Sinsong — _"¿sois vosotros?"_ — and gets no answer back; whoever is lighting fires is not either of them [Td:3039-3049].
2. Nyx offers the opening they have been circling: she will disguise herself as a bullywug and burn one of their huts in open sight of the lizardfolk — enough to light the quarrel between the two camps, and then she will run [Td:2976-2985]. If the castle empties to put the fire down, the back breach may be theirs for the taking.
3. The patrols below are no longer bullywugs or lizardfolk alone — mixed groups now, with a cultist at the head of each. The lesson from the drum has been learned [Td:2525-2534 ?ord].
4. The party decides to wait for dark. Nothing more they can do by daylight — Mialee's recommendation stands [Td:2938-2951]. The session ends with the five of them at the edge of the trees watching the smoke go up from the first tower, and the rest of the plan held for after sundown [Td:3050-3063].
