## 1 - A duel in the yard
_~13:00?, 9 Hammer 1490 DR_

1. Kaelthar connects to a yard already in motion: **Nuberroca** stripped and trading bare-handed strokes with **Bog Luck** in front of a crowd of workers [Td:1-3]. The punches are called non-lethal from the first exchange — this is a prizefight, not a kill [Td:107-108].
2. The two set in. Nuberroca opens with a swing that misses and a second that lands [Td:14-26]; Bog Luck's return runs heavier — a crit through for thirteen [Td:28-33]. A couple of exchanges on and Bog Luck is barely marked, *"fresco como una lechuga"*, while Nuberroca is already visibly tiring [Td:62-63]. The crowd outside is calling for the half-orc — *"¡Gogol! ¡Gogol!"* — and from the back of the yard Mialee is watching her bet come apart [Td:91-93].
3. Nuberroca throws himself into his fury and loses the discipline of the opening — the extra edge opens Bog Luck up but costs him the next return [Td:68-71]. By the time he goes for his next swing he is one hit from going down [Td:117-118]. He calls on a divine favour and thirty points of hurt knit closed in a single breath [Td:128-131].
4. ***[EPIC]*** Nuberroca immediately extends his hand across the ring — *"eres el ganador"* — and calls the fight: he will not use the favour to actually win a bout he does not need to win [Td:132, Td:142-143]. Bog Luck refuses the call outright, spits blood, calls what has already happened a warm-up: *"no ha estado mal el calentamiento"* [Td:133-139]. Then he returns the hand himself: *"me ha sido un placer pelear contigo, eres un buen guerrero, aunque no me ha gustado el uso que has hecho de tus habilidades mágicas"* [Td:140]. Nuberroca, back: *"no son habilidades mágicas, es el poder innato que tengo de los dioses"* [Td:141].
5. The semi-orc gets up on his own and walks off with a touch of unsteady on his step [Td:157-158]; the crowd slaps his back as he passes inside [Td:170-171]. One of the workers brings a cloth to tend him on the way; he waves it off — *"ahora no, hay cosas más importantes"* [Td:172-174].
6. Before the party disperses, Ardred's lot pay out the ride-wages: six pieces of gold each for the march from the ambush-camp to Carnath [Td:161-168].

## 2 - Nap and a message at the tavern
_~14:15, 9 Hammer_

1. Nuberroca retires straight to his room, bars the door with a chair, and sleeps [Td:178-191]. Sinsong takes a short rest at the same hour [Td:189-190]. Food is not part of the caravan's hospitality — the roadhouse serves it from the upstairs tavern at five silver a plate [Td:193-204].
2. Mialee goes upstairs with a cup of milk, picks a table near the fire, and listens for anyone from the cult. The read holds — she picks a message out of the chatter and keeps it to herself for now [Td:208-218].
3. The party have been told they have two days of half-rest before road-work begins; most of the afternoon is still theirs to spend [Td:221-224].

## 3 - A hunt and an eaten elk
_~15:30?, 9 Hammer_

1. Nuberroca proposes a hunt — meat for the kitchen and a diversion for the afternoon. Mialee goes with him; Sinsong comes running up some minutes behind, surprising everyone with a tracker's eye no one knew he had [Td:226-289]. The crowd at the roadhouse cheers them out, hoping for free meat [Td:259-272].
2. The three roll poorly for the woodcraft and spend the first stretch finding nothing [Td:272-285]. Sinsong puts them on an elk trail — hours old, not fresh [Td:287-299]. What they catch first is the smell: rot, and strong [Td:293-298]. Behind a stand of brush is the carcass itself, a bull elk, half-devoured and a few hours dead [Td:299-315].
3. Alongside the large bites that killed the beast, a few smaller missing patches look different — holes smaller than mouths that size should leave, more than one of them, spaced in a cluster that recalls the bone-things from the march up [Td:313-315]. Mialee, flat: *"qué pena que no esté Nyx aquí para descubrir qué tipo de bocado es este"* [Td:316-320].
4. The meat can still be used. Sinsong lays a working on the carcass — a glow at the bald head, a shimmer across the body — and the flies abandon it, except for the small cluster of suspect wounds where they stay interested [Td:338-363]. Nuberroca cuts the suspect flesh out and throws it aside, then opens the belly to finish the gutting [Td:366-379].
5. He wraps three hundred pounds of meat in the elk's own hide, lashes it over his shoulder with his rope, and brings it with them — speed cut to twenty-five with the load, but moving. The horns are winter-ruined and will not fetch [Td:421-440]. Mialee throws a prestidigitation across the lot of him so the party does not have to walk downwind: lavender and rosemary over the raw-meat smell [Td:436-438]. They decide to press on and find whatever made the big bites [Td:442-472] — Nuberroca pointing out to Mialee that the predator's hide might just cover what she lost on the fight [Td:461-462].

## 4 - The owlbear's lair
_~17:00?, 9 Hammer_

1. The trail runs clean for another twenty-five minutes and brings them in sight of a flat spread of ground littered with old carcasses — most devoured past the meat, down to the bones and the horns and the hoofs. A lair [Td:474-488].
2. The occupant stands up on two legs the moment it sees them — an owlbear, white, larger than the usual, roaring and already on the run [Td:530-544]. Sinsong catches up by the sound of it and finds the fight already starting [Td:546-548].
3. Kaelthar engages at the front, longsword drawn [Td:566-568]; he throws up a shield of faith around himself [Td:598], then swings in twice with the longsword and the second cut finds the beast [Td:586-612 ?ord]. Sinsong, with no time to prepare a real working, pulls an oil bottle from his pack, strikes a spark across it, and lobs the lot at a nearby thicket shouting *"¡fireball!"* The flame goes up on the thrown bottle [Td:620-631].
4. Mialee shoots once and finds the beast [Td:633-635], then slips into cover in the brush [Td:636]. Nuberroca waddles up at reduced speed with the full elk still over one shoulder [Td:642, Td:651], sets the short spear's point to the owlbear's flank for an opening strike [Td:649-653], and after a second poke settles on the spear-and-shield to wait for the beast to come at him [Td:671-676].

## 5 - Blood in the lair
_~17:30?, 9 Hammer_

1. ***[EPIC]*** The lair itself begins to come apart. From one of the piled carcasses something assembles — a shapeless amalgam of broken bone and tendon walking on four — and runs not at the party but at the owlbear itself [Td:687-694]. A second comes from the top of the lair and bites the owlbear in the arm; the owlbear screams and starts shaking the limb where the teeth went in [Td:695-699]. A third flanks around behind and lands a crit on it [Td:707-709].
2. One of the small ones peels off and sprints at Nuberroca from behind. It climbs the raised spear with a dexterity no bone-amalgam should have, pins his arm, and finds his bracer rather than the flesh under it [Td:737-748]. A larger kin — halfling-sized now, four legs — rises from the back of the lair and runs at Nuberroca first for being closest, then dashes past him for the owlbear [Td:749-754, Td:761-762].
3. Sinsong plants his feet and calls down the radiant guardians. The circle rolls out from his bald head; the small ones caught inside are struck by the light but none drop on the first pass [Td:782-815].
4. The owlbear's hide begins to show crimson bulges that swell and burst in a twenty-foot ring of fire-blood. The party is caught in it — Kaelthar's dragon-born blood takes the worst of the heat without harm; Nuberroca and Sinsong soak the rest [Td:1191-1222]. Both the shield around Kaelthar and the circle around Sinsong hold through the burn [Td:1223].
5. The large Metagorger closes with the owlbear, bites a strip out of its flank, and swallows — and the party can see the thing's wounds knit closed as it digests [Td:1245-1250]. Another one rises from the far side of the lair, this one closer to human-size [Td:891-895 ?ord], and a third halfling-sized kin tries to break through from the brush later [Td:985-991].
6. Nuberroca sheds the spear and shield to the ground and draws his halberd, going black with his fury [Td:910-912 ?ord]. One stroke light, the next hitting with a shimmer of divine light he turns radiant on the swing — the halberd takes a leg off the near Metagorger and one of the stump-legs drops to the ground [Td:1264-1273].
7. Kaelthar swings a cone of dragon-fire that catches two of the small ones in its spread; the third sits just outside the line and does not. Both the two in the cone take the full of it [Td:717-732 ?ord]. Later he brings the sword down with a divine stroke of light that cleaves a leg off a lingering Metagorger — the thing limps on but will not fall [Td:1442-1446].
8. The last of the Metagorgers takes a bite out of Sinsong before he can shift — a wound that leaves something more than the puncture under the skin, something the dwarf cannot read for himself [Td:1282-1297]. Sinsong closes the surface hurt with his domain's balm and walks the circle, passing the heal along to the others as he moves [Td:1355-1368].
9. Mialee's last arrow comes out of a thicket on the flank, catches the final creature in the neck as it leaps for Kaelthar, and the fight is over [Td:1476-1482]. The owlbear itself went down at some point in the middle of it; by the time anyone looks back it is already dead on the ground [Td:1383-1385].

## 6 - Picking the bones
_~18:30?, 9 Hammer_

1. The lair is still. Most of the owlbear's hide is torn past the point of selling and the carcass is only good for the beak; the carnomorph remains are left where they fell [Td:1484-1510].
2. Among the half-devoured kills is the body of what looks to have been one of the road-workers — gone looking for somewhere to piss and never returned. Nothing of value on him; the party has already been told that anything worth keeping goes into Bog Luck's locked box [Td:1515-1525].
3. Sinsong crosses to Nuberroca and wraps him in an embrace. The bald head glows and a round of hurt knits closed under the hands [Td:1526-1529]. Nuberroca, catching the trick, asks for a second one; the dwarf obliges, but there is no glow on the second embrace, nor on the third — the favour runs out one hug at a time [Td:1530-1542].
4. Sinsong clamps a light-cantrip on Kaelthar's armour as an improvised torch for the return. Kaelthar: *"¿que soy un gruesito?"* [Td:1598-1607]. Only on the way back does Mialee catch what no one burned: *"¿no deberías de haber quemado esos bichejos como haces siempre?"* — too late [Td:1596-1597 ?ord].

## 7 - The elk gone and the walk home
_~19:00?, 9 Hammer_

1. On the way back they pass the site of the morning elk. There is nothing left of it — not the bones, not the tendons, not the horns. The ground is dragged and bloodied, and the woods around rustle with something small or middle-sized that does not quite close with them [Td:1608-1625].
2. Nuberroca wants to look; Mialee refuses outright; Sinsong starts physically shoving him forward: *"corre, corre, puto Nuberroca"* [Td:1631-1646]. They push on and reach Carnath in another hour [Td:1647-1650].

## 8 - Sold at the tavern

2. The elk meat goes to the tavern cook, **Grisel Pit**: one silver a ration [Td:1696-1700], four rations kept back for the party's own dinner [Td:1718-1721], one hundred and forty-two silver for the rest — thirty-five pieces each [Td:1722-1727]. ## 9 - A dorm with four bunks
_~19:30?, 9 Hammer_

1. The warmer rooms are the upper ones — the animals in the pens below leak heat up through the floor [Td:1772-1773]. After some circling and door-slamming the party settle in one four-bunk dorm together [Td:1783-1823]. Sinsong sends word to Eguzkilore and Nyx to join them in the dorm when they can [Td:1855-1858].
2. Nuberroca, bored, winds up and punches the wall to test the stone. The plaster takes a dent; the stone behind does not shift [Td:1807-1818 ?ord]. Sinsong sighs and mends the plaster afterward so no one coming in notices [Td:1819-1820].

## 10 - Signals at the window
_~19:30?, 9 Hammer_

1. Settling into the bunks, Nuberroca cracks the window open and shouts a joke-alarm to the yard — *"¡cuidado, nos atacan!"* — then shuts it and announces he wants to sleep [Td:1861-1867].
2. Almost at once, the sounds come for real: running on the roof above the dorm, three or four pairs of feet; voices up there shouting *"¿dónde? ¿dónde?"*; from the yard below the metal clatter of guards drawing weapons [Td:1868-1878]. Sinsong trusts the guards to it, Nuberroca is spent and will not get up for a lie he already shouted, and Mialee has her own errands waiting [Td:1886-1898].
3. From the glass later they hear the real fight — a cry of pain, the ring of steel on something that was not armour [Td:1960-1961]. Nuberroca opens the window a second time, this time with a warning that actually means something — *"cuidado con esos bichos, si os muerden, os transformáis en ellos"* — closes the window and goes back to bed [Td:1964-1966].

## 11 - The storehouse chest
_~20:00?, 9 Hammer_

1. Mialee asks Sinsong for something that will let her move with less sound. He lays a benediction on her head — a glow at the bald, a cat's grace on her shoulders for the hour [Td:1906-1912, Td:1941-1944]. She slips out of the dorm [Td:1944-1948].
2. She lets herself into the ground-floor storehouse under cover of the yard's distraction [Td:2010-2015]. Inside is the ordinary freight of a road-crew depot — crates of tools, provisions, nothing worth lifting [Td:2017-2020]. A second door is locked; she picks it with her thieves' tools [Td:2021-2027], checks the frame for a trap (none [Td:2033-2039]), and lets it open a hand's breadth [Td:2047-2048].
3. Beyond is a stacked room of crates, chests, and sacks — the valuables Bog Luck had said he keeps apart [Td:2051-2056]. Mialee cannot pick the cult's crates out from any of the others; the cult uses the same freight boxes everyone else does [Td:2052-2056]. She picks the lock of one of the single-standing chests [Td:2079-2086] and finds, inside, coin bags and **two spheres** she cannot name — plainly magical to the hand that touches them [Td:2088-2100].
4. She takes the spheres and the coin, closes the chest, re-locks the lid [Td:2115-2118], slips back out the way she came, and watches a passing patrol climb the stair before stepping into the yard [Td:2155-2157]. She is gone close to half an hour.

## 12 - Eoman's leg
_~21:00?, 9 Hammer_

1. Crossing the yard Mialee sees **Wex** carrying one of the small bone-things on the point of his spear. He stops her: *"¿os habíais encontrado antes con estas cosas? ¿Por qué no habíais avisado?"* [Td:2163-2173]. Mialee says Ardred should have passed the word and that they themselves only ran into them that morning while hunting; Wex throws the carcass into the yard [Td:2174-2186].
2. Wex points her to the kitchen — **Eoman** was bitten and taken up to be tended [Td:2188-2191]. Mialee finds him beside Bog Luck, bleeding out, the better half of one leg severed and lying on the table [Td:2232-2261]. She tries Sinsong's grey stone first — Eoman takes it, drops it to the ground, and gives up on it — and she runs for the dwarf [Td:2201-2213 ?ord].
3. Sinsong comes down in his bedclothes, protesting the whole way [Td:2206-2225]. By the time he arrives the others at the table are stanching the hemorrhage with a tourniquet [Td:2262-2263]; Bog Luck has a hand in his pocket on what the party can see is a vial of red liquid [Td:2269]. Sinsong lays the staff over Eoman and casts; a vast roll, the hemorrhage stops in the same instant, and the muscle begins to re-form around the stump as the party watches [Td:2264-2268 ?ord].
4. Bog Luck slips the vial back into his pocket and takes Sinsong's measure. *"Está bien saber que tenemos un curandero en la venta, puede sernos muy útil"* [Td:2269]. He claps Eoman on the mended leg and sends him off to bed: *"ahora, ya estás fresco, a dormir"* [Td:2270-2273].

## 13 - A benediction for the rats
_~22:00? → 10 Hammer, ~06:30_

1. Sinsong steers Mialee and Eoman to the bar for beers [Td:2280-2290] and asks the barman what plagues the roadhouse. Grisel complains of rats in the walls — months of them, keeping him up [Td:2292-2308]. From the back of the tavern a regular shouts a crude blessing on the dwarf's generosity [Td:2275-2278 ?ord].
2. Sinsong spreads a pair of radiant wings behind him [Td:2310-2312] and walks the whole tavern — common room, kitchen, well-shaft — every pass of the circle killing the small lives inside the beams and the floors [Td:2358-2402]. Dead insects — ants, flies, little spiders, a bloom of aquatic bugs on the well's surface — rain down on the drinkers' heads and cover the water of the well [Td:2374, Td:2402-2405]. One drinker, looking up at the floating dwarf, protests to the room that *"los enanos no vuelan"* [Td:2377]; Mialee, deadpan, points at her mug and tells him *"esto es cerveza"* [Td:2384]. Grisel adjusts the price of beer by one silver *por el espectáculo* [Td:2388].
3. The party finally takes a long rest. The night rolls over without further attack, and the next morning the sun comes up at half past six on 10 Hammer. Road-work will begin the day after [Td:2425-2430].
