# Quick recovery rule

> When you are unconscious and get healed, you can spend your **reaction** and your **next move** to move immediately, generating **no opportunity attacks**. You can only move by **mundane ways** — no special features, actions, nor bonus actions are allowed.

## Application

- **Triggers** when a PC at 0 HP is healed (back to ≥1 HP).
- **Cost:** the PC's reaction + next move.
- **Effect:** the PC can move up to their normal walking speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Restriction:** **mundane movement only.** No Misty Step, no Dash action, no Cunning Action, no class-feature teleports, no Fey Step, no Step of the Wind. Other movement that consumes an action / bonus action / spell slot happens on its own normal cost — it does **not** piggyback on the quick-recovery free move.
