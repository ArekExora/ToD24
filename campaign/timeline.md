# Campaign timeline

_Authoritative chronological anchor of campaign events, populated session-by-session. Inclusion criteria and editorial style live in [Timeline inclusion rules](../.claude/skills/draft/SKILL.md#timeline-inclusion-rules)._

**Columns:** *In-fiction date* in DR calendar form (e.g., *22 Marpenoth 1489 DR*); *Event* as one short line per row — a short title clause plus a context clause, no bullet lists; *Session* is the session (or session range) the event was first recapped from. Entries are appended chronologically by in-fiction date, not by session number.
