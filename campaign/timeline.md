# Campaign timeline

## Arc: Greenest
22 Marpenoth 1489 DR (S01) → 
Greenest is burning, bandits and a blue dragon are raiding the town. The cult of the dragon must be stopped.

- 22 Marp (S01) — Party arrives at **Greenest** during a raid.
- 22 Marp (S01) — **Tarbaw Nighthill** tasks the party with clearing the keep's old tunnel.
- 22 Marp (S01) — **Sinsong** killed by a spider swarm in the tunnel, resurrected at the keep by a cleric.
- 22 - 23 Marp (S02) — Party rescues the civilians sheltering in the **Chauntea temple** at **Tarbaw**'s direction.
- 23 Marp (S02) — **Blue dragon** attacks the keep and withdraws wounded; parting line reveals it was hired.
- 23 Marp (S03) — Party clears a cultist ambush at **Greenest's mill** at **Tarbaw**'s direction.
- 23 Marp (S03) — **Nuberroca** duels **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** at the keep gate; falls but lives.
- 23 Marp (S03) — **Cult of the Dragon** identified as the raiders via the **Tiamat** insignia.
- 23 Marp (S04) — **Tarbaw** tasks the party with scouting the cult camp for motivations, leaders, numbers, and plans.
- 23 Marp (S04) — Party tasked with finding the monk **Leosin Erlanthar**, captured while tracking the cult.
- 23 Marp (S04) — Party recruits the wounded keep guard **Miguel** as guide.
- 23 - 24 Marp (S05) — Cult camp located in the hills south of **Greenest** — strength roughly 200–300.
- 24 Marp (S05) — **Leosin Erlanthar** found and freed at the cult camp.
- 24 Marp (S05) — Cult leadership named — **Rezmir** (black half-dragon chief), **Frulam Mondath**, and an unidentified red-robed mage.
