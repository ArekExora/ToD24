# Campaign ideas

GM-side ideas for future inclusion in the campaign. Not canon until applied. Living document — append as ideas surface, prune when applied or discarded.

## NPC / character ideas

- **Add Druzzo (or other characters from previous campaigns) as key characters.** Use legacy PCs/NPCs from earlier campaigns as recurring or pivotal figures here, giving the table cross-campaign continuity for veteran players. _(Captured: while walking the session calendar, May 2026.)_
- **The real person behind a slain doppelganger.** If any doppelganger the party kills had been impersonating a specific human, the actual human — alive, innocent, and ignorant of the crimes committed in their shape — could later walk into the party's path. Lets a "we already killed you" beat flip into guilt, mistaken identity, or a reputation the real person now has to live down. _(Captured: 2026-10-22.)_

## Encounter ideas

- **Roper rematch.** The party encountered a Roper in the dragon-hatchery cave on 2025-03-23 — they couldn't defeat it and had to retreat after putting it to sleep with a spell. Bring a Roper back later for a return engagement (same monster, or a different one) so the players get a chance to settle the score. _(Captured during calendar walk, originally GM idea.)_
