### Biography

#### The Story behind the shell

"Leo" was born close to Westgate, far East from Baldur's Gate. His kind was not well received in such a small town and his parents ended up dead when he and his 3 brothers were very young. It was then when Sensei Hiroshi rescued them and brought them to his safehouse. It wasn't a castle, but it was well hidden. The 5 of them lived underground for several years.

It was hard to get food, job or gold, but thanks to Hiroshi's knowledge of the Arcane, they managed fairly well.

Although society repudiates them, Hiroshi taught them that every life is precious and that most people deserve to be saved. That's why, from the shadow, they keep other people safe when in danger.

His master, the wise and enigmatic, Sensei Hiroshi, raised "Leo" alongside his three tortle brothers, training them in both martial and magical disciplines to balance their minds and bodies. Each of the brothers excelled in different fields, but "Leo" found his passion in the way of the blade, imbuing their strikes with arcane magic. Amidamaru, while naturally gifted with magic, preferred to combine his swordsmanship with his spells rather than rely solely on crafting magical devices. His master was not only a tutor in magic but also a practitioner of blending magic and martial prowess, which became Amidamaru's philosophy.

One day, Hiroshi woke the brothers with fear in his eyes. An apocalyptic dream has been tormenting him for several weeks and each time is clearer: The world is destroyed by cold, choking fumes, lightning storms, waves of acid and horrible fire. Each time, the dream ends with ten evil eyes glaring at him from the darkness. It's not clear yet when, where or how this will happen, but it's almost inevitable. For that reason we decided to go out and change the story. We parted on 5 different ways, looking for answers and with the promise of reuniting once again to save the world.

#### His way to Baldur's Gate

For several months he was traveling East to find clues about the dreams. He didn't know much about other towns and cities, so he had to learn. The first thing he learnt was that racism was not just happening on Westgate nor only to him. Thankfully, Hiroshi had taught them a good way to avoid the eyes of society: stealth and disguise self.

At some point, it crosses paths with a particular Halfling. Although they didn't share objectives nor past, they shared 1 destination: Adventure. They decided to join forces.

#### The Journey into Wizardry

Leo, a brilliant tortle artificer, always believed that magic and technology could merge into something greater. As a young apprentice, he marveled at the arcane knowledge of wizards but felt constrained by the fragility of traditional spellbooks. Paper burned, ink smudged, and books were bulky — none of which suited his inventive nature.

One fateful expedition led Leo to an ancient ruin where he discovered remnants of an old, forgotten civilization. There, he uncovered a mysterious, arcane-infused device — an **Aetheric Archive** — a mechanical tome with rotating gears, shifting plates, and luminescent runes. This artifact, when attuned to its user, could store spells like a spellbook but through arcane engraving and crystalline memory rather than parchment and ink.

Seeing the potential, Leo modified and perfected the device, integrating it with his own crafting expertise. Instead of scribing spells with ink, he etched them onto enchanted plates using a delicate engraver, allowing him to store, erase, and modify spells with precision. This innovation let him embrace wizardry in a way that resonated with his artificer roots.

Now, as a wizard, Leo charts his own path — not bound by tradition, but forging a new one where technology and magic unite.

- **Appearance:** A sword handle with no blade, forged from polished black steel with brass inlays. A rotary selector runs perpendicular to where the blade would emerge, featuring engraved icons for various cantrips. - **Function:** This handle acts as a multi-functional spellcasting focus. By rotating the selector to the desired icon, the user channels a specific cantrip through the hilt, which projects the magical energy as though it were a blade or ranged force.
- **Flair:** When the selector rotates, the hilt glows faintly in a color corresponding to the chosen cantrip. Magical energy swirls at the end of the handle, ready to be unleashed.

##### Alchemical Mask

- **Appearance:** This mask is a marvel of alchemical ingenuity, crafted from polished bronze and silver. Small vents and tubes line the sides, emitting a soft magical glow. At the center of the mask, a nozzle is designed to project area-effect spells. A rotating dial near the nozzle allows the user to select the type of spell to be cast.
- **Function:** The mask serves as a focus for area-effect spells like *Burning Hands* and *Tasha's Caustic Brew*. The wearer channels magic through the mask, which releases the spell in a controlled spray or burst from the nozzle.
- **Flair:** When a spell is prepared, the runes on the mask glow brightly, and the tubes release a faint hiss of magical vapor. During casting, the nozzle emits a colorful burst of magical energy corresponding to the spell.
