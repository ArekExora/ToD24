# Sulesdeg

## Concept

Seven-and-a-half-feet-tall human bodyguard from a place the Sword Coast does not immediately recognise. Employed as Beyd Sechepol's single guard on the ale cart. Known up and down the road as *The Pole* for his height — shorter than a goliath, but taller than any other man on the caravan.

## Goal

None.

## Appearance

Human; approximately 7'5" tall (~230 cm); from an unfamiliar, exotic-looking origin the party could not place.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Neutral.
- Beyd Sechepol's caravan-employed guard; the only guard on Beyd's ale cart.
- Nicknamed *The Pole* / *el mástil* across the Sword Coast for his height.

## Campaign notes

- 6 Uktar 1489 (S15) — First on-camera on the caravan out of Baldur's Gate. Real name given to Mialee by Beyd when she remarked on his height.
