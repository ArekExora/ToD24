# Noohar Serelim

## Concept

A Moon Elf merchant hauling exquisite wooden carvings made by Cormyr elves. Extraordinarily articulate — talks constantly. Travels with his mute brother **Selvek**.

## Goal

Deliver the Cormyr carving shipment to Waterdeep and see his brother safe on the road.

## Appearance

Not defined.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- Merchant on the Baldur's Gate → Waterdeep caravan; runs one cart.
- Employs **Amidamaru** as bodyguard (10 gp/week), with the standing condition that Amidamaru protects him *and his brother Selvek*. Wages paid week-in-arrears; food and shelter included on the road; paid nights when the caravan stops in a village with a bed.

## Campaign notes

- 6 Uktar 1489 DR (S14) — Contracted **Amidamaru** as bodyguard (10 gp/week) in front of Ackyn Selebon's shop.

## Notes

None.

### Selvek (Noohar's brother)

Mute; communicates only through sign language. Travels always with Noohar on the cart. Part of the standing protection contract that binds Amidamaru to *"protect him and his brother"*. Not yet named a specific role or trade on-camera.
