# Escobert the Red

## Concept

Castellan and master of the Greenest keep.

## Goal

None.

## Appearance

- Shield dwarf.
- Knotted, tangled, bright red hair.
- Carries an enormous ring of iron and brass keys at his belt — keys to every door in the keep.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- In charge of the keep's defense.
- Best source of information on the keep's locations.
- Coordinates the keep's officers during the assault.
- Interrogates captured cultists when the party brings them in.
