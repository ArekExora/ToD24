# Sohey

## Concept

Chief of the Nashkag, the hidden goblin tribe of the Greypeak Mountains. Former adventurer — ran with a Sword Coast gray-market crew called **The Jackals** for roughly ten winters before a sold-out job cost two of them their lives and sent him home to lead his tribe.

## Goal

Keep the tribe alive and rebuild it after the losses to Trepsin's hunt. Hold the alliance with the party against the Cult of the Dragon for as long as it serves the Nashkag.

## Appearance

- Small, lean goblin — fast-moving and light on his feet; moves in combat like a dancer.
- Carries a scimitar and a short sword at his hip; neither is tribal-issue.
- Speaks fluent Common; verbal in and out of combat.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly (allied).
- Chief of the Nashkag ([../factions/nashkag.md](../factions/nashkag.md)).
- Alliance formalised with the party on 17 Hammer 1490 DR (S56–57) after the party turned on Trepsin mid-ambush; sealed with a tribal totem gift, a friendly duel, and a sending-stone pact for a future rematch against the whole party.
- Has given the party 4–5 days of invisibility from the cult (the Nashkag extermination will be reported as *done* to Talis's lodge).

## Backstory

Sohey was not born to the chieftainship. In his youth he slipped out of the Nashkag village — curious, bored, and convinced the world outside the hidden paths was wider than the elders said it was. He was right. His first year alone on the road taught him most of what a goblin boy learns walking the Sword Coast.

Then he tried to steal a bowl of stew from a human in a traveller's camp, and the human — **Oshin Vey**, exiled from a mountain monastery for a quarrel he never named — put a hand on his wrist and gave him the bowl instead. There were four other travellers at the camp that night. They called themselves **The Jackals**.

They were a wandering gray-market crew of five, held together by their founder, the half-elf bard **Branwen Moorcroft** — honey-tongued, long-memoried, the kind of leader who collected strays and called the sum *a company*. The other two were **Durmara Oakshield**, a shield dwarf battlemaster who ran logistics and judged everyone by whether they pulled their share, and **Ysalde**, a tiefling archfey-warlock whose pact she had never named to any of them. None of them cared that Sohey was a goblin; most of them had their own reasons for not caring where strangers came from.

He stayed. His first year he was helper-boy — watching horses, picking locks under Branwen's eye, running messages. Oshin eventually took him on as a **Way-of-Shadow** student, and the darkness-throw, the arrow-catch, and the walking-speed that unsettled the party in the Greypeak woods are Oshin's techniques, Oshin's teaching.

They worked the Sword Coast together for ten winters — jobs they could stomach, buyers who paid, a code that was more felt than spoken. Then came the last job.

A **verified Harper contact** commissioned the Jackals — via Branwen alone; the others never saw their face or heard their name — to lift the **Book of Vile Darkness** from the private vault of the Grand Duke of Baldur's Gate, for delivery to a sealed Harper vault. The job was coded as containment, not profit. Branwen took it.

The vault was warded far past anything the brief had described. The **Flaming Fist** guards killed Oshin and Branwen holding the stair while the other three fled. The book stayed where it was. Sohey, Durmara, and Ysalde got clear and scattered. No one ever came for them afterward — not to pay out, not to make them answer.

All three surviving Jackals know in their bones that the Harper sold them. Branwen died carrying the only name. Sohey has not seen Durmara or Ysalde in the five winters since; he knows the cities they went to — **Waterdeep** and **Luskan** — and nothing further. The scimitar at his hip was Branwen's. He walked back up the hidden paths to the Nashkag village with it and told the elders he was home.

## Campaign notes

- 16 Hammer 1490 DR (S54) — Ambushed the party at their shelter shortly before dawn with a small goblin band; nearly dropped Nuberroca with a Monk flurry (*cultista de mierda*); threw *Darkness* as cover and fled dragging a downed goblin.
- 17 Hammer 1490 DR (S56) — Second ambush turned alliance; Mialee parleyed via *message* mid-fight (*we're not cultists, we want this troll dead too*). Sohey turned every goblin arrow onto Trepsin, held the troll's back through the fight that killed him, and lifted a thumbs-up at Nuberroca as the body fell.
- 17 Hammer 1490 DR (S57) — Mourned his dead with the party watching; sprinted west, east, then south to return every stolen item and carry back a tribal totem as proof-of-extermination for Talis. Traded gifts (magical spectacles to Mialee, magical pen to Eguzkilore, a sealed ceramic jar to the party); fought Nuberroca a friendly round and read the goliath minute-by-minute before Sinsong called it off. Agreed to a future rematch via sending-stone; promised 4–5 days of cult-invisibility.
