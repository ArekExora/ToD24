# Echo

## Concept

Mialee's familiar — a small bat summoned via *Find Familiar*. Used as a forward scout in tight spaces where Mialee's own stealth can't reach.

## Goal

None.

## Appearance

- Small bat; eyes give off no light. Hangs from rafters and ceiling beams when stationary.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- Scout / sensory extender for Mialee. Carries Mialee's *blindsight* perception range into corridors and chambers ahead of the party.
- Communicates by telepathic link with Mialee (within 100 ft, per *Find Familiar*).

## Campaign notes

- 26 Marpenoth 1489 (S9) — First mention at the table. Sent ahead through the corridor to scout the upper barracks; returns the count *five kobolds and one drake roosting on the old barracks beds*, the cult re-occupying the chamber after Frulam's departure.
