## Campaign notes

- 23 Marpenoth 1489 (S3) — Name overheard from a cultist mid-mill-fight.
- 24 Marpenoth 1489 (S5) — Named under interrogation by a captured cult hunter as a mage whose right hand is Langdedrosa Cyanwrath, herself answering to Rezmir. Identified as one of four figures emerging from the central tent during the distraction fire — visually consistent with the *fourth figure* in the group (the half-mage, half-scholar human caster), not with the red-robed Thay-style mage who cast the spiritual weapon on Sinsong. Not engaged.

- 25–26 Marpenoth 1489 (S7) — Pursues the retreating party out of the corridor (Sinsong's Spiritual Weapon called against her again, missed). Did not reappear in the dawn re-entry skirmish. Whereabouts unknown to the party. Status alive.
