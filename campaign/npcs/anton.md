# Anton

## Concept

A quiet traveller riding with Darla and Gus the humble's family cart in the Waterdeep-bound caravan.

## Goal

None.

## Appearance

Not defined.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Hostile.
- Travelled with Darla and Gus the humble's family cart as cover; revealed on 28 Nightal 1489 (S27) as a Cult of the Dragon infiltrator.
- At the caravan's final night camp before Waterdeep, stole one of the party's wagons and cut the horses loose while an ally mage attacked the camp.

## Campaign notes

- 12 Uktar 1489 (S19) — Travelling with Darla and Gus the humble on their cart when the party was fired at *The Wayside Rest*; the family takes Nuberroca on as guard for a share of their food.
- 25 Nightal 1489 (S26) — At the camp melee, Nuberroca's halberd backswing killed Gus; Darla was revealed as a doppelganger impersonator and left the cart in combat with the party. Anton's own position at session close is off-stage.
- 28 Nightal 1489 DR (S27) — Revealed as a Cult of the Dragon infiltrator; stole one of the party's wagons at dawn after the Azbara Jos attack, cut the caravan's horses loose, and vanished on the Trade Way four hours short of Waterdeep.
