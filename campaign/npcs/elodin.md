# Elodin

## Concept

Elf warlock hunting Sinsong. Face tattooed; knives threaded through the stitching of his clothes. Travels light and alone.

## Goal

Kill Sinsong.

## Appearance

- Elf, mounted on a hard-ridden horse.
- Tattoos across the face.
- Knives threaded through the stitching of his clothing.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Hostile.
- Independent tracker with a personal grudge against Sinsong.

## Campaign notes

- 23 Nightal 1489 DR (S25) — Rode up to the Daggerford traffic hunting a dwarf with a tree tattoo (Sinsong). Did not stay with the caravan despite Nuberroca's invitation; left a message asking the party to tell Sinsong that *Elodin* is looking for him.
