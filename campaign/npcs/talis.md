# Talis the White

## Concept

Cult of the Dragon sub-cell leader; runs the Hunting Lodge as a cult waypoint in the mountains east of Castle Naerytar. Receives orders from Dralmorrer and reports upward toward Rezmir.

## Goal

Clear the Nashkag goblin tribe from the surrounding country and keep the lodge functioning as a cult waypoint under Dralmorrer's chain of command.

## Appearance

- Half-elf; pale-skinned; pointed ears.
- Full white plate armour.
- Carries a lance in one hand and a staff of command in the other.
- Speaks a tongue the party does not recognise — a single word stills (or wakes) the lodge's gargoyle sentries.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Wary.
- Cult sub-cell leader at the Hunting Lodge; commands guards, a cultist door-courier (Kusphia), and Trepsin's drake patrols.
- Received the party as reinforcements supposedly sent by Dralmorrer; handed them the mission to wipe out the Nashkag.
- Quietly clocked them when their counter-sign to "Glory to Tiamat" came back wrong — reads them as not actually of the cult, but has not yet acted on the suspicion.

## Campaign notes

- 16 Hammer 1490 DR (S55) — First met by the party at the Hunting Lodge; posed as cult reinforcements sent by Dralmorrer, briefed on the Nashkag goblin problem, and tasked with wiping out the tribe by morning. Flicker of surprise when the lodge's gargoyles failed to subdue Nuberroca on first command.
