# Eldkin Agetul

## Concept

Caravan-veteran shield dwarf hired guard. Reputation up and down the road for having fought bandits and, once, a troll — enough that other merchants recognise her by name.

## Goal

None.

## Appearance

Shield-dwarf woman; caravan-veteran build.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- Caravan-employed guard on Lai Angesstun's cart.
- Perfectionist with the animals — rubs the horses down clean, walks them to cool off at halts.
- Sneaks dried meat from her own bag into Lai's rice-and-beans pot to salvage the flavour, knowing him for a skinflint.
- Thinks she — not Nyx — should have been sergeant on this trip, given her road experience.

## Campaign notes

- 6 Uktar 1489 (S15) — First on-camera on the caravan out of Baldur's Gate. Introduced herself to Nyx and voiced that her road experience should have earned her the sergeant slot instead.
