# Jorath Vexundar

## Concept

Leader of the Company of the Boar, a professional adventuring party plying the Sword Coast. Self-styled "slayer of villains without number".

## Goal

Not defined.

## Appearance

- Human; dresses in fine clothes.
- Wears a pendant with a very large tusk against his chest.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Neutral.
- Leader of the Company of the Boar ([../factions/company-of-the-boar.md](../factions/company-of-the-boar.md)).
- Pitches the company for hire on sight to merchants and caravans.

## Campaign notes

- 11 Uktar 1489 DR (S18) — Met at *El Reposo en el Camino*; climbed on a table to announce the company. Sold Nyx the "troll trick" for one gold piece, revealing the tusk pendant.
