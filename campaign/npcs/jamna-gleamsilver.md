# Jamna Gleamsilver

## Concept

Small gnome traveller attached to the Waterdeep-bound caravan at Daggerford; carries multiple daggers, watches the party closely, and openly names a cult infiltration of the convoy.

## Goal

Identify what the Cult of the Dragon is hauling north on the Trade Way and where it is headed, in time to deliver the intel to her handlers before the caravan reaches Waterdeep.

## Appearance

- Small, unassuming build; moves and sits quietly in the convoy line.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Wary.
- Travels with the caravan on the Trade Way from Daggerford.
- Watches the party openly from her seat in the convoy; probes for intel on the lead two wagons and on the gagged cultist strapped to Nuberroca.
- Pulled a bone thorn-ring out of Nuberroca's porridge at breakfast and told the party, plainly, that someone had tried to murder them.

## Campaign notes

- 22 Nightal 1489 (S25) — Attached to the caravan at Daggerford for the departure north.
- 25 Nightal 1489 (S26) — At breakfast on the second camp beyond Daggerford, lifted a bone thorn-ring out of Nuberroca's porridge and named an attempted murder; called a nightfall meeting with the party. Clammed up when Sinsong cast Zone of Truth around them.

## Notes

- Mission brief (module): Zhentarim leadership tasked her with uncovering what the cult caravan is hauling and its destination. She argued her superiors out of Plan A (mercenaries-disguised-as-bandits ransacking the caravan and torturing cultists for intel) in exchange for a subtler run of her own. **Plan B is still live: if she has not produced useful intel by Waterdeep, the Zhent heavies move on the convoy.**
- Common-enemy angle: Zhentarim and Harpers both oppose the Cult of the Dragon; the two organisations tolerate each other on this axis despite the gulf in outlook. This is the cover story Jamna will lean on when she needs the party on-side.
- GM-only: the S26 thorn-ring "assassination attempt" was a Jamna sleight-of-hand — the bone thorn-ring was on her dagger the whole time; she slid the blade into Nuberroca's porridge and "pulled it out" as if finding it there. Pure trick to earn the party's trust. There is no outside assassin.
