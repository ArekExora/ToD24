# Varram the White

## Concept

Cult of the Dragon **wyrmspeaker** — one of the few who holds parley with the dragons themselves. Craggnor named him on-camera as his direct superior; Talis kept a dossier on him. Operates out of Parnast in the Greypeak foothills.

## Goal

Not defined.

## Appearance

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Hostile.
- Senior Cult leadership; above Talis's cell head in the chain of command.
- Craggnor was his personal lieutenant and friend.
- Working out of Parnast — Craggnor would not say what the cult's business there was, and insisted he did not know Varram's current whereabouts.

## Campaign notes

- 17 Hammer 1490 DR (S58) — First named at the table. Talis kept a written dossier on him in her writing-room; Mialee recovered the journal after the lodge was cleared. Craggnor, under interrogation in the lodge cellar, confirmed Varram's rank and relationship before Mialee cut his throat.
