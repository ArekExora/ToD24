# Leosin Erlanthar

## Concept

Half-elf Harper monk from Berdusk; expert on the Cult of the Dragon.

## Goal

Investigate and counter the Cult of the Dragon's operations across the Sword Coast.

## Appearance

Not defined.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- Harper investigator; mission-giver against the Cult.
- Carries a broken silver-dragon (Bahamut) pendant on a leather cord — a recognition token among allies.

## Campaign notes

- 23 Marpenoth 1489 (S4) — Flagged as missing / captured / infiltrated; intercepted by the Cult outside Greenest the previous night. His apprentice delivers the broken Bahamut pendant to the party as identification if Leosin is found.
- 24 Marpenoth 1489 (S5) — First appearance at the table. Found tied to a stake at the cult camp; explained under Nyx's *Message* that he had let himself be captured to infiltrate. Freed by Mialee's invisible mage hand picking the knots from the cliff rim and Sinsong's healing (boosted further than the spell's normal range). Slipped into a tent of his own accord and disappeared; fate after escape unknown to the party.
- 25 Marpenoth 1489 (S6) — Returns to Greenest after his own escape; debriefs the party and tasks them with intel on the eastern cave ("hatchery"), reward 150 gp per head. Departs north to Elturel to report to his order; fallback contact Ontharr Frume of the Order of the Gauntlet.
