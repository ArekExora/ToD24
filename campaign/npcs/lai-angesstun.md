# Lai Angesstun

## Concept

A Gold Dwarf oil-and-perfume merchant hauling scented cooking oil and perfumes from Amn to Waterdeep aristocrats. Short-handed, agitated, and always in a hurry — talks about money constantly and won't buy anyone else a drink or a roasted turnip for the whole trip.

## Goal

Get his cart to Waterdeep on time and turn a profit on the aristocrat trade.

## Appearance

Not defined.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- Merchant on the Baldur's Gate → Waterdeep caravan; runs one cart.
- Employs **Kaelthar** as bodyguard (10 gp/week), **Nyx** as sergeant (8 gp/week), and **Eguzkilore** as guard under Nyx (5 gp/week). Wages paid week-in-arrears; food and shelter included on the road; paid nights when the caravan stops in a village with a bed.
- Paired with caravan-employed guard **Eldkin Agetul**.

## Campaign notes

- 6 Uktar 1489 DR (S14) — Contracted **Kaelthar** (bodyguard), **Nyx** (sergeant) and **Eguzkilore** (guard) in front of Ackyn Selebon's shop.
