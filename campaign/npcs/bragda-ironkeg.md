# Bragda Ironkeg

## Concept

A Dwarf field medic in a battered apron, pouring divine healing the way she once poured ale. Her brewery is ash, her workers buried with it; what's left of her keeps Moradin's hands busy on the battlefield.

## Goal

Keep her adopted party alive long enough to break the Cult of the Dragon and witness one last sunrise over a liberated taproom.

## Appearance

- Scuffed half-plate worn over a brewer's apron.
- Holy symbol of Moradin stamped on the plate rather than worn as a pendant.
- Smells of malt and medicinal herbs.
- Moves slowly, except when rushing to heal — then unnervingly fast.
- Warm amber-gold eyes, thick auburn locks, ruddy tan skin.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- Field medic for the party, acting under Moradin's final call.
- Hidden-companion role — the players are not currently aware of her presence; she tends wounds between scenes rather than in open view.

## Campaign notes

- 22 Marpenoth 1489 (S1) — Began travelling with the party as hidden field medic, after Moradin's cave-in vision called her out of the brewery.

## Notes

- **Hidden from the party.** Bragda is GM-controlled and travels with the party, but the players are not currently aware of her existence. Her actions in scenes should not surface in player-facing recaps or in-character interactions until the GM signals otherwise.

### Personality traits

Motherly but weary. Forces food and drink on everyone. Refers to healing spells as "a top-off" and death as "tapping the final keg." Speaks little, watches constantly.

### Biography

Bragda ran the Keg & Anvil brewery until a natural cave-in buried her cellar for three days. Trapped in the dark, Moradin showed her visions of the Cult of the Dragon marching across the surface — towns burning, chromatic dragons darkening the sky, and Tiamat's shadow swallowing the mountains. The god didn't save her to brew ale; he saved her because healers would be scarce in the wars to come.

She dug herself out with her tankard to find the Cult had razed her village while she was buried, taking her stockpiles for their war-effort and leaving her workers in ash. Now she marches with the party as their field medic, pouring her remaining reserves into keeping them alive long enough to break the Cult. She knows the tankard's blessing is temporary – Moradin only keeps it full until the final battle. She's fine with that. She just wants to see one more sunrise over a liberated taproom before she taps out.
