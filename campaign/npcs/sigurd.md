# Sigurd

## Concept

A scholar-wizard hunting the lost grimoires of **Felstad** — the mythological city of mages buried eons ago under a blizzard of epic proportions, frozen past recognition and never found again. Travels the Sword Coast under **Lord Zelan**'s escort, keeping to private rooms and leaving the road to his noble sword-arm. Severely uneasy around strangers — freezes under unannounced contact and offloads anything that needs a face to Zelan. Reaches the rooms his aversion keeps him out of through his **Awakened Spellbook**'s *Manifest Mind*, which rides in the small lantern Zelan carries.

## Goal

Recover the grimoires of the lost city of **Felstad**.

## Appearance

- Rich robes.
- Hooded; face unseen by the party.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Wary
- Principal of Lord Zelan's travelling retinue — [**Zelan**](zelan.md) commands the guards and the road; Sigurd commands Zelan.
- Keeps to private rooms; operates through Zelan when engaging with people.
- Freezes with strangers — unannounced contact (incl. magical whispers) is likely to yield silence rather than a response.
- Casts remotely through his Awakened Spellbook's *Manifest Mind* — the "tiny blue sage" in Zelan's lantern.

## Campaign notes

- 11–12 Nightal 1489 (S23) — At Barlon's tavern on the Trade Way; observed reading upstairs by **Munin** and ignored **Mialee**'s breakfast *Message*; party departed 13 Nightal without meeting him.

## Notes

- The party's guess that Sigurd matches the Company of the Boar's warlock ([**Thorvin Caldorath**](thorvin-caldorath.md)) is wrong — he is not of the Boar.
- **Felstad** is a mythological lost city of wizards, frozen and destroyed in an eon-old blizzard and never recovered; its grimoires are the object of Sigurd's current search.
- Social-aversion. Any scene that forces Sigurd into unexpected direct contact should default to overwhelm / shutdown rather than engagement.
- *Manifest Mind* flavour: the spellbook's intellect projects as a tiny blue sage riding inside Zelan's lantern. Mechanically it still functions as the Order of the Scribes subclass feature — spells can originate from its position, within range of Sigurd.
