# Carlon Amoffel

## Concept

Harper agent who rides infiltrated inside cult-run merchant caravans on the Trade Way, tracing the stolen cargo north.

## Goal

Expose the Cult of the Dragon's merchant-convoy pipeline between Baldur's Gate and the north, and get word to the Harpers in Waterdeep.

## Appearance

- Harper tattoo — harp within a crescent moon — on bare skin above the shoulders, visible only when his shirt is off.
- Full beard.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- Attached to the party's caravan as of 27 Uktar 1489 DR.
- Opens the Waterdeep Harper channel — names **Vauser** at *The Broken Jar* as his contact in the city.
- Carries a prepared cover story (broken engagement to a bandit's daughter, brothers tracking him down for refusing the trade) for use in front of unaligned merchants.

## Campaign notes

- 27 Uktar 1489 (S22) — First appearance. Found buried to the neck at the Trade Way crossroads with *"Oathbreaker"* painted on his forehead, left there by merchants his own cult-infiltrated caravan had turned against him on the cultists' word. Rescued by **Nuberroca**, healed by **Amidamaru**; held to his broken-engagement cover until **Nyx** and **Mialee** drew him aside at the next halt and identified as Harpers, at which the cover fell away — he named **Leosin** before being prompted and gave **Vauser** as a Waterdeep fallback. Agreed to ride with the caravan as third Harper on the hunt.
