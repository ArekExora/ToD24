# Rezmir the Black

## Concept

Black Wyrmspeaker of the Cult of the Dragon. Chief of the Greenest-area camp; senior commander above Frulam Mondath and Langdedrosa Cyanwrath.

## Goal

Advance the Cult of the Dragon's Tiamat-summoning plan — gather hoards, deliver them to the Wearer of Purple, and protect the cult's chain of operations.

## Appearance

Not defined.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Hostile.
- One of the five Wyrmspeakers of the Cult of the Dragon (Black, per draconic colour).
- Operational chief of the Greenest-area cult camp — Frulam Mondath and Langdedrosa Cyanwrath both answer to her.
- Does not engage personally unless escalated; relies on Frulam, Langdedrosa, and allied half-dragons / dragons to handle field combat.

## Campaign notes

- 24 Marpenoth 1489 (S5) — Named to the party for the first time, under interrogation of a captured cult hunter, as the chief of the Greenest-area camp. Sighted (un-named at the time) emerging from the central tent of the camp during the party's distraction fire alongside Langdedrosa Cyanwrath, Frulam Mondath, and Azbara Jos. Not engaged.
