# Trepsin

## Concept

Four-armed troll, enforcer at Talis the White's hunting lodge in the Greypeak Mountains. Demon-worshipper (Baphomet); commands the lodge's pack of ambush drakes. Standing orders from Talis to exterminate the Nashkag goblin tribe — a task he has pursued since the cult took the lodge, without yet finding their village, and which he treats as personal sport as much as duty.

## Goal

The hunt.

## Appearance

- Massive four-armed troll, all shoulder and reach.
- Muddy cape kept perpetually damp with well-water; camouflage in wet woodland.
- Trophies strung along the harness and belt.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Hostile.
- The lodge's bouncer and enforcer — asks for the cult recognition signal at the door and eats those who cannot give it.
- Master of the ambush drakes kennelled at the lodge; the drakes obey him as pack leader.
- Standing orders from Talis to hunt down and exterminate the Nashkag ([../factions/nashkag.md](../factions/nashkag.md)).

## Campaign notes

- 17 Hammer 1490 DR (S56) — Killed in the Greypeak woods during the Nashkag hunt. Felled by Eguzkilore's longbow (shaft through the throat) and finished by Nuberroca's lance to the back of the neck, with the goblin tribe fighting alongside the party against him. Corpse and severed arms burned in the clearing to prevent regeneration; moss cloak stripped and examined (no magic).

## Notes

- **Homebrew upgrade to CR 10.** The module's four-armed troll variant is CR 6. This campaign's Trepsin is bumped to CR 10 (5.900 XP). Diffs against a standard troll listed below (base stats otherwise unchanged).

## Diffs against a standard troll

Apply these to the Monster Manual troll stat block; everything else stays as printed.

- **Challenge:** 5 → **10** (XP 1.800 → 5.900).
- **Armour Class:** 15 → **16** (+1 natural armour).
- **Hit Points:** 84 → **195**.
- **Strength:** 18 → **20** (recompute Athletics, Str-based attack and damage bonuses accordingly).
- **Constitution:** 20 → **22** (recompute Con saves and HD accordingly).
- **Regeneration:** 10 hp per turn → **20 hp per turn**. Fire and acid still shut it off for one round as normal.
- **Damage Resistances (new):** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from non-magical attacks.
- **Multiattack (module four-arm variant, preserved):** three attacks (1 bite + 2 claws) → **five attacks (1 bite + 4 claws)**. If two or more claws hit the same target on his turn, the target takes an extra **2d6 slashing damage** from the rending grip.
- **Speed:** 30ft → **40ft**.
