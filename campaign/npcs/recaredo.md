# Recaredo

## Concept

Captain of the Order of the Companion, one of the two militant orders that jointly run Elturel alongside the Hellriders.

## Goal

None.

## Appearance

- Large, armoured; the two-suns emblem of Elturel on his breastplate.
- Fights with a greatsword.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Neutral.
- Captain of the Order of the Companion, seated at the fortress that holds the city's prisoners.
- Delivers judgement on killings committed within Elturel.

## Campaign notes

- 2 Uktar 1489 (S13) — Received Ontharr's intercession at the fortress over the market killing; beat Nuberroca down non-lethally in the cells as a lesson in non-lethal damage; released the three prisoners.
