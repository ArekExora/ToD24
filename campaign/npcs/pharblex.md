# Pharblex Spattergoo

## Concept

Rare bullywug shaman — mastered a mishmash of confused tradition, borrowed mysticism, hallucinogenic intoxicants and manufactured lore. Chief of the bullywug band serving the Cult of the Dragon at Castle Naerytar; his spellcasting holds his followers together, which is what makes him useful to Rezmir.

## Goal

When Tiamat returns, be handed Castle Naerytar by Rezmir and rule the entire Mere of Dead Men from it. Short-term, remove Dralmorrer Borngray — the castle's current master — from the picture by any means necessary when the time is right. Will switch sides to save his own skin if the cult operation at the castle looks doomed.

## Appearance

- Bullywug of larger-than-average build; two-handed staff; carried into audiences by an escort of bullywug guards.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Hostile.
- Bullywug chief at Castle Naerytar; shamanic / druidic caster.
- Lives in the subterranean breeding chamber beneath the castle (castle barracks areas 1G / 2G per module), accessed through the castle doors or via an underwater route from the moat.
- Flanked by an escort at all times; party audiences are reserved for the breeding chamber, not the surface village.
- Fears Rezmir more than he fears any adventurers; the fear is what keeps him loyal.
