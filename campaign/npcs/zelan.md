# Lord Zelan

## Concept

A travelling noble, sword-arm to [**Sigurd**](sigurd.md) — the scholar-wizard he escorts along the Sword Coast. Bought out Barlon's tavern on the Trade Way for his retinue, keeping to the upper rooms. Carries a small glass-paned lantern housing a tiny blue-skinned wizened sage — the *Manifest Mind* of Sigurd's Awakened Spellbook, through which Sigurd casts into the rooms Zelan walks.

## Goal

Not defined.

## Appearance

- Noble, elegantly dressed.
- Red cap.
- Light armour hidden under his clothing.
- Carries a small glass-paned lantern housing a tiny blue-skinned wizened sage — [**Sigurd**](sigurd.md)'s *Manifest Mind*.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Wary
- Sword-arm of [**Sigurd**](sigurd.md); travels the Sword Coast at his principal's direction.
- Travels the Trade Way with three guards.

## Campaign notes

- 11–12 Nightal 1489 (S23) — First met at Barlon's tavern on the Trade Way: bought out the house for his retinue, insulted the caravan from his table, and let the lantern's sage (Sigurd's *Manifest Mind*) break **Nuberroca** in a duel fought by his guards. Paid two more days at the tavern when the party departed 13 Nightal.
