# Langdedrosa Cyanwrath

## Concept

Blue half-dragon champion. Dueling-honour type.

## Goal

Champion the Cult in honorable combat; prove personal martial supremacy.

## Appearance

Not defined.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Hostile.
- Field champion / lieutenant under Frulam Mondath.
- Spear and greatsword fighter; lightning breath weapon.
- Honors single-combat conventions; spares opponents he defeats and frees their hostages per the duel terms.

## Campaign notes

- 23 Marpenoth 1489 (S3) — Defeats Nuberroca in a duel at the Greenest keep gate, stabbing him on the ground before withdrawing.
- 24 Marpenoth 1489 (S5) — Sighted at the central tent during the distraction fire; not engaged. Captured cult hunter confirms his identity, his subordination to Frulam Mondath (Frulam's right hand, herself answering to Rezmir), and the descriptor of him as honour-bound and dangerous to anger.
- 25 Marpenoth 1489 (S6) — First re-engagement since the Greenest duel. Shields Frulam in the corridor; recognises Nuberroca by sight; breathes lightning down the corridor, forcing the party's retreat. Status alive.
- 25–26 Marpenoth 1489 (S7) — Pursues the retreating party out of the cave; Fog Cloud breaks the chase and he returns to the cave with the berserkers. Pre-dawn rematch in the first chamber: ambushes Mialee from behind a column, dueled by Nuberroca via Cloud's Jaunt flank, downed by Nyx's arrow. Initially captured alive — unconscious, bound by Nuberroca — but **dies before the party leaves the chamber**, failing his death saves while bound; GM-verified off-record (transcript missed the explicit *NO* to Nuberroca's "¿sigue respirando?" check). Last words before falling: *"no eres más que un maldito cobarde que necesita de muchos compañeros para abatirme."* Nuberroca strips the great two-handed sword from the body before leaving; the corpse stays in the cave intact.
- 26 Marpenoth 1489 (S9) — Nuberroca returns to the corpse during the watch-cave retreat, confirms the half-dragon is dead, and **decapitates him** with the great-two-handed sword. The severed head is tied visibly to his belt as a trophy: *"Si me ven, hago un kobold más para que lo vea."* The head is brandished in the egg-chamber ambush later that morning to break a kobold pack with a Primal-Knowledge Intimidation roll.
