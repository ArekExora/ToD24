# Azbara Jos

## Concept

A Red Wizard of Thay attached to the Cult of the Dragon as Thay's eyes on the Tiamat-summoning plan. Casts at distance, watches more than he fights, and reports back to Szass Tam's circle.

## Goal

Monitor the Cult of the Dragon's hoard-gathering and Tiamat plans on behalf of Thay; intervene only when Thayan interest demands it.

## Appearance

- Tall human male.
- Shaved scalp, tattooed in the style of the Red Wizards of Thay.
- Red robes — Eastern cut, distinctly not Sword Coast.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Hostile.
- Thayan envoy / observer embedded with the cult; not formally part of the cult's chain of command.
- Field caster — favours conjuration and ranged spell pressure (e.g. Spiritual Weapon) over melee.
- Cult rank-and-file are kept ignorant of his identity — captured cultists deny knowing his name.
