# Beyd Sechepol

## Concept

Well-spoken half-elf ale merchant who works the Baldur's Gate ↔ Waterdeep road as a moving tavern. Not brokered by any local merchant guild — he walks in on his own, sells beer to caravans and travellers along the way, and makes his living from the road itself rather than from a destination market.

## Goal

None.

## Appearance

Half-elf; well-spoken; educated bearing.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Friendly.
- Independent ale merchant travelling on the caravan.
- Sells beer at 10cp/jar en route, versus 2cp/jar back in the city — the road is his margin.
- Runs this road often.
- Travels with one guard, Sulesdeg (*The Pole*).

## Campaign notes

- 6 Uktar 1489 (S15) — Joined the caravan out of Baldur's Gate.
