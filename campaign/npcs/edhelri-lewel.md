# Edhelri Lewel

## Concept

A Moon Elf merchant hauling exotic wood from the Jungle of Chult to Waterdeep master carpenters. Impatient with people, exacting about her wagon, and doting on her animals.

## Goal

Deliver the Chultan wood shipment to Waterdeep intact.

## Appearance

Not defined.

## Role and party stance

- **Stance:** Neutral.
- Merchant on the Baldur's Gate → Waterdeep caravan; runs two carts.
- Employs **Mialee** as bodyguard (10 gp/week) and **Nuberroca** as sergeant (8 gp/week). Wages paid week-in-arrears; food and shelter included on the road; paid nights when the caravan stops in a village with a bed.

## Campaign notes

- 6 Uktar 1489 DR (S14) — Contracted **Mialee** (bodyguard) and **Nuberroca** (sergeant) in front of Ackyn Selebon's shop.
