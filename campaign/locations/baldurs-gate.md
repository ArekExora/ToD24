# Baldur's Gate

Major walled port city on the Chionthar, downstream of Elturel. A mountain rises above the city; the docks sit at the river.

## Blushing Mermaid

Portside tavern near the docks. Room and full board 3 gp/night; packed and loud, private back-room available for hire.

## Black Dragon Gate

Northern gate of the city. Working area of [Ackyn Selebon](../npcs/ackyn-selebon.md), a merchant.
