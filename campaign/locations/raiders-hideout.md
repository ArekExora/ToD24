# Raiders hideout

Cult of the Dragon staging site in the hills south of Greenest, ~12 miles south of town. Active loot-stockpile camp and likely jumping-off point for further raids on the Greenfields. Located and surveyed by the party during session 5; senior cadre identified (Rezmir as chief, Frulam Mondath, Langdedrosa Cyanwrath, plus an unidentified red-robed mage).

The site comprises two connected parts. The cultist camp sits in a horseshoe of high hills — two terraces wrapped around a lower bowl. A makeshift gate parapet at the entrance holds two crossbowmen behind cover and four guards on the path. Stunted watchtowers and a single proper tower break the skyline at the bowl's heart. Tents and torch-fires are scattered across the floor. Concentric tiers run from outer to inner: a triage zone for incoming wounded, a kobold quarter (kobolds and human cultists keep socially separate), a human-cultist quarter with bone-and-hide shelters daubed with crude five-headed dragon symbols, and a permanent central-tent precinct at the east end ringed by drake-handlers. Strength was estimated at 200–300 cultists at this site, plus attached hunter-scouts (not formal members). By session 6 the surface camp had been broken down — the kobold quarter burned to the ground, the lean-tos torched along their entire run, the better-built shelters and the central tower still standing but stripped; the cult had dispersed in many small parties through the main gate, with the heavier-loaded tracks all bearing west.

At the bowl's eastern end is a heavily-guarded cave, into which cultists in heavy packs streamed and came back without their loads — the camp's treasure stockpile and probable ritual-offering site. Entered in session 6: past the threshold the corridor opens into a wider hall lit purple by knee-high and person-tall fungi (necrotic on contact). Beyond the fungi chamber is a corridor with a broken-down storeroom off the right side and a broader chamber at the end, the latter held by Frulam Mondath, Langdedrosa Cyanwrath, and at least four barbarian berserkers. Leosin Erlanthar calls the cave a "hatchery"; the term itself is what he most wants verified.

Lower level (descended in session 8). Beneath Frulam Mondath's quarters — an elegantly appointed two-room suite (a bed, two chests, a writing table laid with cult notes and a map, and a bookshelf) — a concealed trapdoor under a yellow rug drops a 30-foot chute to the level below. The chute lands in a Tiamat shrine: three walls and the floor tooled smooth and carved with bas-relief dragons (disproportionately many of them black), a vast five-headed Tiamat statue climbing from an erupting volcano, and a decorated wooden chest at its foot wired to an acid trap — striking the chest sprays acid from the carved black-dragon mouths and fills the chamber with toxic vapour. North of the shrine, an antechamber with a key on a wall-hook opens through a barred iron gate into the dragon hatchery: three metre-tall eggs in bloodstained straw, guarded by drakes and kobolds, with a Roper lurking disguised as a stone column. By session's end the party had destroyed two of the three eggs, recovered the (now lock-broken) chest, and retreated up the chute — leaving the Roper asleep behind the locked iron gate.
