# Waterdeep

Great port city on the Sword Coast.

## Overview

Walled city. Guards queue incoming caravans at the arches and collect per-head fees. Civil quarter is unfriendly to dragonborn and loose beasts — hooding required at the gate.

## Faction presence

- **Harpers** — Calem Varis' *Lily's Shadow* sub-cell based at Mialee's old orphanage.

## Campaign notes

- 29 Nightal 1489 DR (S28) — Elodin confronted the party at the south gate; one city guard killed in the plaza; Elodin teleport-escaped before arrest.
