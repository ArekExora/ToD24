# Skyreach Castle

A flying fortress of enchanted, stone-hard ice built in antiquity by cloud giants to fight dragons from the sky. Walls, towers, and two courtyards layered over a hollowed iceberg core that serves as the white dragon's lair; the whole thing can veil itself in fog and cloud, or drift with the wind. Currently docked in a fog-shrouded ravine outside [Parnast](parnast.md) and being loaded with looted treasure for shipment to the Well of Dragons; departure hangs on [Rezmir](../npcs/rezmir.md)'s word. Owned by the cloud giant **Blagothkus**, a grudging host to the cult — he plays along because he hopes the rise of dragons will rouse giantkind back into action against them; the castle itself is piloted by his late wife's spirit, **Esclarotta**, from a sealed steering tower only a giant can enter. Gate pass-phrases (and the five-coloured cult banner flown on a lance) mark a bearer as friendly: *"Tiamat, Our Mother and Strength"* or *"Hail Blagothkus"*.

## Who's aboard

- **Blagothkus**, cloud giant, plus two ogre stewards and a bound air elemental in his keep-top bedroom
- **Wiglof and Hulda**, stone-giant guests of Blagothkus holed up in their hall off the lower courtyard, mid-augury about the cult alliance
- **[Rezmir](../npcs/rezmir.md)**, Wyrmspeaker, from a locked suite — her iron-banded chest carries the Black Dragon Mask and teleports to the Well of Dragons the moment she dies
- **Rath Modar**, Red Wizard of Thay, running summoning-logistics from his skull-ceilinged study; invisibility + fly escape via a 1-minute wall-vanish onto an outer platform
- **[Azbara Jos](../npcs/azbara-jos.md)**, Rath's Red Wizard associate
- **Sandesyl Morgia**, moon-elf vampire of the old Sammaster faction, patrols both courtyards at night from a crumbling tower where her coffin sits with two consort spawn
- **Glazhael the Cloudchaser**, adult white dragon, lairs in the ice tunnels above the cult's main hoard; vain, dim, fanatically devoted to Tiamat
- Garrison: around 24 ogres between gate towers, honour-guard, and barracks; ~10 cult dragonwings resting on duty; ~20 kobold cooks under a griffon enforcer; ~20 kobold servants dorming off the upper courtyard

## Layout at a glance

- **Lower courtyard** — fog-covered at night; main gate flanked by two stone-golem cloud-giant statues (trigger on unspoken pass-phrase); two gate towers topped by ogres and ballistae; off it: stables (two more wyverns), cultist barracks, stone-giants' hall, Rezmir's suite, Rath Modar's study, Azbara's quarters, storeroom, kitchen
- **Upper courtyard** — windswept open landing deck for aerial mounts; ogre daytime drills, Sandesyl's night patrol
- **Blagothkus's blue tower** — iron-locked (Blagothkus's only key); hollow 90-ft cylinder with no stairs or ladders; anyone inside who speaks *"Esclarotta"* is teleported to Esclarotta's tomb
- **Steering tower** — a separate tower, arcane-locked (giant or *knock* only — DC 70 Str otherwise); Esclarotta's spirit operates 18 command runes in Dwarvish/Giant and can lock out abusers; ripping out the control jewels (10,000 gp) sends the castle drifting northward to crash on the Miklos Glacier in the Spine of the World
- **Esclarotta's tomb** — buried ice cyst with her marble sarcophagus; no physical entry — reached only via the blue tower's *"Esclarotta"* teleport, and left by speaking *"Blagothkus"* to return to the blue tower's ground floor
- **Blagothkus's keep** — three-level tower on top of the castle; Blagothkus's bedroom and his coin-and-gold-bar chests on top
- **Ice tunnels / main vault** — hollowed through the iceberg core; Glazhael's lair opens onto a two-level cavern where the cult's hoard is frozen into the floor (500k cp, 100k sp, 5k gp, 800 pp, 21 sapphires, +1 gear)

Crashing or seizing the castle ends *Hoard of the Dragon Queen* and lifts the party to 8th level.
