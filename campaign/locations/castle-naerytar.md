# Castle Naerytar

The castle is a large, old stone keep rising from the swamp — wooden roofs mostly staved in, most windows broken or boarded over, ringed by a moat. From the west three groups of outbuildings are visible: a stockaded enclosure to the south, two large buildings in the centre outside the walls, and a cluster of mud-and-filth hovels to the left.
