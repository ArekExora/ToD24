# Greenest

Small frontier town in the Greenfields. Site of the opening Cult of the Dragon raid (22–23 Marpenoth 1489 DR), where the party first entered play. Governed by [Tarbaw Nighthill](../npcs/tarbaw-nighthill.md); the keep's defense is run by [Escobert the Red](../npcs/escobert.md). [Miguel](../npcs/miguel.md), a young keep guard, is recruited as a guide in session 4.

## Greenest keep

Fortified stronghold at the heart of Greenest, site of most of the ToD ch. 1 action. Includes the inner courtyard, the **lower tunnel** (started by the party in session 1 — partially cleared, retreated mid-clear after Sinsong's death; finished and rehabilitated as a covert sortie route in session 2), the **sally port** (a small sortie-gate breached by cultists in session 3 and subsequently repaired by Amidamaru), the wall and front gate, and the barracks.

## Greenest temple

Temple of Chauntea, Greenest's main shrine. Surrounded by an external wall with front and back doors; sanctuary interior in the center. Sheltered the town's panicked civilians during the cultist assault in session 2 — front-door ram team battered through, back-door arson detail produced a heavy smoke cloud, and a perimeter patrol circled outside. The party broke the arson detail, calmed and extracted the civilians under an illusion at the front entry, and brought them safely to the keep. Run by [Eadyan Falconmoon](#), half-elf priest of Chauntea (no NPC file — one-scene cameo).

## Greenest mill

A water-mill on the river. Two-story stone-and-timber structure: the millstone on the lower floor, flour bags and grain sacks plus pulley access on the upper. Site of the **mill ambush** (session 3) — the cult set white-smoking wet straw upstairs as a deliberate lure, then 10 attackers hidden in the rafters opened with a spear-and-arrow volley before dropping into melee (several wearing keep livery as deception). The party survived, killed nine of the ambushers, and recovered a Tiamat insignia and a cult map from the corpses.

## Greenest hills

Hill country south of Greenest, ~12 miles / four hours' march. Terrain shifts from cultivated farmland into rolling knolls, then progressively steeper rocky hills with caves, sparse herd-pasture, and no settled villages — country well-suited to a cult hideout. The X on the recovered cult map (session 3) sits in this region. Scouted south by the party in session 4: cleared a four-cultist plus ~10-kobold camp ~3 hours south, retreated from a defile picket (cultists in matching black livery, two acolytes, one veteran) ~30 minutes shy of the X, alerting the cult ahead.
