# Elturel

Walled city on the Trade Way, protected by a permanent light, the **sun of Elturel**, hanging in the sky above the region — a device that keeps undead from Elturgard.

## A Pair of Black Antlers

Tavern on the main street, under a stag's-head sign with the antlers painted black. Local gathering house of the Order of the Gauntlet; [Ontharr Frume](../npcs/ontharr-frume.md) is the local leader.
