# Daggerford

Walled castle town on the north bank of the Delimbiyr, and the largest settlement on the Trade Way between Baldur's Gate and Waterdeep. Shallow water upstream makes it the trans-shipment point where river cargo swaps onto caravans and vice versa, giving a modest farming community outsized traffic. Capital of the Duchy of Daggerford, ruled by a Duke and a Council of Guilds.
