# Hunting Lodge

A large stone-ground, timber-upper hunting lodge in the snowy mountain forest east of the Castle Naerytar teleport circles. Previously the hunting seat of Lord Marsten; a few months before the party's arrival the lodge was handed over to Talis la Blanca, who holds it as a cult waypoint under Dralmorrer's chain of command out of Naerytar. The entry hall is flanked by two animated gargoyle sentries that answer to a single word in an unknown tongue; the upper storey carries a hall of five enormous tapestries dominated by a gold-thread Tiamat of five heads. Staffed by a human steward (Gastyn), a dwarven housekeeper (Arlaenga), and a cowed kobold work-crew. Trepsin, Talis's four-armed moss-skinned warden, keeps the drake patrols from the grounds.
