# Hunting Lodge

A large stone-ground, timber-upper hunting lodge high in the Greypeak Mountains, at the end of a cluster of ancient standing-stone portals (one from Castle Naerytar's dungeon, three others re-tuned by the cult to a northern white-dragon lair, Thay, and Mulhorand). Previously the hunting seat of Lord Marsten; a few months before the party's arrival the lodge was handed over to [Talis the White](../npcs/talis.md), who holds it as a cult waypoint under [Dralmorrer](../npcs/dralmorrer.md)'s chain of command out of Naerytar. Parnast and Skyreach Castle lie about fifty miles away across the mountain range — four days on foot through difficult, broken terrain (steep forested slopes, scree, creek crossings). Staffed by a resentful trio of Marsten's inherited servants (Gastyn the butler, Arlaenga the head maid, Angrath Woodwise the chief groom) and a cowed kobold work-crew of two dozen; [Trepsin](../npcs/trepsin.md), Talis's four-armed troll warden, keeps the drake kennel and (presently empty) wyvern stables out back. A mated pair of perytons nests on the roof above Talis's chamber.

The entry hall is flanked by two animated gargoyle sentries and two cold-rune-warded suits of elven plate (both triggered by anyone entering with weapon drawn). The upstairs **feast hall**, now set aside for cult gatherings, is dominated by five enormous tapestries — four showing dragons of different colours hunting across identifiable Sword Coast locations, one a 35-ft gold-and-silver-threaded Tiamat on the south wall. Three prisoners are chained in the **cellar**: Craggnor (a dwarf cult operative, friend of Varram, held for interrogation), Brother Caemon (a priest of Amaunator being softened up by Talis), and an unnamed young woman from Baldur's Gate who stumbled into cult business in Parnast.
