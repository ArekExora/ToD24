# Parnast

A small logging village under covert cult control, tucked in the Greypeaks about fifty miles from Talis's [hunting lodge](hunting-lodge.md) — four days on foot through difficult, broken terrain. A few dozen houses around a central square (well, tavern, stable, shrine, wheelwright); the villagers are either paid-up cultists, intimidated collaborators, or cowed holdouts. **Captain Othelstan** — a cult veteran and dragonsoul, [Rezmir](../npcs/rezmir.md)'s deputy — commands the garrison and monitors all comings and goings; he has one veteran lieutenant, six guards, and three dragonclaws on call. Parnast is Skyreach Castle's dockyard and supply pipeline: the castle is parked in a fog-shrouded ravine on the village's outskirts until Rezmir orders it airborne, and wagons of loot and provisions move between village and gate through the fog all day. Pass-phrases for the castle approach: *"Tiamat, Our Mother and Strength"* or *"Hail Blagothkus"*.

## Golden Tankard

Rustic tavern on the square, yellow-tankard sign over the door. Owner **Raggnar Redtooth** is secretly on the cult's take — serves travellers ale and stalls them with hospitality while quietly dispatching a runner to Othelstan. Treats as an unarmoured veteran (AC 10); keeps his weapons behind the bar. Named for his tankard of plenty, a magic item he found years ago and still uses as a serving stein.

## Stable

Shuttered and "out of business" — actually houses two trained wyverns the cult uses to reach the castle in flight. An occasional roar from inside gives them away to anyone listening at the doors.

## Shrine

Small wooden shrine to the four seasonal deities. Angharradh (spring, elven) has had her face and hands chopped off; a dead squirrel lies at Auril's feet; Mielikki's statue has been freshly painted and set with fresh offerings — the cult's working favourite. Chauntea unmolested.

## Wheelwright

Small shop on the square run by **Gundalin the Wheelwright** — the one villager actively willing to help outsiders against the cult, though terrified of being seen. Knows the pass-phrases and the whole rumour chain; speaks only in safe corners.
