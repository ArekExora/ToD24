# La Sombra del Lirio (The Lily's Shadow)

A Harper sub-faction operating from the Waterdeep orphanage. Specializes in espionage and the recovery of secrets and riches from Waterdeep's elite. Funds itself by feeding orphans and training them as informants, pickpockets, and spies.

## Names

- **In-fiction (Spanish):** La Sombra del Lirio
- **English:** The Lily's Shadow

## Headquarters

The orphanage in Waterdeep. Nodes also exist in Neverwinter, Baldur's Gate, and Athkatla.

## Structure

- **Los Dedos** ("Fingers") — children aged 8–15; field operatives.
- **Las Manos** ("Hands") — adult coordinators who train and supervise the Fingers.
- **El Puño** ("Fist") — Calem Varis, the cell leader and Harper liaison.

## Members in the campaign

- **Brother Calem Varis** — founder of the Waterdeep orphanage; head of the cell; senior Harper contact.
- **Mialee** ([../pcs/mialee.md](../pcs/mialee.md)) — sits at the Las Manos tier.

## Emblem and recognition

A stylized lily, often worn as a small tattoo on the inner right wrist. Recognition sign: with the left hand, two fingers (middle + index) touched first to the right wrist (over the tattoo), then to the heart.
