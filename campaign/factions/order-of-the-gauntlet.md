# Order of the Gauntlet

A militant order of paladins, clerics, and dedicated warriors sworn to oppose evil directly across the Sword Coast. Sister organisation to the Harpers — allied in spirit, more direct in method.

## Names

- **In-fiction (Spanish):** La Orden del Guantelete
- **English:** Order of the Gauntlet

## Mission and methods

- Confront evil in the field; back the oppressed; back just authorities.
- Prefer open action over subterfuge.

## ToD-era role

Active partner of the Harpers in disrupting the Cult of the Dragon's hoard-gathering operation.
