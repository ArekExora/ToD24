# Harpers

An ancient, secret organization dedicated to preserving knowledge, opposing tyranny, and maintaining balance of power across the Realms. Operates as a network of small autonomous cells; agents trust their own judgment in the field.

## Names

- **In-fiction (Spanish):** Los Arpistas
- **English:** Harpers

## Mission and methods

- Preserve lore and history; oppose tyrants; act subtly to keep any single power from dominating.
- Prefer infiltration, intelligence work, and targeted intervention over open battle.
- Allied in spirit with the Order of the Gauntlet (paladin-led, more direct), but methods often diverge.

## Symbol

A silver harp on a black background, sometimes paired with a crescent moon.

## ToD-era role

Active investigators of the Cult of the Dragon's hoard-gathering operation across the Sword Coast. Track caravans, identify Wyrmspeakers, and cultivate allies (PC parties among them) to disrupt cult plans.
