# Cult of the Dragon

A fanatical organization founded centuries ago by the archmage Sammaster, devoted to the supremacy of dragons over the Realms. Now reinterpreted under modern leadership: the goal is to summon **Tiamat** from the Nine Hells and usher in an age of chromatic dragon rule.

## Names

- **In-fiction (Spanish):** Culto del Dragón
- **English:** Cult of the Dragon

## Origin

Founded by **Sammaster** based on his prophecy: *"the dead dragons shall rule the world entire."* Historically interpreted as creating dracoliches. Under current leadership, reinterpreted: living chromatic dragons (and Tiamat) are to rule, fueled by tribute hoards.

## Leadership

- **Severin Silrajin**, *Wearer of Purple* — overall cult leader. Architect of the modern reinterpretation and the Tiamat-summoning plan.
- **Five Wyrmspeakers**, one per chromatic color (red, blue, green, black, white), each operationally responsible for a strand of the cult's work.

## ToD-era role

The cult is gathering **massive hoards of treasure** across the Sword Coast as ritual material to summon Tiamat from the Nine Hells (eventually, at the Well of Dragons). The party encounters this operation in its early stages — raids, hoard caravans, hatchery operations.

## Tactics

- Cult fanatics (true believers) and Cult of the Dragon acolytes/priests for spellcasting.
- **Kobolds** as drudges, porters, and shock troops; treated as inferiors by the human cultists.
- Mercenary half-dragons (e.g., Langdedrosa Cyanwrath) and allied chromatic dragons.
- Iconography: purple robes, dragon-tooth jewelry.

## Ideology (as the party has heard it)

- The world should be ruled by those best prepared — the dragons.
- Sacrifices are necessary for a just end.
- Loyalty to **Tiamat** is supreme: *"Fate is decided only by Tiamat."*

## Internal politics

- **17 Hammer 1490 DR (S56)** — **Talis the White** openly brokered against **Varram** and **Rezmir** with the party, trading a counter-mission for a Thayan spellbook; evidence of a cell-level schism inside the Wyrmspeaker tier.

## Operations near Greenest

- **Raiders' camp** ([../locations/raiders-hideout.md](../locations/raiders-hideout.md)). Forward staging base for the Greenest assault, located in the hills south of Greenest (confirmed S5; matches the **X** on the cult map recovered from the Greenest mill ambush in session 3). Horseshoe-shaped plateau, single fortified gate, concentric tiers inside (triage, kobold quarter, human-cultist quarter, leadership tents at the east end), and a heavily-guarded eastern cave used as the treasure stockpile / ritual-offering site. Site strength estimated at **200–300 cultists** at this camp, plus attached hunter-scouts who are not formal cult members. By session 6 the surface camp has been broken down — kobold quarter burned, leaders gone, parties dispersed in many small groups through the main gate, with heavier-loaded tracks all bearing west. The eastern cave remains as the cult's deeper hideout; **Frulam Mondath** has since slipped away (S8) and **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath** is dead (killed at end of S7); Leosin Erlanthar calls it a *"hatchery"*.
- **Chain of command at this camp** (per S5 interrogation of a captured hunter): **Rezmir** (chief, black half-dragon, female) over **Frulam Mondath** (mage tier, human caster), whose right hand is **Langdedrosa Cyanwrath**. The un-named **red-robed mage** ([Azbara Jos](../npcs/azbara-jos.md), undisclosed at the table) operates alongside but outside the named chain — the captured hunter pretended not to know him; Nyx's insight read the denial as a lie.
- **Regional movement.** Tracks observed south of Greenest in S4 indicate ~400–500+ raiders moved through the area during the Greenest raid — consistent with the camp's S5 head-count plus the assault force that descended on the town.
- **Alert status.** As of session 6, the surface camp has been emptied as part of the cult's broader withdrawal. The eastern cave remains held — the four hunters posted at the cave mouth waved the party off without engaging, then mounted and rode east.
- **Hatchery damage (S8).** Two of the three dragon eggs held at the lower-level egg chamber were destroyed by the party (Nuberroca cracked each open with the greatsword, killing the half-formed hatchlings as they emerged); the third egg was set alight in its straw but the shell remained intact.
- **Hatchery cleared (S9).** The **last of the three dragon eggs was destroyed** — Mialee cracked it open in the hatchery and burned the nest-straw. On the same descent the party re-triggered the Tiamat-shrine acid trap, killing most of the lower level's resident kobolds (four fled). The cult had **re-occupied the upper barracks** since S8 (a drake and five kobolds settling in), all cleared by the party — the site was still operational on-site after Frulam Mondath's departure.

## Operations at Castle Naerytar

- **Castle Naerytar** ([../locations/castle-naerytar.md](../locations/castle-naerytar.md)) — fortified cult-held site in the Mere of Dead Men. First reached by the party **15 Hammer 1490 DR (S38)**.
- Used by the cult to transport treasure and goods onward via a teleportation circle sited within the castle.

## Identification by the party

- **Session 3 (23 Marpenoth 1489 DR)** — the party recovered a Tiamat insignia and a cult map from the Greenest mill ambush. General religion lore at the table identifies the symbol; the attackers are **confirmed as the Cult of the Dragon**. The map shows an X marked southeast of Greenest — the party reads it as a likely point of interest but does not yet know it is the raiders' camp.
