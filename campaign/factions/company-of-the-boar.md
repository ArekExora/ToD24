# Compañía del Jabalí (Company of the Boar)

A rival adventuring company encountered on the Trade Way during the party's caravan leg to Waterdeep.

## Names

- **In-fiction (Spanish):** Compañía del Jabalí
- **English:** Company of the Boar

## Reputation

Self-advertised at *El Reposo en el Camino* as having killed a good count of trolls, a lich in the Realms of the Dead, and a giants-threat in the mountains. The pitch is a hire-on-the-spot sales line delivered to the assembled merchants.

## Members in the campaign

- **Jorath Vexundar** — leader.
- **Kaldan Voss** — paladin.
- **Thorvin Caldorath** — warlock.
- **Khorvalis** — barbarian.

## Party stance

Rival / competitive, hostile after S20. First contact at *El Reposo en el Camino* on 11 Uktar 1489 DR (S18).

## Campaign notes

- 17 Uktar 1489 DR (S20) — Abandoned the caravan during the overnight troll ambush. All four members and four of the caravan's horses missing when the party counted the camp after the fight. Watches two and three (the Compañía's) had been uncovered when the attack came. Trust: broken; alignment relative to the party is now hostile.
