# Nashkag ("the Hidden Ones")

A hidden goblin tribe living in a concealed village high in the Greypeak Mountains, in the forested hills around Talis the White's hunting lodge.

## Origin

The tribe has occupied its current village in the Greypeak Mountains for a long time — long enough that no living Nashkag remembers a different home.

## Way of life

- **Hidden and settled.** The village lies in a concealed spot in the mountains and does not migrate. Concealment is the tribe's oldest survival practice; the paths to the village are marked only in ways a Nashkag reads.
- **Hunter-gatherer.** Mountain hunting, foraging, and small subsistence plots. A small herd of mountain goats.
- **Council under the chief.** The chief (currently Sohey) speaks for the tribe; decisions of consequence are taken with a council of elders.
- **Hospitality within, hostility without.** Outsiders who reach the village unarmed are heard out; outsiders in the surrounding forest are either avoided or, when the odds allow, killed.

## Population

Approximately three hundred souls in total, of whom roughly one hundred are trained warriors.

## Territory

A concealed village high in the Greypeak Mountains, several hours' hard climb from Talis the White's hunting lodge. Trepsin has hunted the surrounding forest since the cult took the lodge without yet locating the village itself.

## Relationships

- **Trepsin, the four-armed troll ([../npcs/trepsin.md](../npcs/trepsin.md)).** Enemy. Trepsin hunts Nashkag for sport under standing orders from Talis. In the months since the cult took the lodge he has already killed dozens of the tribe — a rate of attrition the Nashkag have not faced before, and one they cannot sustain indefinitely. The tribe would trade a great deal for his death.
- **Cult of the Dragon.** Opportunistic prey. Cult couriers moving treasure between Talis's lodge and Parnast cross Nashkag territory; when Nashkag scouts spot a courier party small enough to be overwhelmed with certainty of no losses, the tribe ambushes and takes the goods back to the village. Larger cult movements are avoided. The cult knows the tribe exists but has never located the village.
- **Talis the White.** Head of the hunting lodge, Trepsin's master, effective architect of the Nashkag's persecution. The tribe has never seen her face but they know her name and role from ambushed cult couriers.
- **The party.** No prior contact.

## Knowledge of the Cult of the Dragon

What the Nashkag have pieced together from ambushed couriers, interrogated stragglers, and scouts watching the lodge from cover:

- The lodge belongs to a cultist called Talis.
- The cultists move treasure — chests, wrapped bundles, and occasional live prisoners — along a small number of forest trails between the lodge and a village to the north-east (Parnast, though the tribe does not always name it).
- The cultists worship dragons and expect one to *return*; the tribe has heard the phrase *"they shall rise"* often enough from dying couriers to recognise it as a password of some kind.
- Cultists sometimes arrive at the lodge from *elsewhere* — appearing near the lodge with no approach through the forest. The tribe correctly suspects magic but has no detail.
