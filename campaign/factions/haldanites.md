# Hijos de Halda (Sons of Halda / Haldanites)

A nomadic Halfling tribe currently traveling the Sword Coast.

## Names

- **In-fiction (Spanish):** Hijos de Halda; also Haldanos, Haldanitas
- **English:** Sons of Halda, Haldanites

## Origin

Originated in Luiren several centuries ago. Their namesake, Halda, is described in oral tradition as a Halfling "strong as a small pony, tough as an ox", credited with absurd feats. Most assume the legend has more myth than fact; some hold that "Halda" was a group rather than a single Halfling. The tradition is oral, transmitted from elders to children at the campfire, and has distorted over centuries. The tribe carries the name proudly regardless.

## Way of life

- **Nomadic.** Migrate seasonally to follow resources across multiple seasonal sites. Sustained by gathering, animal husbandry, and trade. Spend brief periods in towns and cities along their routes for trade and supply.
- **Council of elders.** No rigid hierarchy. Important decisions go to the council, with the eldest holding the final word.
- **Stewardship of nature.** Plant trees when they cut, plant fruit trees in places they intend to revisit, manage animal populations carefully, avoid scarce species.

## Population

Membership oscillates between 30 and 60. Members occasionally leave to settle in towns; new members occasionally join. Departure is permitted but generally disapproved of.

## Emblem

A stylized fern. Origin unknown. Represents resistance, resilience, and independence — the tribe rejects political and social ties and considers itself unbound by civilization.

## Faith

Members generally venerate the Halfling pantheon, the **Children of Yondalla**. Most prominent:

- **Yondalla** — mother of the pantheon and of Halflings. Cornucopia on a shield.
- **Sheela Peryroyl** — minor goddess of agriculture, love, and weather. Daisy.
- **Cyrrollalee** — goddess of friendship, trust, hospitality, home. Open door.
- **Urogalan** — god of earth and death. Dog's head.
