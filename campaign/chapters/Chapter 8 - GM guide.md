# Chapter 8 — Castle in the Clouds, GM guide

## The chapter in a paragraph

Parnast is a small logging village on the lee of the Greypeaks, secretly in the cult's grip. In a fog-shrouded ravine on the village's outskirts sits **Skyreach Castle** — a flying fortress of stone-hard ice built in antiquity by cloud giants to fight dragons from the sky. Owned (and barely tolerated of the cult) by the cloud giant **Blagothkus**, piloted by his dead wife **Esclarotta**'s bound spirit, Skyreach is the cult's hoard-transport to the Well of Dragons. [Rezmir](npcs/rezmir.md) has already given the order to prepare for departure. Three plausible paths: **smash-and-grab** (storm the castle while it's still docked), **infiltrate** (slip in under disguise or a supply wagon and work from within), or **ally with Blagothkus** (play on his fears, let him crash or fly the castle north while the party deals with the cultists). The chapter closes *Hoard of the Dragon Queen*.

**Milestone.** Crashing or seizing the castle → 8th level.

---

## Timing the departure (the chapter's master clock)

Skyreach leaves when it's most dramatic — but three tempo scenarios drive the DM's call:

### Smash it before it flies
- Castle defences **plus** Parnast reinforcements: Captain Othelstan + 1 veteran lieutenant + 6 guards + 3 dragonclaws, with Othelstan and his lieutenant riding the pair of wyverns from the Parnast stable.

### Dawdle in the village (an hour or more, poking around)
- Castle leaves **one hour after the cult realises the infiltration**.
- After it's gone, villagers drop the pretence; Othelstan + veteran + 6 guards try to capture or kill; Othelstan calls 3 dragonclaws, one of whom releases the stable's two wyverns.

### Last recourse (party is slow and the castle takes off without them)
- Gundalin or another sympathetic villager suggests rushing the stable, mounting the two wyverns, and giving chase.
- The castle is spotted up to 5 miles out; wyverns fly faster than the castle.
- A few rounds before Othelstan surrounds the stable and demands surrender.

### Supply-wagon infiltration (always available while docked)
- Cultists haul wagons of treasure and supplies to the gate every 1–2 hours during daylight; party can hide in or walk behind one and hear the pass-phrase called at the gate.

## Key NPCs

### Blagothkus — cloud giant, castle owner (area 22C)
- Blue-skinned, snowy white hair, hulking morningstar. Two ogre stewards always at his elbow; an **air elemental** is bound to his bedroom (bonus action to summon; follows commands but can't leave the room).
- Not an enthusiastic cult supporter — sees the cult as a way to stir giantkind out of complacency back into action against dragons. Playing along while building his own small army of giants north of the Spine of the World.
- No hostility to "small folk". Will openly discuss the castle's destination with the Well of Dragons if the party talks to him politely.
- **If swayed to the party's side**: locks himself in the steering tower (area 19) and flies the castle north while the party deals with the cultists and the dragon.
- **If anyone tries to seize the castle from him**: he goes to the steering tower and crashes it in the Spine of the World near the Miklos Glacier (see Esclarotta below for the mechanism).
- **If killed on the castle**: his spirit replaces Esclarotta's and does the same thing — a revenge crash into the Miklos Glacier. Rath Modar knows this, and if the dragon is slain and the cultists routed, Rath will murder Blagothkus specifically to crash the castle under the party's feet before escaping by *fly*.
- Ogre stewards defend him to the death.

### Esclarotta — the pilot (area 19 / area 21)
- Spirit of Blagothkus's deceased cloud-giant wife, bound to the castle by a magic that **cannot be dispelled**. She operates all propulsion, buoyancy, and weather. Castle will not obey the cult.
- Operates 18 glowing white command runes in the steering chamber (Dwarvish or Giant to interpret them). Can "lock out" anyone who misuses them.
- **DC 15 Arcana** senses the intelligence at work.
- **DC 14 Persuasion** to have her act on a request; advantage if the speaker speaks Giant.
- Kind-hearted, hates the cult infestation, longs for news of Blagothkus.
- If anyone wrecks the steering chamber she triggers the alarm rune and Blagothkus + 2 stewards arrive in 3 rounds.
- Her sarcophagus is in area 21 (buried ice cyst), reachable only by the blue-tower teleport (see below).

### [Rezmir](npcs/rezmir.md) — Wyrmspeaker (area 11)
- In her locked suite with **two guard drakes** and a **rug of smothering** on the floor (rug waits for someone to walk onto it before attacking; Rezmir and drakes wait for the rug to open before springing).
- Holds keys to her room, her chest, and the storeroom (area 13).
- **If captured**: refuses to cooperate. Prefers death. Berates and insults the party.
- **Her iron-banded chest** is her treasure *and* a trap:
  - Padlock: **DC 20 Dex** with thieves' tools; fail by 5+ triggers a **poison-needle trap**.
  - Needle found on **DC 20 Investigation**, disarmed on **DC 15 Dex**.
  - Wyvern poison on trigger: **DC 13 Con**, 24 (7d6) poison on fail, half on save.
  - **Magically attuned to Rezmir — the contents teleport to the Well of Dragons the instant she dies.** So the chest has to be opened with her alive.
  - **Contents (if captured intact)**: the **Black Dragon Mask** (appendix C), peridot necklace on gold chain (400 gp), silver dragon-head torc (200 gp), 6 moonstones (300 gp total), 20 loose pearls (3,000 gp total), 600 sp, 200 gp, 50 pp.

### Rath Modar — Red Wizard of Thay (area 12)
- Running the summoning-logistics end of the cult operation from his skull-ceilinged study.
- **Expert at seeing through illusions and deceptions.** Suspicious of strangers, especially the wounded or inappropriately equipped.
- **Escape route**: a 35 × 20 ft section of ice wall between two windows **vanishes for 1 minute when touched**, opening onto an outdoor landing platform. His SOP: invisibility + *fly* + touch wall + leap. The living gargoyle in his study covers his retreat; the other three are statues. **Treat his escape as very likely** unless the party seals it fast.
- **Loot**: spell scrolls of *dimension door*, *feather fall*, *fireball*; **staff of fire**; letters from Severin exposing the cult-Red Wizard links; letters from Thay revealing that some Red Wizards are *not* onboard with the cult's plans; a book in Infernal titled ***"Beyond the Iron Gates"*** — the first chapters cover devil summoning, the **final chapter** describes the mass-summoning + hundreds-of-souls ritual to haul Tiamat bodily out of the Nine Hells. The actual formulae and chants are **not** in this volume.
- Delivering the letters to Leosin, Ontharr, or another allied contact → **1,000 XP**.

### [Azbara Jos](npcs/azbara-jos.md) — Red Wizard (area 14)
- Rath Modar's associate; usually with Rath in area 12 when both are present (mid scholarly argument that clams up when strangers arrive).
- His own quarters (area 14) carry a **second vanishing-wall escape route** onto a landing platform.

### Sandesyl Morgia — vampire (area 18)
- Moon-elf vampire of the **old Sammaster faction** — joined the cult long before Severin, talks about "the true path" of raising dragons as dracoliches. Hates the new cult leadership but is forced to work with them.
- **Active by night only**; patrols both courtyards.
- Lives in the crumbling tower (area 18); coffin on the top floor, guarded day and night by **2 vampire spawn consorts** (moon elves). She summons both as reinforcements if outnumbered.

### Glazhael the Cloudchaser — adult white dragon (area 25)
- Lairs in the hollowed ice tunnels above the cult's main hoard. Vain, fanatically devoted to Tiamat, and a bit dim — sees visitors as either servants (bring food) or enemies (don't).
- **Rarely talks to cultists other than Talis** and is **suspicious of Rezmir** and the rest of the inner circle.
- Flatter him to a ludicrous degree and he'll listen; offer tribute (food or treasure) and he'll spare lives; challenge him and get the breath weapon.
- **Combat**: clings to the vault ceiling; opens with breath weapon + Frightful Presence; melee until breath recharges. **Flees at <40 HP**, navigating narrow tunnels with wings tucked.
- If he gets outside, he shouts *"They're after the treasure!"* in Draconic — **castle-wide alert**.
- A party that lures him into a narrow tunnel gives him **disadvantage on melee attacks**.

### Wiglof + Hulda — stone giants (area 10)
- Guests of Blagothkus, worried about his cult alliance but supportive; helping with castle repairs and defence.
- **Wiglof** is performing an **augury ritual** over his iron cauldron to try to see the outcome of the alliance. **Attacking Wiglof disrupts the ritual and enrages him.**
- **Hulda** is Wiglof's companion and bodyguard; meets any hostility with brutal force.
- Both know the steering tower (area 19) is the control point and that only giants can enter.
- **If defeated and either threatened or bribed, they might cooperate.**

### Captain Othelstan — Parnast's cult commander
- Dragonsoul and cult veteran; Rezmir's deputy on the ground. Commands the Parnast garrison (1 veteran lieutenant + 6 guards + 3 dragonclaws + 2 wyverns from the stable).
- Monitors visitors and merchandise through the village; characters asking for someone in charge are sent to him with a warning that he has no time for outlaws.

### Gundalin the Wheelwright — the party's inside man
- Male human; makes wheels and fixes axles for the villagers, merchants, and woodsfolk. The one villager actively willing to help outsiders against the cult.
- Found at the Golden Tankard (silent in public) or at his small shop on the square. Often tries to catch strangers' eye; speaks only in safe havens.
- **Knows all the rumours + both pass-phrases** (*"Tiamat, Our Mother and Strength"* and *"Hail Blagothkus"*).

### Raggnar Redtooth — Golden Tankard owner
- Secretly on the cult's take — serves travellers ale while quietly dispatching a runner to Othelstan. **Unarmoured veteran (AC 10)**, weapons behind the bar.

- **Cult dragonwings** — 10 at any time in the cultist barracks (area 9), half sleeping / half idle

### Alarm propagation

- **Combat in the lower courtyard** → alerts ogres (area 6), cultists (area 9), stone giants (area 10), kobolds (area 15). One cultist runs to Rezmir (area 11); she arrives in **3 rounds** with her two guard drakes.
- **Loud disturbance in the upper courtyard** → investigated by ogres in area 20.
- **Attack on Wiglof** → disrupts his augury, enrages him; Hulda responds instantly.
- **Any ogre on the blue tower (area 17) yells** → castle-wide alarm.
- **Wrecking the steering chamber (area 19)** → Esclarotta triggers the alarm rune; **Blagothkus + 2 ogre stewards arrive in 3 rounds**.
- **Glazhael escapes the vault** → shouts *"They're after the treasure!"* in Draconic → castle-wide alert.

## Parnast

### Rumours and information (DC 18 Persuasion, one per success — doled out in order)
1. **Rezmir** is the highest-ranking cultist known to frequent Parnast. If still alive and free, she's gone into hiding; no one in the village knows where, though they'll say Skyreach is the most likely place.
2. Rezmir's deputy is **Captain Othelstan**, cult veteran and dragonsoul. He commands the Parnast cult forces and monitors all visitors and merchandise through the village.
3. The cult has controlled Parnast for over a year. They brought in a company of thugs to take over; some villagers were already infiltrators, others signed up when it became clear the cult is rich and has a private army.
4. The cult keeps **trained wyverns** in the village stables to reach the castle while it's airborne. (No one in the village, including Othelstan, knows where the castle goes after it leaves.)

**From Gundalin (or anyone turned friendly)**: the two castle-approach pass-phrases — *"Tiamat, Our Mother and Strength"* or *"Hail Blagothkus"*.

### Area 1 — Golden Tankard
- Rustic tavern on the square, yellow-tankard sign over the door, muttonchopped Raggnar running the bar.
- Beer 3 cp, strong mead 1 sp, sausage / cabbage / heavy bread 4 cp a plate. No rooms — if pressed, Raggnar offers *his own* chamber for 10 gp as a stalling tactic.
- Prying info out of Raggnar or his clientele is hard — the villagers deliberately ask after the party's journey, families, origin, and route to run down the clock while a messenger goes to Othelstan.
- **The Golden Tankard itself** is a magic item — a **tankard of plenty** Raggnar found years ago and uses as his serving stein.

### Area 2 — Stable
- Doors sealed, "out of business" — actually holds **2 trained wyverns** for the cult's aerial shuttle. A bellowing roar is audible to anyone listening carefully.
- **Wyvern riding** (sidebar): trained to carry up to 2 Medium or 6 Small riders.
  - **DC 10 Animal Handling** to put the harness on; fail by 5+ → wyvern objects and attacks the character once with its tail stinger as a reaction.
  - **DC 15 Animal Handling** (action) to make the wyvern fly or move in a specific direction; fail → wyvern ignores the rider until the rider's next turn.
  - After two successful commands, the wyvern obeys for the next hour (or until it takes damage or the rider dismounts).
- Stealing one or both wyverns from under the cult's nose → **4,000 XP** divided among the party (more than killing them).

### Area 4 — Shrine
- Combined wooden shrine for woodcutters, housing four seasonal deities.
  - **Angharradh** (spring, elven) — face and hands chopped off.
  - **Auril** (winter) — a dead squirrel at her feet.
  - **Chauntea** (summer) — untouched.
  - **Mielikki** (autumn) — freshly painted and set with cups, bits of bread, and small sacrifices. The cult's working favourite.

### Village square
- A well at the centre — 40 ft deep, cold, pure. **5 human guards** loyal to the cult watch it at all times. If confronted, one runs to fetch Othelstan while the villagers scatter. Only **Gundalin** stays to watch (from behind a heavy wooden oxcart).

## Castle general features

- Carved from **opaque ice magically reinforced to be as hard as stone**. Walls deflect ground attacks and block the wind when airborne.
- **Docked** in a wide, fog-shrouded ravine on Parnast's outskirts. Approach via the ravine is covered by the fog — party can get close unseen.
- **Ceilings** 30 ft (cloud-giant scale).
- **Doors** made of 1-ft-thick ice (as hard as stone, half the weight), iron hinges, giant-sized: 20 ft tall, 8 ft wide, handles 10 ft off the floor.
- **Fog cover** — lower courtyard is heavily obscured by fog at night to 40 ft (the height of the walls); upper courtyard lightly obscured to 30 ft. Daytime courtyards are clear.

## Outside the castle

### Area 5 — Main gate
- Lowered drawbridge spans a foggy moat; portcullis up behind; covered gateway to the lower courtyard. Figures loom in the gateway (see 6A).
- Supply wagons roll in every 1–2 hours during daylight. Password is spoken aloud at each cart — **free intelligence for a party within earshot.**
- Cultists haul supplies to the kitchen (area 15); treasure chests go down to the dragon's hoard (area 25).

### Area 6A — Covered gateway
- Portcullis up, two life-size 18-ft "cloud giant" statues flanking the gate. They are **Huge stone golems**.
- **Without the pass-phrase**, a creature passing through activates one golem. A second unsanctioned passage activates the second.
- **Only a cloud giant can command the golems to return to their posts** once activated.

### Area 6B — Gate towers
- **North tower 120 ft, south tower 80 ft.** 3 ogres atop each with javelins **and** a mounted **ballista** (AC 10, HP 50, immune poison and psychic; action to load/fire; once per round).
- Trapdoor in each roof → spiral ice staircase down the interior wall → ground-floor unlocked giant door opening on the lower courtyard (area 7) or the cultist barracks (area 9).
- **Winches**: drawbridge in the north tower's ground floor, portcullis in the other. One ogre operates each winch. **Standing orders: do not leave the winch even if the general alarm sounds.** Turning the crank is an action + **DC 15 Str**; half-raises or half-lowers (2 actions for a full open/close).

### Area 8 — Castle stables (off the lower courtyard)
- 20 × 20 ft double doors; stench of droppings, rotted flesh, cracked bones on the floor.
- **2 wyverns** inside (separate from the two in Parnast), trained as aerial mounts but **hungry** — they attack anyone they don't recognise.
- **Loot**: 4 fine wyvern bridles with jade and mithral bits — **500 gp each**.

## Lower courtyard zone

### Area 7 — Lower courtyard
- Overhung by the upper courtyard, held aloft by sweeping 100 ft ice arches. Ice doors to all outbuildings and the main keep.
- **DC 22 Perception** finds a hidden opening in the wall opposite the main gate → spiral staircase (area 7A).
- **Combat here alerts areas 6, 9, 10, and 15.** One cultist runs to Rezmir (area 11); she arrives in 3 rounds with her guard drakes.

### Area 7A — Spiral staircase
- Connects upper and lower courtyards and the tunnels to the main vault (area 25). Crunchy ice-frost on the steps — **cannot be climbed quietly** (but not slippery).

### Area 9 — Cultist barracks
- 14 fur-covered beds, chests, table, chairs. Smells of unwashed bedding.
- **10 cult dragonwings** at any time — half sleeping, half idle. Don't roam unless an alarm sounds, they hear courtyard combat, or the gate ogres yell for reinforcements.

### Area 10 — Stone giants' hall
- Two 20 × 8 ft archways to the lower courtyard — any loud disturbance there alerts the giants.
- Frost-glazed room: 9-ft stone table surrounded by 3 giant stone chairs, iron cauldron etched with runes on the table, large iron chest in the far corner.
- **If no alarm has sounded**: Wiglof gazing into the cauldron (augury), Hulda sitting nearby. Party gets a surprise round if the alarm hasn't gone up.
- **Loot**:
  - **32 small-but-perfect gemstones** (500 gp each → **16,000 gp**) secured by a **globe of force** inside the **iron chest** (6 × 4 × 4 ft, 500 lb). Chest lid: **DC 12 Str** + action to lift. Globe of force breaks with **antimagic field** or **dispel magic (DC 16)**.
  - **Iron cauldron** — 50 lb, 25 gp, substitutes for the material components of *augury*.
  - Masonry tools in the chest (useless to smaller creatures).
- **If defeated and either threatened or bribed, the stone giants might cooperate.**

### Area 11 — Rezmir's suite
- Door **always locked**; **DC 25 Dex** with thieves' tools, or knock, or walk in behind a kobold on legitimate business.
- Icy 10-ft-high room, brazier, large bed, desk with Rezmir's iron-banded chest on it. **Rug of smothering** covers the icy floor.
- Unless lured elsewhere by an alarm, **Rezmir + 2 guard drakes** are here. In combat they let the rug attack first.
- Chest full details above under Rezmir's NPC entry — **open it with her alive or the Black Dragon Mask teleports to the Well of Dragons.**

### Area 12 — Rath Modar's study
- Door unlocked.
- Hundreds of horse skulls nailed to the ceiling (ornament, no value). Thick carpets, desks, chairs, lecterns strewn with books, scrolls, potion vials, bits of meat and fur.
- **4 gargoyles frozen in place around the room** — 3 statues, 1 living. The living one serves Rath and covers his escape.
- Rath Modar lives here; **Azbara Jos** is usually with him when both are alive and present. If both are here they're mid-argument when the party arrives and clam up instantly.
- Full escape-route + loot under Rath's NPC entry.

### Area 13 — Storeroom
- Secured, locked. **DC 17 Dex** with thieves' tools. Rezmir and Blagothkus carry keys.
- Huge sides of beef, ham hocks, enormous barrels, hundreds of crates. All average quality; nothing remarkable beyond the natural refrigeration.

### Area 14 — Azbara Jos's quarters
- Comfortably furnished for a human.
- **Vanishing wall**: a large section of the outer wall opposite the door **vanishes for 1 minute when touched**, exposing the room to the elements and an outdoor landing platform. Second escape route out of the Red Wizard quarter.

### Area 15 — Kitchen
- Chaos of **20 kobolds** chopping, mixing, hauling, stirring great cauldrons. Could easily be mistaken for an alchemy lab.
- Huge beef sides, whole sheep, dried fish, bacon, onions, beans — vast amounts.
- **A griffon roosts on a ledge above the fray** — Blagothkus's pet, keeps the kobolds in line with a single shriek, and protects them if they come under attack.
- **Loot**: chests of black pepper, cinnamon, nutmeg (2 lb each, **130 gp total**).

## Upper courtyard zone

### Area 16 — Upper courtyard
- Tall slender ice towers and walls enclose a windswept open deck — the primary landing site for dragons, wyverns, and flying spellcasters while the castle's airborne.
- **Day**: 2d6 ogres practice javelin hurling here.
- **Night**: lightly obscured by fog to 30 ft; **Sandesyl Morgia** patrols.
- Characters landing on the upper courtyard must show a banner or cult token immediately or the ogres / vampire sound the alarm and attack.
- A loud disturbance up here is investigated by the ogres in area 20.
- **If disguised arrival succeeds**: they're taken to meet Blagothkus (if caught by ogres) or Rezmir (if caught by the vampire).

### Area 17 — Blue tower (Blagothkus's) — **the teleport link**
- Pale-blue-ice tower with mirror-like windows. **Iron-locked door, Blagothkus's only key**; **DC 15 Dex** with thieves' tools.
- 2 ogres on the top with javelins, rocks, and a ballista. They keep long coils of rope for exterior climbing.
- Interior: hollow 90-ft cylinder with **no stairs or ladders**.
- Any creature inside the tower that speaks **"Esclarotta"** is instantly teleported to **area 21 (Esclarotta's tomb)**. The tomb's return word is **"Blagothkus"** → ground floor of this tower.
- **If the ogres above start firing the ballista or dropping boulders — castle-wide alarm as soon as any ogre yells.**

### Area 18 — Crumbling tower (Sandesyl's)
- Nearly 100 ft tall, in shoddy repair — windows sealed with ice, cracks in the walls and roof.
- Two entrances: a base door (CANNOT be opened; blocked by 20 ft of packed icy debris on the ground floor) and a working door at the balcony **75 ft up**.
- Climbing the walls: cracked ice gives abundant handholds — **DC 10 Athletics**.
- **Balcony is weakened**: breaks away if **more than 150 lb** is placed on it.
- Original tower had 4 levels; all but the top are shattered — the ground floor is packed with 20 ft of icy debris.
- **Top level (Sandesyl's crypt)**: unlocked balcony door, no windows, coffin in the centre with grave dirt and no treasure; **2 vampire spawn consorts** guard it day and night. A short ice staircase hugs one wall and ends suddenly 50 ft above the lower debris.

### Area 19 — Steering tower
- **Separate tower from area 17.** Door **arcane-locked**; opens by giant, by *knock*, or by **DC 70 Strength** (effectively impossible for anyone else).
- Staircase of ice from the ground floor up to the steering chamber above.
- Chamber: walls and ceiling encrusted with moonstones, emeralds, mithral wands, turquoise-and-gold spheres, dozens of copper levers. After a moment the walls seem to vanish → perfect aerial view in all directions. **18 glowing white runes drift about the room like snowflakes.**
- **Esclarotta** operates the castle via the runes; anyone inside can touch a rune to issue a command, interpretable in Dwarvish or Giant; Esclarotta can lock out misusers.
- **DC 15 Arcana** detects the intelligence at work; **DC 14 Persuasion** to get her to act on a request (advantage if the speaker speaks Giant).
- Wrecking the chamber: Esclarotta triggers the alarm rune → Blagothkus + 2 stewards arrive in 3 rounds.
- **Stripping the control jewels** for loot (emeralds, turquoise, moonstones, jade, amber, chunks of mithral) → **10,000 gp**. Destroys the castle's propulsion and weather control; the castle drifts north on the wind and crashes on the **Miklos Glacier in the Spine of the World**. Repair requires long, expensive work, or *wish*.
- **If Blagothkus dies on the castle**, his spirit takes over the chamber and crashes it in the same place out of spite.

### Area 20 — Ogre barracks
- 12 ogres sleeping on piles of fur unless an alarm or disturbance has drawn them elsewhere.
- **Party gets surprise** if they can approach the chamber quietly.
- **Loot**: 30 minutes of searching → 800 sp, 300 gp. **DC 20 Investigation** finds a pale blue sapphire worth **500 gp** wedged in a crevice.
- **Interrogation (captured / charmed / fast-talked)**: they'll volunteer that they serve Blagothkus, whose wife was slain years ago; know of "the wizard in red robes" (Rath Modar), "the dragon talker" (Rezmir), and "a big white dragon that lives in the caves" (Glazhael). **DC 19 Persuasion** per additional titbit:
  1. A vampire watches the castle at night and sometimes feeds on them.
  2. The cult uses the castle to visit sites without roads or portals — they don't know where other than *"the green forests"*, *"that stretch of the moors"*, *"the old mountains"*.
  3. The castle's mists and navigation are controlled by Blagothkus's magic — without him, the castle won't fly. (Actually **untrue** — it's Esclarotta — but it's what Blagothkus told them and they believe it.)

### Area 21 — Esclarotta's tomb
- Buried icy cyst — **no physical entry or egress**. Reach it only via the blue-tower teleport (area 17: speak *"Esclarotta"* inside).
- Oval ice cave, 30-ft ceiling lined with icicles, broken ice all around a **massive white marble sarcophagus** (20 × 10 × 10 ft) sculpted to the likeness of a female giant with long flowing hair.
- Dwarvish runes on the base spell **ESCLAROTTA**.
- Lid: **DC 30 Strength** to push aside. Bones of Blagothkus's deceased wife within. **No treasure.**
- Speaking **"Blagothkus"** inside the tomb → teleports back to the ground floor of area 17.

### Area 22 — Blagothkus's keep
- 70-ft tall tower, parapet rooftop, **two unlocked ground-level doors, no windows**. Interior split into two levels (30-ft ceilings each) with an ice staircase up the interior wall.

#### Area 22A — Rooftop
- 3 ogres + ballista.

#### Area 22B — Lower level (honour-guard hall)
- **Chime on the inside of each door rings when the door opens** — early warning for everyone above.
- Staircase up the inside wall to a landing with a door. Giant-size furniture, table + 4 chairs. Ice murals depicting an army of giants — hill, frost, fire, stone, cloud.
- **4 "honour guard" ogres** in fancy plumed helmets. The helmets are their badge and could be revoked, which keeps them alert and loyal.

#### Area 22C — Upper level (Blagothkus's bedroom)
- Ice murals of cloud giants riding giant birds. Enormous bed with cloud-ice headboard, bear furs, 2 large wooden chests at the foot.
- **Blagothkus** sits cross-legged on the floor while 2 ogre stewards comb his snowy white hair. His morningstar leans against the bed within reach.
- **Air elemental** magically bound to the room: Blagothkus summons it as a **bonus action**; it follows his commands but cannot leave the room and remains until dismissed.
- **If ogres above or below him have raised the alarm, Blagothkus cannot be surprised.**
- **Alarm or sudden appearance startles him**; he reaches for his weapon. **Pretending to be cultists** makes him furious at the intrusion but he doesn't attack unless provoked. The ogres defend him to the death.
- **Loot** (chests, unlocked): **12,000 gp** in mixed silver and gold; one chest also holds a **bag of holding**, 4 gold bars (1,000 gp each), 40 silver bars (100 gp each). Each bar ~5 lb.

### Area 23 — Giant guest rooms
- Giant-sized furnishings.
- Each room's outer wall has a **1-minute vanishing-wall section** → outdoor icy landing platform. Long ago, giants landed roc mounts here.

### Area 24 — Kobold servants' quarters
- 20 kobolds trying to sleep on small piles of bedding and fur. Exhausted, ignore the characters unless in great danger. **No treasure.**
- **DC 12 Persuasion** per bit of info (no check if the party is convincingly dressed as cultists). **First failure** → the kobolds unite in terror, go silent, and start screaming for help if pressed further.
  1. An elf vampire lives in the crumbling tower (area 18).
  2. Blagothkus still speaks to his dead wife; her spirit is the castle's pilot and protector. If the giant dies, all the castle's magic is undone.
  3. "The human dragon-cult people don't really understand dragons the way that kobolds do. Dragons are fine creatures, but grumpy. The treasure here is for the queen of all dragons, to keep her from being grumpy."
  4. A red-robed wizard is working with the cultists; his room (area 12) is off-limits to kobolds.
  5. **The white dragon Cloudchaser loves frozen meat.** Toss it into the cavern (area 25) and run.

## The dragon's vault

### Area 25 — Ice tunnels and main vault
- The castle's icy core is hollowed out with tunnels that break the surface at multiple points. Glazhael enters and leaves by a wide funnel-shaped passageway that narrows toward the main vault.
- The tunnels are walls of partly-transparent blue ice — things visible embedded in them include coins, helmets, livestock, a handful of kobolds, and an ogre or two. Frost on the floor, crunchy underfoot.
- The main vault: glittering ice cavern, jagged walls, icicles the size of stalactites. **Two-level** — egg-shaped upper level with a sheer ledge overlooking a **sunken level 30 ft below** where the hoard rests under an icy glaze.
- **Glazhael clings to the ceiling above the hoard**, wings tucked in tight, claws gripping the ice.
- Clever players can lure Glazhael into a narrow tunnel → **disadvantage on his melee attacks**. See his NPC entry above for full combat behaviour.

#### The hoard
- Floor **carpeted in gold, silver, copper, and jewels, sealed under a sheet of ice**. Dozens of old human skulls and bones also frozen in.
- **Melting the ice requires fire spells, or a long wait with bonfires.**
- Totals:
  - **500,000 cp**
  - **100,000 sp**
  - **5,000 gp**
  - Frozen chest with **800 pp + 21 small blue sapphires (300 gp each = 6,300 gp)**
  - Frozen **potion of gaseous form**
  - **+1 longsword**, **+1 longbow**, **+1 leather armour**, **bracers of defence**

#### Development
- **Glazhael slain** → the cultists are enraged, call out the ogres, wake Sandesyl, and set the Red Wizards to hunting. **Castle on high alert** until the party is found and killed.

## Resolution branches

### Smash-and-grab
- Immediate assault. Deal with gate golems, tower ballistae, the main-courtyard reinforcement cascade (areas 6/9/10/15 + Rezmir in 3 rounds), plus the Parnast-side reinforcements if still in or near the village (Othelstan + lieutenant + 6 guards + 3 dragonclaws + 2 wyverns).
- Priority targets by what they unlock: **Rezmir alive** → open her chest without the mask vanishing; **Glazhael down** → access the melted hoard; **Rath Modar down fast** → prevent the invisibility + fly + vanishing-wall escape. If anyone reaches Blagothkus and kills him, **Rath Modar's fallback is to murder the cloud giant deliberately to crash the castle under the party before escaping by fly.**

### Infiltration
- Supply-wagon or disguise entry past area 5. Kobold-servant quarter (area 24) is a cheap intel source (DC 12 Persuasion). Rath Modar's study is the single highest-info stop — letters from Severin + the *Beyond the Iron Gates* book cover the whole summoning arc.
- Rezmir's chest needs her alive; the Black Dragon Mask is the chapter's main prize for the cult-knowledge arc.

### Deal with Blagothkus
- Play on his concerns about cult betrayal (areas 10 and 22C); Esclarotta can be enlisted in parallel through the steering chamber conversation (DC 14 Persuasion, Giant advantage). Wiglof + Hulda in area 10 may cooperate if defeated and either threatened or bribed.
- Payoff: Blagothkus locks himself in the steering tower (area 19) and flies Skyreach north while the party handles the cult and the dragon inside. End-state canonicalised in *The Rise of Tiamat*.

### Crash
- Any path that kills Blagothkus on the castle, or a party stripping the steering-chamber jewels (10,000 gp), ends with Skyreach drifting north and crashing on the Miklos Glacier in the Spine of the World. Rath Modar may force this ending deliberately as his fallback. Follow-ups covered in *The Rise of Tiamat*.
