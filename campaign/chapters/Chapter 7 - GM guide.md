# Chapter 7 — Hunting Lodge, GM guide

## The chapter in a paragraph

Characters who chased Rezmir or Azbara through the Castle Naerytar portal land high in the Greypeak Mountains, at a cluster of standing-stone portals in front of a cult hunting lodge. The lodge is Talis the White's — a Wearer of Purple who runs it as a central cult meeting-place while quietly scheming to discredit her rivals, [Rezmir](npcs/rezmir.md) and [Varram](npcs/varram.md) (the module's two wyrmspeakers she most hates). Three plausible paths: **fight** through the lodge, **cut a deal** with Talis against Rezmir, or **skip** the lodge and follow Rezmir's trail to Parnast. If Rezmir is alive she's already on the move — she does **not** warn Talis of pursuit, because she's happy for the party to kill her rival for her.

The chapter is also where the players can finally hear the big picture — the five wyrmspeakers, the flying castle, the Red Wizards, Tiamat's planned return. Most of that comes out of a surrendered or negotiating Talis.

**Milestone.** Clearing the lodge *or* forging a deal with Talis → 7th level.

## General lodge features

- **Ceilings** 8 ft (feast hall is the exception — 30 ft).
- **Light** only from fireplaces and candles. With shutters closed, dark inside.
- **Entry points**: front door; kitchen door; shuttered windows (**DC 10 Dex** or the shutter bangs — **DC 12 Stealth** to open one without alerting occupants); hole in the moss roof above area 17 (**DC 15 Athletics** to climb up, drops into the armoury).
- **Claw-scarred floors** — **DC 20 Nature** reveals these are ambush-drake marks, not hounds.

### Cult recognition signals
- **Tiamat salute** — open hand, five fingers extended.
- **Pass phrases** — *"All hail Tiamat!"* and *"They shall rise!"*
- **Dress tells** — five-coloured band, or red/blue/green coded stripes.
- **Castle-approach banner** — five-stripe (black/blue/green/red/white) on a lance. Lives in area 15. (Owed to the party on completion of mission 1.)
