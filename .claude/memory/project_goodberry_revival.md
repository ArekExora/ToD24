Nyx prepares **Goodberries** before risky encounters and distributes them to teammates; the berries become the party's pooled stock of single-use revival items. A teammate dropped to 0 HP is fed one of Nyx's berries (any holder may do so as an action) and recovers to exactly 1 HP. **All recoveries-to-1-HP in this campaign use a Nyx Goodberry**; recoveries that bring a PC back above 1 HP are something else (Cure Wounds, healing potion, etc.).

**Why:** The convention is canon for the party but isn't visible from PC sheets alone (Goodberry is just one spell among many on Nyx's list). Without this knowledge, transcript references to a *baya* / *valla* / "vial" handed over before combat get misread as healing potions, leading to wrong narrative descriptions in the minutes (the 0→1 HP revival is mechanically Goodberry, not a vial).

**How to apply:**
- When the transcript shows a teammate revived from 0 HP to 1 HP, render the mechanic as one of Nyx's Goodberries — never as a "potion" or "vial". The party-pool framing (Nyx distributes before combat, anyone can administer) is the correct narrative shape.
- Spanish *baya* in the transcripts is the right word; STT often renders it as *valla* (homophone, also "fence"). Don't canonicalize to *valla*; treat *valla* as a STT garble of *baya* when it appears in a healing context.
- For 0→N>1 recoveries, default to Cure Wounds / Lay on Hands / a healing potion instead — Goodberry can't deliver more than 1 HP per berry consumed.
- Convention is documented in `campaign/pcs/nyx.md` under Signature; the canonical place to verify Nyx's spell list and feats.
